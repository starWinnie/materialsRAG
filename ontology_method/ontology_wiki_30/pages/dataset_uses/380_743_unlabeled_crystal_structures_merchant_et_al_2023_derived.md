# 380,743 Unlabeled Crystal Structures (Merchant et al. 2023-derived)

## Ontology Type
DatasetUse

## Usage Description
A curated set of 380,743 crystal structures generated by a deep generative tool (Merchant et al. 2023), filtered to remove duplicates with downstream benchmarks and physically or chemically irrational structures. Contains full crystal structure representations: atom types (from the 95-element periodic table), fractional atomic positions in the unit cell, and six lattice constants (a, b, c, α, β, γ). Used exclusively for unsupervised denoising pre-training—specifically to train models to reconstruct masked atom types, perturbed atom positions, and perturbed lattice constants—thereby learning generalizable structural representations before fine-tuning on property prediction tasks.
