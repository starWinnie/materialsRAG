# 80_Inverse design of catalytic active sites via interpretable topology-based deep generative models - Task 1

## Task Description

Designing catalytic active sites with targeted *OH adsorption energies for oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) on high-entropy alloy (HEA) surfaces via inverse design — i.e., generating novel atomic-scale surface structures (bridge sites on IrPdPtRhRu HEA facets) that achieve optimal adsorption energy (within 0.1 eV above Pt(111)) by jointly controlling coordination geometry and ligand composition.

## Supporting Datasets

### [IrPdPtRhRu HEA *OH Adsorption Dataset](../datasets/IrPdPtRhRu_HEA_OH_Adsorption_Dataset.md)

- Usage page: [usage note](../dataset_uses/80_Inverse_design_of_catalytic_active_sites_via_interpretable_topology-based_deep_generati.md)
- Original title in paper: IrPdPtRhRu HEA *OH Adsorption Dataset
- Link: https://zenodo.org/records/15266141

A DFT-calculated dataset of 1159 labeled adsorption configurations, each representing a *OH adsorbed bridge site on various Miller-index facets ((111), (100), (110), (211), (532)) of IrPdPtRhRu high-entropy alloys; includes Cartesian coordinates of relaxed surface structures, elemental identities of bridge atoms and first/second-nearest neighbors, and relative *OH adsorption energies (ΔE_OH − ΔE_OH,Pt(111)) used to train and validate the PGH-VAEs model for inverse design and property prediction.

### [Semi-supervised Expanded *OH Adsorption Dataset](../datasets/Semi-supervised_Expanded_OH_Adsorption_Dataset.md)

- Usage page: [usage note](../dataset_uses/80_Inverse_design_of_catalytic_active_sites_via_interpretable_topology-based_deep_generati.md)
- Original title in paper: Semi-supervised Expanded *OH Adsorption Dataset
- Link: https://zenodo.org/records/15266141

An augmented dataset of 4636 total samples, comprising the original 1159 DFT-labeled structures plus 3477 pseudo-labeled structures generated by modifying atomic species in averaged DFT-optimized Cartesian coordinates and predicting their *OH adsorption energies using a Gradient Boosted Regression (GBR) model trained on PGH-derived topological features; used exclusively for training the PGH-VAEs generative model while preserving original DFT data for unbiased evaluation.
