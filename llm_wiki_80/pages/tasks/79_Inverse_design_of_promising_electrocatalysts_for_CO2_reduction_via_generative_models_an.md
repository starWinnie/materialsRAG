# 79_Inverse design of promising electrocatalysts for CO2 reduction via generative models and bird swarm algorithm - Task 1

## Task Description

Designing novel alloy electrocatalyst surface structures with high activity and selectivity for the CO2 reduction reaction (CO2RR), specifically by generating surfaces predicted to exhibit optimal CO adsorption energy (ΔECO ≈ −0.67 eV) while simultaneously satisfying constraints on hydrogen adsorption energy (to suppress HER) and thermodynamic stability (via formation energy).

## Supporting Datasets

### [GASpy database](../datasets/GASpy_database.md)

- Usage page: [usage note](../dataset_uses/79_Inverse_design_of_promising_electrocatalysts_for_CO2_reduction_via_generative_models_an.md)
- Original title in paper: GASpy database
- Link: None

A database of catalyst surfaces created by Tran et al., containing diverse surface structures used to pretrain the CDVAE generative model; it provides the structural foundation for generating new alloy surfaces and serves as the training data for both the CDVAE and the supervised DimeNet++ models predicting ΔECO and ΔEH, enabling property-guided inverse design of CO2RR-active surfaces.

### [OQMD dataset](../datasets/OQMD_dataset.md)

- Usage page: [usage note](../dataset_uses/79_Inverse_design_of_promising_electrocatalysts_for_CO2_reduction_via_generative_models_an.md)

- Link: https://oqmd.org/

The Open Quantum Materials Database, used to train the MEGNet model for predicting formation energy (Ef); this dataset provides thermodynamically stable inorganic compound structures and energies, allowing the framework to evaluate and filter generated surfaces for thermodynamic stability without performing costly DFT calculations.

### [Generated_surfaces (Figshare dataset)](../datasets/Generated_surfaces_Figshare_dataset.md)

- Usage page: [usage note](../dataset_uses/79_Inverse_design_of_promising_electrocatalysts_for_CO2_reduction_via_generative_models_an.md)
- Original title in paper: Generated_surfaces (Figshare dataset)
- Link: https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Generated_surfaces/2798653161

A curated collection of 110 DFT-validated alloy surface structures generated by MAGECS and screened for CO2RR activity, selectivity, and stability; it contains DFT-calculated ΔECO, ΔEH, and formation energies, and was used to benchmark ML model accuracy and support experimental validation—serving as the final candidate set for synthesis and characterization.
