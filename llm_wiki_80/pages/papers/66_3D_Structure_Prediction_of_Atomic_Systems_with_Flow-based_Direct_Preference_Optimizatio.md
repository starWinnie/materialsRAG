### [Task 1](../tasks/66_3D_Structure_Prediction_of_Atomic_Systems_with_Flow-based_Direct_Preference_Optimizatio.md)

Predicting high-fidelity 3D atomic structures of antibody variable domains (specifically CDR regions) given their amino acid sequences and contextual framework/antigen structures, with the goal of generating conformations closely aligned to experimentally determined ground-truth structures while suppressing hallucinated or physically invalid predictions.

- Tags: 3D structure prediction, antibody modeling, conformation generation
- Datasets: [SAbDab](../datasets/SAbDab.md), [DiffAb test set](../datasets/DiffAb_test_set.md)

### [Task 2](../tasks/66_3D_Structure_Prediction_of_Atomic_Systems_with_Flow-based_Direct_Preference_Optimizatio.md)

Predicting stable 3D crystal structures (unit cells) solely from chemical composition, including lattice matrix and fractional atomic coordinates, with the objective of generating experimentally realistic structures that match known materials while reducing low-quality or mismatched predictions through preference-based alignment.

- Tags: crystal structure prediction, unit cell generation, materials discovery
- Datasets: [Perov-5](../datasets/Perov-5.md), [MP-20](../datasets/MP-20.md), [MPTS-52](../datasets/MPTS-52.md)
