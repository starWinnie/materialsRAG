### [Task 1](../tasks/79_Inverse_design_of_promising_electrocatalysts_for_CO2_reduction_via_generative_models_an.md)

Designing novel alloy electrocatalyst surface structures with high activity and selectivity for the CO2 reduction reaction (CO2RR), specifically by generating surfaces predicted to exhibit optimal CO adsorption energy (ΔECO ≈ −0.67 eV) while simultaneously satisfying constraints on hydrogen adsorption energy (to suppress HER) and thermodynamic stability (via formation energy).
