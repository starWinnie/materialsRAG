## Description Examples

- A curated collection of 110 DFT-validated alloy surface structures generated by MAGECS and screened for CO2RR activity, selectivity, and stability; it contains DFT-calculated ΔECO, ΔEH, and formation energies, and was used to benchmark ML model accuracy and support experimental validation—serving as the final candidate set for synthesis and characterization.
