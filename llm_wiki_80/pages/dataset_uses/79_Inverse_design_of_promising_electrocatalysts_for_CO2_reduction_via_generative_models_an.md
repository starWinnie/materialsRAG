# 79_Inverse design of promising electrocatalysts for CO2 reduction via generative models and bird swarm algorithm - Generated_surfaces (Figshare dataset)

## Dataset Use

A curated collection of 110 DFT-validated alloy surface structures generated by MAGECS and screened for CO2RR activity, selectivity, and stability; it contains DFT-calculated ΔECO, ΔEH, and formation energies, and was used to benchmark ML model accuracy and support experimental validation—serving as the final candidate set for synthesis and characterization.

## Task Context

Designing novel alloy electrocatalyst surface structures with high activity and selectivity for the CO2 reduction reaction (CO2RR), specifically by generating surfaces predicted to exhibit optimal CO adsorption energy (ΔECO ≈ −0.67 eV) while simultaneously satisfying constraints on hydrogen adsorption energy (to suppress HER) and thermodynamic stability (via formation energy).
