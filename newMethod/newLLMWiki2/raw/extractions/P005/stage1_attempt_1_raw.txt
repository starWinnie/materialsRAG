    "research_summary": "This paper proposes an ensemble machine learning framework—Electron Configuration models with Stacked Generalization (ECSG)—to predict the thermodynamic stability of inorganic compounds. ECSG integrates three base-level models grounded in distinct domain knowledge: electron configuration (via ECCNN), atomic properties (Magpie), and interatomic interactions (Roost). The framework achieves high predictive accuracy (AUC = 0.988 on JARVIS) and exceptional sample efficiency (requiring only 1/7 the data of existing models). It is validated across multiple databases (MP, OQMD, JARVIS), applied to unknown composition spaces (perovskite halides, Li-containing oxides, transition metal oxides), and demonstrated in two case studies: discovery of 2D wide bandgap semiconductors and double perovskite oxides—both confirmed via first-principles calculations."
