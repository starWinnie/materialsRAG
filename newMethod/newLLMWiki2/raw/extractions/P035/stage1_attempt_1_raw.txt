    "research_summary": "This paper presents a two-stage correction framework to improve the agreement of density functional theory (DFT) formation energies with experimental values. First, it leverages foundational machine learning interatomic potentials (fMLIPs), specifically PET-OMATPES trained at the r2SCAN level, to provide zero-shot formation energy estimates on DFT-relaxed geometries, reducing the mean absolute error (MAE) by more than 40% relative to GGA without additional DFT calculations. Second, it trains classical machine learning models (KRR, RF, GPR) in a delta-learning framework to predict the residual between these fMLIP-estimated energies and experiment, using the information-rich latent features of the fMLIP as input. This second stage further reduces the MAE below 50 meV/atom, bringing it down to values comparable with experimental uncertainty, while carefully balancing accuracy with minimal distortion to relative phase stability through regularization."
