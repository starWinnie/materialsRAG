## Evidence

- P041, PDF page 4, reEWC: "reEWC is a hybrid approach that combines the Replay and EWC methods. In the present work, the reEWC method is implemented by adopting the mini-batch training process, like in the Replay approach, while incorporating the EWC regularization term into the loss function during parameter updates."
- P041, PDF page 3, Technical backgrounds: "The loss function in EWC training is deﬁned as a sum of the conventional loss function for the ﬁne-tuning set (LFTðθÞ) and an additional penalty term based on the FIM: Lw=EWCðθÞ ¼ LFTðθÞ þ λ/2 Σi Fi(θi − θi,pre)2"
