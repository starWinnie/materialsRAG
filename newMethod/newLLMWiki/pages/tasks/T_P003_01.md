# Task: prediction of tensorial spectra

- Task ID: `T_P003_01`
- Canonical name: `prediction_of_tensorial_spectra`
- Paper: `P003`

## Objective

Map crystal structures directly to their full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors

## Material scope

- bulk semiconductors

## Target properties

- frequency-dependent dielectric tensor
- complex dielectric tensor
- optical tensor

## Constraints

- crystalline symmetry constraints
- rotational and inversion symmetries
- isotropic and anisotropic sequential components

## Input requirements

- crystal structure
- periodic crystal graph
- atomic species
- relative atomic positions

## Output goal

- full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors
- complex dielectric tensor εαβ(ω) = εαβ₁(ω) + iεαβ₂

## Research stages

1. ab initio calculation of dielectric tensors (`data_acquisition`)
2. spherical-harmonics decomposition of dielectric tensors (`data_preparation`)
3. training TSENN on spherical-harmonic coefficients (`model_training`)
4. evaluation of dielectric tensor prediction accuracy (`model_evaluation`)
5. reconstruction of real dielectric part via Kramers–Kronig relations (`computational_validation`)
6. symmetry-preserving validation across crystal systems (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- ab initio calculation of dielectric tensors: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dataset (source, DU_P003_001)
- spherical-harmonics decomposition of dielectric tensors: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dataset (label_source, DU_P003_002)
- training TSENN on spherical-harmonic coefficients: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dataset (training, DU_P003_003)
- evaluation of dielectric tensor prediction accuracy: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dataset (test, DU_P003_004)
- reconstruction of real dielectric part via Kramers–Kronig relations: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dataset (computational_validation, DU_P003_005)
- symmetry-preserving validation across crystal systems: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dataset (screening, DU_P003_006)

## Evidence

- P003, PDF page 1: "Here we introduce the Tensorial Spectra Equivariant Neural Network (TSENN), an equivariant graph neural network architecture that maps crystal structures directly to their full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors."
- P003, PDF page 1: "By encoding isotropic sequential scalar components and anisotropic sequential tensor components into spherical tensor representations, TSENN ensures symmetry-aware predictions consistent with crystalline symmetry constraints."
- P003, PDF page 1: "Trained on frequency-dependent dielectric tensors of 1,432 bulk semiconductors, the model achieves a mean absolute error of 0.127, demonstrating its potential for efﬁcient and general modeling of optical properties."
