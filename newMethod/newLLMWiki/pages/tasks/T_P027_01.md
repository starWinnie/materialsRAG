# Task: Full Phonon Prediction

- Task ID: `T_P027_01`
- Canonical name: `full_phonon_prediction`
- Paper: `P027`

## Objective

Predict phonon spectra and full phonon band structures directly from atomic coordinates

## Material scope

- crystalline inorganic materials
- alloys
- high-entropy alloys
- topological Weyl semimetals

## Target properties

- Γ-phonon spectra
- full phonon band structure
- phonon dispersion relations

## Constraints

- complex unit cells
- material-dependent, variable-length output
- no prior knowledge of interatomic forces

## Input requirements

- atomic coordinates
- crystal structure (e.g., POSCAR)
- unit cell vectors

## Output goal

- 3m Γ-phonon energies for m atoms per unit cell
- full phonon band structure at arbitrary k points in the Brillouin zone

## Research stages

1. Phonon data acquisition from ab initio databases (`data_acquisition`)
2. Phonon data preparation and splitting (`data_preparation`)
3. Training VGNN models (VVN, MVN, k-MVN) (`model_training`)
4. Evaluating phonon prediction accuracy (`model_evaluation`)
5. Computational validation on alloy systems and large databases (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- Phonon data acquisition from ab initio databases: DFPT phonon database (source, DU_P027_001)
- Phonon data acquisition from ab initio databases: Togo phonon database (source, DU_P027_002)
- Phonon data preparation and splitting: DFPT phonon database (training, DU_P027_003)
- Phonon data preparation and splitting: Togo phonon database (candidate_pool, DU_P027_004)
- Evaluating phonon prediction accuracy: DFPT phonon database (benchmark, DU_P027_005)
- Evaluating phonon prediction accuracy: Togo phonon database (benchmark, DU_P027_006)
