# Task: extrapolative prediction of material properties

- Task ID: `T_P015_01`
- Canonical name: `extrapolative_prediction_of_material_properties`
- Paper: `P015`

## Objective

To develop a machine-learning methodology that enables extrapolative (not just interpolative) prediction of physical properties for materials outside the training data distribution.

## Material scope

- polymeric materials
- hybrid organic–inorganic perovskites

## Target properties

- specific heat at constant pressure (Cp)
- refractive index
- bandgap

## Constraints

- limited availability of data resources
- need to predict beyond boundaries of existing data
- models must generalize to unexplored material spaces

## Input requirements

- material composition and structural features encoded as descriptors (e.g., Morgan fingerprint for polymers, crystal structure for perovskites)

## Output goal

- real-valued physical property prediction (y ∈ ℝ) for unseen material domains

## Research stages

1. acquisition of polymer and perovskite datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. descriptor generation for materials (`data_preparation`)
3. generation of extrapolative episodes (`candidate_generation`)
4. meta-training of matching neural networks via extrapolative episodic training (E2T) (`model_training`)
5. evaluation of extrapolative prediction performance (`model_evaluation`)
6. fine-tuning for downstream extrapolative domains (`candidate_screening`)
