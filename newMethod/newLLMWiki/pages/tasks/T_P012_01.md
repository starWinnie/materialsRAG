# Task: Crystal material property prediction

- Task ID: `T_P012_01`
- Canonical name: `crystal_material_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P012`

## Objective

Predict scalar physical properties of crystalline materials from their 3D atomic structure

## Material scope

- crystalline materials

## Target properties

- Formation Energy
- Bandgap(OPT)
- Total Energy
- Ehull
- Bandgap(MBJ)
- Bulk Moduli(Kv)
- Shear Moduli(Gv)

## Constraints

- continuity under atomic position perturbations
- geometric completeness
- computational efficiency

## Input requirements

- 3D crystal structure (unit cell, atomic positions, atomic types, lattice vectors)

## Output goal

- scalar property values (e.g., eV/atom, eV, GPa)

## Research stages

1. Acquisition of crystal structure datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. Crystal graph construction with PDD variants (`data_preparation`)
3. Training PDDFormer models (WPDDFormer/UPDDFormer) (`model_training`)
4. Evaluation on benchmark property prediction tasks (`model_evaluation`)
5. Continuity and completeness validation using EMD and theoretical proof (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- Acquisition of crystal structure datasets: Materials Project (source, DU_P012_001)
- Acquisition of crystal structure datasets: JARVIS-DFT (source, DU_P012_002)
- Training PDDFormer models (WPDDFormer/UPDDFormer): Materials Project (training, DU_P012_003)
- Training PDDFormer models (WPDDFormer/UPDDFormer): JARVIS-DFT (training, DU_P012_004)

- Continuity and completeness validation using EMD and theoretical proof: JARVIS-DFT (computational_validation, DU_P012_008)
