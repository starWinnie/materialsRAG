# Task: Out-of-Distribution Property Prediction

- Task ID: `T_P017_01`
- Canonical name: `out_of_distribution_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P017`

## Objective

Train predictor models that extrapolate zero-shot to higher ranges than in the training data, given the chemical compositions of solids or molecular graphs and their property values.

## Material scope

- solid-state materials
- molecules

## Target properties

- band gap
- bulk modulus
- Debye temperature
- shear modulus
- thermal conductivity
- thermal expansion
- refractive index
- yield strength
- formation energy
- elastic anisotropy
- aqueous solubility
- hydration free energy
- lipophilicity
- binding affinity

## Constraints

- predictions must generalize to property value ranges outside training support (top 5% OOD)
- method must work with stoichiometry-based representations for solids and RDKit descriptors for molecules
- model is biased toward predicting higher property values

## Input requirements

- chemical compositions of solids
- molecular graphs encoded as SMILES representations

## Output goal

- predicted property values for OOD samples
- improved extrapolative precision and recall of top-performing candidates

## Research stages

1. Curating benchmark datasets for solids and molecules (`data_acquisition`)
2. Preprocessing representations and splitting datasets into ID/OOD sets (`data_preparation`)
3. Training Bilinear Transduction model on ID data with analogical loss (`model_training`)
4. Selecting high-performing OOD candidates via top-k prediction (`candidate_screening`)
5. Evaluating OOD prediction accuracy and distributional alignment (`model_evaluation`)
