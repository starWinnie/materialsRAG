# Task: electron density prediction

- Task ID: `T_P025_01`
- Canonical name: `electron_density_prediction`
- Paper: `P025`

## Objective

predict electron charge density in atomic systems

## Material scope

- molecules
- materials
- inorganic materials
- battery cathode materials
- zeolites
- Si(111)-2 × 1 surface

## Target properties

- electron charge density

## Constraints

- must be E(3)-equivariant
- must use only atomic species and positions as input
- must scale linearly with system size

## Input requirements

- atomic species
- atomic positions
- probe point locations
- periodic boundary cell (for periodic systems)

## Output goal

- predicted charge density at probe points

## Research stages

1. collecting DFT-computed charge density data (`data_acquisition`)
2. constructing atom-probe graphs (`data_preparation`)
3. training ChargE3Net (`model_training`)
4. evaluating charge density prediction performance (`model_evaluation`)
5. using predicted charge densities to accelerate DFT (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- collecting DFT-computed charge density data: QM9 (training, DU_P025_001)
- collecting DFT-computed charge density data: NMC (training, DU_P025_002)
- collecting DFT-computed charge density data: Materials Project (training, DU_P025_003)
- collecting DFT-computed charge density data: GNoME (test, DU_P025_004)
