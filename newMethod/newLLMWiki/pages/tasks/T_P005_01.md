# Task: Predicting thermodynamic stability of inorganic compounds

- Task ID: `T_P005_01`
- Canonical name: `predict_thermodynamic_stability_of_inorganic_compounds`
- Paper: `P005`

## Objective

Accurately predict whether an inorganic compound is thermodynamically stable or unstable

## Material scope

- inorganic compounds
- perovskite halides
- Li-containing oxides
- transition metal oxides
- two-dimensional materials
- double perovskite oxides

## Target properties

- thermodynamic stability

## Constraints

- compounds must be inorganic crystalline
- stability defined by decomposition energy ΔHd < 0 meV/atom

## Input requirements

- chemical formula (composition)
- electron configuration of constituent elements
- atomic properties
- interatomic interaction features

## Output goal

- binary classification: stable vs. unstable
- probability score of stability

## Research stages

1. Collecting thermodynamic stability data from DFT-computed databases (`data_acquisition`)
2. Preprocessing composition and encoding electron configuration (`data_preparation`)
3. Assigning stability labels based on decomposition energy thresholds (`label_generation`)
4. Training base-level models (Magpie, Roost, ECCNN) and meta-level model (MLR) (`model_training`)
5. Benchmarking model performance using multiple metrics (`model_evaluation`)
6. Enumerating candidate compounds in unexplored composition space (`candidate_generation`)
7. Applying ECSG to rank and filter candidate compounds by predicted stability probability (`candidate_screening`)
8. Validating predictions via first-principles DFT calculations (`computational_validation`)
