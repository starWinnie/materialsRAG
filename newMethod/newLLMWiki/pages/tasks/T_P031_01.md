# Task: designing novel superconducting candidates

- Task ID: `T_P031_01`
- Canonical name: `designing_novel_superconducting_candidates`
- Paper: `P031`

## Objective

Design novel superconducting candidates with high Tc values

## Material scope

- crystals
- superconductors
- ordered and disordered structures

## Target properties

- superconducting transition temperature (Tc)

## Constraints

- high Tc
- structural validity
- novelty (not in training set)

## Input requirements

- crystal structure representation (including ordered/disordered sites)
- chemical composition
- lattice matrix
- Cartesian coordinates

## Output goal

- novel crystal structures predicted to have high Tc

## Research stages

1. constructing SuperCon3D dataset (`data_acquisition`)
2. representing disordered crystal graphs (`data_preparation`)
3. training SODNet for Tc prediction (`model_training`)
4. training DiffCSP-SC for inverse design (`model_training`)
5. generating novel crystal structures with DiffCSP-SC (`candidate_generation`)
6. screening ICSD database with SODNet (`candidate_screening`)
7. performing DFT calculations on candidates (`computational_validation`)
8. wet lab synthesis and Tc characterization (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- constructing SuperCon3D dataset: SuperCon (source, DU_P031_001)
- constructing SuperCon3D dataset: Inorganic Crystal Structure Database (ICSD) (source, DU_P031_002)
- constructing SuperCon3D dataset: SuperCon3D (candidate_pool, DU_P031_003)
- training SODNet for Tc prediction: SuperCon3D (training, DU_P031_004)
- training DiffCSP-SC for inverse design: SuperCon3D (training, DU_P031_005)
- training DiffCSP-SC for inverse design: Materials Project, OQMD, Matgen, and ICSD pre-training pool (pretraining, DU_P031_006)
- screening ICSD database with SODNet: Inorganic Crystal Structure Database (ICSD) (candidate_pool, DU_P031_007)
