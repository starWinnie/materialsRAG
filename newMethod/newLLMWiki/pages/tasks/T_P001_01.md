# Task: Crystal Property Prediction

- Task ID: `T_P001_01`
- Canonical name: `crystal_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P001`

## Objective

estimate a physical property y from the crystal structure, represented as a tuple (A, P, L)

## Material scope

- crystals

## Target properties

- formation energy
- band gap
- bulk modulus
- shear modulus
- Young's modulus
- metal/non-metal classification
- exfoliation energy
- refractive index
- total energy
- energy above the hull

## Constraints

- full invariance to crystallographic symmetries
- E(3)-invariant
- permutation invariance
- translation invariance
- rotation invariance
- periodic invariance
- symmetry invariance

## Input requirements

- crystal structure represented as M = (A, P, L) where A denotes atomic types, P contains coordinates, and L defines the periodic lattice

## Output goal

- physical property y which may be continuous (y ∈ R) in regression tasks or categorical (y ∈ {1, 2, ..., C}) in classification tasks

## Research stages

1. Retrieving crystal datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. Crystal structure representation (`data_preparation`)
3. Training PRDNet with Huber loss and AdamW optimizer (`model_training`)
4. Evaluating model performance on benchmark datasets (`model_evaluation`)
