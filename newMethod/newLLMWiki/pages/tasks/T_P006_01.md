# Task: Generating universal atomic embeddings (UAEs) for crystal property prediction

- Task ID: `T_P006_01`
- Canonical name: `generate_universal_atomic_embeddings`
- Paper: `P006`

## Objective

To generate atomic embeddings that capture complex atomic features and roles within materials, enabling improved and transferable prediction of crystal properties (e.g., formation energy, bandgap) across diverse deep learning models and datasets.

## Material scope

- crystals
- hybrid organic-inorganic perovskites
- oxides

## Target properties

- formation energy
- bandgap
- total energy
- total magnetization

## Constraints

- must be transferable across different back-end models and datasets
- must support multi-task learning
- must preserve rotational and translational invariance

## Input requirements

- crystal structure data (CIF files)
- atomic species (one-hot encoded)
- atomic coordinates

## Output goal

- dense, continuous vector representations (tensors) for each atom — ct-UAEs — that serve as enhanced atomic fingerprints

## Research stages

1. Collecting crystal structure datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. Pretraining CrystalTransformer to generate ct-UAEs (`model_training`)
3. Generating ct-UAE tensors for downstream models (`candidate_generation`)
4. Evaluating ct-UAE transfer performance on property prediction (`model_evaluation`)
5. Validating symmetry invariance and embedding stability (`computational_validation`)
6. Clustering and interpreting ct-UAEs via UMAP/K-means (`candidate_screening`)
7. Deriving atomic property labels for reverse training (CatBoost) (`label_generation`)
