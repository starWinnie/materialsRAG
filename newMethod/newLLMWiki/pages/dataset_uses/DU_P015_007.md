# Dataset Use: RadonPy polymer dataset in meta-training of matching neural networks via extrapolative episodic training (E2T)

- DatasetUse ID: `DU_P015_007`
- Paper: `P015`

## Research task

extrapolative prediction of material properties (`T_P015_01`)

## Research stage

meta-training of matching neural networks via extrapolative episodic training (E2T) (`model_training`, `S_P015_04`)

## Dataset

RadonPy polymer dataset (`D_P015_radonpy_polymer_dataset`)

## Usage role

training

## Purpose

To train matching neural networks via extrapolative episodic training (E2T) using episodes with (x,y) sampled from one polymer class and support set S from the other 19.

## Used fields

- Cp
- refractive index
- chemical structure of polymer repeating unit

## Construction method

Episodes generated by sampling (x,y) from one of 20 polymer classes and S of size m=30 from the remaining 19 classes.

## Filter conditions

- 19 of 20 polymer classes used for training
- support set size m = 30

Not stated.
