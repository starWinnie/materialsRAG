## Purpose

Generate synthetic crystal pairs with ground-truth orbit distances to pretrain and validate the symmetry-adapted encoding module.

## Used fields

- atomic positions
- space group
- lattice vectors

## Construction method

Each sample consists of a pair of random atomic positions as fractional coordinates in [0, 1)3, a space group, and randomly generated lattice vectors that satisfy the constraints of the Bravais lattice for the group.
