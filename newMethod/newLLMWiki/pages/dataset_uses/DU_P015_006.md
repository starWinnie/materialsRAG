# Dataset Use: HOIP dataset in generation of extrapolative episodes

- DatasetUse ID: `DU_P015_006`
- Paper: `P015`

## Research task

extrapolative prediction of material properties (`T_P015_01`)

## Research stage

generation of extrapolative episodes (`candidate_generation`, `S_P015_03`)

## Dataset

HOIP dataset (`D_hoip_dataset`)

## Usage role

candidate_pool

## Purpose

To generate extrapolative episodes by partitioning into 12 anion–cation domains and sampling test instances from one domain and support sets from the remaining ten.

## Used fields

- crystal structure
- bandgap

## Construction method

Classified into 12 categories based on combinations of four inorganic anions (F, Cl, Br, I) and three cations (Ge, Sn, Pb); 11 of 12 categories used for training, support set sampled from remaining ten combinations.

## Filter conditions

- excluded all samples with Ge and F or with Pb and I
- 11 of 12 categories included in training dataset D
