## Datasets used

## Evidence

- P001, PDF page 4, PRELIMINARIES: "Crystal structures can be described using a fundamental unit, such as a primitive cell, conventional unit cell, Bravais lattice, or asymmetric unit, together with its associated atoms. The unit is defined by a 3 × 3 lattice matrix that specifies its geometry in real space. Formally, a crystal structure is represented as M = (A, P, L), where A denotes atomic types, P = [p1, . . . , pN] ∈R3×N contains the fractional or Cartesian coordinates of the N atoms, and L = [ℓ1, ℓ2, ℓ3] ∈R3×3 defines the periodic lattice in real space."
- P001, PDF page 5, Graph embedding: "A crystal structure is represented as a graph G = (V, E), where vertices V = {v1, v2, . . . , vN} correspond to atoms one-hot encoded attributes (Xie & Grossman, 2018) and edges E ⊆V × V encode atomic interactions within a cutoff radius."
