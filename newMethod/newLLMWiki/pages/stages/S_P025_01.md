## Datasets used

- QM9 — training: train and validate ChargE3Net on organic molecules
- NMC — training: train and validate ChargE3Net on nickel manganese cobalt battery cathode materials
- Materials Project — training: train and validate ChargE3Net on inorganic materials across the periodic table
- GNoME — test: evaluate generalization capabilities on out-of-distribution materials
