# Stage: collecting labeled crystal property data

- Stage ID: `S_P023_02`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P023_01` — crystal property prediction

## Goal

Acquire labeled crystal structure–property pairs for fine-tuning and evaluation

## Input

- None stated

## Output

- JARVIS-DFT dataset (55,722 materials with 19 properties)
- OQMD-EXP dataset (1,500 experimental formation energy values)

## Datasets used

- JARVIS-DFT — training: fine-tuning for crystal property prediction and evaluation on nine properties
- OQMD-EXP — training: fine-tuning for crystal property prediction to mitigate DFT error using experimental formation energy data

## Evidence

- P023, PDF page 6, Experimental Settings: "Further, to evaluate the fine-tuning performance of CrysDiff compared with other crystal property predictors, we select the 2021.8.18 version of JARVIS-DFT (Choudhary et al. 2020), another popular materials database, for the downstream property prediction task. JARVIS-DFT consists of 55,722 materials with 19 properties, like formation energy, bandgap, total energy, bulk modulus, etc., which depend significantly on crystal structures and atom features."
- P023, PDF page 6, Experimental Settings: "Therefore, to investigate how our model helps to mitigate the DFT error, we also take a small dataset OQMD-EXP (Kirklin et al. 2015), containing 1,500 available experimental data of formation energy."
