# Stage: evaluation metrics establishment

- Stage ID: `S_P020_06`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P020_01` — crystal tensor property prediction

## Goal

establish evaluation metrics tailored for ML-based predictions of general tensor properties

## Input

- curated dataset
- predicted tensor properties

## Output

- evaluation metrics: success rate in capturing zero elements, success rate in identifying mutually dependent elements, Frobenius norm distance, high-quality prediction rate

## Datasets used

- curated crystal tensor property dataset — test: used for model evaluation with tailored metrics

## Evidence

- P020, PDF page 7, Experimental Setup: "To thoroughly assess the quality of tensor predictions, we propose the following metrics, each targeting a specific aspect of the tensor prediction quality. (1) Success rate in capturing zero elements that evaluates the ability of the model to correctly identify tensor elements that should be zero-valued due to symmetry constraints inherent in crystal structures. (2) Success rate in identifying mutually dependent elements that measures the model’s accuracy in capturing the equality between tensor elements that are dependent due to symmetry. (3) Frobenius norm (Fnorm) distance that is an E(3) invariant measure for crystal tensor properties, averaged across different crystal systems. (4) High-quality prediction rate (EwT) that is determined by the ratio of Fnorm(error) to Fnorm(label) being less than 25%."
