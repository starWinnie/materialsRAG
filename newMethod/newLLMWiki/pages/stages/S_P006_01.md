# Stage: Collecting crystal structure datasets

- Stage ID: `S_P006_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P006_01` — Generating universal atomic embeddings (UAEs) for crystal property prediction

## Goal

Acquire large-scale, high-quality crystal structure and property datasets for pretraining and evaluation.

## Input

- Materials Project (MP and MP*)
- JARVIS dataset
- MC3D dataset
- hybrid organic-inorganic perovskite (HOIP) datasets

## Output

- CIF files
- formation energy (Ef)
- PBE bandgap (Eg)
- total energy (E)
- total magnetization

## Datasets used

- Materials Project — source: Data acquisition for formation energy and PBE bandgap evaluation
- JARVIS-DFT — source: Data acquisition for formation energy and bandgap evaluation
- MC3D dataset — source: Data acquisition for total energy evaluation
- Hybrid Organic-Inorganic Perovskite (HOIP) dataset — source: Data acquisition for hybrid organic-inorganic perovskite property prediction
