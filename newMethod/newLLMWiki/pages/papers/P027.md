## Research summary

The paper introduces the Virtual Node Graph Neural Network (VGNN) to predict phonon spectra and bandstructures directly from atomic coordinates, addressing challenges of variable-length output and complex unit cells. It presents three variants—vector (VVN), matrix (MVN), and momentum-dependent matrix (k-MVN) virtual nodes—to predict Γ-point phonons and full phonon dispersion. The models are trained on ab initio DFPT phonon data and validated on diverse alloy systems; MVN is used to build a Γ-phonon database of 146,000+ Materials Project materials.
