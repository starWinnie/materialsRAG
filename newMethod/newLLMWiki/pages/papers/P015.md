## Research summary

This study develops a meta-learning methodology—extrapolative episodic training (E2T)—to enhance machine learning models' ability to predict material properties beyond the distribution of training data. Using attention-based matching neural networks (MNNs) trained on arbitrarily generated extrapolative tasks, the approach demonstrates superior generalization for unseen material domains, validated on two case studies: predicting specific heat and refractive index of polymeric materials, and bandgap of hybrid organic–inorganic perovskites.
