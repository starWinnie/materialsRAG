## Research summary

This paper develops LLM-Prop, a method that leverages large language models (LLMs) to predict properties of crystalline materials from their text descriptions, outperforming state-of-the-art graph neural network (GNN)-based methods and domain-specific LLMs like MatBERT. It introduces the TextEdge benchmark dataset containing crystal text descriptions and their properties, and demonstrates that natural language input enables better capture of space group symmetry and Wyckoff sites than GNNs.
