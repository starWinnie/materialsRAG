## Research summary

This paper introduces the Tensorial Spectra Equivariant Neural Network (TSENN), an equivariant graph neural network that maps crystal structures directly to their full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors—specifically, the complex dielectric tensor εαβ(ω) = εαβ₁(ω) + iεαβ₂—trained on frequency-dependent dielectric tensors of 1,432 bulk semiconductors. The model encodes isotropic (ℓ=0) and anisotropic (ℓ=2) sequential tensor components into spherical tensor representations to ensure symmetry-aware predictions consistent with crystalline symmetry constraints, achieving a mean absolute error of 0.127.
