## Research summary

The paper proposes a machine learning method to predict quantum-mechanical charge density for atomic systems, aiming to overcome the accuracy–efficiency trade-off in existing approaches. It introduces three key ingredients: (1) representing charge density using atomic and virtual orbitals (spherical fields centered at atoms and bond midpoints), (2) using expressive, domain-informed, and learnable even-tempered Gaussian basis sets, and (3) employing a high-capacity equivariant neural network (eSCN). The method achieves state-of-the-art accuracy while being >30× faster than prior methods on the QM9 charge density benchmark.
