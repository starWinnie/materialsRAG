## Research summary

This paper presents a comprehensive benchmark study evaluating graph neural networks (GNNs) for predicting materials properties on out-of-distribution (OOD) test sets—materials whose structures or properties deviate significantly from the training distribution. It introduces five OOD test set generation methods (LOCO, SparseXsingle, SparseYsingle, SparseXcluster, SparseYcluster) applied to three MatBench datasets (dielectric, elasticity, perovskites), and systematically compares eight SOTA GNN models across 250 folds. The study reveals a critical generalization gap: current top-performing GNNs underperform substantially on OOD tasks compared to their i.i.d. MatBench baselines, identifies CGCNN, ALIGNN, and DeeperGATGNN as comparatively robust, and analyzes latent physical spaces to explain performance differences.
