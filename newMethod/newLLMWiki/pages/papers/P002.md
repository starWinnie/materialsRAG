## Research summary

This paper introduces the Crystal Fourier Transformer (CFT), a single neural architecture that adapts its weights to enforce exact invariance to any of the 230 crystallographic space groups. It achieves this by analytically deriving constraints that group operations impose on Fourier coefficients, constructing symmetry-adapted Fourier bases via a dual graph representation, and encoding these constraints into a group-conditional routing matrix (MG) within a Transformer. The model is validated on material property prediction and zero-shot generalization to unseen space groups.
