## Research summary

This paper proposes a novel atomic embedding strategy called universal atomic embeddings (UAEs), generated via a custom Transformer-based model named CrystalTransformer (ct-UAEs), to improve the accuracy of machine learning predictions for crystal properties such as formation energy and bandgap. The ct-UAEs are pretrained on large crystal databases (MP*), transferred to various back-end GNN models (e.g., CGCNN, ALIGNN, MEGNET), and shown to yield consistent accuracy improvements (e.g., 14% MAE reduction for formation energy on MP). The embeddings demonstrate transferability across datasets (JARVIS, MC3D, HOIP) and tasks, interpretability via clustering (UMAP/K-means), and multi-task adaptability (MT@2p–MT@4p).
