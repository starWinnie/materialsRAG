# Paper: Cross-scale covariance for material property prediction

- Paper ID: `P014`
- Year: 2025
- DOI: 10.1038/s41524-024-01453-w
- File: `14_Cross_Scale_Covariance.pdf`
- Extraction status: completed

## Research summary

The paper proposes a statistical approach to predict large-scale material properties (specifically plastic flow strength in FCC metals) using regression on small-scale 'indicator' properties computed with the same interatomic potential (IP). It analyzes covariance between MD-simulated flow strength (QoI) and 39 small-scale properties (e.g., elastic constants, surface energies, vacancy migration energy) across a pool of 153 IPs from OpenKIM. A multi-linear regression model is built and validated to predict flow strength from small-scale predictors, with error bounds estimated via cross-validation. The model is then applied to DFT-computed predictors to estimate flow strength for seven FCC metals.
