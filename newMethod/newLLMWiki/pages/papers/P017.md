## Research summary

The paper proposes a transductive machine learning method—Bilinear Transduction—to improve zero-shot extrapolation for predicting out-of-distribution (OOD) property values of materials and molecules, i.e., property values beyond the range observed in training data. It targets compositionally driven variation in solids (using stoichiometry-based representations) and graph-based variation in molecules (using SMILES-derived RDKit descriptors), aiming to boost precision and recall of high-performing OOD candidates during virtual screening.
