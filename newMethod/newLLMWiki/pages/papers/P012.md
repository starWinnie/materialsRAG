## Research summary

The paper introduces WPDDFormer and UPDDFormer—graph transformer models that integrate atom-weighted and unit-cell pairwise distance distribution (PDD) representations into multi-edge crystal graphs—to achieve continuous and geometrically complete crystal structure representations for improved property prediction. It addresses discontinuity and ambiguity in traditional crystal graph representations caused by atomic vibrations and lattice non-uniqueness, and demonstrates state-of-the-art accuracy on Materials Project and JARVIS-DFT benchmarks.
