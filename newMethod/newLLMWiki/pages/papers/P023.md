## Research summary

The paper proposes CrysDiff, a diffusion-based pretrain-finetune framework to address the challenge of limited labeled data for crystal property prediction. In pre-training, it reconstructs crystal structures (lattice and fractional coordinates) from atom compositions using a joint denoising diffusion model with equivariant GNNs to respect crystal symmetries. In fine-tuning, it models the conditional distribution of target properties given crystal structures by diffusing on the property value while keeping the structure fixed. It demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on multiple properties in the JARVIS-DFT dataset, especially under sparse labeling.
