## Research summary

This paper proposes an ensemble machine learning framework—Electron Configuration models with Stacked Generalization (ECSG)—to predict the thermodynamic stability of inorganic compounds. ECSG integrates three base-level models grounded in distinct domain knowledge: Magpie (atomic properties), Roost (interatomic interactions), and ECCNN (electron configuration). The framework uses stacked generalization to combine their outputs into a meta-level model, achieving high predictive accuracy (AUC = 0.988 on JARVIS) and exceptional sample efficiency (requiring only 1/7 the data of existing models). It is validated across multiple databases (MP, OQMD, JARVIS), applied to unknown composition spaces (perovskite halides, Li-containing oxides, transition metal oxides), and deployed in two case studies: discovery of 2D wide bandgap semiconductors and double perovskite oxides—both confirmed via first-principles DFT calculations.
