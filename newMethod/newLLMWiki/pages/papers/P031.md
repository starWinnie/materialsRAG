## Research summary

The paper introduces SuperCon3D, a new dataset containing both 3D crystal structures (ordered and disordered) and experimental superconducting transition temperatures (Tc), and proposes two deep learning models—SODNet for screening known structures and DiffCSP-SC for generating novel high-Tc superconductors. It validates both models via computational (e.g., DFT) and interpretability analyses, and reports candidate materials for experimental validation.
