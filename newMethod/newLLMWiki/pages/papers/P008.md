## Research summary

The paper proposes a hierarchy-boosted funnel learning (HiBoFL) framework that integrates unsupervised and supervised learning to efficiently identify semiconductors with ultralow lattice thermal conductivity (κL), circumventing large-scale brute-force ab initio calculations. It uses unsupervised clustering on descriptors from the Materials Project database to narrow the search space, performs low-cost high-throughput calculations (PET model) on selected clusters to label κL, and trains an interpretable CatBoost classifier to predict ultralow κL, revealing new structural descriptors governing anharmonicity.
