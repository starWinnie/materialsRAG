## Research summary

The paper introduces an unsupervised Denoising Pre-training Framework (DPF) for crystal structures to overcome the scarcity of labeled data in crystal property prediction. DPF pre-trains models on 380,743 unlabeled crystal structures by reconstructing masked atom types, perturbed atom positions, and perturbed lattice constants. The pre-trained models are then fine-tuned on downstream crystal property prediction tasks (e.g., formation energy, band gap, bulk moduli). Experiments show DPF improves prediction accuracy across multiple benchmarks compared to prior models.
