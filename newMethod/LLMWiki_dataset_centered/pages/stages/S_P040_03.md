## Goal

Elucidate atomic-scale mechanism and energy landscape of Ni dispersion driven by Ni-O-La/Ce bonding

## Input

- NiOy/CeO2−x and NiOy/LaCeO2−x surface models
- DFT + U method
- GemNet-OC ML-MD model

## Output

- adsorption energies (e.g., Eads = −0.75 eV for Ni on O adjacent to La)
- migration pathways
- thermodynamic stability of Ni-O-La configurations
