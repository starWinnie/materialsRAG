# Stage: Continuity and completeness validation using EMD

- Stage ID: `S_P012_05`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P012_01` — Crystal material property prediction

## Goal

Quantitatively verify continuity and general geometric completeness of WPDD/UPDD crystal graphs

## Input

- perturbed crystal structures S and Q
- EMD metric

## Output

- EMD(G(S), G(Q)) ≤ Cϵ (continuity proof)
- G(S) = G(Q) ⟹ S ≃ Q (completeness proof)

## Datasets used

- JARVIS-DFT — computational_validation: Continuity and completeness validation using EMD on perturbed crystal structures
- Materials Project — computational_validation: Continuity and completeness validation using EMD on perturbed crystal structures

## Evidence

- P012, PDF page 2, Introduction: "Finally, we employ the Earth Mover’s Distance (EMD) [Rubner et al., 2000] to assess the continuity of crystal graphs, demonstrating that WPDD crystal graphs constructed using only Euclidean distances maintain continuity and general completeness under slight atomic position perturbations, providing a more accurate depiction of actual crystal structures."
- P012, PDF page 5, 4.4 The Continuity of Proposed Crystal Graphs: "Proposition 1. The WPDD and UPDD multi-edge crystal graph is continuous. Proof. For any neighbor count k ≥1, if the periodic crystal S, Q ∈Rn satisfy dB (S, Q) < r (S), then we have: EMD (G (S) , G (Q)) = EMD ((SX , SXI, SE, SPDD) , (QX , QXI, QE, QPDD)) = EMD ((SX , QX )) + EMD ((SXI, QXI))+ EMD ((SE, QE)) + EMD ((SPDD, QPDD)) ."
