# Stage: unsupervised learning for clustering materials with similar κL

- Stage ID: `S_P008_03`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P008_01` — identifying semiconductors with ultralow lattice thermal conductivity

## Goal

Use unsupervised k-means clustering on featurized first-level dataset to group materials by structural/compositional similarity and identify problem-specific clusters likely to contain low-κL materials.

## Input

- 2675 materials (first-level dataset)
- composition-based features ({E})
- structure-based features ({S})
- 83 principal components (PCs)

## Output

- seven clusters (C1–C7)
- problem-specific clusters C1 and C2 (704 materials, second-level dataset)

## Datasets used

- first-level dataset — source: Provide featurized input (composition + structure descriptors) for k-means clustering to identify problem-specific clusters.
- second-level dataset — candidate_pool: Serve as the reduced search space for subsequent high-throughput calculation of κPET.

## Evidence

- P008, PDF page 3, Results: "K-means clustering41,42 is then employed to identify materials with similar κL, which is visualized in a low-dimensional space using t-distributed stochastic neighbor embedding (t-SNE)43, and problem-speciﬁc clusters are selected based on similarity design rules to form the second-level dataset."
- P008, PDF page 4: "Since the materials with a high likelihood of possessing relatively low κL tend to group into the two clusters of C1 and C2, the exploration scope for ﬁnding materials with low κL is reduced from 2675 materials to 704 materials, narrowing by approximately three-quarters. As a result, these two problem-speciﬁc clusters (C1 and C2) further constitute our second-level dataset."
