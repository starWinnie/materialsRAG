# Stage: transfer learning for data-scarce properties

- Stage ID: `S_P013_04`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P013_01` — materials property prediction

## Goal

Fine-tune pre-trained models on data-scarce mechanical properties

## Input

- pre-trained models on primary properties (Ef, Eg, EHull)
- data-scarce mechanical properties datasets

## Output

- CrysCoT models (e.g., CrysCoT-Mp_eform, CrysCoT-Mp_ehull)

## Datasets used

- MP21 Energy Above Convex Hull Dataset — pretraining: Pretrain CrysCoT models on energy above convex hull for transfer learning to data-scarce properties
- MP21 Formation Energy Dataset — pretraining: Pretrain CrysCoT models on formation energy for transfer learning to data-scarce properties

## Evidence

- P013, PDF page 10, Methods: "To overcome the challenge of data scarcity, we employed TL techniques to leverage existing knowledge and enhance the performance of our model. By pretraining the model on a large-scale dataset of DFT computed formation energies, we were able to leverage the learned representations and fine-tune the model (CrysCoT)."
