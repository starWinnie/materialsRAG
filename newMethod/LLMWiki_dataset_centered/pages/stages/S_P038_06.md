# Stage: Nanoscale stability assessment via nanoparticle formation energy

- Stage ID: `S_P038_06`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P038_01` — Rational design of nanoscale stabilized oxide catalysts for OER

## Goal

Evaluate thermodynamic viability of metastable oxides as nanoparticles using GNP_f calculation.

## Input

- Pourbaix formation energy per volume (EV)
- weighted surface energy (γ̄)
- nanoparticle radius (r)
- chemical potential of metal (ΔμM)

## Output

- nanoscale stability phase diagrams (e.g., Fig. 2)
- 886 materials stabilizing at 10–100 nm
- 121 nanoscale candidates

## Datasets used

- University of Houston Dataverse OER database — computational_validation: Used to compute nanoparticle formation energy (GNP_f) for nanoscale stability assessment.

## Evidence

- P038, PDF page 3, 2.3 Surface thermodynamics: "To determine the nanoscale stability of metastable and unstable materials under operating conditions, we assessed the nanoparticle formation energy given by: GNP_f = EV(pH, V, T)(4/3 πr³) + γ̄(pH, V, T, ΔμM)(4πr²)"
- P038, PDF page 8, 3.4 Alternative screening frameworks: "Using our nanoscale stability diagrams (see Fig. 2), we were able to identify 2778 Pourbaix stable materials with 886 stabilizing at the nanoscale regime (10 to 100 nm)."
