to investigate the role of coordinates in CrystalTransformer and compare single-task vs. multi-task embeddings

## Input

- coordinates part of CrystalTransformer
- single-task embeddings (Ef-only, Eg-only)
- multi-task embeddings (MT@2p, MT@3p, MT@4p)

## Output

- MAE comparisons
- similarity analysis via DTW
- clustering results

- None stated

## Evidence

- P006, PDF page 8: "To further investigate the role of the coordinates part of CrystalTransformer in model performance, an ablation study and qualitative analysis are conducted as described in Supplementary 7, which shows that the coordinates part encapsulates important geometric characteristics of crystal systems and is important in training the embeddings."
- P006, PDF page 4: "Further experiments focus on multi-task-generated embeddings (MT). As listed in Table 2, the embeddings trained from two properties (formation energy and bandgap), denoted as MT@2p, yield better performance compared to single-task-generated embeddings."
