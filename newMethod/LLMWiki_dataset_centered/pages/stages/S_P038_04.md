# Stage: Interpolation of total energies for OER intermediates

- Stage ID: `S_P038_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P038_01` — Rational design of nanoscale stabilized oxide catalysts for OER

## Goal

Predict total energies for all slabs and OER surface intermediates (O*, OH*, OOH*) for 4119 oxides.

## Input

- pre-trained OC22 S2EF-Total model
- slab structures
- adsorption site configurations

## Output

- database of 6,068,572 predictions
- including 1,972,166 OH*, 667,266 O*, 3,237,238 OOH*

## Datasets used

- OC22 oxide subset (4119 materials) — candidate_pool: Source of 4119 oxide materials for interpolation of slab and OER intermediate total energies.
- University of Houston Dataverse OER database — computational_validation: Primary database used for high-throughput screening and nanoscale stability assessment.
