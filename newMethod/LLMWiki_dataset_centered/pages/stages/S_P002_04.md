## Goal

To operationalize the analytical constraints by constructing a dual graph representation and using it to algorithmically generate the symmetry-adapted Fourier basis.

## Input

- reciprocal lattice L*
- symmetry operations ϕ(x) = Ax + t

## Output

- constraint graph G
- phase-consistent orbits {Ok}
- symmetry-adapted basis functions {eOk}
