# Stage: Screening candidate materials in unexplored composition space (e.g., perovskites, 2D materials)

- Stage ID: `S_P005_07`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 7
- Task: `T_P005_01` — Predicting thermodynamic stability of inorganic compounds

## Goal

Apply trained ECSG to rank millions of hypothetical compounds by predicted stability probability and select high-confidence candidates for validation

## Input

- candidate composition space (e.g., 4.5M perovskite oxides)
- trained ECSG model

## Output

- ranked list of stable candidates (e.g., 1,877,443 perovskite oxides with p > 0.5)
- selected high-probability candidates (e.g., 35 with p > 0.9)

## Datasets used

- Perovskite Oxide Database — candidate_pool: Screen candidate materials in unexplored composition space (double perovskite oxides) / Enumerate candidate space of perovskites (ABO3, A2BB′O6, AA′B2O6, AA′BB′O6) and predict stability
