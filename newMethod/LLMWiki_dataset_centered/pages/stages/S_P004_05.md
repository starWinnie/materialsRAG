# Stage: Ranking and screening of hypothetical materials by predicted hull distance

- Stage ID: `S_P004_05`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P004_01` — ML-guided materials discovery

## Goal

Rank WBM candidates by model-predicted hull distance and simulate resource-constrained validation campaigns to compute discovery acceleration factor (DAF).

## Input

- model-predicted hull distances for WBM structures

## Output

- ranked candidate lists
- DAF values
- cumulative precision/recall curves

## Datasets used

- WBM unique prototype test set — candidate_pool: Ranking and screening of hypothetical materials by predicted hull distance.

## Evidence

- P004, PDF page 5, Article: "A typical discovery campaign will rank hypothetical materials by model-predicted hull distance from most to least stable and validate the most stable predictions first."
- P004, PDF page 4, Article: "The discovery acceleration factor (DAF) quantifies how many times more effective a model is at finding stable structures compared with random selection from the test set."
