# Stage: High-throughput screening with progressive criteria

- Stage ID: `S_P038_05`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P038_01` — Rational design of nanoscale stabilized oxide catalysts for OER

## Goal

Filter candidates using tiered criteria: (1) presence in OC22 training set, (2) bulk Pourbaix stability, (3) Wulff shape surface stability, (4) low overpotential, (5) energy above hull, (6) material cost; plus nanoscale extension.

## Input

- interpolated energy database
- Pourbaix decomposition energy (EPBX)
- nanoparticle formation energy (GNP_f)
- Wulff shape analysis
- material cost data

## Output

- 122 bulk candidates
- 68 nanoscale candidates
- 190 total candidates

## Datasets used

- University of Houston Dataverse OER database — screening: Used as input to the high-throughput screening framework applying progressive criteria (bulk Pourbaix stability, Wulff shape, overpotential, energy above hull, material cost).

- P038, PDF page 8, 3.4 Alternative screening frameworks: "In total we have identified 190 candidates (122 bulk – and 68 nanostable) with 145 distinct chemical systems when considering all the different screening frameworks listed in Table 2."
