# Stage: comparative evaluation of La-doped vs. undoped support

- Stage ID: `S_P040_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P040_01` — reverse regulation from particles to clusters

## Goal

Identify La doping as critical enabler of reverse dispersion by contrasting structural and electronic behavior

## Input

- 10Ni/30La-CeO₂
- 10Ni/CeO₂
- in situ XRD
- H₂-TPR
- XPS
- FTIR
- Raman

## Output

- La doping confirmed to enhance support thermal stability, oxygen vacancy concentration, Niδ⁺ fraction, and Ni-support interaction strength

## Datasets used

- 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst — screening: Comparative evaluation against 10Ni/CeO2 to identify La doping as critical enabler of reverse dispersion
- 10Ni/CeO2 catalyst — screening: Reference catalyst for comparative evaluation of La-doping effect

## Evidence

- P040, PDF page 3: "The markedly different evolution of 10Ni/CeO2 and 10Ni/30La-CeO2 under identical conditions clearly demonstrates that electron beam irradiation is not the primary cause of particle dispersion; rather, the reverse sintering is an intrinsic property of the catalyst."
- P040, PDF page 3: "Notably, the 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst exhibited a significantly higher proportion of Niδ+ species (51.1%) compared to the 10Ni/CeO2 reference (30.6%)..."
- P040, PDF page 4: "La doping substantially enhanced the reducibility of CeO2... indicating a stronger interaction between Ni species and the 30La-CeO2 support."
