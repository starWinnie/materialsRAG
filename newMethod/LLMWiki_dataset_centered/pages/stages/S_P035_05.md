## Goal

Evaluate the performance of the ML-corrected formation energies against experimental references and quantify improvements in MAE and impact on relative phase stability (stability flip rate).

## Input

- trained ML models
- test set formation energies (PBEsol or MLIP)
- experimental references

## Output

- ML-corrected formation energies
- MAE <50 meV/atom
- stability flip rate metrics

## Datasets used

- None stated
