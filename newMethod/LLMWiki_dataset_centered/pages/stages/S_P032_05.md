# Stage: benchmark evaluation on force prediction accuracy

- Stage ID: `S_P032_05`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P032_01` — molecular dynamics simulation using machine learning force fields

## Goal

Assess the mean absolute error (MAE) of predicted atomic forces against ground truth on standard datasets (QCML, MD17, Ko2020, xxMD, SPICE).

## Input

- pretrained/fine-tuned MD-ET models
- ground truth force labels

## Output

- MAE scores
- RMSE scores

## Datasets used

- Ko2020 — test: Assess the root mean squared error (RMSE) of predicted atomic forces against ground truth on the Ko2020 dataset.
- MD17 — test: Assess the mean absolute error (MAE) of predicted atomic forces against ground truth on the MD17 dataset.
- QCML — test: Assess the mean absolute error (MAE) of predicted atomic forces against ground truth on the QCML evaluation set.
- SPICE — test: Assess the mean absolute error (MAE) of predicted atomic forces against ground truth on the SPICE dataset.
- xxMD — test: Assess the mean absolute error (MAE) of predicted atomic forces against ground truth on the xxMD dataset.
