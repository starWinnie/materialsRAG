Construct slab models for all facets up to maximum Miller index of 1 for oxide materials.

## Input

- Materials Project bulk materials
- pymatgen and ASE packages

## Output

- slabs with atomic and vacuum layers ~12.5 Å thick
- all terminations for each facet (excluding slabs >200 atoms)
