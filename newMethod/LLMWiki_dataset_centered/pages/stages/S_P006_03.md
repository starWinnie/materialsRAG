# Stage: evaluating transferability of ct-UAEs across databases and tasks

- Stage ID: `S_P006_03`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P006_01` — universal atomic embeddings (UAEs)

## Goal

to test the ability and transferability of ct-UAEs on different databases (JARVIS, MC3D) and tasks (formation energy, bandgap, total energy, interatomic potential modeling)

## Input

- JARVIS dataset
- MC3D dataset
- MPtrj dataset
- hybrid perovskite datasets

## Output

- MAE improvements across datasets
- performance metrics for interatomic potential models

## Datasets used

- hybrid organic-inorganic perovskite dataset — computational_validation: to apply ct-UAEs to predict formation energy in hybrid perovskites database
- JARVIS-DFT — computational_validation: to test the ability and transferability of ct-UAEs on different databases and tasks
- MC3D dataset — computational_validation: to test the ability and transferability of ct-UAEs on different databases and tasks
- MPtrj dataset — computational_validation: to investigate the suitability of ct-UAE on energy-conserving interatomic potential (IAP) models
