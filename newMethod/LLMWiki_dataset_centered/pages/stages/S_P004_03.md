# Stage: Training of ML models on MP data

- Stage ID: `S_P004_03`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P004_01` — ML-guided materials discovery

## Goal

Train diverse ML models—including universal interatomic potentials and energy-only predictors—on MP training data to predict relaxed energies or hull distances.

## Input

- MP v.2022.10.28 training set (or subsets like MPtrj, MPF, etc.)

## Output

- trained models: EquiformerV2+DeNS, ORB, SevenNet, MACE, CHGNet, M3GNet, ALIGNN, MEGNet, CGCNN, CGCNN+P, Wrenformer, BOWSR, Voronoi RF

## Datasets used

- Materials Project — training: Training diverse ML models—including universal interatomic potentials and energy-only predictors—on MP training data to predict relaxed energies or hull distances.
- MP-crystals-2022.10.28 — training: Training energy-only models (CGCNN, CGCNN+P, Wrenformer, ALIGNN, Voronoi RF) on formation energies.
- MPF.2021.2.8 dataset — training: Training M3GNet model on energies, forces, and stresses.
- MPtrj dataset — training: Training universal interatomic potential (UIP) models (EquiformerV2+DeNS, ORB, SevenNet, MACE, CHGNet) on energies, forces, and stresses.
