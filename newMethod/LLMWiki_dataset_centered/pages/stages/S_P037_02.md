# Stage: preprocessing raw conductivity and band gap data

- Stage ID: `S_P037_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P037_01` — accelerating the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs)

## Goal

process raw data to remove unphysical entries and ensure wide chemical diversity

## Input

- raw conductivity data (39 862 entries)
- raw band gap data (6354 entries)

## Output

- processed conductivity dataset (8034 entries)
- processed band gap dataset (4732 entries)

## Datasets used

- Validated experimental band gap dataset — training: training dataset for ML models predicting band gap
- Validated experimental electrical conductivity dataset — training: training dataset for ML models predicting electrical conductivity

## Evidence

- P037, PDF page 3, Electrical conductivity dataset: "Several preprocessing steps were conducted on the raw data. Initially, we excluded all pure elements and noble gases and selected all chemical formulas reported within a window of room temperature (298 ± 5 K), reducing the dataset to 14 307 entries."
- P037, PDF page 4, Band gap dataset: "Preprocessing steps were applied to the raw data. Specifically, we excluded groups of duplicated formulas with band gap measurements having a standard deviation greater than 0.1 eV."
