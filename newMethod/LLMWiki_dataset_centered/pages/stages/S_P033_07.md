# Stage: validation via synthesis and optical measurement of new perovskite

- Stage ID: `S_P033_07`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 7
- Task: `T_P033_01` — bandgap prediction of ABX3-type perovskites

## Goal

Experimentally synthesize a novel perovskite and measure its bandgap to validate model predictions

## Input

- predicted bandgap for Cs0.05(FA0.85MA0.15)0.95Pb(I0.85Br0.15)3

## Output

- experimental bandgap (1.61–1.62 eV) vs. predicted (1.60 eV)

## Datasets used

- Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (227 samples) — experimental_validation: Reference experimental bandgap for validating prediction on newly synthesized Cs0.05(FA0.85MA0.15)0.95Pb(I0.85Br0.15)3

## Evidence

- P033, PDF page 4, four: "To verify the accuracy of our ML model and predication ability for new perovskites, we synthesize the Cs0.05(FA0.85MA0.15)0.95Pb(I0.85Br0.15)3 perovskite thin films for bandgap determination. ... The bandgap of the present perovskite thin films is in a small variation range of 1.61–1.62 eV. According to the predication of our ML model (BPM-ABX3-type perovskites), the obtained bandgap value is 1.60 eV ... which has a difference with the experimental value less than 1.2% relative."
