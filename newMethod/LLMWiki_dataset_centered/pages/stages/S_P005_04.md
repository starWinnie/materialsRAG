# Stage: Training base-level models (ECCNN, Magpie, Roost) and meta-level model (MLR) via stacked generalization

- Stage ID: `S_P005_04`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P005_01` — Predicting thermodynamic stability of inorganic compounds

## Goal

Train three complementary base models on compositional inputs, then train a meta-level linear regressor to combine their outputs into a super learner (ECSG)

## Input

- preprocessed inputs
- binary stability labels

## Output

- trained ECCNN, Magpie, Roost models
- trained multi-response linear regression (MLR) meta-model
- ECSG ensemble

## Datasets used

- JARVIS-DFT — training: Train base-level models (ECCNN, Magpie, Roost) and meta-level model (MLR)
- Materials Project — training: Train base-level models (ECCNN, Magpie, Roost) and meta-level model (MLR)
- Open Quantum Materials Database (OQMD) — training: Train base-level models (ECCNN, Magpie, Roost) and meta-level model (MLR)
