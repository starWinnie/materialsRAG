# Stage: Acquiring DFT formation energy datasets

- Stage ID: `S_P035_02`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P035_01` — Correcting DFT formation energies towards experimental accuracy

## Goal

Obtain formation energies from multiple DFT databases (MC3D, OQMD, Materials Project) for cross-validation and baseline comparison.

## Input

- None stated

## Output

- MC3D formation energies (PBE-v1, PBEsol-v1)
- OQMD formation energies
- Materials Project formation energies

## Datasets used

- Materials Cloud three-dimensional crystals database (MC3D) PBE-v1 — source: Provide DFT formation energies (PBE-v1) for cross-validation and baseline comparison against experimental data and other DFT databases.
- Materials Cloud three-dimensional crystals database (MC3D) PBEsol-v1 — source: Provide DFT formation energies (PBEsol-v1) for cross-validation and baseline comparison against experimental data and other DFT databases.
- Materials Project (MP) v2023.11.1 — source: Provide DFT formation energies for cross-validation and baseline comparison against MC3D and experimental data.
- Open Quantum Materials Database (OQMD) — source: Provide DFT formation energies for cross-validation and baseline comparison against MC3D and experimental data.

## Evidence

- P035, PDF page 2, RESULTS: "We then compare the DFT calculated formation energies against experimental formation enthalpies (see Methods for dataset details). This comparison is practically important, as thermodynamic stability is the most widely used descriptor for filtering materials in computational materials science studies [22, 55–60], and it is particularly relevant for MC3D, which focuses on experimentally known compounds and is intended to support experimentalists."
- P035, PDF page 10, METHODS: "In addition to the comparison with experimental data, the MC3D formation energies are compared against the established high-throughput databases Materials Project [7, 8] and Open Quantum Materials Database [10]. We query the OQMD v1.5 (locally hosted) and MP v2023.11.1, and match structures by their ICSD ID."
