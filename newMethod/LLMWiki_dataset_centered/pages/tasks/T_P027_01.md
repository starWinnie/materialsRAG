# Task: phonon spectra and bandstructure prediction

- Task ID: `T_P027_01`
- Canonical name: `phonon_spectra_and_bandstructure_prediction`
- Paper: `P027`

## Objective

Direct prediction of Γ-phonon spectra and full phonon bandstructures from atomic coordinates

## Material scope

- crystalline materials
- alloy systems
- high-entropy alloys
- semiconducting inorganic materials

## Target properties

- Γ-phonon spectra
- full phonon bandstructures
- phonon dispersion relations
- phonon density of states
- group velocities
- heat capacities

## Constraints

- input limited to atomic coordinates only
- no prior knowledge of interatomic forces
- must handle variable/complex unit cells (up to 400+ atoms per unit cell)

## Input requirements

- atomic coordinates
- unit cell vectors
- atomic numbers
- atomic masses

## Output goal

- 3m Γ-phonon energies for m-atom unit cell
- full phonon band structure at arbitrary k-points in Brillouin zone

## Research stages

1. acquisition of ab initio phonon data (`data_acquisition`)
2. phonon data preparation and splitting (`data_preparation`)
3. training of VGNN models (VVN, MVN, k-MVN) (`model_training`)
4. evaluation of phonon prediction accuracy (`model_evaluation`)
5. validation on alloy systems and complex materials (`computational_validation`)
6. generation of Γ-phonon database for Materials Project (`candidate_generation`)
