# Task: predicting the experimental band gap values of HOIPs

- Task ID: `T_P036_01`
- Canonical name: `predict_band_gap_of_hybrid_perovskites`
- Paper: `P036`

## Objective

To predict the experimental band gap values of hybrid organic−inorganic perovskites (HOIPs) accurately and quantitatively interpret the physical factors affecting the band gap.

## Material scope

- hybrid organic−inorganic perovskites (HOIPs)
- ABX3 (A = Cs, FA, or MA; B = Pb or Sn; X = Br, Cl, or I)

## Target properties

- band gap

## Constraints

- ideal band gap range (1.3−1.4 eV)
- wide band gap range (1.7−2.1 eV)
- exclude Cl-containing compositions due to difficulty in controlling Cl content in annealing process

## Input requirements

- experimental band gap values
- physical features (e.g., electronegativity difference χB−X, octahedral factor μ, electron affinity difference EAB−X)

## Output goal

- accurate band gap prediction (RMSE < 0.060 eV)
- interpretable mathematical formula linking physical features to band gap
- novel HOIP compositions with targeted band gaps
