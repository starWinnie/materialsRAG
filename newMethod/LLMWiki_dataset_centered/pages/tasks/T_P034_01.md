# Task: training machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs)

- Task ID: `T_P034_01`
- Canonical name: `train_machine_learned_interatomic_potentials`
- Paper: `P034`

## Objective

to learn an MLIP, which can accurately predict the potential energy and atomistic forces for an atomic system exposed to local perturbations

## Material scope

- molecular and material systems
- organic molecules
- inorganic materials
- bulk materials
- transition-metal oxide
- Li-Mo-Ni-Ti oxide (LMNTO)
- aspirin
- buckyball catcher

## Target properties

- potential energy
- atomistic forces
- conservative forces
- total energies
- atomic forces

## Constraints

- sparse training data sets
- limited sizes of available data sets
- high computational cost of ab initio or first-principles approaches
- force calculations infeasible for some high-accuracy ab initio methods

## Input requirements

- atomic configurations S = {ri, Zi}Nat i=1
- reference energies Eref S
- reference atomic forces {Fref i,S}Nat i=1
- ab initio or first-principles data

## Output goal

- accurate prediction of potential energy and atomistic forces
- improved robustness during MD simulations
- reduced energy and force errors
- improved fine-tuning of foundation models on sparse, highly accurate ab initio data

## Research stages

1. training MLIPs with PIWSL loss (`model_training`)
2. benchmarking models trained with PIWSL (`model_evaluation`)
3. evaluating robustness during MD simulations (`experimental_validation`)
4. fine-tuning foundation models with PIWSL (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- training MLIPs with PIWSL loss: ANI-1x (training, DU_ani_1x)
- benchmarking models trained with PIWSL: ANI-1x (test, DU_ani_1x)
- training MLIPs with PIWSL loss: LMNTO (training, DU_lmnto)
- fine-tuning foundation models with PIWSL: MD17(CCSD) (candidate_pool, DU_md17_ccsd)
- training MLIPs with PIWSL loss: MD22 (training, DU_md22)
- fine-tuning foundation models with PIWSL: MD22 buckyball catcher (candidate_pool, DU_md22_buckyball_catcher)
- training MLIPs with PIWSL loss: revised MD17 (training, DU_revised_md17)
- benchmarking models trained with PIWSL: revised MD17 (test, DU_revised_md17)
- evaluating robustness during MD simulations: revised MD17 (experimental_validation, DU_revised_md17)
- training MLIPs with PIWSL loss: TiO2 (training, DU_tio2)
