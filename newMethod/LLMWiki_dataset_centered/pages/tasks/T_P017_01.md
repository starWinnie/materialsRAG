# Task: Out-of-Distribution Property Prediction

- Task ID: `T_P017_01`
- Canonical name: `out_of_distribution_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P017`

## Objective

train predictor models that extrapolate zero-shot to higher ranges than in the training data, given the chemical compositions of solids or molecular graphs and their property values

## Material scope

- solid-state materials
- molecules

## Target properties

- electronic
- mechanical
- thermal
- aqueous solubility
- hydration free energy
- octanol/water distribution coefficients
- binding affinities

## Constraints

- extrapolation to out-of-distribution (OOD) property values that are absent from the training data
- zero-shot extrapolation to unseen property value ranges beyond the training distribution

## Input requirements

- chemical compositions of solids
- molecular graphs

## Output goal

- predicted property values for OOD candidates
- identification of high-performing OOD candidates

## Research stages

1. Dataset collection for solids and molecules (`data_acquisition`)
2. Data preprocessing and splitting (`data_preparation`)
3. Training Bilinear Transduction model (`model_training`)
4. Evaluation on OOD test sets (`model_evaluation`)
5. Screening for high-performing OOD candidates (`candidate_screening`)
