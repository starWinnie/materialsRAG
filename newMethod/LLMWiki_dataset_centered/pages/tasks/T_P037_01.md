# Task: accelerating the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs)

- Task ID: `T_P037_01`
- Canonical name: `discovery_of_transparent_conducting_materials`
- Paper: `P037`

## Objective

accelerating the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs)

## Material scope

- transparent conducting materials (TCMs)
- semiconductors

## Target properties

- band gap
- electrical conductivity

## Constraints

- high electrical conductivity
- low absorption in the range of visible light
- band gap exceeding the energy of visible light (approximately 3 eV)

## Input requirements

- stoichiometry alone
- chemical formulas

## Output goal

- identify new TCMs compositionally similar to those in training data
- highlight material candidates that may have been previously overlooked

## Research stages

1. creating and validating unique experimental databases (`data_acquisition`)
2. preprocessing raw conductivity and band gap data (`data_preparation`)
3. domain expert validation of conductivity and band gap measurements (`label_generation`)
4. training state-of-the-art ML models for property prediction (`model_training`)
5. assessing model performance with bespoke evaluation schemes (`model_evaluation`)
6. screening 55 compositions for TCM characteristics (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- screening 55 compositions for TCM characteristics: List of 55 experimentally-reported chemical compositions (candidate_pool, DU_list_of_55_experimentally_reported_chemical_compositions)
- creating and validating unique experimental databases: Materials Project DFT-computed band gap dataset (pretraining, DU_materials_project_dft_computed_band_gap_dataset)
- creating and validating unique experimental databases: MPDS electrical conductivity dataset (source, DU_mpds_electrical_conductivity_dataset)
- creating and validating unique experimental databases: UCSB thermoelectric dataset (electrical conductivity subset) (source, DU_ucsb_thermoelectric_dataset_electrical_conductivity_subset)
- preprocessing raw conductivity and band gap data: Validated experimental band gap dataset (training, DU_validated_experimental_band_gap_dataset)
- preprocessing raw conductivity and band gap data: Validated experimental electrical conductivity dataset (training, DU_validated_experimental_electrical_conductivity_dataset)
- creating and validating unique experimental databases: Zhuo et al. experimental band gap dataset (source, DU_zhuo_et_al_experimental_band_gap_dataset)
