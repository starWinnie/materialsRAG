# Task: reverse regulation from particles to clusters

- Task ID: `T_P040_01`
- Canonical name: `reverse_regulation_from_particles_to_clusters`
- Paper: `P040`

## Objective

Achieve thermodynamically stable subnanometer Ni clusters via in situ dispersion of pre-formed Ni particles under high-temperature reducing conditions

## Material scope

- Ni
- La-doped CeO₂ (30La-CeO₂)
- undoped CeO₂

## Target properties

- high thermal stability
- high H₂ production rate (279.2 mmolH₂·gNi⁻¹·min⁻¹ at 500 °C)
- stable 1–2 nm Ni clusters

## Constraints

- high-temperature operation (>500 °C)
- resistance to classical sintering
- structural integrity of support under reducing atmosphere

## Input requirements

- Ni-loaded La-doped CeO₂ catalyst (10Ni/30La-CeO₂)
- ammonia decomposition reaction conditions (NH₃ or H₂ atmosphere, 450–600 °C)

## Output goal

- thermodynamically stable Ni clusters (1–2 nm) anchored via Ni-O-La/Ce covalent bonding

## Research stages

1. synthesis of catalysts (`data_acquisition`)
2. in situ observation of particle-to-cluster transformation (`experimental_validation`)
3. DFT and ML-MD simulations of interfacial atomic migration and adsorption energetics (`computational_validation`)
4. comparative evaluation of La-doped vs. undoped support (`candidate_screening`)
5. catalytic performance testing and kinetic analysis (`model_evaluation`)

## Dataset uses

- in situ observation of particle-to-cluster transformation: 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst (experimental_validation, DU_10ni_30la_ceo2_catalyst)
- DFT and ML-MD simulations of interfacial atomic migration and adsorption energetics: 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst (computational_validation, DU_10ni_30la_ceo2_catalyst)
- comparative evaluation of La-doped vs. undoped support: 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst (screening, DU_10ni_30la_ceo2_catalyst)
- catalytic performance testing and kinetic analysis: 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst (benchmark, DU_10ni_30la_ceo2_catalyst)
- comparative evaluation of La-doped vs. undoped support: 10Ni/CeO2 catalyst (screening, DU_10ni_ceo2_catalyst)
- synthesis of catalysts: 30La-CeO2 nanorods (source, DU_30la_ceo2_nanorods)
- synthesis of catalysts: undoped CeO2 nanorods (source, DU_undoped_ceo2_nanorods)
