# Task: designing high Tc superconductors

- Task ID: `T_P031_01`
- Canonical name: `designing_high_tc_superconductors`
- Paper: `P031`

## Objective

designing novel high Tc superconducting candidates

## Material scope

- superconductors

## Target properties

- superconducting transition temperature (Tc)

## Constraints

- experimental Tc values
- ordered and disordered crystal structures

## Input requirements

- crystal structure M (including ordered and disordered sites)
- chemical composition A
- Cartesian coordinates X
- lattice matrix L

## Output goal

- novel high Tc superconductor candidates

## Research stages

1. constructing SuperCon3D dataset (`data_acquisition`)
2. training SODNet (`model_training`)
3. training DiffCSP-SC (`model_training`)
4. screening ICSD database with SODNet (`candidate_screening`)
5. generating novel structures with DiffCSP-SC (`candidate_generation`)
6. DFT verification of candidates (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- constructing SuperCon3D dataset: ICSD (source, DU_icsd)
- screening ICSD database with SODNet: ICSD (candidate_pool, DU_icsd)
- training DiffCSP-SC: Materials Project, OQMD, Matgen (pretraining, DU_materials_project_oqmd_matgen)
- constructing SuperCon3D dataset: SuperCon (source, DU_supercon)
- constructing SuperCon3D dataset: SuperCon3D (training, DU_supercon3d)
- training SODNet: SuperCon3D (training, DU_supercon3d)
- training DiffCSP-SC: SuperCon3D (training, DU_supercon3d)
- DFT verification of candidates: SuperCon3D (computational_validation, DU_supercon3d)
