# Task: materials property prediction

- Task ID: `T_P013_01`
- Canonical name: `materials_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P013`

## Objective

Accurately predict energy-related and mechanical properties of inorganic materials

## Material scope

- inorganic materials

## Target properties

- total energy
- energy above the convex hull
- energy band gap
- bulk modulus
- shear modulus
- phonon frequency
- refractive index
- Fermi energy

## Constraints

- data scarcity for mechanical properties
- capturing thermodynamic stability
- capturing global crystal structure and periodicity

## Input requirements

- material compositions
- crystal structures
- tabulated physical and chemical descriptors

## Output goal

- predicted property values for energy-related and mechanical properties

## Research stages

1. using computational data from the MP21 public database (`data_acquisition`)
2. graph representation of crystal structure (`data_preparation`)
3. training the CrysCo hybrid model (`model_training`)
4. transfer learning for data-scarce properties (`model_training`)
5. benchmarking CrysCo's performance (`model_evaluation`)
6. ablation study on number of EGAT layers (`ablation_study`)
