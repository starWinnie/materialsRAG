# Task: Correcting DFT formation energies towards experimental accuracy

- Task ID: `T_P035_01`
- Canonical name: `correct_dft_formation_energies_towards_experimental_accuracy`
- Paper: `P035`

## Objective

Improve the agreement of density functional theory (DFT) calculated formation energies with experimental formation enthalpies.

## Material scope

- three-dimensional inorganic crystal structures
- experimentally known compounds

## Target properties

- formation energy
- energy above the convex hull

## Constraints

- avoid requiring additional DFT calculations
- limit impact on relative phase stability
- reduce mean absolute error below 50 meV/atom

## Input requirements

- DFT-relaxed geometries (e.g., PBEsol)
- foundational MLIP (e.g., PET-OMATPES) trained at r2SCAN level
- experimental formation enthalpy references

## Output goal

- formation energies with MAE <50 meV/atom vs. experiment
- stability classifications (e.g., energy above hull) that preserve physically meaningful relative phase ordering

## Research stages

1. Acquiring experimental formation enthalpy references (`data_acquisition`)
2. Acquiring DFT formation energy datasets (`data_acquisition`)
3. Evaluating zero-shot fMLIP formation energies (`model_evaluation`)
4. Training classical ML models in delta-learning framework (`model_training`)
5. Evaluating ML-corrected formation energies (`model_evaluation`)
6. Studying regularization impact on accuracy-stability trade-off (`ablation_study`)
