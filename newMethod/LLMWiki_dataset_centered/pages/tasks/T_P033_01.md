# Task: bandgap prediction of ABX3-type perovskites

- Task ID: `T_P033_01`
- Canonical name: `bandgap_prediction_of_abx3_perovskites`
- Paper: `P033`

## Objective

To accurately predict the experimental bandgap values of ABX3-type perovskites for solar cell device design

## Material scope

- ABX3-type perovskites
- all-inorganic perovskites
- inorganic-organic hybrid perovskites
- tin-free perovskites

## Target properties

- bandgap

## Constraints

- model must be interpretable via SHAP
- predictions must align with physical mechanisms (B-X bond length/angle)

## Input requirements

- chemical composition descriptors: A-site cations (Cs+, MA+, FA+), B-site metals (Pb2+, Sn2+), X-site halogens (I−, Br−, Cl−)

## Output goal

- numerical bandgap value in eV

## Research stages

1. collection of experimental bandgap data (`data_acquisition`)
2. database establishment and cleaning (`data_preparation`)
3. generation of 24 candidate bandgap prediction models (`candidate_generation`)
4. model optimization via GridSearchCV and 10-fold cross-validation (`model_training`)
5. evaluation using RMSE and Pearson correlation coefficient (`model_evaluation`)
6. selection of best-performing models per perovskite subclass (`candidate_screening`)
7. validation via synthesis and optical measurement of new perovskite (`experimental_validation`)
8. validation against DFT-calculated bandgaps (`computational_validation`)
9. SHAP-based interpretation of feature contributions to bandgap (`label_generation`)

## Dataset uses

- selection of best-performing models per perovskite subclass: All-inorganic perovskites subset (training, DU_all_inorganic_perovskites_subset)
- generation of 24 candidate bandgap prediction models: CBM and VBM database for ABX3 perovskites (training, DU_cbm_and_vbm_database_for_abx3_perovskites)
- validation against DFT-calculated bandgaps: DFT-calculated bandgap values for validation (computational_validation, DU_dft_calculated_bandgap_values_for_validation)
- collection of experimental bandgap data: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (227 samples) (source, DU_experimental_abx3_type_perovskite_bandgap_dataset_227_samples)
- database establishment and cleaning: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (227 samples) (training, DU_experimental_abx3_type_perovskite_bandgap_dataset_227_samples)
- generation of 24 candidate bandgap prediction models: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (227 samples) (candidate_pool, DU_experimental_abx3_type_perovskite_bandgap_dataset_227_samples)
- validation via synthesis and optical measurement of new perovskite: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (227 samples) (experimental_validation, DU_experimental_abx3_type_perovskite_bandgap_dataset_227_samples)
- SHAP-based interpretation of feature contributions to bandgap: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (227 samples) (label_source, DU_experimental_abx3_type_perovskite_bandgap_dataset_227_samples)
- selection of best-performing models per perovskite subclass: Inorganic-organic hybrid perovskites subset (test, DU_inorganic_organic_hybrid_perovskites_subset)
- selection of best-performing models per perovskite subclass: Tin-free perovskites subset (training, DU_tin_free_perovskites_subset)
