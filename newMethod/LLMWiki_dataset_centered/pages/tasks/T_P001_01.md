# Task: Crystal Property Prediction

- Task ID: `T_P001_01`
- Canonical name: `crystal_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P001`

## Objective

estimate a physical property y from the crystal structure, represented as a tuple (A, P, L)

## Material scope

- crystals

## Target properties

- formation energy
- band gap
- bulk modulus
- shear modulus
- Young's modulus
- metal/non-metal classification
- exfoliation energy
- refractive index
- total energy
- energy above the hull

## Constraints

- full invariance to crystallographic symmetries
- E(3)-invariance (rotation, reflection, translation)

## Input requirements

- crystal structure represented as (A, P, L) where A denotes atomic types, P contains coordinates, L defines periodic lattice

## Output goal

- physical property y, continuous for regression or categorical for classification
