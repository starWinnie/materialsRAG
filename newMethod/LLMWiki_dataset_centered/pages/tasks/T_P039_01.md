# Task: Develop a foundation machine learning interatomic potential with explicit polarizable long-range interactions

- Task ID: `T_P039_01`
- Canonical name: `develop_foundation_ml_interatomic_potential_with_polarizable_long_range_interactions`
- Paper: `P039`

## Objective

Construct a mapping from atomic coordinates ri and atomic types Zi to the total potential energy Epot that integrates equivariant message-passing neural network potentials with explicit polarizable long-range physical interactions

## Material scope

- elements across the periodic table up to Pu
- charged clusters (e.g., Ag3+/−, Na8/9Cl8+)
- hydrogenated carbon chains (C10H2/C10H3+)
- Au clusters on MgO surfaces
- benzotriazole on Cu surfaces
- Li7La3Zr2O12 (c-LLZO)
- BaTiO3
- β-Li3PS4
- Li6PS5Cl

## Target properties

- total potential energy
- forces
- stress
- electrostatic interaction energies
- molecular polarizability
- bulk modulus
- ionic diffusivity
- phase transition temperatures
- solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) formation

## Constraints

- maintain computational efficiency
- capture long-range phenomena beyond typical 5 Å cutoff
- accurately reproduce polarization effects under external electric fields
- balance accuracy and computational efficiency

## Input requirements

- atomic coordinates ri
- atomic types Zi
- boundary conditions

## Output goal

- total potential energy Epot = E0 + EPQEq + ED3
- forces
- stress
- partial charges
- potential energy surfaces
- diffusion coefficients
- phase transition behavior
- SEI structure and composition

## Research stages

1. Acquire training datasets including diverse charge-state systems and periodic table elements (`data_acquisition`)
2. Prepare dataset configurations with DFT-calculated energies, forces, and stresses (`data_preparation`)
3. Train the foundation equivariant neural network potential with polarizable long-range interactions (`model_training`)
4. Benchmark model performance on diverse charge-state datasets and physical properties (`model_evaluation`)
5. Validate long-range interaction capture through dimer energy curves and polarization response (`computational_validation`)
6. Apply model to simulate real materials phenomena and compare with experimental observations (`experimental_validation`)
7. Screen candidate systems for finetuning to achieve ab initio accuracy (`candidate_screening`)
