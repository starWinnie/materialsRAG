# Task: universal atomic embeddings (UAEs)

- Task ID: `T_P006_01`
- Canonical name: `generate_universal_atomic_embeddings`
- Paper: `P006`

## Objective

to generate universal atomic embeddings (UAEs) for broad applicability as atomic fingerprints that accurately capture complex atomic features and improve prediction accuracy of crystal properties

## Material scope

- crystals
- hybrid organic-inorganic perovskites
- oxide compounds

## Target properties

- formation energy
- bandgap
- total energy
- total magnetization

## Constraints

- must be transferable across databases and tasks
- must not rely on predeﬁned atomic attributes
- must capture atomic roles and interactions within materials

## Input requirements

- chemical information in crystal databases
- atom input (one-hot encoding of atomic species)
- coordinate input (atom position coordinates)

## Output goal

- atomic embedding tensors (ct-UAEs) that serve as universal atomic fingerprints

## Research stages

1. pre-training CrystalTransformer on MP* dataset (`model_training`)
2. comparing atomic embeddings from different front-end models (`candidate_screening`)
3. evaluating transferability of ct-UAEs across databases and tasks (`model_evaluation`)
4. ablation study on coordinates part and multi-task variants (`ablation_study`)
5. clustering and interpretability analysis of ct-UAEs (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- evaluating transferability of ct-UAEs across databases and tasks: hybrid organic-inorganic perovskite dataset (computational_validation, DU_hybrid_organic_inorganic_perovskite_dataset)
- evaluating transferability of ct-UAEs across databases and tasks: JARVIS-DFT (computational_validation, DU_jarvis_dft)
- comparing atomic embeddings from different front-end models: Materials Project dataset (benchmark, DU_materials_project_dataset)
- evaluating transferability of ct-UAEs across databases and tasks: MC3D dataset (computational_validation, DU_mc3d_dataset)
- pre-training CrystalTransformer on MP* dataset: MP* dataset (pretraining, DU_mp_dataset)
- evaluating transferability of ct-UAEs across databases and tasks: MPtrj dataset (computational_validation, DU_mptrj_dataset)
- clustering and interpretability analysis of ct-UAEs: oxide compounds subset from Materials Project (computational_validation, DU_oxide_compounds_subset_from_materials_project)
