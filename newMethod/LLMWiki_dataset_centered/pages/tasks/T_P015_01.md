# Task: extrapolative prediction of material properties

- Task ID: `T_P015_01`
- Canonical name: `extrapolative_material_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P015`

## Objective

To develop a machine-learning methodology that predicts material properties beyond the distribution range of training data — i.e., extrapolatively — rather than interpolatively.

## Material scope

- polymeric materials
- hybrid organic–inorganic perovskites

## Target properties

- specific heat at constant pressure (Cp)
- refractive index
- bandgap

## Constraints

- limited availability of data resources
- need to generalize to unexplored material spaces
- requirement for rapid adaptability to unseen domains with few samples

## Input requirements

- chemical structure descriptors (e.g., Morgan fingerprint for polymers, crystal structure graphs for perovskites)

## Output goal

- real-valued physical property predictions (y ∈ ℝ) for materials outside training domain

## Research stages

1. acquisition of polymer and perovskite datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. descriptor generation and dataset partitioning (`data_preparation`)
3. training MNNs via extrapolative episodic training (E2T) (`model_training`)
4. evaluation of extrapolative prediction performance (`model_evaluation`)
5. hyperparameter sensitivity analysis for E2T (`ablation_study`)
6. fine-tuning for downstream extrapolative domains (`candidate_screening`)
