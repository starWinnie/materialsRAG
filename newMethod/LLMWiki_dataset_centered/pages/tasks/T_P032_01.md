# Task: molecular dynamics simulation using machine learning force fields

- Task ID: `T_P032_01`
- Canonical name: `molecular_dynamics_simulation`
- Paper: `P032`

## Objective

Develop and evaluate a minimally biased neural network model for predicting atomic forces to enable stable and faithful molecular dynamics simulations.

## Material scope

- molecules
- ionic systems
- metallic systems

## Target properties

- atomic forces
- energy conservation
- rotational equivariance
- NVE/NVT simulation stability
- observables (e.g., h(r), velocity autocorrelation spectra)

## Constraints

- no built-in rotational equivariance
- no built-in energy conservation
- off-the-shelf transformer architecture with minimal modification

## Input requirements

- 3D atomic positions
- atomic numbers
- spin and charge states
- pairwise distances and displacement vectors

## Output goal

- predicted atomic forces
- stable NVE and NVT molecular dynamics trajectories
- faithful reproduction of physical observables

## Research stages

1. acquisition of QCML dataset (`data_acquisition`)
2. data splitting and augmentation (`data_preparation`)
3. supervised pretraining on QCML (`model_training`)
4. fine-tuning on downstream datasets (`model_training`)
5. benchmark evaluation on force prediction accuracy (`model_evaluation`)
6. evaluation of MD simulation stability and faithfulness (`model_evaluation`)
7. analysis of equivariance and energy conservation (`ablation_study`)
