# Task: Rational design of nanoscale stabilized oxide catalysts for OER

- Task ID: `T_P038_01`
- Canonical name: `rational_design_of_nanoscale_stabilized_oxide_catalysts_for_oer`
- Paper: `P038`

## Objective

Identify viable candidate anode catalysts for OER under varying reaction conditions for bulk, surface, and nanoscale Pourbaix stability as well as material cost, overpotential, and metastability.

## Material scope

- oxide materials
- 4119 oxides
- unary and binary oxides
- ternary oxides

## Target properties

- low overpotential
- thermodynamic stability
- nanoscale stability
- low material cost

## Constraints

- bulk Pourbaix stability (EPBX ≤ 0.5 eV/atom)
- nanoscale Pourbaix stability
- material cost < $8346/kg
- overpotential η < 0.75 V
- energy above hull (Ehull) ≤ 0.1 eV/atom

## Input requirements

- slab structures up to Miller index 1
- surface intermediates O*, OH*, OOH*
- OC22 pre-trained ML model
- Materials Project bulk data

## Output goal

- 122 bulk-stable and 68 nanoscale-stable OER catalyst candidates

## Research stages

1. Slab generation from Materials Project (`data_acquisition`)
2. Fine-tuning OC22 S2EF-Total model (`model_training`)
3. Validation of ML predictions against DFT (`model_evaluation`)
4. Interpolation of total energies for OER intermediates (`candidate_generation`)
5. High-throughput screening with progressive criteria (`candidate_screening`)
6. Nanoscale stability assessment via nanoparticle formation energy (`computational_validation`)
