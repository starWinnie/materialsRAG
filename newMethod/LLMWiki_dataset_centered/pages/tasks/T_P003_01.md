# Task: prediction of tensorial spectra

- Task ID: `T_P003_01`
- Canonical name: `predict_tensorial_spectra`
- Paper: `P003`

## Objective

map crystal structures directly to their full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors

## Material scope

- bulk semiconductors

## Target properties

- frequency-dependent dielectric tensor

## Constraints

- crystalline symmetry constraints
- rotation, translation, and inversion equivariance

## Input requirements

- crystal structure
- atomic species
- relative atomic positions

## Output goal

- full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors

## Research stages

1. ab initio calculations of dielectric tensors (`data_acquisition`)
2. spherical-harmonics decomposition of dielectric tensors (`data_preparation`)
3. training TSENN on spherical-harmonics coefficients (`model_training`)
4. evaluation of dielectric tensor prediction accuracy (`model_evaluation`)
5. Kramers–Kronig reconstruction and symmetry validation (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- ab initio calculations of dielectric tensors: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dielectric tensor dataset (source, DU_materials_project_1432_semiconductor_dielectric_tensor_dataset)
- spherical-harmonics decomposition of dielectric tensors: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dielectric tensor dataset (source, DU_materials_project_1432_semiconductor_dielectric_tensor_dataset)
- training TSENN on spherical-harmonics coefficients: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dielectric tensor dataset (training, DU_materials_project_1432_semiconductor_dielectric_tensor_dataset)
- evaluation of dielectric tensor prediction accuracy: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dielectric tensor dataset (validation, DU_materials_project_1432_semiconductor_dielectric_tensor_dataset)
- Kramers–Kronig reconstruction and symmetry validation: Materials Project 1432-semiconductor dielectric tensor dataset (computational_validation, DU_materials_project_1432_semiconductor_dielectric_tensor_dataset)
