# Task: Crystal material property prediction

- Task ID: `T_P012_01`
- Canonical name: `crystal_material_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P012`

## Objective

Predict material properties of crystalline materials from their atomic structure

## Material scope

- crystalline materials

## Target properties

- formation energy
- band gap
- total energy
- energy above hull (Ehull)
- bulk moduli
- shear moduli

## Constraints

- continuity under atomic position perturbations
- geometric completeness
- computational efficiency

## Input requirements

- crystal structure (unit cell, atomic positions, atomic types, lattice vectors)

## Output goal

- scalar predictions of material properties

## Research stages

1. Crystal graph construction with WPDD/UPDD (`data_preparation`)
2. Training PDDFormer (WPDDFormer/UPDDFormer) (`model_training`)
3. Evaluation on benchmark property prediction tasks (`model_evaluation`)
4. Ablation study on (W/U)PDD contribution (`ablation_study`)
5. Continuity and completeness validation using EMD (`computational_validation`)
