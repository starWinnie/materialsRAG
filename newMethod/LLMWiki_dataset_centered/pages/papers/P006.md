## Research summary

This paper proposes a novel atomic embedding strategy called universal atomic embeddings (UAEs), generated by a custom-built CrystalTransformer model based on the Transformer architecture. The ct-UAEs are designed as broadly applicable atomic fingerprints that capture complex atomic features more effectively than traditional methods (e.g., one-hot encoding). Evaluated on formation energy and bandgap prediction across multiple databases (Materials Project, JARVIS, MC3D, hybrid perovskites), ct-UAEs consistently improve prediction accuracy—e.g., 14% MAE reduction for formation energy in CGCNN—and demonstrate strong transferability and interpretability via clustering and property correlation analysis.
