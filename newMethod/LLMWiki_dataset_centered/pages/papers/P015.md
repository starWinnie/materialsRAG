## Research summary

This study develops a meta-learning methodology—extrapolative episodic training (E2T)—to enhance the ability of machine learning models to predict material properties outside the domain of training data. Using attention-based matching neural networks (MNNs) trained on arbitrarily generated extrapolative episodes, the method demonstrates superior generalization for unseen material domains, particularly in predicting physical properties of polymeric materials and hybrid organic–inorganic perovskites. The approach enables rapid adaptation to new domains with limited data via fine-tuning, outperforming conventional supervised learning and state-of-the-art baselines like ANE in extrapolative settings.
