## Research summary

This paper presents a comprehensive benchmark study evaluating graph neural networks (GNNs) for predicting materials properties on out-of-distribution (OOD) test sets—materials whose structures or properties deviate significantly from the training distribution. It introduces five OOD test set generation methods (LOCO, SparseXsingle, SparseYsingle, SparseXcluster, SparseYcluster) applied to three MatBench datasets (dielectric, elasticity, perovskites), and benchmarks eight SOTA GNNs. The study reveals a substantial generalization gap: current top-performing GNNs underperform dramatically on OOD tasks compared to their i.i.d. MatBench baselines, identifying CGCNN, ALIGNN, and DeeperGATGNN as comparatively robust. It further analyzes latent physical spaces to explain performance differences and advocates for domain adaptation and physics-informed modeling to improve OOD generalization.
