## Research summary

This paper proposes a data-driven framework to accelerate the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs) by predicting band gap and electrical conductivity from stoichiometry alone, using experimentally validated datasets and state-of-the-art ML models evaluated via bespoke methods including leave-one-TCM-family-out to assess ability to identify previously unseen TCM families.
