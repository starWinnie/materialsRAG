## Research summary

This paper presents MD-ET, an edge transformer adapted for molecular dynamics with minimal physical inductive biases (no built-in rotational equivariance or energy conservation), trained on the QCML dataset. It evaluates performance on benchmarks (QCML, MD17, Ko2020, xxMD, SPICE), analyzes approximate equivariance and energy conservation, and assesses stability and faithfulness of NVE and NVT simulations.
