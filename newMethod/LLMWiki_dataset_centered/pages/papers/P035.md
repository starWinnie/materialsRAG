## Research summary

The paper introduces thermodynamic stability data for the Materials Cloud three-dimensional crystals database (MC3D) and validates it against other DFT databases. It then improves agreement of DFT formation energies with experimental formation enthalpies by first using zero-shot foundational machine learning interatomic potentials (fMLIPs) trained at the r2SCAN level (PET-OMATPES), reducing mean absolute error (MAE) by >40% relative to GGA without additional DFT calculations. Finally, it trains classical ML models (KRR, RF, GPR) in a delta-learning framework on latent features from PET-OMATPES to further correct formation energies, achieving MAE <50 meV/atom — comparable to experimental uncertainty — while limiting distortion of relative phase stability via regularization.
