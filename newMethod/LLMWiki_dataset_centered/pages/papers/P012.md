## Research summary

The paper introduces WPDDFormer and UPDDFormer—graph transformer models that integrate atom-weighted and unit-cell pairwise distance distribution (PDD) representations into multi-edge crystal graphs to achieve continuous and geometrically complete crystal structure representations, enabling improved prediction of material properties such as formation energy, band gap, bulk modulus, and shear modulus on Materials Project and JARVIS-DFT benchmarks.
