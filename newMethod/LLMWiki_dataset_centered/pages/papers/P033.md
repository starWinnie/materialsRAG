## Research summary

This paper develops machine learning models to predict the experimental bandgaps of ABX3-type perovskites using 227 experimentally measured bandgap values collected from 1254 publications. It evaluates 24 model variants across 4 databases and 6 algorithms, identifies Xgboost and MLP as top performers, validates predictions against new experimental synthesis and DFT calculations, and uses SHAP to interpret chemical composition effects—finding B-site metal and X-site halogen ions dominate bandgap control, consistent with B–X bond length and angle physics.
