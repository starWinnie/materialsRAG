## Research summary

Here we introduce the Tensorial Spectra Equivariant Neural Network (TSENN), an equivariant graph neural network architecture that maps crystal structures directly to their full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors. By encoding isotropic sequential scalar components and anisotropic sequential tensor components into spherical tensor representations, TSENN ensures symmetry-aware predictions consistent with crystalline symmetry constraints. Trained on frequency-dependent dielectric tensors of 1,432 bulk semiconductors, the model achieves a mean absolute error of 0.127, demonstrating its potential for efficient and general modeling of optical properties.
