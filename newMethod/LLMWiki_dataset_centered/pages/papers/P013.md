## Research summary

We propose a framework utilizing a Graph Neural Network with composition-based and crystal structure-based architectures, combined with a transfer learning scheme. This approach accurately predicts energy-related properties (e.g., total energy, energy above the convex hull, energy band gap) and data-scarce mechanical properties (e.g., bulk and shear modulus). Our model incorporates four-body interactions, capturing periodicity and structural characteristics. It outperforms state-of-the-art models in 8 materials property regression tasks.
