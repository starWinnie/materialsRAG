## Research summary

This study proposes and demonstrates a reverse regulation strategy—from metal particles to subnanometer clusters—enabled by rational design of catalytic environment and metal-support interaction, overcoming classical sintering limitations at high temperature. Using in situ characterization and theoretical calculations, it reveals atomic migration pathways and thermodynamic steady-state reconstruction underlying stable Ni cluster formation on La-doped CeO₂.
