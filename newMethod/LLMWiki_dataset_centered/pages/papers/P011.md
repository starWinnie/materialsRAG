## Research summary

The paper introduces an unsupervised Denoising Pre-training Framework (DPF) for crystal structures to overcome the scarcity of labeled data in crystal property prediction. DPF trains models to reconstruct original crystal structures by recovering masked atom types, perturbed atom positions, and perturbed lattice constants. It pre-trains on 380,743 unlabeled crystal structures and fine-tunes on downstream property prediction tasks, demonstrating improved performance across multiple benchmarks.
