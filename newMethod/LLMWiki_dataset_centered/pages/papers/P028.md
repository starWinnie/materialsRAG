## Research summary

This work introduces JARVIS-Leaderboard, an open-source and community-driven platform that facilitates benchmarking and enhances reproducibility for materials design methods across multiple categories: Artificial Intelligence (AI), Electronic Structure (ES), Force-fields (FF), Quantum Computation (QC), and Experiments (EXP). It enables users to set up benchmarks with custom tasks and contributions in the form of dataset, code, and metadata submissions, covering various data modalities including atomic structures, spectra, images, and text. The platform includes 1281 contributions to 274 benchmarks using 152 methods with more than 8 million data points.
