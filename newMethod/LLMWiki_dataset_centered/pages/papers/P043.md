## Research summary

This work presents a machine learning approach to systematically correct intrinsic energy resolution errors in density functional theory (DFT) calculations of alloy formation enthalpies, improving reliability for ternary phase stability predictions. A neural network model is trained to predict the discrepancy between DFT-calculated and experimentally measured enthalpies for binary and ternary alloys and compounds, using elemental concentrations, atomic numbers, and interaction terms as features. The model is validated on Al–Ni–Pd and Al–Ni–Ti systems.
