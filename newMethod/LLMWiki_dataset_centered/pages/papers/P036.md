## Research summary

This study developed an interpretable machine learning strategy combining feature engineering, gradient-boosted regression tree (GBRT), and genetic algorithm-based symbolic regression (GASR) to predict the experimental band gaps of hybrid organic–inorganic perovskites (HOIPs) and quantitatively interpret the physical factors affecting band gaps. It identified χB−X as the most important feature and derived the formula Eg = χB−X² + 0.881χB−X. The model screened and experimentally validated three novel HOIP compositions, including MA₀.₂₃FA₀.₀₂Cs₀.₇₅Pb₀.₅₉Sn₀.₄₁Br₀.₂₄I₂.₇₆ with a targeted band gap of 1.39 eV.
