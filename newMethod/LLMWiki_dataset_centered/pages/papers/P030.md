## Research summary

This paper proposes a machine learning method to predict the electronic charge density of atomic systems — a core quantity in density functional theory (DFT) from which all ground-state physicochemical properties can be derived. It addresses the accuracy–efficiency trade-off by introducing three key ingredients: (1) an orbital-based representation augmented with virtual orbitals placed at bond midpoints; (2) expressive, learnable even-tempered Gaussian basis sets with trainable orbital exponents; and (3) a high-capacity SE(3)-equivariant neural network architecture (eSCN). The method is evaluated on the QM9 charge density dataset and achieves state-of-the-art accuracy while being over 30× faster than prior methods.
