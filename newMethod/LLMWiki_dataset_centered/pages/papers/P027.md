## Research summary

The paper introduces the Virtual Node Graph Neural Network (VGNN) to predict phonon spectra and bandstructures directly from atomic coordinates, addressing challenges of variable-length output and complex unit cells. It presents three variants—vector virtual nodes (VVN), matrix virtual nodes (MVN), and momentum-dependent matrix virtual nodes (k-MVN)—to predict Γ-point phonons and full dispersion. The method is validated on alloy systems and used to build a Γ-phonon database of over 146,000 materials from the Materials Project.
