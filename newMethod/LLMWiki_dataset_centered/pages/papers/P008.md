## Research summary

The paper proposes a hierarchy-boosted funnel learning (HiBoFL) framework that integrates unsupervised and supervised learning to efficiently identify semiconductors with ultralow lattice thermal conductivity (κL), circumventing large-scale brute-force ab initio calculations. It uses unsupervised clustering on a high-throughput Materials Project dataset to narrow the search space, performs low-cost phonon-elasticity-thermal (PET) model-based high-throughput calculations on selected clusters to build a labeled local database, trains an interpretable CatBoost classifier to predict ultralow κL, and discovers a new structural descriptor (Lmin) governing structural anharmonicity and phonon lifetime.
