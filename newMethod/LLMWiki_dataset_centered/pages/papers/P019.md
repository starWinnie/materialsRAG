## Research summary

This study develops DTNet, an equivariant neural network that leverages pretrained multi-rank structural embeddings from the universal neural network potential PFP to predict total, electronic, and ionic dielectric tensors for inorganic materials across 72 elements. It benchmarks performance against state-of-the-art models and conducts virtual screening on thermodynamically stable Materials Project candidates to discover high-dielectric and highly anisotropic materials, experimentally validating top candidates via DFPT.
