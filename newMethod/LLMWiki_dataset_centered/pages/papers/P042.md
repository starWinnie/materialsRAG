## Research summary

This paper describes the creation of an open-access, FAIR-compliant database for metal-halide perovskite solar cell device data, containing over 42,400 devices extracted manually from peer-reviewed literature. It includes development of interactive web-based tools for exploration, analysis, filtering, and visualization; establishment of metadata ontology and data extraction protocols; and demonstration of analytical insights (e.g., efficiency trends, HTL impact on Voc, bandgap-composition relationships). The project aims to transform perovskite research data management by enabling community-wide data sharing, reuse, and AI/ML readiness.
