## Research summary

This paper proposes 'dynamic frames' for SE(3)-invariant crystal structure modeling, challenging the conventional use of static, structure-aligned frames. Dynamic frames are constructed per atom and per message-passing layer using learned attention weights to reflect local interatomic interactions, rather than global structural symmetry. The concept is implemented in CrystalFramer, a transformer-based architecture extending Crystalformer, and evaluated on crystal property prediction tasks across JARVIS, Materials Project, and OQMD datasets.
