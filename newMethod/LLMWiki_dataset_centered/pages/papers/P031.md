## Research summary

The paper introduces SuperCon3D, a new dataset containing both 3D crystal structures (ordered and disordered) and experimental superconducting transition temperatures (Tc), and proposes two deep learning models: SODNet for screening known structures to predict Tc, and DiffCSP-SC for generating novel high-Tc superconductor candidates. It validates both models experimentally and presents candidate materials for future experimental validation.
