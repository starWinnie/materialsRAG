# Dataset Use: HOIP dataset in generation of extrapolative episodes

- DatasetUse ID: `DU_P015_004`
- Paper: `P015`

## Research task

extrapolative prediction of material properties (`T_P015_01`)

## Research stage

generation of extrapolative episodes (`candidate_generation`, `S_P015_03`)

## Dataset

HOIP dataset (`D_hoip_dataset`)

## Usage role

candidate_pool

## Purpose

Generation of extrapolative episodes where test instances lie outside training domain (e.g., perovskites containing Ge+F or Pb+I excluded from training)

## Used fields

- bandgap
- cation
- anion

## Construction method

HOIP dataset divided into 12 groups by cation/anion combinations; episodes formed by sampling (x,y) from one group and S from 10 other groups

## Filter conditions

- excluded all samples with Ge and F for HOIP-GeF task
- excluded all samples with Pb and I for HOIP-PbI task

## Sample count

Not stated.

## Availability

- Dataset: public
- Recommendable: true

## Confidence

1.0

## Evidence

- P015, PDF page 5, Results: "We performed numerical experiments using the same setting as that employed by Na et al.42,43. The HOIP dataset was divided into 12 groups based on the combinations of four anions and three cations. For creating a training episode, we excluded all samples with Ge and F or those with Pb and I and randomly selected 50 instances of (x, y) from one group while drawing S of size 50 from the 10 remaining groups."
