## Evidence

- P037, PDF page 3, 3.1 Electrical conductivity dataset: "The electrical conductivity dataset was constructed using two primary data sources. Initially, data on conductivity and resistivity, along with associated chemical formulas, were gathered from the Materials Platform for Data Science (MPDS),4 with 38 068 entries available as of December 2024. This source was supplemented with the UCSB dataset21 (1794 entries), which provides a range of experimental thermoelectric properties, including electrical conductivity. In total, we compiled a raw dataset comprising 39 862 material entries with associated conductivity measurements at various temperatures."
