## Evidence

- P105, PDF page 3, Methodology: "Four series of small-scale laboratory glasses (a total of 15 glasses) were made based on the average composition of the CAS glass from the 2019 Tetronics industrial trials: CaO (32 wt %/ 36.0 mol %), Al2 O3 (17.0 wt %/10.5 mol %), SiO2 (51 wt %/ 53.5 mol %), and with compositions adjusted to accommodate increasing concentrations of waste components. Waste additions were Ce, Hf, metal, and PVC. Waste components were added to the base glass at 1, 5, and 10 wt % levels (except for Hf that was added at the molar equivalent of CeO2 ). The metal was added as a mixture of Pb, Cu, Al, and stainless steel (Table S3 ) as close to the required wt % as possible. Two additional glasses with 0.207 wt % CeO2 /HfO2 were made representing the molar equivalent for PuO2 in an average PCM waste drum (50 g of PuO2 in an 80 kg drum) [ 12, 14, 15, 31 ]."
