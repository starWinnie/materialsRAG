# Dataset Use: OpenKIM-derived statistical pool of 178 interatomic potentials in screening IPs for numerical instabilities and anomalous crystal responses

- DatasetUse ID: `DU_P014_002`
- Paper: `P014`

## Research task

predicting plastic flow strength from small-scale indicator properties (`T_P014_01`)

## Research stage

screening IPs for numerical instabilities and anomalous crystal responses (`candidate_screening`, `S_P014_02`)

## Dataset

OpenKIM-derived statistical pool of 178 interatomic potentials (`D_P014_openkim_derived_statistical_pool_of_178_interatomic_potentials`)

## Usage role

screening

## Purpose

Identify and remove IPs exhibiting numerical instabilities or anomalous crystal responses

## Used fields

- interatomic potential (IP)
- MD simulation results

## Construction method

selection of 178 IPs from OpenKIM repository for nine FCC metals, including all 144 EAM and FS potentials plus 34 more expensive potentials (SNAP, ADP, MEAM)

## Filter conditions

- numerical instabilities
- crystal rotation
- phase transformations
- amorphous phase formation
- voids
