## Evidence

- P105, PDF page 4, Analysis of Glass Structure: "To determine the coordination and redox state of individual elements within the glasses x-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS), experiments were conducted at the National Synchrotron Light Source II (NSLS II), Brookhaven National Laboratory, USA, and the Diamond Light Source, UK. To probe glass structure, particularly the Q-speciation of Si and B, Raman spectroscopy was performed... To further probe Si and Al in the glass matrix, NMR spectroscopy was performed..."
