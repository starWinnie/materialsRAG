# Dataset Use: subsampled Alexandria (sAlexandria) in pretraining and fine-tuning of EquiformerV2 and eSEN models on OMat24 and related datasets

- DatasetUse ID: `DU_P053_010`
- Paper: `P053`

## Research task

training Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials (MLIPs) for inorganic materials (`T_P053_01`)

## Research stage

pretraining and fine-tuning of EquiformerV2 and eSEN models on OMat24 and related datasets (`model_training`, `S_P053_04`)

## Dataset

subsampled Alexandria (sAlexandria) (`D_P110_subsampled_alexandria_salexandria`)

## Usage role

training

## Purpose

to fine-tune pretrained models on domain-specific data while preventing data leakage to the WBM test set

## Used fields

- energy
- forces
- cell stress
- atomic positions
- unit cell

## Construction method

subsampled from Alexandria by removing structures matching WBM prototypes, filtering by energy/forces/stress thresholds, and sampling structures with energy difference > 10 meV/atom

## Filter conditions

- energy > 0 eV
- forces norm > 50 eV/Å
- stress > 80 GPa
- energy difference < 10 meV/atom

Not stated.

## Evidence

- P053, PDF page 11, 4.3 OMat24 MLIP training strategies: "Since similar structures exist in the Alexandria and the WBM dataset used for testing, we subsampled the Alexandria dataset for training to ensure there was no leakage between the training and testing datasets. The new subset of Alexandria (sAlexandria) was created by removing all trajectories in which any structure matched a structure in the WBM initial and relaxed structures using structure prototype labels35. Next, we reduced the size of the Alexandria dataset by removing all structures with energies > 0 eV, forces norm > 50 eV/Å, and stress > 80 GPa. Finally, we only sampled structures in the remaining trajectories that had a energy difference greater than 10 meV/atom. The resulting datasets used for training and validation had 10 million and 500 thousand structures respectively."
