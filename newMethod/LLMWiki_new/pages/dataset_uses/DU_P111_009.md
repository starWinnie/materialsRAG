## Evidence

- P111, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "Among all compositions, the BFBT-0.02BTO ceramic exhibits optimal performance, featuring a high breakdown strength of 250 kV/cm, a high energy storage density of 4.14 J/cm3 , and excellent thermal stability."
- P111, PDF page 7, Results and Discussion: "Figure 7a–e presents the P –E curves of the BFBT- x BTO ceramics under different electric fields before breakdown. The Eb , Wrec , and η as functions of x are plotted in Figure 7h . The BFBT ceramic exhibits a relatively low Eb of 170 kV/cm. With the addition of BTO, the Eb increases significantly, reaching a peak value of 250 kV/cm at x = 0.02. Simultaneously, the Wrec is also improved, with the highest value of 4.14 J/cm3 attained at x = 0.02 with an energy efficiency of 68.92%."
