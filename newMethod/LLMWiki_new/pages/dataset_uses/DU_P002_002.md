## Purpose

Validate the geometric fidelity of the G-invariant encoding module by training it to produce embeddings where Euclidean distance corresponds to orbit distance.

## Used fields

- lattice vectors

## Construction method

Constructed synthetically with 100,000 samples per space group using random atomic positions in [0,1)^3, random lattice vectors satisfying Bravais lattice constraints, and ground-truth orbit distances.
