# Dataset Use: DOE-Generated CMAS Glass Compositions in Down-selection and Uniform Spacing of Glass Compositions

- DatasetUse ID: `DU_P106_003`
- Paper: `P106`

## Research task

Optimizing Cementitious Glasses for the Disposal of Radioactive Waste (`T_P106_01`)

## Research stage

Down-selection and Uniform Spacing of Glass Compositions (`candidate_generation`, `S_P106_02`)

## Dataset

DOE-Generated CMAS Glass Compositions (`D_P317_doe_generated_cmas_glass_compositions`)

## Usage role

candidate_pool

## Purpose

To provide the candidate glass compositions for down-selection and uniform spacing to study compositional effects on synthesis and reactivity.

## Used fields

- target composition (mol%)
- target composition (wt%)
- λ
- λn

## Construction method

Design-of-experiments (DOE) using JMP software with classical mixture design, constrained by premix component ranges (Table 1), water-to-solids ratio (W/S), and quaternary basicity (λ) ranges.

## Filter conditions

- λ-group categorization
- uniform spacing in SiO2, Al2O3, CaO, MgO
