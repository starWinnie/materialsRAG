## Evidence

- P115, PDF page 3, Results and Discussion: "To elucidate the potential reactions and phase equilibrium evolution at different temperatures in TiC-(Zr,Hf)C-Si system, an in-depth thermodynamic analysis is conducted using HSC Chemistry 6.0 and FactSage 7.1 software. Given the lack of relevant data for (Zr,Hf)C solid solution in the software databases, the analysis is carried out based on the reaction thermodynamics of TiC-ZrC-HfC-Si quaternary system. Figure 1a illustrates the variation of standard Gibbs free energy change ( ΔG ) for the potential reactions as a function of temperature. Within the temperature range of 1600◦C–1800◦C, the reaction 3/2TiC + Si = 1/2 Ti3 SiC2 + 1/2SiC exhibits a negative ΔG with the largest absolute value."
