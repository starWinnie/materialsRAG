## Evidence

- P116, PDF page 10, MATERIALS AND METHODS: "The mechanical properties of P(HEA4-co-AAm) are measured by a multifunctional mechanical tester (Shimadzu AGS-X, Japan) loaded with a 100-N load sensor. The as-prepared samples were tailored into dumbbell shapes with dimensions of 35 mm in total length, 17 mm in gauge length, and 1.35 mm in width. Both uniaxial and cyclic tensile tests were conducted at a constant stretching rate of 50 mm/min under ambient conditions (25°C and 50% relative humidity). The Young’s modulus of P(HEAx-co-AAm) is calculated from the slope of the stress-strain curve in Origin software [strain ≤3%, R2 (coefficient of determination) ≥ 99%]. The toughness of P(HEAx-co-AAm) is the integral area of a single tensile stress-strain curve."
