## Evidence

- P119, PDF page 4: "The photocatalytic properties of each La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x (0 ≤x ≤0.75) sample were evaluated using photocatalytic water reduction as a model reaction in the presence of sacrificial electron donors (Na2S/Na2SO3) under visible light irradiation (λ > 400 nm). Among the La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x samples, the x = 0 sample exhibited the highest H2 evolution rate... Under λ > 520 nm irradiation, the H2 evolution rates of these two samples became comparable... These results indicate that band gap narrowing through Cl-to-S substitution improves the intrinsic long-wavelength photoresponse."
