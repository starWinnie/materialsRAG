## Research summary

This paper proposes a data-driven framework to accelerate the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs) by creating and validating experimental databases of electrical conductivity and band gap, training state-of-the-art ML models on stoichiometry alone, and assessing their ability to identify previously unseen TCMs using a bespoke evaluation scheme—specifically, leave-one-TCM-family-out—on a list of 55 compositions.
