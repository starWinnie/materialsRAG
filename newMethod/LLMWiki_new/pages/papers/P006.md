## Research summary

This paper proposes a universal atomic embedding strategy called universal atomic embeddings (UAEs) generated by a home-made CrystalTransformer model, which learns unique 'fingerprints' for each atom capturing their roles and interactions within materials. The embeddings (ct-UAEs) are pre-trained on large crystal databases (MP*) for formation energy and bandgap prediction, then transferred to various back-end models (CGCNN, MEGNET, ALIGNN) to improve prediction accuracy across multiple datasets (MP, JARVIS, MC3D, hybrid perovskites). The work demonstrates improved MAE (e.g., 14% reduction for formation energy in CGCNN), transferability across tasks and databases, interpretability via clustering (UMAP/K-means revealing periodic-table-aligned groupings), and applicability to data-scarce domains like hybrid perovskites (34% MAE boost in MEGNET).
