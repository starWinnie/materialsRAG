## Research summary

The paper introduces an unsupervised Denoising Pre-training Framework (DPF) for crystal structures to overcome the scarcity of labeled data in crystal property prediction. DPF pre-trains models on 380,743 unlabeled crystal structures by reconstructing masked atom types, perturbed atom positions, and perturbed lattice constants; then fine-tunes the pre-trained models on downstream crystal property prediction tasks.
