## Research summary

This study employs molecular dynamics (MD) simulations and density functional theory (DFT) calculations to investigate how different functional groups (keto, ester, amide) modulate the structural and mechanical behavior of polyethylene at the atomic scale, with a focus on elucidating the microscopic mechanisms behind enhanced toughness and tensile strength in amide-functionalized PE (AMPE) via N−H···O hydrogen bond dissociation.
