## Research summary

This work synthesizes a series of entropy-increased n-type lead chalcogenide compounds Pb1−ySnySe1−xTexSx-2at%Cu to optimize the thermoelectric performance of n-type PbSe via dynamic doping driven by entropy engineering. The strategy combines configurational entropy enhancement (via Te/S/Sn alloying) with Cu interstitial doping to simultaneously improve Seebeck coefficient and reduce lattice thermal conductivity, achieving a peak ZT of ~1.46 at 623 K and record room-temperature ZT of ~0.64 for n-type PbSe.
