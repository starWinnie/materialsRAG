## Research summary

This manuscript analyzes an extensive creep-rupture dataset for Ni-based superalloy Inconel 740/740H (650–875°C, up to ~70,000 h) using Larson-Miller parameter (LMP) and Wilshire analytical approaches to assess their effectiveness in describing experimental data and—critically—in predicting long-term creep-limited lifetimes (e.g., ≥100,000 h) from shorter-term data (<5,000 h). The study evaluates prediction accuracy by direct comparison of model-predicted rupture times against measured values, identifies mathematical instability ('turnback') in the LMP formalism under extrapolation, and concludes the Wilshire approach is superior for long-term lifetime forecasting.
