## Research summary

This study investigates the temperature-dependent steam oxidation behavior and hoop strength degradation of silicon carbide fiber-reinforced silicon carbide (SiCf /SiC) tube exposed to steam at 600, 900, and 1200◦C for 10 days. Site-specific post-test characterization was combined with mechanical testing to establish the relationship between oxidation-induced damage and mechanical degradation.
