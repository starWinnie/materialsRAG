## Research summary

The paper introduces WPDD (Atom-Weighted Pairwise Distance Distribution) and UPDD (Unit-cell Pairwise Distance Distribution) to construct continuous and geometrically complete multi-edge crystal graphs, integrating them into a transformer-based architecture (PDDFormer) for crystal material property prediction. It addresses discontinuity and incompleteness in prior crystal graph representations by modeling PDD as global information in matrix-based message passing, and validates continuity via Earth Mover’s Distance (EMD) and general geometric completeness via categorical induction. Experiments on Materials Project and JARVIS-DFT show state-of-the-art MAE across multiple properties including formation energy, band gap, bulk moduli, and shear moduli.
