## Research summary

This paper proposes an ensemble machine learning framework (ECSG) that integrates three base-level models—Magpie, Roost, and a newly developed Electron Configuration Convolutional Neural Network (ECCNN)—via stacked generalization to predict the thermodynamic stability of inorganic compounds. The method achieves high accuracy (AUC = 0.988 on JARVIS), exceptional sample efficiency (requiring only 1/7 the data of existing models), and strong generalization in unknown composition spaces (e.g., perovskite halides, Li-containing oxides, transition metal oxides). Validation includes computational (DFT) and experimental case studies on 2D wide bandgap semiconductors and double perovskite oxides.
