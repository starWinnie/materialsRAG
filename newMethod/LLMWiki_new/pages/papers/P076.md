## Research summary

This paper experimentally measures and compares sound velocities (longitudinal and transverse) and Young’s modulus in partially sintered alumina, zirconia, ATZ composite, and mullite ceramics across a broad porosity range, then evaluates how well various theoretical models—such as exponential, percolation, differential, self-consistent, and numerical benchmark relations—predict the observed porosity dependence.
