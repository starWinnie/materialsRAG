## Research summary

The paper introduces Polymer Benchmark 2026 (PolyBench26), an open dataset of ~250,000 polymer-property datapoints across eight physical properties and four polymer architectures (homopolymers, alternating/random/block copolymers), sourced from experimental measurements, DFT, and MD simulations. It defines and executes four standardized evaluation tasks — in-distribution prediction, dataset-size scaling, repeat-unit complexity, and transfer to held-out architectures — to systematically compare language model, graph-based, and descriptor-based ML approaches for polymer property prediction. Graph-based models (PC, PPG) consistently outperform others across tasks.
