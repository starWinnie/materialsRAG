## Research summary

This study investigates the microstructural evolution of a new as-cast alumina-forming austenitic (AFA) heat-resistant alloy under multi-pass isothermal continuous hot deformation, focusing on grain evolution, recrystallization mechanisms (DRX and SRX), and the interaction between recrystallization and the segregated MC-type carbide phase.
