## Research summary

This study leverages multi-rank equivariant structural embeddings from a universal neural network potential to enhance predictions of dielectric tensors. We develop an equivariant readout decoder to predict total, electronic, and ionic dielectric tensors while preserving O(3) equivariance, and benchmark its performance against state-of-the-art algorithms. Virtual screening of thermodynamically stable materials from Materials Project for two discovery tasks, high-dielectric and highly anisotropic materials, identifies promising candidates including Cs2Ti(WO4)3 (band gap Eg = 2.93eV, dielectric constant ε = 180.90) and CsZrCuSe3 (anisotropic ratio αr = 121.89). The results demonstrate our model’s accuracy in predicting dielectric tensors and its potential for discovering novel dielectric materials.
