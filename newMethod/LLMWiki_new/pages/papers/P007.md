## Research summary

The paper proposes an equivariant attention tensor graph neural network (EATGNN) to predict the full third-rank piezoelectric tensor for both bulk and 2D materials, leveraging irreducible representation decomposition to respect crystal symmetry and rotational equivariance. It validates predictions against DFT-calculated tensors, achieves state-of-the-art accuracy, and uses the model to screen and discover new high-performance piezoelectric candidates, subsequently confirmed by DFPT calculations.
