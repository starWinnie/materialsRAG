## Research summary

This paper presents a low-temperature mechanosynthesis route to produce BaS–La2S3 ceramic powders, confirms the existence of a continuous solid solution between La2S3 and BaLa2S4, verifies adherence to Vegard’s law, identifies a sulfur-loss thermal event near 920 K, and demonstrates successful incorporation of up to 6 mol% Dy3+ without phase separation.
