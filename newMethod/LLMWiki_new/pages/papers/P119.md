# Paper: Band Gap Narrowing in Oxychlorosulfide Photocatalysts through Aliovalent Cl-to-S Anion Substitution

- Extraction status: completed

## Research summary

This study demonstrates aliovalent Cl-to-S substitution in the oxychlorosulfide La5Ti6O15S3Cl3 by simultaneously replacing Cl– with S2– and Ti4+ with Ta5+, which elevates the valence-band maximum via increased S 3p orbital contribution, narrows the band gap, and yields visible-light-responsive H2-evolution photocatalysts.
