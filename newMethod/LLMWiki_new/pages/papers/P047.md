## Research summary

The paper proposes a Physics Guided Crystal Generative Model (PGCGM) for generative design of crystal materials with high structural diversity and symmetry, using deep learning integrated with physical constraints (atomic pairwise distance and structural symmetry) to improve generation validity and stability. Generated structures are validated via DFT calculations, with 1,869 out of 2,000 successfully optimized and deposited into the Carolina Materials Database; 39.6% have negative formation energy and 5.3% have energy-above-hull < 0.25 eV/atom.
