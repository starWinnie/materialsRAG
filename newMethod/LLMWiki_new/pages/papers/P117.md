## Research summary

This study uses ReaxFF-based reactive molecular dynamics to translate sustained CH4 and CH4 + H2O exposure on a Ni(211) surface under an accelerated closed-cell protocol at 1000–1350 K into exposure-conditioned hydrogen availability descriptors. It quantifies normalized Ni-associated H inventory, hydrogen partitioning between adsorbed and subsurface regions, and localization across CN-defined step and terrace regions to provide candidate inputs for downstream simulations of hydrogen transport, trapping, and degradation.
