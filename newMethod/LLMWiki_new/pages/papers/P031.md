## Research summary

The paper introduces SuperCon3D, a new dataset containing both 3D crystal structures (ordered and disordered) and experimental superconducting transition temperatures (Tc), and proposes two deep learning models: SODNet for screening known structures to predict Tc, and DiffCSP-SC for generating novel high-Tc superconductor candidates. It validates the models by screening ICSD to identify candidate superconductors and generating novel structures, with some candidates further validated via DFT calculations for electronic features like Van Hove singularities.
