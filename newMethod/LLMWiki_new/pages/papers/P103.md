# Paper: Oxygen-mediated high uniform plasticity in α-β titanium alloys

## Research summary

This paper presents a dual design strategy for α–β titanium alloys that transforms high oxygen from an embrittling liability into a performance enabler by (i) leveraging high oxygen (≥0.40%) to activate prominent pyramidal <c + a> slip in the α-phase, and (ii) engineering a tailored α–β microstructure through alloy design (Ti–O–Fe), laser-based powder bed fusion, and annealing to enable sustained slip transfer across α–β interfaces—achieving record uniform elongations (≥14% at yield strengths ≥980 MPa) while simultaneously resolving the yield strength–uniform elongation trade-off and oxygen embrittlement.
