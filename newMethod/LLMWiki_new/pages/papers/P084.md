## Research summary

This paper reports the design, synthesis, and application of a novel bifunctional chiral peptide catalyst containing both an aminoxyl and a bipyridyl motif (Aminoxyl−Peptide−BpyAla) for the atroposelective copper(I)-catalyzed aerobic oxidation of prochiral biaryl diols to yield axially chiral monoaldehydes with high enantiomeric ratios (up to >99.5:0.5 er). The work demonstrates that bifunctionality—co-localization of both catalytic motifs within a single peptide scaffold—is essential for enantioselectivity, as monofunctional analogues (aminoxyl-only or bipyridyl-only peptides) gave racemic products. Mechanistic studies (kinetics, DFT modeling) support a monomeric active catalyst species with a rigid, sterically confined chiral environment around copper, where selectivity arises from a combination of enantiotopic group selection and secondary kinetic resolution.
