## Research summary

MaterialsGalaxy is a platform that deeply fuses heterogeneous experimental and theoretical data in condensed matter physics using a structure similarity-driven data fusion mechanism. It links cross-modal records (diffraction, crystal growth, computations, literature) based on atomic structures, integrates AI tools (LLMs, generative models, ML property predictors), and enables dynamic data augmentation via structural analogs to bridge experiment-theory gaps and accelerate materials discovery.
