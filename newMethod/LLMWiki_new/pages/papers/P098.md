# Paper: Application of polymethacrylate from waste organic glass as a pour point depressor in heavy crude oil

- Paper ID: `P098`
- Year: 2018
- DOI: 10.1016/j.petrol.2017.12.041
- File: `307_1-s2.0-S0920410517309981-main.pdf`
- Extraction status: completed

## Research summary

This work prepared polymethacrylate (PMA) via transesterification of waste polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA, organic glass) and aliphatic alcohols (hexadecanol or octadecanol), catalyzed by CaO, and evaluated its performance as a pour point depressant and viscosity reducer in heavy crude oil samples from Jinghe Oil Field. PMA-1 (from hexadecanol) achieved up to 7.1 °C pour point depression at 2000 ppm dosage and reduced viscosity by 63.7% at 50 °C; mechanism studies using DSC and polarized microscopy confirmed PMA-1 inhibits paraffin crystallization and modifies crystal morphology.
