## Research summary

This paper presents a comprehensive benchmark study evaluating graph neural network (GNN) models for predicting material properties on out-of-distribution (OOD) test sets—materials structurally or property-wise distinct from the training set. It introduces five OOD test set generation methods (LOCO, SparseXsingle, SparseXcluster, SparseYsingle, SparseYcluster) applied to three MatBench datasets (dielectric, elasticity, perovskites), trains and evaluates eight SOTA GNNs across 250 folds, and analyzes performance degradation relative to i.i.d. baselines, revealing significant generalization gaps and identifying CGCNN, ALIGNN, and DeeperGATGNN as comparatively robust.
