## Research summary

This work introduces JARVIS-Leaderboard, an open-source and community-driven platform that facilitates benchmarking and enhances reproducibility for materials design methods. The platform covers five categories: Artificial Intelligence (AI), Electronic Structure (ES), Force-fields (FF), Quantum Computation (QC), and Experiments (EXP), with benchmarks spanning atomic structures, spectra, images, and text. It includes 1281 contributions to 274 benchmarks using 152 methods and over 8 million data-points, enabling systematic, reproducible, transparent, and unbiased scientific development.
