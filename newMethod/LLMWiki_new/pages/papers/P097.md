## Research summary

This paper investigates the counterintuitive field evaporation sequence in [001]-oriented γ-TiAl using full-dynamics molecular dynamics simulations (TAPSim-MD), revealing that Ti atoms evaporate preferentially over Al despite stronger Ti–Ti bonds, due to a two-step bond-breaking process rather than simultaneous bond rupture. It challenges thermal-activation-based models (e.g., Müller’s formula) that assume uniform charge states and image-charge potentials, showing instead that evaporation is governed by dynamic, anisotropic bond-breaking kinetics decoupled from sublimation energy.
