## Research summary

This paper introduces the NOMAD Artificial-Intelligence Toolkit, a web-browser-based infrastructure for interactive AI-based analysis of FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) materials-science data stored in the NOMAD Archive. It enables users to query, analyze, and model materials data via Jupyter notebooks, supporting reproducible, educational, and community-driven AI workflows — including exploratory clustering and predictive modeling (e.g., topological insulator classification) — without local software installation.
