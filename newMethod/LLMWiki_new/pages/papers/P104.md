## Research summary

This paper investigates how heating rate affects the sintering behavior of ≈40 nm 3 mol% yttria-stabilized zirconia (3YSZ) powder, comparing fast firing (FF) with conventional sintering. Using dilatometry, density measurements, mercury porosimetry, SEM, and finite element modeling, the authors construct a master sintering curve (MSC) and evaluate densification pathways, pore evolution, closed pore formation, and microstructural development. They conclude that rapid heating yields only modest improvements (<2%–3% relative density) and that the MSC model remains valid, indicating the microstructure depends primarily on density—not thermal history.
