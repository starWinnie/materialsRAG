# Paper: MD-HIT: Machine learning for material property prediction with dataset redundancy control

- Paper ID: `P026`
- Year: 2024
- DOI: 10.1038/s41524-024-01426-z
- File: `26_MD_HIT.pdf`
- Extraction status: completed

## Research summary

This paper addresses the overestimation of machine learning model performance in materials science caused by dataset redundancy—i.e., highly similar materials in databases like Materials Project due to historical 'tinkering' in material design. It proposes MD-HIT, a redundancy reduction algorithm inspired by bioinformatics’ CD-HIT, with two variants: MD-HIT-composition (using composition-based distances like Mendeleev and MatScholar) and MD-HIT-structure (using structure-based distances like XRD and OFM). The authors apply MD-HIT to generate non-redundant datasets for formation energy and band gap prediction, then evaluate how redundancy control affects the predictive performance (R², MAE) of state-of-the-art ML models (Roost, CrabNet, ALIGNN, DeeperGATGNN), demonstrating that reduced redundancy leads to lower—but more realistic—performance, especially for out-of-distribution (OOD) samples.
