## Research summary

This paper characterizes the superconducting gap structure in the high-temperature superconductor H3S and its deuterium counterpart D3S using tunnelling spectroscopy measurements. The work provides direct experimental evidence for superconductivity from a microscopic perspective, validates the phonon-mediated pairing mechanism, and identifies multigap features in multiphase hydrogen sulfide samples.
