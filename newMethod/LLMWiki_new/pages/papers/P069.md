## Research summary

This paper presents a curated database of electrochemical metrics for corrosion resistant alloys (CRAs), collected from 85 publications, to enable machine learning–based exploration of compositional space beyond existing empirical models. The database includes six categories of metrics: pitting potential (Epit), repassivation potential (Erp), crevice corrosion potential (Ecrev), pitting temperature (Tpit), crevice corrosion temperature (Tcrev), and corrosion potential (Ecorr), corrosion current density (icorr), passivation current density (ipass), and corrosion rate. It covers Al- and Fe-based alloys, high entropy alloys (HEAs), and Ni-Cr-Mo ternary systems, with associated experimental metadata (e.g., composition, temperature, chloride concentration, pH, test solution, microstructure). The dataset is intended to support the design and optimization of highly corrosion resistant alloys.
