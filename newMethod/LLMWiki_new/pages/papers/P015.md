## Research summary

This study develops a meta-learning methodology—extrapolative episodic training (E2T)—to enhance the ability of machine learning models to predict material properties beyond the distribution of training data. Using attention-based matching neural networks (MNNs) trained on arbitrarily generated extrapolative episodes, the approach demonstrates superior generalization for unseen material domains in two case studies: predicting specific heat and refractive index of polymeric materials, and bandgap of hybrid organic–inorganic perovskites. The method enables rapid adaptation to target domains via fine-tuning with limited data.
