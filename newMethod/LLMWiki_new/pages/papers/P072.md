# Paper: Microstructure Evolution and Mechanical Properties of X6CrNiMoVNb11-2 Stainless Steel after Heat Treatment

## Research summary

This paper investigates how quenching and tempering heat treatments affect the microstructure and mechanical properties of X6CrNiMoVNb11-2 supermartensitic stainless steel, with the goal of optimizing process parameters (e.g., quenching at 1040 °C in oil, tempering at 650 °C in air) to achieve target hardness (~313 HV), elongation (17.9%), reduction of area (52%), and impact toughness (~65 J/cm2). It analyzes phase transformations (Ms, Mf, Ac1, Ac3), nanophase precipitation (M23C6, Ni3Mo, Ni3Nb), and derives a tempered hardness equation.
