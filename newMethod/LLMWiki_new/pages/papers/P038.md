## Research summary

This work uses pre-trained machine learning models from the Open Catalyst 2022 (OC22) framework to interpolate total energy predictions for OER surface intermediates on 4119 oxide materials, then constructs a flexible high-throughput screening framework to identify viable anode catalyst candidates for the oxygen evolution reaction (OER) under bulk and nanoscale Pourbaix stability, material cost, overpotential, and metastability constraints.
