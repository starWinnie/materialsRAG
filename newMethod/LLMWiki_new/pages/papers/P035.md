## Research summary

The paper presents a two-stage correction framework to improve the agreement of density functional theory (DFT) formation energies with experimental values. First, it leverages foundational machine learning interatomic potentials (fMLIPs), specifically PET-OMATPES trained at the r2SCAN level, to compute zero-shot formation energies on DFT-relaxed geometries, reducing mean absolute error (MAE) by over 40% relative to GGA without additional DFT calculations. Second, it trains classical machine learning models (KRR, RF, GPR) in a delta-learning framework using the latent features of the fMLIP to predict the residual between the fMLIP-estimated formation energy and experiment, achieving a final MAE below 50 meV/atom—comparable to experimental uncertainty—while controlling the impact on relative phase stability through careful regularization.
