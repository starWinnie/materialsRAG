## Research summary

This paper introduces the Crystal Fourier Transformer (CFT), a single neural architecture that adapts its weights to enforce exact invariance to any input crystallographic space group. It achieves this by constructing symmetry-adapted Fourier bases through analytical derivation of constraints that group operations impose on Fourier coefficients, encoding these constraints into a group-conditional routing matrix (adjacency matrix of a dual graph). The architecture enables weight sharing across all 230 space groups, supports zero-shot generalization to unseen groups, and demonstrates competitive performance on material property prediction tasks.
