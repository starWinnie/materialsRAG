## Research summary

The paper presents the Lignin Structural (LGS) Dataset, which includes 60 K computer-generated molecular structures of milled wood lignin focusing on coniferyl and syringyl monomeric units and six common interunit linkages. Structural data were generated using a sequence-controlled polymer generation approach calibrated to match experimental lignin properties, covering polymer chains of length 3–25 monomer units. The dataset matches experimental structural compositions with ~90% accuracy and serves as a resource for computational simulation and predictive modelling of lignin.
