## Research summary

This study explores the effects of co-substitution with W and Mn at the B-site on the structural and functional properties of Na0.5Bi4.5Ti4O15 (NBT) ceramics, aiming to enhance piezoelectric performance for high-temperature applications. The optimal co-doped composition NBTWM5 achieves a piezoelectric coefficient (d33) of 34 pC/N — 100% higher than undoped NBT — while retaining a high Curie temperature (TC = 653°C) and elevated electrical resistivity (1.16 × 107 Ω·cm at 500°C). Enhancement is attributed to synergistic structural distortion and domain regulation induced by W/Mn doping.
