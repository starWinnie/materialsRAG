## Research summary

This paper presents the largest computed non-crystalline structure database to date, generated from systematic and accurate ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) calculations, covering 4849 compositions and 79 elements. It demonstrates how the database enables simple machine-learning models to predict Li-ion diffusivity with speed and accuracy as a cost-effective alternative to expensive DFT calculations. The work also explores using universal machine learning potentials (M3GNet, CHGNet) as surrogates for AIMD to accelerate non-crystalline structure generation.
