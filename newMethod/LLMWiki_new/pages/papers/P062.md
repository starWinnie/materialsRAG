## Research summary

The paper introduces the Open Catalyst 2025 (OC25) dataset—the largest solid–liquid interface dataset for electrocatalysis—and uses OC25-trained machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) to perform large-scale, explicit-solvent simulations of CO dimerization on Cu surfaces. It computes free-energy profiles under varied surface charge, cation identity, and surface facet (Cu(100) vs Cu(310)), revealing weak sensitivity to charge/cation except at highly negative potentials, and identifies stepped Cu(310) as a more favorable pathway with exergonic dimerization at modest reducing potentials.
