## Research summary

This paper presents MD-ET, a minimally modified edge transformer architecture for molecular dynamics (MD) that intentionally omits built-in rotational equivariance and energy conservation—two common physical inductive biases in neural MD models. Trained on ∼30 × 10⁶ structures from the QCML database via supervised pretraining, MD-ET achieves state-of-the-art results on multiple benchmarks after few-shot fine-tuning. The study evaluates how well an 'off-the-shelf' transformer can learn approximate equivariance and energy conservation purely from data, and assesses its ability to perform stable NVE and faithful NVT MD simulations.
