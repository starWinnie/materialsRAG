## Research summary

This paper proposes a novel preparation method to synergistically enhance the sinterability and mechanical properties of TiC-based composites via in situ reaction among TiC, (Zr,Hf)C and Si. Based on this method, a multiphase microstructure is successfully constructed, consisting of nano-SiC particles, micro-scale Ti3SiC2, and (Ti,Zr,Hf)C solid solution within TiC matrix. Benefiting from this composite structure, the optimized composite achieves near-full densification (relative density > 99%) and exhibits superior mechanical properties, with a flexural strength of 972 MPa and a fracture toughness of 6.7 MPa ⋅m1/2. The enhancement in mechanical properties is attributed to a multi-scale synergistic strengthening mechanism, which includes solid solution strengthening at the atomic scale, grain boundary pinning by nano-SiC, coupled with crack deflection, and bridging induced by plate-like Ti3SiC2 grains.
