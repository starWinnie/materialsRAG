## Research summary

The paper proposes CrysDiff, a diffusion-based pretrain-finetune framework to address the challenge of crystal property prediction under sparse labeled data. It first pre-trains a model via self-supervised crystal structure reconstruction (learning p(F, L | A) from unlabeled 3D crystal structures), then fine-tunes it on limited labeled data to predict target properties (modeling p(y | A', F', L')). The method enforces crystal symmetries (O(3) invariance and periodic translation invariance) using an EGNN-based denoising model in both phases.
