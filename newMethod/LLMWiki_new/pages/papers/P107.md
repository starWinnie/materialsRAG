## Research summary

A series of mixed alkaline aluminosilicate glasses with low iron concentrations (0.04–0.64 wt% Fe2O3) was studied, covering a wide range of properties, including melt viscosity, liquidus temperature, simulated Rosseland mean radiative absorption/conduction, fiber tensile strength, water contact angle, and surface tension. To elucidate the local environments of ferric and ferrous ions in the glass network, multiple spectroscopic techniques (UV–vis–near infrared (NIR), fourier transform infrared (FTIR), and Raman spectroscopy) and molecular dynamics (MD) simulations were used.
