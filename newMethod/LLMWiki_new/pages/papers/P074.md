## Research summary

This paper develops quantitative structure–property relationship (QSPR) models to predict the dielectric constant (ε) of polymers using machine learning, specifically a genetic algorithm combined with multiple linear regression (GA-MLRA). It uses structural descriptors computed from monomer units of 71 diverse polymers, validates models via internal and external validation (including test set, LOO cross-validation, y-scrambling, and Williams plots), and proposes two mechanistically explainable models (4- and 8-descriptor) with high predictive performance (R2_train = 0.905, R2_test = 0.812 for the best model).
