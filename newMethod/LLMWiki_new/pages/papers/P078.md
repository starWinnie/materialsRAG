# Paper: Constructing high-polarization silica for polyimide-based nanocomposites with enhanced dielectric permittivity and mechanical performances: Molecular dynamics simulations

- Year: 2023
- DOI: 10.1002/app.54321
- File: `214_ased nanocomposites with.pdf`
- Extraction status: completed

## Research summary

This work employs silane coupling agents with different functional groups (PhCOOH, NH2, Ph, PhNH2, PhCOCH3) to modify the interfaces of polyimide/SiO2 (PIS) nanocomposites, and predicts the effects of interfacial structures on dielectric and mechanical properties using molecular dynamics simulation. The results show that the PhCOOH-modified interface simultaneously elevates static dielectric permittivity (εs) and Young modulus due to enhanced intrinsic polarization of SiO2 particles, interface polarization, increased hydrogen bonds, non-bond interactions, and improved interfacial load transfer efficiency.
