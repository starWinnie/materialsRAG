# Paper: Large plasticity in magnesium mediated by pyramidal dislocations

- Paper ID: `P085`
- Year: 2019
- DOI: 10.1126/science.aaw2843
- File: `229_Liu19LiuScience.pdf`
- Extraction status: completed

## Research summary

The paper investigates the fundamental deformation mechanism responsible for large plasticity in submicrometer-size magnesium single crystals, specifically resolving the controversy around the mobility and slip planes of ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations under c-axis compression at room temperature. Using in situ TEM mechanical testing, 3D tomography, and atomistic simulations, the authors demonstrate that ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations are glissile and accommodate plastic strain by gliding on both pyramidal I {101̄1} and pyramidal II {112̄2} planes, including cross-slip between them, thereby explaining the observed high strength and ductility.
