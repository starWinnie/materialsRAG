## Research summary

The paper introduces classical force-field inspired descriptors (CFID) — combining radial, nearest neighbor, bond-angle, dihedral-angle, and core-charge distributions with chemical descriptors — to enable accurate machine learning prediction of multiple material properties. Using gradient boosting decision trees trained on the JARVIS-DFT database (24,549 bulk + 616 monolayer materials), it achieves high accuracy for formation energies, bandgaps, refractive indices, magnetic properties, elastic moduli, and exfoliation energies. The models are applied to screen exfoliable 2D semiconductors and integrated with a genetic algorithm to map energy landscapes (convex hulls) for multicomponent systems, validating against DFT.
