## Research summary

This study evaluates 15 large language models (LLMs) on the MaScQA benchmark—a curated set of 644 questions from materials science and metallurgical engineering—to establish a baseline for their question-answering capabilities, identify top-performing models for future fine-tuning, and highlight domain-specific weaknesses (especially in numerical reasoning). The work supports an overarching project to fine-tune an LLM and integrate it into a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system for materials science applications.
