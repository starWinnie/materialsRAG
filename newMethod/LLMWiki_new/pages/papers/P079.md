# Paper: A dataset of micro-scale tomograms of unidirectional glass fibre/epoxy and carbon fibre/epoxy composites acquired via synchrotron computed tomography during in-situ tensile loading

- Year: 2021

- Extraction status: completed

## Research summary

This paper presents a publicly available dataset of 3D synchrotron X-ray computed tomography (XCT) images of two unidirectional fiber-reinforced composites — glass/epoxy and carbon/epoxy — acquired during in-situ tensile loading at four load levels (0%, 40%, 73%, and 95% of failure load). The data consists of raw and filtered (non-local means + ring artifact removal) 8-bit TIFF image stacks, cropped to volumes of interest (VOIs) from the middle 0° plies, enabling microstructural analysis of fiber distribution, orientation, misalignment, and break formation under load.
