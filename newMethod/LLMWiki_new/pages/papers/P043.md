## Research summary

This work presents a machine learning approach to systematically correct intrinsic energy resolution errors in density functional theory (DFT) calculations of alloy formation enthalpies, improving reliability for ternary phase stability predictions. A neural network model is trained to predict the discrepancy (Hcorr = HDFT − Hexpt) between DFT-calculated and experimentally measured enthalpies of formation for binary and ternary alloys (specifically Al–Ni–Pd and Al–Ni–Ti), using structured features including elemental concentrations, atomic numbers, and interaction terms. The corrected enthalpies (Hpred = HDFT − Hcorr) enable more accurate determination of phase stability.
