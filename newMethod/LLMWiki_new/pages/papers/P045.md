## Research summary

This paper constructs a large-scale dataset of 35,675 solution-based inorganic materials synthesis 'recipes' extracted from scientific literature using advanced machine learning and natural language processing techniques. Each recipe contains precursors and target materials, their quantities, synthesis actions and attributes, and augmented reaction formulas. The work addresses the lack of large-scale databases for inorganic synthesis routes needed to enable data-driven approaches to AI-assisted synthesis design and optimization.
