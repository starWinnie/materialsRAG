## Research summary

This paper introduces MatterChat, a multi-modal large language model that integrates atomic-level material structural data (via graph embeddings from CHGNet) with natural language prompts to enable human-AI interaction for material property prediction, structural analysis, and scientific reasoning. It uses a trainable bridge model to align frozen pretrained uMLIP (CHGNet) and LLM (Mistral-7B) components, enabling tasks such as chemical formula identification, space group classification, stability assessment, formation energy prediction, and step-by-step synthesis guidance — outperforming both general-purpose LLMs and physical ML models.
