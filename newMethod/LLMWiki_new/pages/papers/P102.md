## Research summary

This paper presents LLaMat, a family of domain-adapted large language models for materials science, developed through continued pretraining on materials literature and crystallographic data, followed by instruction- and task-finetuning. It demonstrates superior performance over commercial LLMs in materials-specific NLP, structured information extraction, and crystal structure generation, while revealing an 'adaptation rigidity' phenomenon where LLaMat-2 outperforms LLaMat-3 across diverse tasks.
