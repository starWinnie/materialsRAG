## Research summary

This paper develops an AI framework integrating Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) with a configuration entropy–based acquisition function and Design of Experiments (DoE) to screen and optimize multiprincipal element alloys (MPEAs) for water splitting electrocatalysis. Through Bayesian optimization across 16.2 million compositions, it identifies Fe12Co28Ni33Mo17Pd5Pt5 as the optimal composition, achieving ultralow overpotentials (24 mV for HER, 204 mV for OER at 10 mA·cm−2) and robust stability, surpassing state-of-the-art benchmarks.
