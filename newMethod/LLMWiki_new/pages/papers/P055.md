## Research summary

This study constructs interpretable materials maps that visualize relationships between structural features and experimental thermoelectric properties (zT), using an integrated dataset of computational structures and predicted experimental zT values. The maps are generated via graph-based deep learning (specifically MPNN within MatDeepLearn) followed by t-SNE dimensionality reduction, and are evaluated for structural organization (via nearest neighbor distance analysis) and interpretability — not for predictive accuracy. The work emphasizes supporting experimentalists in navigating synthesis-feasible regions of materials space based on structural similarity.
