## Research summary

This work reports a strategy for the rapid fabrication of stiff, tough, and stretchable polymers via photocuring copolymerization of acrylamide-based and hydroxyl-terminated acrylate monomers, enabling high-performance polymer materials with simultaneously high Young’s modulus (515.0 ± 100.9 MPa), yield strength (57.8 ± 1.8 MPa), toughness (135.7 ± 10.7 MJ/m3), and fracture strain (400.1 ± 46.1%), plus full strain recovery upon heating — all achieved within 1 minute using simple photopolymerization.
