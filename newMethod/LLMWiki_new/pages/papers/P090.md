## Research summary

This study systematically investigates the independent and combined effects of three critical spark plasma sintering (SPS) parameters—temperature, applied pressure, and dwell time—on the densification behavior, microstructure, and mechanical properties of ZrB2–20 vol% SiC composites. It evaluates two powder preparation routes (WC-milled vs. ZrO2-milled) to assess how sintering parameter sensitivity depends on processing history and impurity levels, aiming to optimize additive-free, energy-efficient processing for aerospace and thermal protection applications.
