## Research summary

This study synthesizes Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramics to investigate how Nd doping drives a structural transition from polar R3c to antipolar Pbam phase, identifying a morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) near x = 0.16; it demonstrates significantly enhanced dynamic magnetoelectric coupling (~300 μV/(cm·Oe)) and exceptional thermal stability of the ME response up to 480 K at the MPB composition, attributing the enhancement to synergistic piezoelectricity and magnetostriction.
