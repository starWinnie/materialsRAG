## Research summary

This editorial outlines five high-impact research areas in materials informatics: (1) validation by experiment or physics-based simulation; (2) ML approaches tailored for materials data and applications; (3) high-throughput data acquisition capabilities; (4) ML that makes scientists more effective (e.g., explainability, accelerated characterization); and (5) integration of physics within ML and with physics-based simulations. It emphasizes challenges including sparse/noisy/heterogeneous data, need for validation beyond model fitting, uncertainty quantification, extrapolation detection, descriptor development, generative design, and physics-informed modeling.
