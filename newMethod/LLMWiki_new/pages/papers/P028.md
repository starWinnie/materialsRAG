## Research summary

JARVIS-Leaderboard is an open-source, community-driven platform for benchmarking and enhancing reproducibility in materials science. It integrates computational, experimental, and data-analytics methods across five categories—Artificial Intelligence (AI), Electronic Structure (ES), Force-fields (FF), Quantum Computation (QC), and Experiments (EXP)—and supports multiple data modalities (atomic structures, spectra, images, text). The platform hosts 274 benchmarks, 152 methods, 1281 contributions, and over 8 million data points, enabling systematic, transparent, and unbiased validation of materials design approaches.
