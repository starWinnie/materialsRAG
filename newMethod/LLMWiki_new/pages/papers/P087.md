# Paper: Experiment and Modelling of the Pre-Strain Effect on the Creep Behaviour of P/M Ni-Based Superalloy FGH96

- Paper ID: `P087`
- Year: 2023
- DOI: 10.3390/ma16103874
- File: `232_materials-16-03874.pdf`
- Extraction status: completed

## Research summary

This study investigates how room-temperature pre-strain affects the high-temperature creep behaviour of P/M Ni-based superalloy FGH96, combining experimental creep testing (at 700 °C and 690 MPa) with microstructural characterization (OM, SEM, TEM) and development of a physics-based steady-state creep rate model that explicitly incorporates the micro-twinning mechanism and pre-strain-induced mobile dislocation density evolution.
