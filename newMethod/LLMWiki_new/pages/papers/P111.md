## Research summary

To develop high-performance capacitor materials, (1- x )(0.65BiFeO3 -0.35BaTiO3 )- x Bi2 Ti2 O7 ceramics (abbreviated as BFBT- x BTO, x = 0, 0.02, 0.04, 0.06, and 0.08) were synthesized by a solid-state reaction method. First, BFBT and BTO ceramic powders were prepared separately by the solid-state reaction method. Subsequently, these two powders were mixed homogeneously to fabricate BFBT- x BTO solid solution ceramics. x-Ray diffraction (XRD) analysis confirms that BTO diffuses into the crystal lattice of BFBT and forms a perovskite solid solution. SEM analysis reveals that the grain size of BFBT is reduced and the grain boundary density is increased after BTO doping, which facilitates the improvement of energy storage performance. Moreover, benefiting from the intrinsic advantages of BTO, such as low dielectric loss and low leakage current density, the breakdown strength ( Eb ) and the energy storage density of BFBT- x BTO ceramics are significantly enhanced. Meanwhile, the concentration of oxygen vacancies rises prominently in the outer grain regions, constructing a continuous protective barrier that also facilitates the enhancement of Eb . Among all compositions, the BFBT-0.02BTO ceramic exhibits optimal performance, featuring a high breakdown strength of 250 kV/cm, a high energy storage density of 4.14 J/cm3 , and excellent thermal stability.
