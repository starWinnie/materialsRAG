# Stage: Synthesis of Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramics

- Stage ID: `S_P109_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P109_01` — Enhanced Magnetoelectric Coupling and Thermal Stability in Nd-Doped BiFeO3 Ceramics Near the Morphotropic Phase Boundary

## Goal

To prepare a compositional series of Nd-doped BiFeO3 ceramics across the suspected MPB region.

## Input

- Bi2O3 (99.9%)
- Nd2O3 (99.99%)
- Fe2O3 (99.9%)
- stoichiometric ratios for x = 0.05–0.22

## Output

- Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramic pellets (x = 0.05, 0.08, 0.10, 0.14, 0.16, 0.18, 0.22)

## Datasets used

- Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramic composition series — source: Source material for synthesis of Nd-doped BiFeO3 ceramic pellets across x = 0.05–0.22

## Evidence

- P109, PDF page 2, 2.1 Sample Preparation: "Bi1- x Ndx FeO3 ceramics were synthesized via a conventional two-step solid-state reaction method. High-purity powders of Bi2 O3 (99.9%), Nd2 O3 (99.99%), and Fe2 O3 (99.9%) (Aladdin, China) were utilized as starting materials. These precursors were stoichiometrically weighed and ball-milled in an ethanol medium for 8 h. The dried mixtures were then calcined at 750◦C for 4 h to facilitate phase formation. The resulting powders underwent a secondary 10 h ball-milling process, after which polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) was added as a binder. The powders were then uniaxially pressed into pellets at 100 MPa for 2 min. Following binder burnout at 550◦C for 4 h, the green bodies were sintered for 2 h at temperatures ranging from 790◦C to 900◦C, optimized according to the Nd doping concentration."
