# Stage: deterministic validation using simplified model system

- Stage ID: `S_P097_05`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P097_01` — interpreting field evaporation through dynamic bond-breaking processes

## Goal

Confirm the two-step Ti bond-breaking mechanism under identical external conditions by isolating atomic identity in a controlled LAMMPS model.

## Input

- simplified LAMMPS model with fixed-direction external force on kink atom
- identical structural setup for Ti vs. Al kink atoms

## Output

- direct comparison of Ti vs. Al critical detachment forces (1.81 vs. 1.87 eV/Å)
- displacement and velocity profiles confirming earlier Ti evaporation

## Datasets used

- TAPSim-MD simplified LAMMPS model system for deterministic analysis — computational_validation: Provide deterministic validation of the two-step Ti bond-breaking mechanism by isolating atomic identity in a controlled LAMMPS model with identical external conditions.
