# Stage: Post-PBF-LB annealing to optimize microstructure and eliminate metastable phases

- Stage ID: `S_P103_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P103_01` — Design of high-strength, high-uniform-elongation α–β titanium alloys resistant to oxygen embrittlement

## Goal

Select optimal annealing temperature (800 °C for Ti-0.45O-4Fe; 900 °C for Ti-0.5O-5Fe) to eliminate ω-phase, relieve residual stresses, stabilize α–β lamellar structure, and minimize dislocation density while maintaining coherent interfaces and desired lamellar thickness.

## Input

- as-printed specimens
- annealing furnace with vacuum quartz tube
- temperature range 600–900 °C

## Output

- annealed Ti-0.45O-4Fe (800 °C × 1 h)
- annealed Ti-0.5O-5Fe (900 °C × 1 h)

## Datasets used

- Ti-0.45O-4Fe alloy — source: Post-PBF-LB annealing to optimize microstructure and eliminate metastable phases
- Ti-0.5O-5Fe alloy — source: Post-PBF-LB annealing to optimize microstructure and eliminate metastable phases

## Evidence

- P103, PDF page 9, Methods: "Post-PBF-LB annealing treatments To eliminate metastable phases, relieve residual stresses, and achieve a stable lamellar α−β structure, we annealed our PBF-LB-fabricated Ti-0.45O-4Fe alloy plate samples at 600 °C, 700 °C, 800 °C, and 900 °C for 1 h each, where the β-transus temperature of our alloy is predicted to be 910 °C using the JMatPro 7.0 software."
- P103, PDF page 3, Results and discussion: "At 600 °C, the ω-phase persisted (Supplementary Figs. 8, 9), whereas annealing at ≥800 °C eliminated it (Supplementary Fig. 8)."
- P103, PDF page 3, Results and discussion: "Annealing at 800 °C for 1 h yielded a RT microstructure containing 22 vol.% β-phase... JMatPro simulations predicted a negligible oxygen content in the β-phase (0.001% at 800 °C), thereby concentrating the oxygen in the α-phase to approximately 0.58%."
