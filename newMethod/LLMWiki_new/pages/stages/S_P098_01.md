# Stage: synthesis of polymethacrylate (PMA) from PMMA and aliphatic alcohol

- Stage ID: `S_P098_01`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P098_01` — preparation of polymethacrylate (PMA) from waste organic glass as a pour point depressor in heavy crude oil

## Goal

To chemically convert waste PMMA into two candidate polymethacrylate derivatives (PMA-1 and PMA-2) via base-catalyzed transesterification with aliphatic alcohols.

## Input

- PMMA powder
- hexadecanol or octadecanol
- CaO catalyst
- toluene solvent

## Output

- PMA-1 (waxy solid from hexadecanol)
- PMA-2 (waxy solid from octadecanol)

## Datasets used

- Jinghe Oil Field heavy crude oil samples OS-1 to OS-4 — source: source material for candidate synthesis via transesterification with PMMA

## Evidence

- P098, PDF page 2, 2.2. Preparation of polymethacrylate: "As shown in Scheme 1, the hexadecanol/octadecanol and PMMA powder by the molar ratio of 1: 1 mixture were added into a flask. Then 1 wt% CaO of reactant and 10 times the mass of toluene of reactant were added in. The reactant was refluxed under stirring for 4 h, cooled to room temperature, CaO was filtrated, and the toluene was evaporated to give a waxy solid (PMA) (Chen et al., 2016a,b,c). The product synthesized from hexadecanol and PMMA was named PMA-1, and the product synthesized from octadecanol and PMMA was named PMA-2."
