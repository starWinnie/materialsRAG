# Stage: Strengthening mechanisms modeling

- Stage ID: `S_P070_03`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P070_01` — Design of a new Al-0.3Zr-0.2Ce-0.2Cu (wt.%) alloy for additive manufacturing that overcomes the classical tradeoff between conductivity and creep resistance

## Goal

Estimate yield stress contributions from various strengthening mechanisms

## Input

- microstructural data (grain size, precipitate size/distribution, Zr concentration)
- theoretical models (Orowan, solid-solution, Hall-Petch)

## Output

- theoretically estimated yield stress values

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P070, PDF page 17, 4.3 Strengthening mechanisms: "The yield stress of 75 MPa measured for the as-fabricated alloy is a combination of grain-boundary, solid-solution, and dispersion strengthening mechanisms through the following general equation: σYS= σ0 + ∆σOr+ ∆σSS+ ∆σGB, where σYS is the theoretically estimated YS, σ0 is the Peierls-Nabarro stress (which is equal to 20 MPa for Al), ∆σOr is the Orowan strengthening contribution of (Ce,Cu)-rich particles, ∆σSS is the solid-solution, and ∆σGB is the grain-boundary strengthening contributions."
