# Stage: Application of ML-corrected enthalpies to phase stability assessment

- Stage ID: `S_P043_07`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 7
- Task: `T_P043_01` — ML-based correction of DFT formation enthalpy errors for improved phase stability prediction

## Goal

Validate the utility of ML-corrected formation enthalpies (Hpred) for determining relative stability of competing phases in ternary systems.

## Input

- ML-corrected enthalpies (Hpred = HDFT − Hcorr)

## Output

- improved phase stability predictions
- reduced RMSE in Hpred vs. Hexpt

## Datasets used

- Experimental formation enthalpy dataset for Al–Ni–Pd and Al–Ni–Ti ternary systems — computational_validation: Validate utility of ML-corrected enthalpies (Hpred) for phase stability assessment by comparing against experimental ground truth

## Evidence

- P043, PDF page 4, Results and discussion: "The total DFT corrected enthalpy is given by Hpred = HDFT − Hcorr, where HDFT is the enthalpy from DFT calculations. As is demonstrated below, a neural network that is trained for certain concentrations of a ternary system where Hexpt is known, can give reliable values for Hpred even for concentrations where Hexpt is missing."
- P043, PDF page 7, Conclusion: "The presented results demonstrate that the ML-corrected predictions of formation enthalpy are significantly closer to experimental values, reducing the typical DFT error by a factor of 4–5."
