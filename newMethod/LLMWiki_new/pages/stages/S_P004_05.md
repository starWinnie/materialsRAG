# Stage: Train ML models on MP data to predict convex hull distance

- Stage ID: `S_P004_05`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P004_01` — ML-guided materials discovery

## Goal

Train diverse ML models—including universal interatomic potentials, graph neural networks, and random forests—on MP data to predict convex hull distance from unrelaxed inputs.

## Input

- MP v.2022.10.28 training data (energies, forces, stresses, magnetic moments)
- WBM convex hull distance labels

## Output

- trained ML models (e.g., EquiformerV2+DeNS, ORB MPtrj, SevenNet, etc.)

## Datasets used

- Materials Project — training: Train ML models to predict convex hull distance from unrelaxed inputs

- P004, PDF page 8, Article: "To test a wide variety of methodologies proposed for learning the potential energy landscape, our initial benchmark release includes 13 models."
- P004, PDF page 7, Methods: "We define the MP3 v.2022.10.28 database release as the maximum allowed training set for any compliant model submission. Models may train on the complete set of relaxation frames, or any subset thereof such as the final relaxed structures. Any subsets of the energies, forces and stresses are valid training targets."
