## Goal

Obtain uniform, representative specimens from Y-shaped ingots for experimental reliability.

## Input

- as-cast Cr26 high-chromium cast iron ingots

## Output

- mechanically ground and finely polished specimens

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P089, PDF page 2, Materials and methods: "The alloy was melted in an induction furnace and then cast into standard Y-shaped ingots. To ensure microstructural uniformity and experimental reliability, all specimens were taken from the region far from the ingate and cut along the casting direction of the Y-shaped ingot via electrical discharge machining (EDM). The as-obtained specimens were mechanically ground and finely polished to remove surface oxide layers and the mechanically affected layer."
