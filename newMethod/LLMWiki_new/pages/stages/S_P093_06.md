# Stage: Measure hydrogen distribution near crack tips using ToF-SIMS

- Stage ID: `S_P093_06`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P093_01` — Study the susceptibility to SCC of 7050 aluminum alloy

## Goal

Quantify hydrogen ion intensity (IH+) depth profiles to assess hydrogen embrittlement contribution to SCC

## Input

- fractured DCB specimens (mechanically ruptured when crack growth rate reduced to 10−7 mm/s)

## Output

- ToF-SIMS sputtering profiles of IH+ near crack tips
- hydrogen diffusion depth and concentration trends

- None stated
