## Evidence

- P107, PDF page 4, Fiber Pristine Strength: "The fiber tensile test was conducted within 2 h of fiber drawing. The test followed a standard procedure for the fiber tensile test [ 31 ]. The sample gauge length was fixed to 2.55 cm (1 inch). The Kawabata evaluation system (KES-G1, Kato Tech Co., Ltd., Japan) was used to measure fiber breaking stress under tension. The average tensile strength of each type of glass fiber was calculated from 30 to 40 measurements. A pulse-echo sonic modulus method was used to determine the fiber Young’s modulus ( Efib ) according to Efib = ρfib VE 2 ."
