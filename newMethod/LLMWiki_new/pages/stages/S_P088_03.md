# Stage: real-time thermal analysis using ATAS

- Stage ID: `S_P088_03`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P088_01` — engineering optimisation of as-cast HCWCIs

## Goal

benchmark thermodynamic predictions against experimentally measured cooling curves and validate predicted phase assemblages

## Input

- molten alloys
- ATAS quick cups
- embedded thermocouple
- MetStar thermal analysis system

## Output

- experimentally measured cooling curves
- liquidus/solidus temperatures
- eutectic undercooling/recalescence signatures

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P088, PDF page 2, Materials and experimental methods: "These predictions were benchmarked against experimentally measured cooling curves obtained via ATAS thermal analysis. Molten alloys were poured into standard ATAS quick cups (64.7 mm height, 51.5 mm diameter, ~7 mm wall thickness), and the temperature evolution during solidification was recorded by an embedded thermocouple connected to MetStar thermal analysis system. Predicted phase assemblages were subsequently validated through microstructural characterisation."
- P088, PDF page 8, Results: "The comparison of the predicted data with NovaCast ATAS MetStar thermal analysis showed an overestimation of liquidus and solidus temperatures by the equilibrium predictions."
