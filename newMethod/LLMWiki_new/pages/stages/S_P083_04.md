# Stage: Performing DFT calculations to analyze intermolecular interactions and binding energies

- Stage ID: `S_P083_04`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P083_01` — Enhancing the degradability of polyethylene (PE) without compromising its mechanical integrity

## Goal

Quantify and compare the strength and nature of intermolecular interactions (especially hydrogen bonding) among MPE systems using electronic-structure calculations.

## Input

- representative MPE segment pairs
- HF/6−31G* level for RESP charge derivation
- B3LYP/def2-TZVP and M06−2X/def2-TZVP for geometry optimization and binding energy calculation

## Output

- electrostatic potential (ESP) distributions
- binding energies
- hydrogen bond donor–acceptor geometries
