# Stage: Quantification of folding angle and plastic strain

- Stage ID: `S_P100_05`
- Stage type: `label_generation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P100_01` — Grain boundary-induced plasticity during thin film buckling

## Goal

Compute folding angle θ_i and plastic strain ε_p from dislocation counts and geometric measurements of deformed surfaces.

## Input

- Burgers vector projections of emitted dislocations
- Measured upper/lower surface lengths L+_i and L−_i from relaxed configuration (Fig. 3c)

## Output

- Folding angles θ1, θ2, θ_f = π − θ1 − θ2
- Plastic strain ε_p = (ΔL^m1 + ΔL^m2)/L0

## Datasets used

- Al thin film molecular dynamics simulation system with Σ51 (551) grain boundary — label_source: Quantify folding angle θ_f and plastic strain ε_p from geometric measurements of deformed surfaces in relaxed configurations

## Evidence

- P100, PDF page 3, 4. Dislocation-induced folding and plastic strain: "In order to estimate the plastic deformation resulting from the dislocation emission, two equivalent procedures have been used. They both consist in the determination of the length variations with respect to the initial half-length L0 of the film, of the upper and lower surfaces, ΔL+_iand ΔL−_i, respectively, for the grain i, with i= 1, 2 (Fig. 5a). The first one is related to the plastic mechanisms that take place during buckling and consists in counting the number of the emitted dislocations... The second one consists in a direct measurement of the length variation due to plastic deformation... The variations (taken to be positive quantities) of the upper and lower surfaces of the grain iare calculated as ΔL+_i= |||L+_i−L0||| and ΔL−_i= |||L−_i−L0|||, respectively, with i= 1, 2."
