# Stage: benchmark evaluation on standard MD datasets

- Stage ID: `S_P032_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P032_01` — molecular dynamics simulation using a neural network force field

## Goal

Assess pretrained MD-ET’s zero-shot and few-shot performance across diverse MD benchmarks (QCML, MD17, Ko2020, xxMD, SPICE) measuring force MAE, simulation stability, faithfulness (h(r)), and inference speed.

## Input

- pretrained MD-ET checkpoint
- MD17, Ko2020, xxMD, SPICE datasets

## Output

- benchmark scores (MAE, stability time, h(r), FPS)

## Datasets used

- QCML — benchmark: Benchmark evaluation of pretrained MD-ET's zero-shot and few-shot performance measuring force MAE.
- MD17 — benchmark: Benchmark evaluation of pretrained MD-ET's zero-shot and few-shot performance measuring force MAE, simulation stability, and faithfulness (h(r)).
- Ko2020 — benchmark: Benchmark evaluation of pretrained MD-ET's zero-shot and few-shot performance measuring force RMSE on molecular and metallic systems.
- xxMD — benchmark: Benchmark evaluation of pretrained MD-ET's zero-shot and few-shot performance measuring force MAE on nonadiabatic MD trajectories capturing chemical reactions.
- SPICE — benchmark: Benchmark evaluation of pretrained MD-ET's zero-shot and few-shot performance measuring force MAE on drug-like molecules and peptides up to 96 atoms.
