# Stage: Correlation of Glass Structure with Cementitious Reactivity

- Stage ID: `S_P106_04`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P106_01` — Optimizing Cementitious Glasses for the Disposal of Radioactive Waste

## Goal

Establish statistical correlation between Raman-derived structural metrics (Q1, Q2 concentration, O/T) and reactivity kinetics (peak heat flow, peak time).

## Input

- Qn distribution (Figure 8)
- isothermal calorimetry heat flow curves (Figure 12)
- peak normalized heat flow and timing (Figure 13, Table 5)

## Output

- moderate to strong correlations (R2 = 0.66–0.78) between Q1/Q2 and reactivity metrics (Figure 15)

## Datasets used

- Raman Spectroscopy-Derived Qn Speciation Data — training: To serve as structural input variable for statistical correlation with reactivity kinetics.
- Isothermal Calorimetry Heat Flow and Kinetic Data — training: To serve as reactivity output variable (kinetic metrics) for statistical correlation with structural descriptors.

## Evidence

- P106, PDF page 12, 3.4 Correlations between Glass Structure and Reactivity: "A moderate to strong correlation is observed between the glass structure and both of these kinetic factors for both Q1 and Q2 glass species."
- P106, PDF page 12, 3.4 Correlations between Glass Structure and Reactivity: "The results of the fitting demonstrated a moderate to strong correlation [20] for all cases, with the peak times showing adjusted-R2 values of 0.66 and 0.67 for Q1 and Q2, respectively. The peak heat flows were strongly correlated [20] with adjusted-R2 values of 0.74 and 0.78 for Q1 and Q2 species, respectively."
