# Stage: Powder mixing and milling

- Stage ID: `S_P115_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P115_01` — Preparation of TiC-based composites via reactive sintering

## Goal

Achieve uniform blending of raw powders to enable homogeneous reaction during sintering

## Input

- TiC
- (Zr,Hf)C
- Si
- cemented carbide balls
- argon atmosphere

## Output

- uniformly blended powder mixture

## Datasets used

- Compositional design of TiC-based composites (Table 1) — source: Provide exact molar ratios for powder mixing and milling

## Evidence

- P115, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedure: "After being precisely weighed according to the preset molar ratios of TiC/(Zr,Hf)C/Si, the mixed powders were uniformly blended via planetary rolling ball milling under an argon atmosphere, using cemented carbide balls as the milling media. The ball-to-powder mass ratio was set at 5:1, with a rotation speed of 150 rpm and a milling duration of 24 h. To improve powder dispersibility, the milled powders were sieved through an 80-mesh screen."
