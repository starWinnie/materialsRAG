# Stage: Isothermal Calorimetry and Post-Reaction XRD Analysis

- Stage ID: `S_P106_05`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P106_01` — Optimizing Cementitious Glasses for the Disposal of Radioactive Waste

## Goal

Experimentally validate cementitious reactivity by measuring exothermic heat release and confirming formation of cementitious mineral phases (C-S-H, LDH) in reacted solids.

## Input

- milled CMAS glass powders
- simulated LLW solution (Table 3)
- TA Instruments TAM Air 2 isothermal calorimeter
- Malvern Panalytical Empyrean diffractometer

## Output

- heat flow curves and total heat generation (Table 5, Figure 12)
- XRD patterns confirming C-S-H and LDH phases (Figure 14b)

## Datasets used

- Isothermal Calorimetry Heat Flow and Kinetic Data — experimental_validation: To experimentally validate cementitious reactivity by measuring exothermic heat release.
- Post-Reaction XRD Phase Identification Data — experimental_validation: To confirm formation of cementitious mineral phases (C-S-H, LDH) in reacted solids.

## Evidence

- P106, PDF page 6, 2.4 Cementitious Reactivity Measurements: "The resulting solid materials were then analyzed for cementitious minerals by X-ray diffraction using a Malvern Panalytical Empyrean diffractometer with a Cu Kα-radiation source under working conditions of 45 kV and 40 mA. The XRD patterns were collected over a 2 θ range of 5◦–70◦with a step size of 0.131◦."
- P106, PDF page 12, 3.3 Isothermal Calorimetry Results: "Several secondary phases appeared throughout the duration of the experiments, including calcium silicate hydrate (C-S-H) [46, 47] and a talcite-like layered double hydroxide (LDH) [48]—each of these phases is consistent with cementitious reactions having occurred."
