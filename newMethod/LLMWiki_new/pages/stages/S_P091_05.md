# Stage: Analysis of thermal stability via nitrogen physisorption and XRD

- Stage ID: `S_P091_05`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P091_01` — Postsynthetic modification of yttria-stabilized zirconia aerogels with silica coatings for enhanced thermal stability

## Goal

Evaluate the effectiveness of the silica coating in enhancing thermal stability by measuring specific surface area, pore volume, pore size, and crystalline phase evolution.

## Input

- Heat-treated coated and uncoated aerogels

## Output

- Specific surface area (SSA), pore volume, pore size, crystallite size, phase identification

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P091, PDF page 3, EXPERIMENTAL PROCEDURES: "Nitrogen adsorption/desorption experiments were conducted on Micromeritics ASAP 2020 (NASA Glenn Research Center) and Micromeritics 3Flex (University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, School of Chemical Sciences, Microanalysis Laboratory) to measure the SSA via the method of Brunauer–Emmett–Teller (BET) and porosity via the method of Barrett–Joyner–Halenda (BJH). Average pore size and cumulative pore volume were calculated using the BJH desorption method."
- P091, PDF page 3, EXPERIMENTAL PROCEDURES: "Powder X-ray diffraction (XRD) was used to identify the crystalline phase and calculate the crystallite size via the Scherrer equation."
