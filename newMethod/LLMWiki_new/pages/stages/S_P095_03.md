# Stage: Synthesis of regularly cross-linked single networks

- Stage ID: `S_P095_03`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P095_01` — Investigate the effect of cross-link homogeneity on the high-strain behavior of elastic polymer networks

## Goal

To prepare regularly cross-linked poly(n-butyl acrylate) networks via thiol-bromine click reaction using star polymers.

## Input

- bromine-terminated tetrafunctional poly(n-butyl acrylate) star polymers
- 3,6-dioxa-1,8-octanedithiol (cross-linker)
- N-methyl-1,3-diaminopropane (MPDA base)
- anhydrous dimethylformamide (DMF)

## Output

- regularly cross-linked single networks (REGx)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P095, PDF page 3, EXPERIMENTAL METHODS: "Regularly cross-linked networks were synthesized using a thiol-bromine click reaction between the bromine-terminated tetrafunctional poly(n-butyl acrylate) star polymers and 3,6-dioxa-1,8-octanedithiol cross-linker in the presence of N-methyl-1,3-diaminopropane (MPDA) base."
