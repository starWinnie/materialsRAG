# Stage: fabricated a single-leg thermoelectric device and characterized power generation performance; assembled a seven-pair thermoelectric cooling device

- Stage ID: `S_P068_05`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P068_01` — improved the thermoelectric efficiency in SnS crystals by promoting the convergence of energy and momentum of four valance bands, termed quadruple-band synglisis

## Goal

Experimentally validate thermoelectric performance in devices

## Input

- SnS0.9Se0.1+0.03SnS2 crystals
- commercial Bi2Te2.7Se0.3

## Output

- peak conversion efficiency of ~6.5%
- cooling ΔTmax of ~37.7 K at Th = 303 K and ~48.4 K at Th = 353 K

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P068, PDF page 6: "Ultimately, we fabricated a single-leg thermoelectric device and used the commercial Mini-PEM instrument to characterize the power generation performance of SnS0.9Se0.1+0.03SnS2 crystal (Fig. 5, D and E, and fig. S23)."
- P068, PDF page 6: "Furthermore, we assembled a seven-pair thermoelectric cooling device using SnS0.9Se0.1+0.03SnS2 crystals as p-legs and commercial Bi2Te2.7Se0.3 as n-legs."
- P068, PDF page 6: "P reaches a maximum value of ~0.035 W, and h reaches ~6.5% when DT is ~480 K, surpassing that of the reported p-type SnS crystals with triple-band convergence (17)."
