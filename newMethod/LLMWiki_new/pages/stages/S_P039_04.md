# Stage: benchmarking the foundation model on the MPtrj dataset and long-range dimer interactions

- Stage ID: `S_P039_04`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P039_01` — developing a foundation machine learning interatomic potential with polarizable long-range interactions

## Goal

to assess the foundation model's accuracy on large-scale materials property prediction and its ability to capture extended-range ionic interactions beyond typical MLIP cutoffs

## Input

- MPtrj dataset test set
- Na-Na and Na-Cl dimer potential energy surfaces from DFT
- DFT-calculated bulk modulus values

## Output

- mean absolute errors (MAE) for energy, force, and stress
- reproduced DFT potential energy surfaces for dimers up to 12.0 Å
- R² values and MAE for bulk modulus prediction

## Datasets used

- MPtrj dataset — test: to benchmark the foundation model's accuracy on large-scale materials property prediction

## Evidence

- P039, PDF page 4, Foundation model benchmark: "We trained a foundation model for all the periodic table elements up to Pu using the MPtrj dataset16 following our framework (our model), as described in the Methods section... The former one demonstrated mean absolute errors (MAE) of 18 meV per atom for energies, 0.065 eV·Å−1 for forces, and 0.301 GPa for stresses across the test set..."
- P039, PDF page 4, Foundation model benchmark: "As shown in Fig. 2a, both models (our model and the w/o-lr model) trained on the MPtrj dataset accurately reproduce the equilibrium bond lengths of the two dimers. However, the w/o-lr model failed to differentiate interaction energies beyond the MLIPs cutoff distance, i.e., 5.0 Å, while our model successfully captured the DFT potential energy surface throughout the entire distance range..."
