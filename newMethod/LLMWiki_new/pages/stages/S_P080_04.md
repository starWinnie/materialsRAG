# Stage: Photocatalytic hydrogen production measurement

- Stage ID: `S_P080_04`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P080_01` — Photocatalytic Hydrogen Generation

## Goal

To quantify H2 evolution rates under UV illumination and determine the relationship between Pt SA density, TiO2 properties, and photocatalytic activity.

## Input

- Pt-decorated TiO2 samples
- 365 nm or 275 nm UV LED
- quartz reactor with aqueous methanol
- gas chromatograph

## Output

- H2 production volumes over time
- H2 production rates (μL h−1)
- Langmuir-type adsorption isotherm

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P080, PDF page 2, Results and Discussion: "For the differently loaded samples, the photocatalytic H2 production activities were measured in a quartz cell reactor illuminated with a 365 nm light-emitting diode (LED) at 65 mW cm−2 intensity"
- P080, PDF page 2, Results and Discussion: "The results of the photocatalytic experiments in Figure 2e show that for all Pt SA deposited samples, a linearly increasing H2 volume is obtained over illumination time (i.e., H2 is evolving with a constant rate on all samples)."
