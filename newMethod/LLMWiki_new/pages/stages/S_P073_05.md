- Stage ID: `S_P073_05`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 5

## Goal

To corroborate that generated structures match experimental structural compositions and align with structures from alternative computational models (e.g., Lignin-KMC).

## Input

- generated structures
- experimental data from literature
- structures from Lignin-KMC model

## Output

- comparative analysis reports
- validation metrics (e.g., linkage frequency agreement, molecular weight alignment)

## Datasets used

- Lignin-KMC model structures — computational_validation: To compare generated structures with those from an alternative computational model (Lignin-KMC) and assess structural similarity and differences in linkage frequencies and oligomer formation.
