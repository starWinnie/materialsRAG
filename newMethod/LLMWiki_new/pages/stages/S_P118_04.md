# Stage: Low-temperature deposition and characterization of ACIGS absorbers

- Stage ID: `S_P118_04`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P118_01` — Ag precursor-assisted low-temperature growth of Cu(In,Ga)Se2 solar cells

## Goal

Apply the optimized 10-nm Ag precursor condition to low-temperature (350 °C) deposition and experimentally validate improvements in film quality, morphology, crystallinity, and solar cell performance.

## Input

- 10-nm Ag precursor layer
- Three substrate-temperature conditions (600, 475, and 350 °C)

## Output

- (Ag,Cu)(In,Ga)Se2 solar cells with ~15% PCE at 350 °C

## Datasets used

- ACIGS absorber series with fixed 10-nm Ag precursor at 600, 475, and 350 °C — experimental_validation: To apply the optimized 10-nm Ag precursor condition to low-temperature (350 °C) deposition and experimentally validate improvements in film quality, morphology, crystallinity, and solar cell performance.

## Evidence

- P118, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "To balance these competing effects, we select a 10-nm Ag precursor thickness, which improves film quality while limiting the reduction in net acceptor density, and apply this condition to low-temperature growth."
- P118, PDF page 5, 3.2. Low-Temperature Deposition of CIGS with Ag Incorporation: "In the subsequent low-temperature deposition experiments, the Ag precursor thickness was fixed at 10 nm."
- P118, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "As a result, (Ag,Cu)(In,Ga)Se2 solar cells fabricated at 350 °C maintain a power conversion efficiency of approximately 15%, whereas CIGS devices without Ag show severe performance degradation."
