# Stage: dataset selection and ab initio calculation

- Stage ID: `S_P003_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P003_01` — prediction of tensorial spectra

## Goal

acquire frequency-dependent dielectric tensors for 1432 bulk semiconductors via first-principles methods

## Input

- Materials Project database
- bandgap constraints (0.3–3 eV)
- energy-above-hull threshold (0.02 eV/atom)
- structural constraints (fewer than three elements, fewer than ten atoms per unit cell)
- exclusion criteria (magnetic materials, f-electron elements)

## Output

- 1432 frequency-dependent dielectric tensors

## Datasets used

- Materials Project — source: source database for selecting candidate materials
- TSENN 1432-material dataset — training: training data for TSENN model consisting of frequency-dependent dielectric tensors computed via first-principles methods

## Evidence

- P003, PDF page 2, Results: "With our focus on small bandgap semiconductors in mind, we selected materials from the Materials Project41 with bandgaps ranging from 0.3 to 3 eV (photon wavelengths of 4100 nm (infrared) to 413 nm (violet)) that are appropriate for optoelectronic applications42,43. To maintain structural stability, we imposed an energy-above-hull threshold of 0.02 eV/atom. For computational efficiency, we limited our selection to materials with fewer than three elements and fewer than ten atoms per unit cell. Additionally, we excluded magnetic materials as well as materials containing elements with f electrons due to the lack of availability of accurate, predefined pseudo-potential sets. These constraints yielded a dataset of 1432 materials..."
- P003, PDF page 2, Results: "By training TSENN on the aforementioned dataset, we achieve a mean absolute error (MAE) of 0.127 in the predicted imaginary part of the dielectric tensor."
- P003, PDF page 10, Methods: "DFT calculations were performed using OpenMX37–40... The dielectric tensor was computed within the IPA using the Kubo formula..."
