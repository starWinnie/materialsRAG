# Stage: Preprocessing datasets into representation spaces

- Stage ID: `S_P017_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P017_01` — Out-of-Distribution Property Prediction

## Goal

Convert raw material compositions and molecular graphs into fixed descriptor-based representations suitable for modeling.

## Input

- material compositions
- molecular graphs (SMILES)

## Output

- stoichiometry-based representations for solids
- RDKit descriptors for molecules

## Datasets used

- None stated
