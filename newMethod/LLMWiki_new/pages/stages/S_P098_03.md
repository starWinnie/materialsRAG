# Stage: DSC analysis to study paraffin crystallization behavior

- Stage ID: `S_P098_03`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P098_01` — preparation of polymethacrylate (PMA) from waste organic glass as a pour point depressor in heavy crude oil

## Goal

To computationally validate the mechanism of pour point depression by quantifying shifts in paraffin crystallization onset temperature induced by PMA-1.

## Input

- crude oil (OS-1)
- OS-1 + PMA-1 (2000 ppm)

## Output

- DSC cooling curves
- crystallization peak temperatures (23.83 °C for OS-1; 16.67 °C for OS-1+PMA-1)

## Datasets used

- Jinghe Oil Field heavy crude oil samples OS-1 to OS-4 — computational_validation: DSC analysis to quantify paraffin crystallization peak shift induced by PMA-1 in OS-1

## Evidence

- P098, PDF page 4, 3.4. Mechanism study: "The DSC curves of crude oil and crude oil þ PMA-1 (2000 ppm) between 50 and 0 C were graphed in Fig. 4. The results show that the crystallization peak for crude oil is 23.83 C, while the peaks for crude oil þ PMA-1 is 16.67 C respectively, which means PMA-1 can depress the pour point of the crude oil."
