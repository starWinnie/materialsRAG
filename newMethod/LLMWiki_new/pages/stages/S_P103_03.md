# Stage: PBF-LB fabrication of α–β Ti-O-Fe specimens

- Stage ID: `S_P103_03`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P103_01` — Design of high-strength, high-uniform-elongation α–β titanium alloys resistant to oxygen embrittlement

## Goal

Fabricate dense, near-net-shape specimens with fine equiaxed prior-β grains and ultrafine α–β lamellae using optimized laser parameters.

## Input

- gas-atomized Ti-O-Fe powder
- PBF-LB system (BLT S210/S200)
- optimized processing window (VED, laser power, scanning speed, hatch spacing)

## Output

- as-printed Ti-0.45O-4Fe plate specimens (40 mm × 40 mm × 10 mm)
- as-printed Ti-0.5O-5Fe cylindrical specimens (φ9 mm × 80 mm)

## Datasets used

- Ti-0.45O-4Fe alloy — source: PBF-LB fabrication of α–β Ti-O-Fe specimens
- Ti-0.5O-5Fe alloy — source: PBF-LB fabrication of α–β Ti-O-Fe specimens

## Evidence

- P103, PDF page 8, Methods: "PBF-LB fabrication A commercial Bright Laser Technologies (BLT, Xi’an, China) S210/S200 system, with a laser spot size of 60/80 μm and a wavelength of 1060–1080 nm, was utilized for the PBF-LB fabrication. The system offers a maximum laser power of 500 W and a maximum scanning speed of 7000 mm/s."
- P103, PDF page 9, Methods: "We printed 22 plate specimens (40 mm × 40 mm × 10 mm) of the Ti-0.45O-4Fe alloy in two batches (Supplementary Fig. 5c), and 36 cylindrical specimens (φ9 mm × 80 mm) of the Ti-0.5O-5Fe alloy in a single batch."
