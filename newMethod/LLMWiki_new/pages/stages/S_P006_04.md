# Stage: Evaluate ct-UAE transfer performance on back-end models

- Stage ID: `S_P006_04`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P006_01` — Generate universal atomic embeddings (UAEs) for crystal property prediction

## Goal

Assess the improvement in prediction accuracy when ct-UAEs are integrated into various back-end deep learning models (CGCNN, MEGNET, ALIGNN) across multiple datasets and properties.

## Input

- pre-trained ct-UAEs
- back-end models (CGCNN, MEGNET, ALIGNN)
- test datasets (MP, JARVIS, MC3D, HOIP)

## Output

- MAE and R2 scores for formation energy, bandgap, total energy
- comparative performance tables (e.g., Table 1, Table 2)

## Datasets used

- Materials Project (2018.6.1) — test: Evaluating ct-UAE transfer performance on back-end models (CGCNN, MEGNET, ALIGNN) for formation energy and bandgap prediction
- JARVIS-DFT — test: Evaluating ct-UAE transfer performance on back-end models (CGCNN) for formation energy and bandgap prediction
- MC3D dataset — test: Evaluating ct-UAE transfer performance on back-end models (CGCNN, MEGNET, ALIGNN) for total energy prediction
- Hybrid Organic-Inorganic Perovskite (HOIP) dataset — test: Evaluating ct-UAE transfer performance on back-end models (CGCNN, MEGNET) for formation energy prediction in hybrid perovskites
