# Stage: Densification Analysis

- Stage ID: `S_P077_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P077_01` — Low-Temperature Densification of Si3N4 Ceramics With MnO as a Sintering Additive

## Goal

Screen sintering conditions that achieve near-theoretical densification at low temperature.

## Input

- density and relative density data from Table 1

## Output

- optimal sintering condition: 1450°C achieving 97% relative density

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P077, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "Near-theoretical densification was achieved at a relatively low temperature of 1450◦C, demonstrating the strong fluxing effect of MnO in promoting liquid-phase-assisted sintering."
- P077, PDF page 2, 3.1 Densification: "However, increasing the temperature from 1400◦C to 1450◦C significantly improved density; for instance, density increased from 2.85 g/cm3 (RD = 87%) in the 1400-100-5 sample to 3.02 g/cm3 (RD = 92%) in the 1450-100-5 sample. Furthermore, at this temperature, the density of the samples increased with the extension of holding time, such as the density of 1450-100-20 sample (3.18 g/cm3 , RD = 97%) was noticeably higher than that of 1450-100-5 sample (3.02 g/cm3 , RD = 92%)."
