# Stage: Dataset Collection

- Stage ID: `S_P051_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P051_01` — generation of natural language-material narratives

## Goal

To acquire raw material data from publicly available ab initio databases covering diverse compositions and computed properties.

## Input

- publicly available repositories: JARVIS, Materials Project, OQMD, AFLOW2, COD

## Output

- raw material records with structural and property data

## Datasets used

- Open Quantum Materials Database (OQMD) — source: To acquire raw material data with computed properties for narrative generation.
- Materials Project — source: To acquire raw material data with computed properties for narrative generation.
- JARVIS-DFT — source: To acquire raw material data with computed properties for narrative generation.
- Crystallography Open Database — source: To acquire raw material structural data (no properties) for subsequent GNN-based property extrapolation.
- Automatic Flow for Materials Discovery — source: To acquire raw material data with computed properties (especially enthalpy per atom and scintillation attenuation length) for narrative generation.
