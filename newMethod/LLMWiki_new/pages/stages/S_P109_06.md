# Stage: Composite ME testing and mechanical clamping experiments

- Stage ID: `S_P109_06`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P109_01` — Enhanced Magnetoelectric Coupling and Thermal Stability in Nd-Doped BiFeO3 Ceramics Near the Morphotropic Phase Boundary

## Goal

To validate the strain-mediated (piezoelectric + magnetostrictive) origin of enhanced ME coupling via comparative and perturbative experiments.

## Input

- Bi0.84Nd0.16FeO3 and 0.7BFBT ceramics
- PMN-PT single crystals
- Al2O3 clamps
- silver epoxy

## Output

- magnetostriction profiles matching αME(H) shape
- reduced αME under clamping
- confirmation of synergy-driven ME mechanism

## Datasets used

- Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramic composition series — source: Composite ME testing and mechanical clamping experiments to validate strain-mediated coupling mechanism
- 0.7BiFeO3-0.3BaTiO3 (0.7BFBT) ceramic control sample — benchmark: Comparative benchmark to isolate contribution of piezoelectricity vs magnetostriction to ME coupling

## Evidence

- P109, PDF page 7, 3 Results and Discussion: "To test this hypothesis, we employed a composite ME method where the same shaped ceramic samples were bonded to polarized <001>-oriented PMN-PT single crystals using high-strength silver epoxy... Due to the low Young’s modulus of PMN-PT, magnetostrictive strain from the ceramic under an external field is mechanically transferred to the PMN-PT, generating a voltage via the d31 mode that is proportional to the magnetostriction coefficient."
- P109, PDF page 7, 3 Results and Discussion: "To verify this strain-mediated conjecture, we clamped the ceramics between high-Young’s-modulus Al2O3 single crystals to suppress magnetostrictive deformation... The results demonstrate a noticeable reduction in αME when the sample is clamped."
