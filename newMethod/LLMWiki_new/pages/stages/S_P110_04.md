# Stage: Comprehensive physical property characterization

- Stage ID: `S_P110_04`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P110_01` — Synergistically Enhanced Piezoelectricity in W/Mn Co-Doped NBT Ceramics

## Goal

Quantitatively evaluate crystal structure, microstructure, ferroelectric domains, dielectric behavior, conduction mechanisms, and piezoelectric response across the compositional series

## Input

- polished and poled NBTWM100x specimens

## Output

- XRD patterns
- Raman spectra
- SEM images
- PFM amplitude/phase images
- dielectric constant (εr) vs. temperature
- impedance spectra
- d33 values
- P-E hysteresis loops
- DC resistivity (ρdc)

## Datasets used

- NBTWM100x ceramic series — benchmark: Comprehensive physical property characterization across compositional series

## Evidence

- P110, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedure: "The crystal structure of all ceramic samples was examined by x-ray diffraction (XRD; Brook, D8 Advance) with Cu K α radiation... The surface morphology of thermally etched samples was observed using a benchtop scanning electron microscope (SEM; S-3400N Hitachi, Japan)... Ferroelectric domain structures and their behaviors were investigated by piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM; MFP-3D, Asylum Research, USA)... Raman spectra were acquired... The temperature dependence of the dielectric constant and impedance data were measured... The electrical resistivity was evaluated... The piezoelectric coefficient ( d33 ) was measured..."
