# Stage: Fine-tuning pre-trained OC22 S2EF-Total model

- Stage ID: `S_P038_03`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P038_01` — Rational design of nanoscale stabilized oxide catalysts for OER

## Goal

Fine-tune the pre-trained S2EF-Total model on OC22 dataset to better predict total energies of oxide surfaces and surface intermediates.

## Input

- OC20 dataset (1281040 DFT relaxations)
- OC22 dataset (62331 DFT relaxations)

## Output

- fine-tuned S2EF-Total model

## Datasets used

- Open Catalyst 2020 — pretraining: Train the S2EF-Total model before fine-tuning on OC22.
- Open Catalyst 2022 — training: Fine-tune the pre-trained S2EF-Total model to better predict total energies of oxide surfaces and surface intermediates.

## Evidence

- P038, PDF page 2, Methods: "We used a pre-trained model for the Structure to Energy and Forces (S2EF)-Total task from the OC22 framework to perform all machine learning predictions of the relaxed structure and total energy. The entirety of the Open Catalyst 2020 (OC20) dataset (1281040 DFT relaxations) was used to train an S2EF-Total model which was subsequently fine-tuned with the OC22 dataset (62331 DFT relaxations) to better predict the total energies of oxide surfaces and surface intermediates."
