# Stage: construction of crystal graphs and attribute assignment

- Stage ID: `S_P061_02`

- Stage order: 2

## Goal

Represent molecules and crystals as graphs with atomic, bond, and global state attributes

## Input

- QM9 molecules
- MP-crystals-2018.6.1 crystals

## Output

- crystal graphs with radius cutoff of 4 Å
- molecular graphs with bond information (bond existence, bond order, etc.)
- atomic, bond, and state attribute vectors

## Datasets used

- QM9 — source: Construction of molecular graphs with atomic, bond, and global state attributes
- Materials Project — source: Construction of crystal graphs with radius cutoff of 4 Å and atomic, bond, and global state attributes
