- Stage ID: `S_P100_04`

- Task: `T_P100_01` — Grain boundary-induced plasticity during thin film buckling

## Goal

Identify and characterize specific dislocation reactions and stacking fault formations activated near the GB under strain.

## Input

- Atomic configurations from MD simulations
- Thompson’s tetrahedron orientation (Fig. 2)
- Dislocation notation (Hirth and Lothe, 1982)

## Output

- Enumerated plasticity mechanisms: e.g., C_D → A_γ + B_γ + C_γ (grain 1); δ_D → C_δ + C′D (grain 2); D_γ + C_D → C_γ → δ_γ + C_δ

## Datasets used

- Al thin film molecular dynamics simulation system with Σ51 (551) grain boundary — screening: Identify and characterize dislocation reactions and stacking fault formations near the grain boundary under strain

## Evidence

- P100, PDF page 3, 3. Plasticity mechanism analysis: "Fig. 4 shows the plastic mechanisms at different strain levels near the grain boundary, leading to the folding of the film. From Fig. 4a, it can be observed that in the lower part of the film under compression, two Shockley’s dislocations (Aγand Bγ) are nucleated in the grain 1 from the second perfect dislocation of the GB... These two reactions can be summarized as: CD→Aγ+ Bγ+ Cγ, for grain 1, (1) CD→δA+ δB+ δD, for grain 2. (2)"
