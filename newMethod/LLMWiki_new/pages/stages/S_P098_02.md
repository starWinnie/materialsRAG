# Stage: evaluation of PMA candidates as pour point depressants and viscosity reducers in heavy crude oil

- Stage ID: `S_P098_02`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P098_01` — preparation of polymethacrylate (PMA) from waste organic glass as a pour point depressor in heavy crude oil

## Goal

To screen PMA-1 and PMA-2 for efficacy in reducing pour point and viscosity across multiple heavy oil samples, identifying the superior candidate and optimal dosage.

## Input

- PMA-1
- PMA-2
- heavy crude oil samples (OS-1 to OS-4)

## Output

- ranking of PMA-1 > PMA-2 for pour point depression
- optimal dosage of 2000 ppm PMA-1 for OS-1
- performance data across oil samples

## Datasets used

- Jinghe Oil Field heavy crude oil samples OS-1 to OS-4 — test: screening of PMA-1 and PMA-2 for pour point depression and viscosity reduction efficacy across multiple heavy oil samples

## Evidence

- P098, PDF page 2, 3.1. Influence of PMAs on the pour points: "The influence of the PMAs on the pour points of crude oil samples (OS-1) from Jinghe Oil Field of China was tested, and the results were summarized in Table 2. From the results, it can be found that the PMAs can reduce the pour points highest with the concentration of 500 ppm, PMA-1 is better for the crude oil, and the maximal reduced pour point is 5.4 C, while PMA-2 can only depress the pour point by 3.2 C. Then the influence of mass fraction of PMA-1 on the pour points was investigated... the PMA-1 can reduce the pour points highest with the concentration of 2000 ppm by 7.1 C."
- P098, PDF page 3, 3.2. Influence of PMAs on the viscosity: "Then the influence of mass fraction of PMA-1 on the viscosity was investigated... Using 2000 ppm, the viscosity was reduced by 63.7% (50 C) compared with the blank, and the depression of the pour point is the highest, 7.1 C."
