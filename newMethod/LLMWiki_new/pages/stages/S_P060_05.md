## Goal

demonstrate the effectiveness of the fusion mechanism by horizontally integrating multi-modal data for CrGeTe3 and vertically comparing it against structural analogs to fill data gaps and guide discovery

## Input

- fused multi-modal material profiles
- structural analogs

## Output

- validated horizontal integration (e.g., linking experimental structure with theoretical band structure)
- validated vertical comparison (e.g., synthesis guidance from analogs, candidate screening from AI-generated structures)
