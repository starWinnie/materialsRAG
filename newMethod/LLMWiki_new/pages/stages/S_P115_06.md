## Goal

Fabricate dense composites and validate mechanical performance against target properties

## Input

- sieved powder mixtures
- hot-pressing furnace
- universal material testing machine
- SENB apparatus
- HV tester

## Output

- dense TiC-based composites
- measured flexural strength
- measured fracture toughness
- measured Vickers hardness
- relative density

- Mechanical properties of TiC-based composites (Table 2) — experimental_validation: Report measured mechanical properties (flexural strength, fracture toughness, hardness, relative density) for validation against target properties

## Evidence

- P115, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedure: "Subsequently, the sieved powder mixtures were densified via hot-pressing sintering. The powders were heated to the target temperature at a rate of 10◦C/min with a uniaxial pressure of 30 MPa applied, and the dwell time of 1 h was adopted. After the holding period, the pressure was released and the specimens were cooled to room temperature inside the furnace."
- P115, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedure: "The flexural strength was measured using a three-point bending method on a universal material testing machine (Instron-1186, USA). The specimen dimensions were 2 mm × 3 mm × 20 mm, with a span of 14 mm and a loading rate of 0.5 mm/min, with the reported value being the average of five tests. Fracture toughness was evaluated via the single-edge notched beam (SENB) method. The specimens had dimensions of 1.5 mm × 3 mm × 18 mm, with a span of 12 mm, a notch depth of approximately 1.5 mm, and a loading rate of 0.05 mm/min, with the reported value being the average of five tests. Vickers hardness (HV) was tested using an HV tester (HVS-5, Lanzhou, China) under a load of 1 kg with a dwell time of 15 s, averaging over at least 10 indentations per sample."
