- Task: `T_P119_01` — Band gap narrowing in oxychlorosulfide photocatalysts through aliovalent Cl-to-S anion substitution

## Goal

Experimentally measure band gap narrowing, VBM upshift, and confirm suitability for water splitting via UV–vis, PYS, and band-edge analysis.

## Input

- diffuse reflectance spectra
- photoelectron yield spectra (PYS)

## Output

- experimentally determined band gaps (2.21 → 2.10 eV)
- VBM shift to more negative potentials
- confirmed suitable band-edge positions for water splitting

## Datasets used

- La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x (x = 0, 0.5, 0.75, 1) — experimental_validation: Experimental measurement of band gap narrowing, VBM upshift, and confirmation of suitable band-edge positions for water splitting via UV–vis and PYS.

The band gaps of La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x samples determined from the absorption edge are plotted as a function of x in Figure 2b. The band gap decreased almost linearly from 2.21 to 2.10 eV as x increased from 0 to 0.75..."
- P119, PDF page 4: "The band-edge positions were estimated from the lowest ionization energies measured by photoelectron yield spectroscopy (PYS).
