# Stage: TAPSim-MD simulation execution with electrostatic voltage ramping

- Stage ID: `S_P097_03`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P097_01` — interpreting field evaporation through dynamic bond-breaking processes

## Goal

Run full-dynamics field evaporation simulation by incrementally increasing applied voltage and computing atom trajectories under combined interatomic and electrostatic forces.

## Input

- virtual TiAl tip
- EAM interatomic potential (Zope and Mishin)
- Langevin thermostat for bulk atoms
- voltage ramping protocol (10 V → 2 V increments)

## Output

- evaporation event sequences
- atom trajectories
- net/interatomic/electrostatic force time series
- surface morphology evolution

## Datasets used

- TAPSim-MD virtual [001]-oriented TiAl crystalline tip — training: Provide the structural input for TAPSim-MD simulation execution, enabling ab initio prediction of evaporation events and atom trajectories under electrostatic voltage ramping.

## Evidence

- P097, PDF page 3, Methods: "The simulation was then initiated in the form of a loop, consisting of (1) increasing the virtual voltage by 10 V, (2) recalculating the electrostatic field, and (3) running 1000 MD steps until 420 V was reached. Subsequently, the process is continued with voltages increasing in increments of 2 V instead of 10 V until an evaporation event is triggered."
- P097, PDF page 3, Methods: "TAPSim-MD evaporates atoms on the surface of a specimen as a result of the competition between interatomic forces and electrostatic forces, and generates an ‘‘ab-initio’’ prediction of evaporation events as well as atom trajectories."
