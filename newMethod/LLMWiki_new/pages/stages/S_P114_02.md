# Stage: Post-test microstructural characterization at identical locations

- Stage ID: `S_P114_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P114_01` — Effect of Steam Oxidation on Microstructure and Hoop Strength of Duplex SiCf /SiC Tube

## Goal

Systematically characterize pre-defined identical locations on oxidized specimens to enable direct before/after comparisons of microstructural evolution.

## Input

- oxidized SiCf/SiC tube specimens
- pre-defined identical observation locations

## Output

- SEM images
- EDS maps
- XPS spectra

## Datasets used

- as-received SiCf/SiC tube — source: Reference for site-specific post-test microstructural characterization
- SiCf/SiC tube oxidized at 600°C — source: Subject for site-specific post-test microstructural characterization
- SiCf/SiC tube oxidized at 900°C — source: Subject for site-specific post-test microstructural characterization
- SiCf/SiC tube oxidized at 1200°C — source: Subject for site-specific post-test microstructural characterization

## Evidence

- P114, PDF page 3, Experimental Procedures: "Post-test microstructural characterization was systematically conducted on identical, pre-defined locations of the SiCf /SiC tube to enable direct before/after comparisons. A total of four identical, pre-defined observation locations were systematically characterized at each temperature using SEM, enabling direct before/after comparisons of microstructural evolution induced by steam oxidation."
- P114, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedures: "Post-test microstructural characterization was performed using scanning electron microscopy (SEM; SU8220, Hitachi) by revisiting identical, pre-defined regions of the specimens before and after steam oxidation. An energy spectrometer (EDS, Oxford X-MaxN, UK) was employed to identify elemental distributions and relative compositional trends associated with steam oxidation."
