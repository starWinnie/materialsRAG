- P001, PDF page 6, Model Invariance: "Model Invariance Crystals are inherently symmetrical. Following existing work (Yan et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Zheng et al., 2024), we force the model prediction to be invariant to E(3) group (i.e., the rotation, reflection, and translation of input crystals). Specifically, according to Eq. 7, the final representation output zfused depends on the g and d. Since g depends on the relative geometric features dij and θijk (Eq.6) that remain unchanged under E(3) operations, g is E(3)-invariant. Consequently, because d is also E(3)-invariant, the model output is E(3)-invariant, as shown in Section 4.3."
