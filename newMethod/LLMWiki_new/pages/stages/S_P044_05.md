# Stage: Ranking known target materials by similarity to novel target

- Stage ID: `S_P044_05`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P044_01` — Precursor recommendation for inorganic synthesis

## Goal

Compute cosine similarity between the PrecursorSelector encoding of the novel target and all known targets in the knowledge base to generate a ranked list of analogues.

## Input

- PrecursorSelector encoding of novel target
- PrecursorSelector encodings of known targets

## Output

- ordered list of known target materials ranked by similarity

## Datasets used

- None stated
