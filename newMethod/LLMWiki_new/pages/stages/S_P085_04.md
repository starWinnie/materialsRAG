# Stage: Burgers vector determination using g·b analysis

- Stage ID: `S_P085_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P085_01` — Resolving the fundamental behavior of pyramidal ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations in magnesium

## Goal

To identify and screen for dislocations with the specific ⟨c+a⟩ Burgers vector among all observed dislocations.

## Input

- dark-field TEM images
- diffraction vectors (g)
- Burgers vector (b)

## Output

- confirmed ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations
- exclusion of ⟨a⟩ and ⟨c⟩ dislocations

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P085, PDF page 1: "We performed g · b analyses (where g is the diffraction vector and b is the Burgers vector) to determine the Burgers vector of dislocations (19)."
- P085, PDF page 7, Materials and Methods: "Based on the g·b invisibility criterion, only 〈c+a〉 dislocation is visible in the dark-field images obtained with g=(0002) and with g=(1̅21̅0). 〈a〉 dislocation is invisible when g=(0002), and 〈c〉 dislocation is invisible when g=(1̅21̅0)."
