# Stage: Sample processing and electrode fabrication

- Stage ID: `S_P110_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P110_01` — Synergistically Enhanced Piezoelectricity in W/Mn Co-Doped NBT Ceramics

## Goal

Prepare sintered, polished, and metallized ceramic specimens suitable for electrical characterization and poling

## Input

- sintered NBTWM100x green pellets
- silver paste
- silicone oil

## Output

- polished ceramic disks (0.6 mm thickness) with silver electrodes
- poled specimens (200°C, 10 kV/mm, 20 min)

## Datasets used

- NBTWM100x ceramic series — source: Sample processing and electrode fabrication for electrical characterization and poling

## Evidence

- P110, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedure: "The resulting slurry was dried and subsequently calcined at 850◦C for 4 h in air. The calcined powder was then re-milled to ensure homogeneity, after which it was granulated using a polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) solution as a binder. The granulated powder was uniaxially pressed into disk-shaped green pellets with a diameter of 13 mm and an approximate thickness of 1 mm. After binder removal at 600◦C for 2 h, the green pellets were sintered in sealed alumina crucibles at 980–1020◦C for 3 h. For electrical characterization, the sintered ceramics were polished to a thickness of 0.6 mm. Silver paste was screen-printed on both surfaces of the samples and subsequently fired at 750◦C for 10 min. The specimens were poled in silicone oil at 200◦C under an electric field of 10 kV/mm for 20 min, followed by cooling to room temperature under the applied electric field."
