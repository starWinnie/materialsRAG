# Stage: DFT and ML-MD simulations of adsorption and dispersion energetics

- Stage ID: `S_P040_03`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P040_01` — reverse regulation from particles to clusters

## Goal

Validate thermodynamic and kinetic feasibility of Ni particle dispersion via Ni–O–La/Ce bonding and quantify interfacial energy evolution

## Input

- NiOy/CeO2−x and NiOy/LaCeO2−x surface models
- DFT + U method
- GemNet-OC ML-MD model

## Output

- adsorption energies (Eads)
- migration pathways
- stable Ni-O-La configuration (−0.75 eV)
- covalent bonding strength exceeding Ni lattice cohesion

## Datasets used

- 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst — computational_validation: Validate thermodynamic and kinetic feasibility of Ni particle dispersion via Ni–O–La/Ce bonding and quantify interfacial energy evolution
- 10Ni/CeO2 catalyst — computational_validation: Comparative computational validation to demonstrate weaker Ni–O–Ce interaction

## Evidence

- P040, PDF page 5: "DFT calculations revealed that the reactive Oads coordinated with either Ce or La atoms on the catalyst surface could establish strong MSI with Ni species, consequently promoting the redispersion of Ni nanoparticles (Fig. 4f)."
- P040, PDF page 5: "DFT calculations revealed distinct adsorption behaviors depending on the coordination environment of Ni. When Ni atoms formed direct bonds with Ce (Eads = 1.97 eV) or La (Eads = 1.71 eV) atoms in the support, the positive adsorption energies indicated thermodynamic instability. In contrast, Ni coordination with O atoms adjacent to Ce sites exhibited a lower adsorption energy (Eads = −0.26 eV), favoring stable configurations."
- P040, PDF page 7: "Molecular dynamics simulations confirmed that these effects led to a multi-layer Ni distribution on 10Ni/CeO2. On the contrary, 10Ni/30La-CeO2 promoted Ni dispersion."
