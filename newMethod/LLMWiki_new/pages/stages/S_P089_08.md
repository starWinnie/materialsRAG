# Stage: Impact abrasive wear testing

- Stage ID: `S_P089_08`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 8
- Task: `T_P089_01` — Optimizing impact abrasive wear resistance of Cr26 high-chromium cast iron via combined heat treatment

## Goal

Experimentally validate wear performance under simulated mining conditions and quantify wear loss, wear rate, and relative wear resistance.

## Input

- heat-treated specimens
- silica sand abrasive
- 45# steel counter-ring

## Output

- wear loss (g)
- wear rate (mg/s)
- relative wear resistance
- worn surface and subsurface morphologies

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P089, PDF page 3, Wear performance tests: "Impact abrasive wear tests were performed in accordance with the Chinese Foundry Association standard T/CFA 010604-3-2016... Each wear test lasted for 95 min... To simulate the service conditions of mining mill liners, silica sand with a particle size of 60–80 mesh was adopted as the abrasive medium. The main test parameters were set as follows: impact energy of 2 J, impact frequency of 150 impacts/min, counter-ring rotation speed of 150 r/min, abrasive falling height of 50 mm, and abrasive feed rate of 50 kg/h."
