# Stage: pretreatment to induce structural evolution

- Stage ID: `S_P040_02`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P040_01` — reverse regulation from particles to clusters

## Goal

Generate distinct Ni species states (particles → clusters) through controlled thermal treatment under NH3 or H2

## Input

- as-prepared 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst
- NH3 or H2 atmosphere
- temperature gradient

## Output

- Ni particles (5–10 nm)
- mixed particles/clusters
- fully dispersed Ni clusters (1–2 nm)

## Datasets used

- 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst — candidate_pool: Generate distinct Ni species states (particles → clusters) through controlled thermal treatment under NH3 or H2
- 10Ni/CeO2 catalyst — candidate_pool: Control experiment to demonstrate lack of dispersion under identical conditions

## Evidence

- P040, PDF page 2, Results: "The catalyst, subjected to 12 h of gradient heating treatment with NH3 (for the specific treatment procedure, see Supplementary Fig. 1), was designated as the used catalyst."
- P040, PDF page 2, Results: "HAADF-STEM analysis revealed a large number of particles with sizes of 5−10 nm on the surface of the 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst (Fig. 1a), which was treated with NH3 at 500 °C for 0.5 h. Further, only a limited number of Ni particles along with a substantial amount of Ni clusters were observed after being treated at 600 °C for 1 h (Fig. 1b, Supplementary Fig. 4). On this basis, the dispersion of Ni species was markedly improved when the treatment time was extended to 120 h. The metallic Ni particles were no longer detectable on the surface of the 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst. A significant number of stable Ni clusters were observed, with an average size of 1−2 nm (Fig. 1c, Supplementary Fig. 5)."
