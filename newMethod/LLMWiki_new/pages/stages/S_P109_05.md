# Stage: Dynamic magnetoelectric coefficient measurement

- Stage ID: `S_P109_05`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P109_01` — Enhanced Magnetoelectric Coupling and Thermal Stability in Nd-Doped BiFeO3 Ceramics Near the Morphotropic Phase Boundary

## Goal

To quantify the dynamic ME coupling strength (αME = dE/dH) across compositions and temperatures.

## Input

- polished and silver-coated Bi1-xNdxFeO3 plates
- electromagnet
- cryostat
- lock-in amplifier (SR830)

## Output

- αME(H) curves
- αME(T) stability profiles
- peak αME ≈ 300 μV/(cm·Oe) at x = 0.16 and H = 9 kOe

## Datasets used

- Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramic composition series — source: Dynamic magnetoelectric coefficient measurement across compositions and temperatures

## Evidence

- P109, PDF page 2, 2.2 Characterization and Measurements: "For dynamic ME measurements [29–31], the samples were polished into plates (approximately 3 × 3 × 0.2 mm3) and coated with silver paste on both surfaces. These specimens were then poled in a silicon oil bath under a DC electric field of 150 kV/cm for 30 min. The dynamic ME voltage coefficient αME = d E /d H was extracted using a small AC magnetic field (h0 = 5 Oe at 1 kHz) superimposed on a parallel DC bias field H."
- P109, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "A significantly enhanced dynamic ME coefficient of approximately 300 μV/(cm ⋅Oe) was observed at this critical composition."
- P109, PDF page 5, 3 Results and Discussion: "A significant enhancement in the ME response occurs at x = 0.16. In this composition, the αME at 9 kOe surges to approximately 300 μV/(cm ⋅Oe)."
