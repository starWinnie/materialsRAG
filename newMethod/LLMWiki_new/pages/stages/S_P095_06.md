# Stage: Uniaxial tensile testing

- Stage ID: `S_P095_06`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P095_01` — Investigate the effect of cross-link homogeneity on the high-strain behavior of elastic polymer networks

## Goal

To characterize the mechanical properties—including modulus, extensibility, and strain stiffening onset—of single and double networks.

## Input

- dog-bone-shaped tensile samples of single or double networks

## Output

- stress−strain curves
- Young’s moduli
- strain at break
- onset of strain stiffening (λeff)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P095, PDF page 3, EXPERIMENTAL METHODS: "Networks were cut into dog-bone-shaped tensile samples and analyzed using a uniaxial extension test (1 in/min) on either an ADMET eXpert 5601-2C or a Material Testing Systems Insight Dual Transformer 820 XFMR-DUAL mechanical tester."
- P095, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "The onset of strain stiffening was found to occur later in regular networks than in random networks with the same modulus but was well-predicted by the target molecular weight between cross-links of each sample."
