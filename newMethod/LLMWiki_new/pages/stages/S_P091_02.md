# Stage: Coating YSZ aerogels with silica via TEOS solution

- Stage ID: `S_P091_02`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P091_01` — Postsynthetic modification of yttria-stabilized zirconia aerogels with silica coatings for enhanced thermal stability

## Goal

Generate silica-coated YSZ aerogel candidates by functionalizing surface hydroxyl groups with SiO2.

## Input

- YSZ aerogels with 0, 10, and 30 mol% YO1.5
- tetraethyl orthosilicate (TEOS) solution

## Output

- SiO2-coated YSZ aerogels (SiO2–0YSZ, SiO2–10YSZ, SiO2–30YSZ)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P091, PDF page 3, EXPERIMENTAL PROCEDURES: "Gels to be coated with silica were extracted into a coating solution of TEOS (Si(OC2H5)4, Sigma Aldrich, 98%), DI water, and nitric acid (HNO3, Sigma Aldrich, 70 wt.%) in 200 proof ethanol and aged for 3 days. The molar ratio of TEOS:ethanol:water:nitric acid was 1:7.7:1.5:0.01, adapted from previous work by Zu et al.13"
