# Stage: Fabrication and annealing of Mg pillars

- Stage ID: `S_P085_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P085_01` — Resolving the fundamental behavior of pyramidal ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations in magnesium

## Goal

To prepare high-quality, defect-minimized Mg micropillars suitable for in situ TEM testing.

## Input

- pure Mg single crystal
- focused ion beam (FIB) milling system
- tube furnace

## Output

- submicrometer-size Mg pillars with square or trapezoidal cross-sections
- pillars with minimized FIB-induced surface damage

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P085, PDF page 7, Materials and Methods: "Sub-micron pillars were fabricated from the thin edge using FIB (Ga+ sources, 30 kV and low ion current (several to tens of pA)). The cross-section of the pillar had a square shape (see detailed geometrical parameters in table S1). ... To remove these FIB-induced defects on sample surface, the pillars were annealed at ~180 °C for ~15 min, inside a tube furnace in an Ar atmosphere."
