# Stage: Long-term stability and electronic property validation

- Stage ID: `S_P080_06`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P080_01` — Photocatalytic Hydrogen Generation

## Goal

To confirm stability of Pt SAs under continuous illumination and correlate photocatalytic performance with TiO2 electronic quality (conductivity, defect states).

## Input

- Pt-decorated TiO2 samples
- I–V measurements
- long-term illumination tests

## Output

- stable H2 evolution over time
- maximized conductivity at 450 °C annealing
- evidence that Sn diffusion degrades performance at >450 °C

## Datasets used

- None stated

- P080, PDF page 4, unknown: "Importantly, long-term stability tests (Figure S1d, Supporting Information) confirm that the hydrogen evolution performance remains stable for continuous illumination, despite partial aggregation of Pt single atoms into nanoparticles."
- P080, PDF page 5, unknown: "This trend further supports that Pt SA density is not the dominant factor in determining catalytic efficiency. The hydrogen evolution rate reaches a maximum at 450 °C, even though Pt loading is slightly higher at 800 °C. Instead, the results strongly suggest that the electronic quality of the TiO2 film—especially its conductivity and defect properties—is the primary parameter controlling performance."
