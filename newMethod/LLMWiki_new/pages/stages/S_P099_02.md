# Stage: Specimen preparation for microstructural characterization

- Stage ID: `S_P099_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P099_01` — Investigation of isothermal continuous hot deformation behavior

## Goal

To prepare metallographic sections from deformed specimens for EBSD and TEM analysis.

## Input

- deformed cylindrical specimens

## Output

- polished and electrolytically etched discs for EBSD
- electropolished foils for TEM

## Datasets used

- as-cast alumina-forming austenitic (AFA) alloy — source: To prepare metallographic sections from deformed specimens for EBSD and TEM analysis.

## Evidence

- P099, PDF page 2, Experimental procedure: "To analyze the microstructure of the specimens, discs were cut from cylinders along the compression axis using electron discharge machining (EDM). These discs were mechanically ground and polished using standard techniques. Then they were electrolytically etched with a solution of 15 g CrO3 + 10 mL H2SO4 + 170 mL H3PO4."
- P099, PDF page 2, Experimental procedure: "For transmission electron microscopy (TEM) observation, foils were prepared by manually grinding to a thickness of 30 μm. Then their thickness was reduced using twin-jet electropolishing with a solution of 10 mL HClO4 + 90 mL C2H6O."
