# Stage: Compare DFT formation energies against experimental formation enthalpies

- Stage ID: `S_P035_01`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P035_01` — Correcting DFT formation energies towards experimental accuracy

## Goal

Quantify the initial discrepancy between DFT formation energies and experiment to establish baseline error metrics

## Input

- DFT formation energies (PBE, PBEsol)
- experimental formation enthalpies

## Output

- baseline MAE, ME, RMSE
- systematic bias identification (e.g., overbinding of oxygen)

## Datasets used

- Materials Cloud three-dimensional crystals database — source: Provide DFT formation energies (PBE, PBEsol) for comparison against experimental formation enthalpies
- expt formation enthalpy kingsbury dataset — label_source: Provide experimental formation enthalpy references for baseline error metrics
- experimental references from Kirklin et al. — label_source: Provide additional experimental formation enthalpy references to increase dataset size and diversity
