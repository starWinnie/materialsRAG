# Stage: Evaluating Ag precursor thickness effects on absorber quality and device performance

- Stage ID: `S_P118_02`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P118_01` — Ag precursor-assisted low-temperature growth of Cu(In,Ga)Se2 solar cells

## Goal

Assess how different Ag precursor thicknesses affect film quality (via Urbach energy, diode parameters), net acceptor density, and solar cell parameters (efficiency, Voc, Jsc, FF).

## Input

- CIGS and ACIGS absorber layers grown with 5, 10, and 25 nm Ag precursors

## Output

- Quantitative relationship between Ag thickness and device metrics

## Datasets used

- ACIGS absorber series with varied Ag precursor thickness at 600 °C — screening: To assess how different Ag precursor thicknesses affect film quality (via Urbach energy, diode parameters), net acceptor density, and solar cell parameters (efficiency, Voc, Jsc, FF).
