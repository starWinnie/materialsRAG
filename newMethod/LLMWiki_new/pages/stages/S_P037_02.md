# Stage: preprocessing and expert validation of experimental datasets

- Stage ID: `S_P037_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P037_01` — accelerating the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs)

## Goal

process raw data to remove unphysical entries and ensure wide chemistry coverage, including line-by-line expert review and nonsense-detection strategies

## Input

- raw conductivity and band gap data
- chemical formulas
- oxidation state inference

## Output

- final validated conductivity dataset (8231 entries)
- final validated band gap dataset (4767 entries)

## Datasets used

- electrical conductivity dataset — label_source: expert validation and nonsense-detection strategies applied to the electrical conductivity dataset
- band gap dataset — label_source: expert validation and expansion of the band gap dataset
