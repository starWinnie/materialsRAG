# Stage: Preparation of double networks

- Stage ID: `S_P095_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P095_01` — Investigate the effect of cross-link homogeneity on the high-strain behavior of elastic polymer networks

## Goal

To toughen the single networks by embedding them in a second, loosely cross-linked interpenetrating network to delay fracture and enable access to high-strain behavior.

## Input

- randomly or regularly cross-linked single network
- monomer/cross-linker mixture (n-butyl acrylate/hexanediol diacrylate/benzoyl peroxide)

## Output

- double networks (RANxDN or REGxDN)

## Datasets used

- None stated
