# Stage: Mechanosynthesis of ceramic powders

- Stage ID: `S_P113_02`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P113_01` — Solid Solution in BaS–La2S3 Ceramics Produced by Mechanosynthesis and Their Potential for Rare-Earth Doping

## Goal

Produce homogeneous, nanocrystalline or strained ceramic powders across the BaS–La2S3 compositional range via high-energy ball milling.

## Input

- batched La2S3 and BaS powders
- tungsten carbide milling media
- inert Ar atmosphere

## Output

- as-milled x BaS–(1−x) La2S3 powders (x = 0, 15, 33, 50, 67)

## Datasets used

- as-milled x BaS–(1−x) La2S3 powders (x = 0, 15, 33, 50, 67) — candidate_pool: Serve as nanocrystalline/strained ceramic powders across the BaS–La2S3 compositional range for solid solution assessment

## Evidence

- P113, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedures: "Stoichiometric amounts of La2 S3 (Stanford Elements 99.9%) and BaS (Thermo Fisher 99.9%) were batched in an Ar glovebox... The samples were milled using a Retsch PM-100 ball mill, using 10 mm diameter WC milling media with a sample to media mass ratio of 9.75. The samples were milled at 400 RPM for 60 h, with a milling profile of 3 min milling and 3 min rest with alternating rotating directions after each rest period."
