# Stage: screen candidates using ML-predicted properties

- Stage ID: `S_P064_06`

- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P064_01` — fast screening and mapping energy landscape for multicomponent systems

## Goal

Filter 4079 candidates using ML-predicted MBJ bandgap, formation energy, and exfoliation energy to identify promising exfoliable semiconductors

## Input

- ML-predicted MBJ bandgap
- ML-predicted formation energy
- ML-predicted exfoliation energy
- synthetic complexity constraint (≤3 unique atom types)

## Output

- 65 candidate exfoliable 2D semiconductors (e.g., CuI, Mo2O5, InS)

## Datasets used

- None stated
