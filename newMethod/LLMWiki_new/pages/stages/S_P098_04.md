# Stage: polarized microscopy analysis of paraffin crystal morphology

- Stage ID: `S_P098_04`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P098_01` — preparation of polymethacrylate (PMA) from waste organic glass as a pour point depressor in heavy crude oil

## Goal

To experimentally validate the mechanism by observing and comparing paraffin crystal size, shape, and network formation in treated vs. untreated saturated hydrocarbon fractions.

## Input

- saturated hydrocarbons from OS-1
- saturated hydrocarbons from OS-1 + PMA-1 (2000 ppm)

## Output

- micrographs showing shorter, smaller crystals with PMA-1 vs. larger crystals in control

## Datasets used

- saturated hydrocarbons fraction from OS-1 — experimental_validation: polarized microscopy analysis of paraffin crystal morphology in saturated hydrocarbons from OS-1 with and without PMA-1

## Evidence

- P098, PDF page 4, 3.4. Mechanism study: "The morphology of paraffin crystal in PMA-1 treated crude oil was investigated, and a control experiment was carried out under the same condition. The micrographs were shown in Fig. 5. From the two micrographs, it can be found that more paraffin crystals with larger size in control sample. On the contrast, the paraffin crystals are shorter and smaller as PMA-1 added, which indicates that PMA-1 displays the capability to reduce the tendency of paraffin crystals forming three-dimensional network."
