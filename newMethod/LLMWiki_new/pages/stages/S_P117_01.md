## Goal

To generate production-window trajectories of methane decomposition on Ni surfaces under controlled exposure conditions.

## Input

- Ni slab exposing Ni(211) surface
- CH4-only and CH4 + H2O exposure configurations
- ReaxFF parameterization

## Output

- 18 primary Ni(211) production trajectories
- 22 trajectories in the complete production dataset

## Datasets used

- Primary Ni(211) Production Dataset — source: To generate production-window trajectories of methane decomposition on Ni surfaces under controlled exposure conditions.

## Evidence

- P117, PDF page 3, method: "For each exposure chemistry and temperature, three independent Ni(211) trajectories were generated by changing gas placement, gas orientation, and velocity initialization seeds, giving 18 primary Ni(211) production trajectories. Four seed0-controlled 1250 K checks were also performed using dry Ni(111), wet Ni(111), dry laterally doubled Ni(211), and wet laterally doubled Ni(211) cells, giving 22 trajectories in the complete production dataset."
