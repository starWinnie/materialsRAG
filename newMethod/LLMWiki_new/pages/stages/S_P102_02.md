# Stage: continued pretraining (CPT)

- Stage ID: `S_P102_02`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P102_01` — crystal structure generation

## Goal

Embed broad materials domain knowledge into the base LLaMA models through large-scale language modeling.

## Input

- R2CID dataset
- LLaMA-2-7B and LLaMA-3-8B base models

## Output

- LLaMat base models (LLaMat-2 and LLaMat-3)

## Datasets used

- R2CID — pretraining: Embed broad materials domain knowledge into the base LLaMA models through large-scale language modeling.

## Evidence

- P102, PDF page 2, Results: "The initial stage comprised continued pretraining (CPT) on the base LLaMA models with an extensive and meticulously curated corpus developed in-house, namely R2CID... with greater than 30 billion tokens of MatSci knowledge, encompassing approximately 4 million peer-reviewed publications (94.43%), crystallographic information files (2.499%), and MatSci community discourse (0.019%). Additionally, we incorporated a strategic 3% subset of RedPajama data... to preserve fundamental linguistic capabilities while concurrently mitigating catastrophic forgetting."
