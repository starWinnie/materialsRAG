# Stage: Preparation of green pellets and application of three sintering protocols

- Stage ID: `S_P104_03`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P104_01` — Fast firing of 3 mol% yttria-stabilized zirconia: On the effect of heating rate on sintering

## Goal

Generate candidate sintered samples under three distinct thermal schedules: (i) conventional sintering (10°C min−1), (ii) fast firing with 120 min dwell, and (iii) fast firing with 10 min dwell.

## Input

- TZ-3YB-E 3YSZ granulated powder
- uniaxial press (220 MPa)
- Nabertherm muffle furnace

## Output

- green pellets (49% relative density)
- sintered pellets for each protocol (two per condition)

## Datasets used

- Experimental characterization dataset of sintered 3YSZ pellets under three thermal schedules — candidate_pool: To generate candidate sintered samples under three distinct thermal schedules for comparative analysis.

## Evidence

- P104, PDF page 2, 2.1 Samples preparation: "Cylindrical pellets (diameter = 13 mm, thickness ≈2 mm) were shaped by uniaxially pressing 3YSZ granulated powder (TZ-3YB-E, Tosoh Corporation, Tokyo, Japan) under 220 MPa using a steel die. The green pellets were debinded at 600◦C for 30 min in a muffle furnace (Nabertherm GmbH) under a static air atmosphere... The debinded samples were then subjected to three different sintering procedures using a Nabertherm GmbH muffle furnace operating in static air: (i) conventional sintering using 10◦C min−1 as heating and cooling rate and 120 min isothermal-dwell at different temperatures; (ii) FF with 120 min dwell and (iii) FF with 10 min dwell at the sintering temperature."
