- Stage ID: `S_P085_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P085_01` — Resolving the fundamental behavior of pyramidal ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations in magnesium

## Goal

To acquire real-time mechanical response and microstructural evolution data during plastic deformation.

## Input

- submicrometer-size Mg single-crystal pillars
- c-axis compression loading

## Output

- stress-strain curves
- time-resolved TEM images/movies of dislocation generation and motion

## Datasets used

- None stated
