# Stage: volume of interest (VOI) cropping and coordinate alignment

- Stage ID: `S_P079_03`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P079_01` — A dataset of micro-scale tomograms of unidirectional glass fibre/epoxy and carbon fibre/epoxy composites acquired via synchrotron computed tomography during in-situ tensile loading

## Goal

Extract representative subvolumes from the registered 3D data and align their coordinate system with the laminate geometry.

## Input

- registered 3D volumes
- MATLAB (for rotation)
- Avizo 2019.3 (for cropping)

## Output

- cropped VOIs: 420 × 1030 × 450 μm3 (glass/epoxy), 390 × 830 × 440 μm3 (carbon/epoxy)

## Datasets used

- Micro-scale tomograms of unidirectional glass/epoxy and carbon/epoxy composites under in-situ tensile loading — source: Input for volume of interest (VOI) cropping and coordinate alignment
