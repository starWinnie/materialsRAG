# Stage: Monofilament tensile testing of treated carbon fibers

- Stage ID: `S_P096_03`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P096_01` — Synergetic Improvement of Interfacial Performance and Impact Resistance of Carbon Fiber-Reinforced Epoxy Composite via Continuous Electrochemical Oxidation

## Goal

Acquire mechanical property data on individual carbon filaments to assess treatment-induced damage

## Input

- CF-0 to CF-4 samples

## Output

- tensile strength values
- Weibull distribution parameters

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P096, PDF page 5, 2.3.2. Monofilament Tensile Tests: "Monofilament tensile testing was performed to investigate the influence of electrochemical treatment on the mechanical properties of the CFs. To this end, a mechanical testing machine (Instron 3342, Instron, Boston, MA, USA) was employed. According to ISO 11566-1996 (International Standard) [44] or GB/T 31290 (China National Standard) [45], both sides of a single CF filament were attached onto a backing paper. The distance between two bonding points was 25 mm. Before commencing the tests, the backing paper was snipped. Each CF sample was tested at least 30 times at a loading speed of 2 mm/min. Finally, a bivariate Weibull distribution was used to calculate the tensile strength of each filament."
