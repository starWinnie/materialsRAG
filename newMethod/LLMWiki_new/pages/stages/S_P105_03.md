# Stage: Analysis of Glass-ceramic Phases and Composition

- Stage ID: `S_P105_03`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P105_01` — Immobilization of Plutonium-Contaminated Materials in Calcium Aluminosilicate Glass

## Goal

To identify crystalline phases present in the glass matrix and determine elemental composition.

## Input

- industrial and laboratory glass samples

## Output

- crystalline phase identification (anorthite, corundum, metallic Fe, leucite)
- elemental composition data

## Datasets used

- Industrial PCM Glass Samples — candidate_pool: To identify crystalline phases present in the glass matrix and determine elemental composition using XRD, SEM-EDX, and acid-digest ICP-OES/ICP-MS.
- Laboratory-Made CAS Glass Series — candidate_pool: To identify crystalline phases present in the glass matrix and determine elemental composition using XRD and LA-ICP-OES.

## Evidence

- P105, PDF page 3, Methodology: "To identify crystalline phases present in the glass matrix, XRD was carried out on glass powder using a Bruker D2 Phaser machine in a Bragg-Brentano Geometry with a Cu K-alpha source ( λ = 1.5418 Å), a 10 mA current, and 30 kV accelerating voltage. Data were collected between 5◦and 80◦2 θ with a step size of 0.02◦and a count time of ∼ 2 s per step with 10◦of rotation per second."
