## Goal

Establish causal links between La-induced support stabilization, enhanced MSI, oxygen vacancy concentration, Niδ+ speciation, and catalytic performance

## Input

- XRD, XPS, FTIR, EXAFS, Raman, TPR, TPD data
- H2 production rates
- NH3 conversion
- stability tests

- quantified Niδ+ proportion (51.1% vs. 30.6%)
- Ce3+ content (43% vs. 31%)
- ID/IF2g ratio decrease
- Ea = 120.3 kJ·mol−1
- 279.2 mmolH2·gNi−1·min−1
