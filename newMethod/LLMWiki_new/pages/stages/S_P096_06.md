## Goal

Screen candidate treatments to identify the current density yielding best trade-off between interfacial strength, impact resistance, and post-impact compressive strength

## Input

- surface morphology data
- chemical composition data
- wettability data
- IFSS data
- impact damage area data
- CAI data
