# Stage: Dilatometric measurement of sintering behavior at multiple heating rates

- Stage ID: `S_P104_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P104_01` — Fast firing of 3 mol% yttria-stabilized zirconia: On the effect of heating rate on sintering

## Goal

Acquire experimental relative density vs. temperature data at different heating rates (2.5, 10, and 25°C min−1) to construct the master sintering curve.

## Input

- 3YSZ pellets
- horizontal Linseis L75 dilatometer
- alumina measurement system
- 0.5 N load

## Output

- dilatometric density curves vs. furnace temperature
- Θ(T,t)–ρ data

## Datasets used

- Dilatometric sintering data for ≈40 nm 3YSZ (TZ-3YB-E) — source: To acquire experimental relative density vs. temperature data at multiple heating rates for master sintering curve construction.

## Evidence

- P104, PDF page 3, 2.2 Master sintering curve calculation: "The MSC was determined by the dilatometric method using a horizontal Linseis L75 dilatometer equipped with an alumina measurement system. The dilatometric behavior was evaluated at different heating (2.5, 10, and 25◦C min−1) in air."
