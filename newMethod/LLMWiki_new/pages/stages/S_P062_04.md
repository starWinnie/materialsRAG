# Stage: DFT validation of MLIP-predicted configurations

- Stage ID: `S_P062_04`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P062_01` — CO dimerization on copper surfaces

## Goal

Assess reliability of eSEN-OC25 predictions for large-cell CO dimerization simulations by computing DFT single-points on ∼100 configurations per state (initial, transition, final).

## Input

- MLIP-generated configurations from OPES trajectories

## Output

- energy and force errors < 0.1 eV and < 0.005 eV/Å
- per-ion correction applied to total energy offset

## Datasets used

- Open Catalyst 2025 — computational_validation: Assess reliability of eSEN-OC25 predictions for large-cell CO dimerization simulations by computing DFT single-points on ∼100 configurations per state (initial, transition, final).
