## Goal

Produce dense, phase-pure polycrystalline thermoelectric samples via solid-state reaction and hot pressing.

## Input

- weighed elemental precursors
- quartz tubes
- vacuum furnace
- annealing furnace
- agate mortar
- fast-hot-pressing furnace

## Output

- dense cylindrical sintered samples (12.5 mm diameter, >98% relative density)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P081, PDF page 10, Experimental: "The vacuum-sealed quartz tube was heated to 1423 K within 14 h, held for 8 h, and cooled to room temperature with a furnace. Thereaer, the ingots were annealed at 873 K for 24 h. The obtained products were hand-ground in an agate mortar for 15 min to a ne powder and then sintered at 823 K under a uniaxial pressure of 50 MPa for 20 min to form dense cylinders with a diameter of 12.5 mm using a fast-hot-pressing furnace (FHP-828). The obtained samples all achieved a relative density of over 98%."
