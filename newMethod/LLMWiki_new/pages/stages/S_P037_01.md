# Stage: creating and validating experimental databases of electrical conductivity and band gap

- Stage ID: `S_P037_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P037_01` — accelerating the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs)

## Goal

create and validate two experimental datasets of room-temperature conductivity and band gap measurements

## Input

- MPDS
- UCSB dataset
- primary literature
- Pearson's Crystallographic Database
- ICSD

## Output

- electrical conductivity dataset (8231 entries)
- band gap dataset (4767 entries)

## Datasets used

- MPDS and UCSB datasets — source: source for constructing the electrical conductivity dataset
- Zhuo et al. 2018 experimental band gap dataset — source: source for constructing the band gap dataset
- Materials Project DFT-computed band gap dataset — pretraining: pre-training CrabNet for band gap prediction
