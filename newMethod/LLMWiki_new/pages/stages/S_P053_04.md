# Stage: pretraining and fine-tuning of EquiformerV2 and eSEN models on OMat24 and related datasets

- Stage ID: `S_P053_04`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P053_01` — training Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials (MLIPs) for inorganic materials

## Goal

to train MLIP models that predict energy, forces and stress given an input structure

## Input

- OMat24 dataset (and MPtrj, sAlexandria for fine-tuning)

## Output

- pretrained and fine-tuned EquiformerV2 and eSEN models

## Datasets used

- Open Materials 2024 (OMat24) — pretraining: to pretrain EquiformerV2 and eSEN models before fine-tuning
- subsampled Alexandria (sAlexandria) — training: to fine-tune pretrained models on domain-specific data while preventing data leakage to the WBM test set
- MPtrj dataset — training: to fine-tune pretrained models on domain-specific data

## Evidence

- P053, PDF page 11, 4.3 OMat24 MLIP training strategies: "We train and evaluate EquiformerV2 models26 as it is currently one of the top performing direct force models on the OC201, OC2272 and ODAC234 benchmarks. We also train and evaluate eSEN models, since it is an energy conserving architecture which has shown SOTA performance for phonon and thermal conductivity calculations28. The models are trained to predict energy, forces and stress given an input structure."
- P053, PDF page 2, Results: "We pretrain several variants of the EquiformerV226,27 and eSEN28 architectures on OMat24 and show that, after fine-tuning to account for differences in DFT settings, state-of-the-art results are achieved on the popular MatBench Discovery leaderboard29."
