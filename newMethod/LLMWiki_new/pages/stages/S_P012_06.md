## Goal

Theoretically and computationally verify continuity (via EMD) and general geometric completeness of WPDD/UPDD crystal graphs

## Input

- perturbed crystal structures S and Q
- constructed crystal graphs G(S), G(Q)

## Output

- EMD(G(S), G(Q)) ≤ Cϵ (continuity)
- proof of geometric completeness (Propositions 1 & 2)
