## Goal

Obtain DCB specimens of 7050 aluminum alloy in three distinct aging states (under-aged, peak-aged, over-aged)

## Input

- 7050 aluminum alloy plate
- furnace
- oven

## Output

- DCB specimens aged for 8 h, 16 h, and 24 h

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P093, PDF page 2, 2. Experiments: "The specimens were put in a Muffle furnace with 470 ◦C for 2 h then quenched with cold water, after that put in an oven with 135 ◦C for 8 h (under-aged), 16 h (peak-aged) and 24 h (over-aged) respectively."
