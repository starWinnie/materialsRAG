## Goal

To prepare glass samples in appropriate forms (disks, thin plates, air-cooled pieces) for subsequent physical property measurements and spectroscopic analyses.

## Input

- melted glass

## Output

- polished glass disks for physical properties
- thin plates for spectroscopic measurements
- air-cooled samples for melt properties and fiber preparation

## Evidence

- P107, PDF page 3, Material and Research Methods: "Each glass sample was then cut and polished for subsequent measurements of physical properties and chemical analyses. For spectroscopic measurements, each melt was cast into a stainless-steel mold to form a thin plate, which was then transferred to a muffle furnace and annealed at 650◦C for 2 h. Each glass disk, approximately 40 mm in diameter and 3 mm in thickness, was prepared from annealed glass using a diamond-tip core drill and mechanically polished to optical quality with SiC papers (320 and 600 grit) and a final cerium slurry. For melt properties and fiber preparation, air-cooled glass samples were used."
