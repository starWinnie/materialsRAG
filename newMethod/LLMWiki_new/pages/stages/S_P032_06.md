# Stage: NVE and NVT molecular dynamics simulations

- Stage ID: `S_P032_06`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P032_01` — molecular dynamics simulation using a neural network force field

## Goal

Run and analyze MD simulations (NVE and NVT ensembles) to empirically validate stability, energy conservation, and observables fidelity (e.g., h(r), velocity–velocity autocorrelation spectra, free energy surfaces).

## Input

- pretrained or fine-tuned MD-ET
- molecular structures (e.g., alkanes, n-octane, alanine dipeptide)
- thermostats (SVR/Bussi)

## Output

- NVE energy drift curves
- NVT h(r) distributions
- velocity–velocity autocorrelation power spectra
- free energy surfaces

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P032, PDF page 10, C. Unconstrained models can learn to approximately conserve energy without explicit inductive biases: "We find that NVE simulations with MD-ET are approximately energy-conserving for some small systems, but with increasing system size, the total energy tends to increase with time (see Fig. 3)."
- P032, PDF page 12, D. Unconstrained models are able to reproduce observables in NVT simulations: "To evaluate the degree to which MD-ET produces faithful MD simulations, we run 1 ns NVT simulations using the stochastic velocity rescaling (SVR or Bussi) thermostat [...] We then produce velocity–velocity autocorrelation spectra from these trajectories (see Fig. 7, top)."
