# Stage: Glass preparation and batch mixing

- Stage ID: `S_P107_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P107_01` — Properties and Structures of MgO–CaO–Al2O3–SiO2 Glasses: Effects of Low-Iron Oxide and Redox

## Goal

To prepare a series of MgO–CaO–Al2O3–SiO2 glasses with controlled low-iron oxide concentrations and redox states.

## Input

- reagent-grade chemicals (Alfa Aesar) with purities equal or greater than 99.9% for SiO2, Al2O3, CaCO3, MgO, and Na2CO3, MnO2, SnO2, and CeO2, and 99.8% for Fe2O3

## Output

- glass samples (IRS 1–7) with specified Fe2O3, MnO2, SnO2, and CeO2 concentrations

## Datasets used

- IRS glasses — source: To prepare a series of MgO–CaO–Al2O3–SiO2 glasses with controlled low-iron oxide concentrations and redox states.

## Evidence

- P107, PDF page 2, Material and Research Methods: "Each glass was prepared from a 1000 g batch mixture of reagent-grade chemicals (Alfa Aesar) with purities equal or greater than 99.9% for SiO2 , Al2 O3 , CaCO3 , MgO, and Na2 CO3 , MnO2 , SnO2 , and CeO2 , and 99.8% for Fe2 O3 . The glass batches were prepared by thoroughly mixing the raw materials, then melted in platinum crucibles at 1550◦C for 4 h to ensure homogeneity."
