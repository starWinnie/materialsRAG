# Stage: Data Acquisition and Standardization Layer

- Stage ID: `S_P060_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P060_01` — deep fusion of experimental and theoretical data in condensed matter physics

## Goal

ingest and harmonize multimodal data from disparate sources including public databases, electronic lab notebooks, and scientific literature

## Input

- public databases (e.g., ICSD, COD, CSD, Pauling File, Materials Project, AFLOW, OQMD, MatCloud, NOMAD, Materials Cloud, Atomly)
- electronic laboratory notebooks (MatElab)
- scientific literature

## Output

- raw, heterogeneous multimodal data

## Datasets used

- Crystal Structure and Diffraction Database (COD-derived) — source: serving as the experimental cornerstone of the collection, providing nearly 500,000 crystal structure entries for ingestion and harmonization
- MatElab Single-Crystal Growth Database — source: providing curated experimental records with full provenance for automated ingestion into the platform
- Materiae: Topological Materials Database — source: providing topological materials data for integration, including over 8,000 unique topologically non-trivial materials
- Topological Phonon Database — source: providing phononic band structures and classifications for over 5,000 materials
- 2D Ferroelectric Materials Database — source: providing 2D ferroelectricity data for integration
- Nonlinear Optical Database — source: providing non-linear optical coefficients data for integration

## Evidence

- P060, PDF page 2, Results: "At its core, the architecture follows a multi-stage workflow designed for robust data processing and intelligent analysis. It begins with a Data Acquisition and Standardization Layer that ingests and harmonizes multimodal data from disparate sources, including public databases, electronic lab notebooks, and the scientific literature."
- P060, PDF page 3, Data Sources and Integration Strategy: "Our data acquisition strategy follows a three-pronged approach: (1) aggregation of established public databases, (2) automated ingestion from our in-house electronic laboratory platform (MatElab)[50], which captures curated experimental records with full provenance, and (3) automated extraction of structural and property data from the scientific literature."
