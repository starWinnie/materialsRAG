# Stage: Hot deformation experiments

- Stage ID: `S_P099_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P099_01` — Investigation of isothermal continuous hot deformation behavior

## Goal

To acquire flow behavior data (true stress-strain curves) and deformed specimens for microstructural analysis under controlled isothermal multi-pass conditions.

## Input

- as-cast AFA alloy billets
- Gleeble-3500 thermal simulation tester

## Output

- true stress-strain curves
- deformed cylindrical specimens after each pass

## Datasets used

- as-cast alumina-forming austenitic (AFA) alloy — source: To acquire flow behavior data (true stress-strain curves) and deformed specimens for microstructural analysis under controlled isothermal multi-pass conditions.

## Evidence

- P099, PDF page 2, Experimental procedure: "In this experiment, the cast billets were machined into cylindrical specimens of φ8 × 12 mm. The equipment used for the hot deformation is the Gleeble-3500 thermal simulation tester. This experiment designs a new homogenization process for isothermal continuous hot deformation. The material is deformed in multiple passes under isothermal conditions with holding times between the deformation passes."
- P099, PDF page 3, Results and discussion: "Fig. 2 shows the true stress-strain curve of AFA alloy under different deformation conditions."
