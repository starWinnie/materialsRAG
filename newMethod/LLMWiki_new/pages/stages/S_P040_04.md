# Stage: in situ ETEM and HAADF-STEM observation of dynamic dispersion

- Stage ID: `S_P040_04`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P040_01` — reverse regulation from particles to clusters

## Goal

Directly observe and confirm real-time structural evolution from Ni particles to clusters under operando conditions

## Input

- 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst
- H2 atmosphere (50 Pa, 99.99%)
- heating to 600 °C

## Output

- time-resolved images showing particle contraction → complete dispersion → retained highly dispersed state

## Datasets used

- 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst — experimental_validation: Directly observe and confirm real-time structural evolution from Ni particles to clusters under operando conditions

## Evidence

- P040, PDF page 2, Results: "In order to directly observe the formation process of these Ni clusters, in situ aberration-corrected ETEM was employed to characterize the 10Ni/30La-CeO2 catalyst. The nanoparticles gradually contracted when the temperature increased from room temperature to 450 °C under a H2 atmosphere (Fig. 2a, b). Then, the particles completely dispersed upon further increasing the temperature to 600 °C, and the highly dispersed state was retained even after returning to ambient temperature (Fig. 2c, d)."
