# Stage: Systematic investigation of sintering behavior and microstructural evolution

- Stage ID: `S_P115_05`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P115_01` — Preparation of TiC-based composites via reactive sintering

## Goal

Identify optimal sintering temperature and Si content by evaluating phase composition, microstructure, and mechanical properties

## Input

- XRD patterns
- SEM micrographs
- EDS mappings
- TEM analysis
- mechanical test results

## Output

- optimal composition (TZH2S-1700)
- optimal sintering condition (1700°C, 20 mol% Si)

## Datasets used

- XRD patterns of TiC-based composites — screening: Identify phase composition and detect presence/absence of free carbon and Ti3SiC2
- SEM micrographs and EDS mappings of TiC-based composites — screening: Characterize microstructural morphology and elemental distribution to assess grain dissolution, phase distribution, and carbon elimination
- TEM analysis of TZH2S-1700 specimen — screening: Examine interfacial structure, coherent relationships, and elemental enrichment at atomic scale to validate decomposition mechanisms and strengthening effects

- P115, PDF page 2, Introduction: "The influence of Si content on sintering behavior, microstructural evolution, and mechanical properties is systematically investigated."
- P115, PDF page 9, Mechanical Properties: "The results indicate that the addition of silicon significantly enhances the comprehensive mechanical properties of the material. Among them, the TZH2S-1700 specimen exhibits the optimal comprehensive performance, achieving a relative density of up to 99.9%, a HV of 19.4 GPa, a flexural strength of 972 MPa and a fracture toughness of 6.7 MPa ⋅m1/2."
