# Stage: generation of diverse non-equilibrium structures via Boltzmann sampling, AIMD, and rattled relaxations

- Stage ID: `S_P053_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P053_01` — training Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials (MLIPs) for inorganic materials

## Goal

to obtain diverse non-equilibrium structures for training

## Input

- relaxed structures from the Alexandria PBE bulk materials dataset

## Output

- 118 million structures labeled with energy, forces and cell stress

## Datasets used

- Alexandria — source: to obtain diverse non-equilibrium structures by sampling relaxed structures as input for Boltzmann sampling, AIMD, and rattled relaxations
- Open Materials 2024 (OMat24) — candidate_pool: to generate diverse non-equilibrium structures via Boltzmann sampling, AIMD, and rattled relaxations

## Evidence

- P053, PDF page 10, 4.1 OMat24 structure generation and sampling: "Input structure generation for the OMat24 dataset consists of three different processes intended to obtain diverse non-equilibrium structures: Boltzmann sampling of rattled (random Gaussian perturbations of atomic positions) structures, ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD), and relaxations of rattled structures."
- P053, PDF page 2, 2.1 The OMat24 dataset: "The OMat24 dataset includes a total of 118 million structures labeled with energy, forces and cell stress."
