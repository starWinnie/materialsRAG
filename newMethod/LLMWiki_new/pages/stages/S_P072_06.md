# Stage: Identification of optimal heat treatment parameters

- Stage ID: `S_P072_06`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P072_01` — Optimizing the heat-treatment process for X6CrNiMoVNb11-2 stainless steel to meet performance requirements

## Goal

To screen and identify the optimal combination of quenching and tempering parameters that yields the best comprehensive mechanical properties.

## Input

- mechanical property data
- microstructural analysis
- tempered hardness equation

## Output

- optimal parameters: quench at 1040 °C in oil, temper at 650 °C in air
- performance metrics: hardness ~313 HV, elongation 17.9%, reduction of area 52%, impact toughness ~65 J/cm2

- None stated
