# Stage: Steam oxidation experiments

- Stage ID: `S_P114_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P114_01` — Effect of Steam Oxidation on Microstructure and Hoop Strength of Duplex SiCf /SiC Tube

## Goal

Expose SiCf/SiC tube specimens to controlled steam/air environments at 600, 900, and 1200°C for 10 days.

## Input

- SiCf/SiC composite tubes
- steam/air mixture (H2O:air = 8.3:1)
- tube furnace

## Output

- oxidized SiCf/SiC tube specimens

## Datasets used

- as-received SiCf/SiC tube — source: Baseline material for steam oxidation experiments

## Evidence

- P114, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedures: "Steam oxidation experiments were performed using a tube furnace (GSL-1700X, Hefei Kejing Materials Technology Co., Ltd., China) with a tube diameter of 60 mm, and the SiCf /SiC composite specimens were placed in a corundum boat. Initially, the SiCf /SiC composite specimens were heated at a rate of 10◦C/min under an Ar atmosphere to avoid oxidation of the samples during the heating step. When the predetermined temperature (600, 900, and 1200◦C) was reached, the H2 O/air mixture was introduced into the furnace at a pressure of 0.1 MPa, and the specimens were held at temperature for 10 days. Deionized water was fed at a constant rate of 2 mL/min and vaporized before entering the reaction zone, while the air flow rate was maintained at 300 mL/min using a calibrated flow controller. Accordingly, The volume ratio of water vapor to air in the composite gas was precisely controlled at H2 O:air = 8.3:1, and the equivalent flow rate was 40 mm/s."
