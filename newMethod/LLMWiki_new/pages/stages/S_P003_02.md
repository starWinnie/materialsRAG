## Goal

transform Cartesian dielectric tensors into spherical-harmonics basis to encode rotational symmetry

## Input

- Cartesian dielectric tensors
- crystal symmetry information

## Output

- frequency-dependent spherical-harmonic coefficients

## Datasets used

- TSENN 1432-material dataset — label_source: source of ground-truth Cartesian dielectric tensors for spherical-harmonics decomposition
