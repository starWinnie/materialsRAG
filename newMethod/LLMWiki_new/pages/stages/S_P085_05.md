# Stage: Atomistic simulation of ⟨c+a⟩ dislocation formation and glide

- Stage ID: `S_P085_05`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P085_01` — Resolving the fundamental behavior of pyramidal ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations in magnesium

## Goal

To computationally validate the experimental observations of ⟨c+a⟩ dislocation nucleation and gliding on pyramidal planes.

## Input

- modified embedded atom method (MEAM) potential
- Mg single crystal model
- c-axis compressive loading

## Output

- simulated nucleation and glide of ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations on pyramidal I and II planes
- evidence of no artificial dislocation introduction

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P085, PDF page 2, Dipole: "The mobility of the hc + ai dislocations was further supported by our atomistic simulation in which hc + ai dislocations nucleated during c-axis compression and glided on the pyramidal planes (figs. S9 and S10 and movie S7)."
- P085, PDF page 8: "No dislocation was artificially introduced before loading, and all 〈c+a〉 dislocations were produced by the compressive loading."
