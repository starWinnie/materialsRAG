# Stage: Validation of dislocation-based plastic deformation model

- Stage ID: `S_P100_07`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 7
- Task: `T_P100_01` — Grain boundary-induced plasticity during thin film buckling

## Goal

Verify accuracy of dislocation-counting method against direct geometric measurement of length variations.

## Input

- Length variations ΔL+_i, ΔL−_i computed from dislocation counting ('d' column)
- Same quantities measured directly from simulations ('s' column)

## Output

- Table 1 showing close agreement between 'd' and 's' values
- Justification that dislocations from GB are responsible for folding

## Datasets used

- Al thin film MD dataset subset with three Σ51 (551) grain boundary positions — computational_validation: Validate dislocation-counting method for plastic strain quantification against direct geometric measurement across three GB positions

## Evidence

- P100, PDF page 4, 4. Dislocation-induced folding and plastic strain: "In order to verify the accuracy of this simple model, the key quantities ΔLj_i, with i= 1, 2 and j= ±, determined from the dislocation counting (d) have been compared in Table 1 with the same quantities directly estimated from the measurement of the length variations of the film taken from the simulations (s). It appears from this Table 1 that the model gives relatively accurate values of the length variations... Thus, it justifies that the dislocations from the GB are responsible for the folding of the film of an angle θfand may also explain the presence of a plastic strain εpin the midplane..."
