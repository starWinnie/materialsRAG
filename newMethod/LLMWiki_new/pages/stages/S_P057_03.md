## Goal

train VAE to encode crystal structures into smooth, compact latent representations while preserving physical symmetries

## Input

- crystal structures M = (A, X, L)

## Output

- latent representations Z
- pretrained VAE encoder Eφ

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P057, PDF page 3, 3.2.1. VARIATIONAL AUTOENCODER (VAE): "Our first objective is to encode the 3D crystal geometry into a lower-dimensional latent space that is both meaningful and preserves the physical symmetries inherent to crystal structures. To accomplish this, we employ a Variational Autoencoder (VAE) framework consisting of an encoder Eφ and a decoder Dψ. The encoder module takes the crystal material M = (A, X, L) as input and encodes atomic types, coordinates and lattice structure into the latent space: Z = Eφ(A, X, L)"
