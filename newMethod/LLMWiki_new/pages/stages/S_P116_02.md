# Stage: Preparation of precursor solution

- Stage ID: `S_P116_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P116_01` — Rapid fabrication of stretchable, ultratough, and rigid polymer materials

## Goal

To homogenize monomer mixtures with precise stoichiometric ratios and add controlled amounts of photoinitiator and cross-linker.

## Input

- HEA and AAm monomers
- PEGDA cross-linker
- I2959 photoinitiator
- magnetic stirring

## Output

- homogeneous precursor solution

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P116, PDF page 9, MATERIALS AND METHODS: "The precursor solution was prepared by mixing HEA and AAm at a designated molar ratio [e.g., HEA:AAm = 4 for P(HEA4-co-AAm)], together with PEGDA (0.05 mol % relative to C═C bonds) as a cross-linker and I2959 (0.25 mol % relative to C═C bonds) as a photoinitiator. After thorough homogenization by magnetic stirring for 3 min at room temperature, the mixture was poured into a glass mold..."
