# Stage: converting and standardizing polymer representations (PSMILES/wPSMILES)

- Stage ID: `S_P052_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P052_01` — polymer property prediction

## Goal

Ensure consistent input formatting across models by converting between PSMILES and weighted PSMILES representations

## Input

- raw PSMILES strings
- wPSMILES strings
- polymer architecture labels

## Output

- standardized PSMILES for non-PC models
- standardized wPSMILES for PC model

## Datasets used

- VIPEA — label_source: Converting wPSMILES to PSMILES for non-PC models
- polyVERSE — label_source: Converting PSMILES to wPSMILES for PC model
- PolyMetriX — label_source: Converting PSMILES to wPSMILES for PC model

## Evidence

- P052, PDF page 17, Methods: "For consistent evaluation across models, we converted weighted PSMILES from VIPEA to traditional PSMILES for models other than PC. Conversely, traditional PSMILES from PolyMetriX and polyVERSE were converted to wPSMILES for PC."
- P052, PDF page 20, Polymer Representations: "Homopolymer PSMILES strings were converted to weighted PSMILES (wPSMILES) format by replacing the first and second asterisks with [*:1] and [*:2], respectively, and appending |1|<1-2:1:1<1-1:0:0<2-2:0:0 to encode homopolymer connectivity."
