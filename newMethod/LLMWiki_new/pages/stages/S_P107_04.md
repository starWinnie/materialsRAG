# Stage: Spectroscopic characterization (UV–vis–NIR, Raman)

- Stage ID: `S_P107_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P107_01` — Properties and Structures of MgO–CaO–Al2O3–SiO2 Glasses: Effects of Low-Iron Oxide and Redox

## Goal

To identify and quantify the local environments of ferric and ferrous ions and assess changes in glass network structure.

## Input

- glass disks and thin plates

## Output

- UV–vis–NIR spectra showing Fe2+ absorption band at 1050 nm
- Raman spectra for Qn speciation analysis

## Datasets used

- IRS glasses — source: To identify and quantify the local environments of ferric and ferrous ions and assess changes in glass network structure.

## Evidence

- P107, PDF page 3, Characterization Methods: "The UV–vis spectra of the samples were acquired using a PerkinElmer Lambda 950 UV/vis/NIR spectrophotometer over 250–2450 nm, with a resolution of 5 nm per step. Each spectrum was normalized to sample thickness to determine the FeO concentration of the glasses, using the band intensity at 1050 nm and Beer’s law. Raman spectra of the glasses were acquired using an RM2000 Raman microscope (Renishaw) under 514 nm laser excitation over the range 100–1400 cm−1 with a step size of 2.75 cm−1."
