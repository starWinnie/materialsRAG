## Evidence

- P108, PDF page 2, Materials and Methods: "The relative densities of the bulk HECx –SiC composites were measured in deionized water using the Archimedes method. The particle size distributions of the powders were determined using a laser particle size analyzer (LS13320). Phase compositions were identified by X-ray diffraction (XRD, X’Pert Pro). The microstructures and elemental distributions of the powders and sintered composites were characterized by scanning electron microscopy (SEM, MIRA3-XMU) equipped with energy-dispersive spectroscopy (EDS). The grain sizes of the sintered composites were measured from SEM images using ImageJ software... Vickers hardness and indentation fracture toughness were measured using an HVS-10 hardness tester... Flexural strength was evaluated by three-point bending... High-temperature oxidation tests were conducted in air at 1000◦C for 3 h... The SRIM software (2013) was used to simulate the ion irradiation experiment..."
