## Goal

To construct initial atomic configurations for MD simulations of the glass compositions.

## Input

- glass compositions (IRS1-4)
- experimentally measured redox ratio

## Output

- initial configurations with about 30,000 atoms in cubic simulation cells

## Datasets used

- IRS glasses — source: To construct initial atomic configurations for MD simulations of the glass compositions.

## Evidence

- P107, PDF page 5, Molecular Dynamics Simulations: "Initial configurations with about 30,000 atoms were randomly generated for IRS1-4 compositions (Table 1 ). Large simulation cells were used to ensure reliable statistics for the structural analysis of low-concentration components, such as iron oxides. The number of divalent and trivalent iron atoms was determined according to the experimentally measured redox ratio."
