# Stage: Comparison of experimental sintering densities against master sintering curve

- Stage ID: `S_P104_05`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P104_01` — Fast firing of 3 mol% yttria-stabilized zirconia: On the effect of heating rate on sintering

## Goal

Evaluate whether fast-fired samples deviate from the MSC predicted by dilatometry—indicating heating-rate-dependent sintering kinetics.

## Input

- measured bulk/apparent densities
- FEM-corrected Θ(T,t) values
- dilatometric MSC

## Output

- Θ(T,t)–ρ plot showing agreement/deviation
- quantification of density improvement (≤3%)

## Datasets used

- Dilatometric sintering data for ≈40 nm 3YSZ (TZ-3YB-E) — benchmark: To serve as the reference master sintering curve against which experimental sintering densities are compared to evaluate heating-rate dependence.

## Evidence

- P104, PDF page 4, 2.4 Modeling: "Plotting the relative densities for fast-fired materials together with the MSC helps to reveal the dissimilarities with the conventional behavior."
- P104, PDF page 6, 3.3 Fast firing versus master sintering curve: "We can observe a quite good agreement between the sintering experiments and what extrapolated from dilatometry. However, we can also observe that the samples obtained by FF, either 10 min or 120 min, show a slightly improved densification compared to the conventionally fired materials. The difference is in any case modest, typically below 3%."
