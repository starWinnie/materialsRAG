# Stage: Characterization of Pt speciation and loading

- Stage ID: `S_P080_05`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P080_01` — Photocatalytic Hydrogen Generation

## Goal

To confirm atomic dispersion of Pt and quantify surface loading using SEM, XPS, and HAADF-STEM.

## Input

- Pt-decorated TiO2 samples

## Output

- evidence of Pt single atoms (no nanoparticles)
- Pt 4f XPS peak positions (72.7/75.9 eV)
- Pt SA densities (≈4.1 × 105 μm−2)
- absence of Cl in XPS

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P080, PDF page 2, Results and Discussion: "Importantly, in a high-resolution SEM image of the Pt decorated titania surface (Figure 1d), no distinct particles can be seen—this shows that over the entire surface area, no agglomeration of SAs to nanoparticles in the nm-range has taken place."
- P080, PDF page 2, Results and Discussion: "X-ray photoelectron spectra (XPS) of the surface show for the Pt 4f region (Figure 1e) the presence of Pt (0.27 at.%), evident with a doublet of Pt 4f7/2 and 4f5/2 at peak positions of 72.7 and 75.9 eV, respectively. This position is typical of Pt SAs coordinated with an oxide surface"
