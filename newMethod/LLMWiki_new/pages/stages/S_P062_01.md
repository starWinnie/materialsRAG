## Goal

Construct the largest dataset for solid–liquid interfaces comprising over 7 million DFT calculations sampling explicit solvent configurations across elements, solvents, ions, and non-equilibrium structures.

## Input

- catalyst surface
- solvent
- ion
- adsorbate molecule

## Output

- OC25 dataset: 7,801,261 single-point DFT calculations across 1,511,270 unique systems
