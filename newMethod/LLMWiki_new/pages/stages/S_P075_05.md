- Stage ID: `S_P075_05`

## Goal

Correlate observed microstructural features (particle rounding, neck formation) with measured mechanical properties to benchmark sintering onset

## Input

- sintered pellets
- SEM (Quanta 650 ESEM)

## Output

- SEM images showing particle morphology evolution
- qualitative identification of rounded features and neck formation

- None stated

## Evidence

They were then viewed in an SEM (Quanta 650 ESEM, FEI) at magnifications up to 50 000× to correlate the observed stages of sintering to the measured Young’s modulus."
- P075, PDF page 5, 3.1 SEM analysis: "We first present SEM images in Figure 3 of the surface of samples in the unsintered state and after sintering at a range of temperatures from 130◦C to 1600◦C.
