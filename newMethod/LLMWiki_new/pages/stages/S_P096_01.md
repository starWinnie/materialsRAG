# Stage: Electrochemical treatment of carbon fibers at varying current densities

- Stage ID: `S_P096_01`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P096_01` — Synergetic Improvement of Interfacial Performance and Impact Resistance of Carbon Fiber-Reinforced Epoxy Composite via Continuous Electrochemical Oxidation

## Goal

Generate candidate carbon fiber samples with systematically varied surface chemistries and morphologies via continuous electrochemical oxidation

## Input

- T800H-grade carbon fiber
- NH4HCO3 solution (10 wt%)
- electrolysis temperature of 30 ◦C
- treatment time of 70 s

## Output

- CF-0 (0 mA/cm2)
- CF-1 (0.05 mA/cm2)
- CF-2 (0.10 mA/cm2)
- CF-3 (0.15 mA/cm2)
- CF-4 (0.20 mA/cm2)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P096, PDF page 3, 2.2. CF Preparation Using Different Electrochemical Treatment Parameters: "The current densities considered were 0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, and 0.20 mA/cm2, and the obtained CFs were designated as CF-0, CF-1, CF-2, CF-3, and CF-4, respectively."
