# Stage: experimental APT specimen preparation and data collection

- Stage ID: `S_P097_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P097_01` — interpreting field evaporation through dynamic bond-breaking processes

## Goal

Acquire experimental APT data from [001]-oriented Ti-52Al-2Nb for comparison with simulations.

## Input

- Ti-52Al-2Nb (at.%) ingot in as-cast condition
- SEM/FIB lift-out and sharpening
- EBSD orientation identification
- SAD verification

## Output

- APT datasets collected in voltage and laser modes
- reconstructed 3D atom positions
- z-SDM and AtomVicinity analyses

## Datasets used

- Experimental APT dataset from Ti-52Al-2Nb (at.%) ingot — experimental_validation: Provide experimental ground truth for comparison with TAPSim-MD simulations, specifically for surface morphology and mixed-layer reconstruction artifacts.

## Evidence

- P097, PDF page 4, Experimental Methods: "Specimens for experimental APT were prepared using a standard lift-out and sharpening process performed with a Thermo Fisher Helios 650 Nanolab scanning electron microscope/focused ion beam (SEM/FIB) from a Ti-52Al-2Nb (at.%) ingot in as-cast condition. A [001]-oriented grain was identified by electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD)... APT experiments were conducted using a Cameca LEAP 5000XR instrument. Data collection was conducted with a base sample temperature of 50 K and a detection rate of 1% in voltage and laser modes... The APT data was reconstructed using the Cameca AP Suite software 6.3."
