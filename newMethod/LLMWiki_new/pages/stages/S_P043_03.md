# Stage: Calculation of DFT-experiment discrepancy (Hcorr)

- Stage ID: `S_P043_03`
- Stage type: `label_generation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P043_01` — ML-based correction of DFT formation enthalpy errors for improved phase stability prediction

## Goal

Compute the target labels (Hcorr) as the difference between DFT-calculated and experimental formation enthalpies for each material in the dataset.

## Input

- DFT-calculated formation enthalpies (HDFT)
- experimental formation enthalpies (Hexpt)

## Output

- Hcorr = HDFT − Hexpt

## Datasets used

- Experimental formation enthalpy dataset for Al–Ni–Pd and Al–Ni–Ti ternary systems — label_source: Compute target labels Hcorr = HDFT − Hexpt for supervised learning

## Evidence

- P043, PDF page 3: "The error inherent in DFT calculations (that has its origin from the approximation used for the exchange and correlation functional) can be quantified as the difference between the experimental and theoretical determined enthalpy of formation. Hence, we introduce the term Hcorr as Hcorr = HDFT − Hexpt, and we strive here to use machine learning algorithms to make good estimates of Hcorr when experimental data (Hexpt) are missing."
