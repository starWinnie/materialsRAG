# Stage: preprocessing material structures into graph representations and generating task-specific instruction-answer templates

- Stage ID: `S_P050_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P050_01` — material property prediction and human-AI interaction for materials science

## Goal

To convert raw crystal structures into graph-based inputs for the Material Processing Branch and to construct standardized multimodal training examples (structure + prompt → answer).

## Input

- atomic structures (positions, elements, lattice)
- 12 material tasks (3 descriptive + 9 property prediction)

## Output

- graph-encoded material inputs
- 12 sets of paraphrased instruction templates and corresponding answer templates

- None stated
