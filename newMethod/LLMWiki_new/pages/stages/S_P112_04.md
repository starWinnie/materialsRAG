# Stage: systematic generation of doped and non-stoichiometric HfO2 configurations

- Stage ID: `S_P112_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P112_01` — melting point regulation of HfO2 via cation doping and oxygen vacancy-mediated non-stoichiometry

## Goal

To construct supercells representing HfO2-based solid solutions and non-stoichiometric HfO2-x systems for melting point calculation

## Input

- dopant elements (Y3+, Yb3+, Lu3+, Sc3+, In3+, La3+, Ta5+, Ca2+)
- oxygen vacancy concentrations (x = 0.0–0.3)

## Output

- doped HfO2 solid solutions
- non-stoichiometric HfO2-x systems

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P112, PDF page 2, Results: "Based on the DP_SCAN model, we systematically calculated the melting point evolution of HfO2 -based solid solutions with a series of dopant elements (Y3 + , Yb3 + , Lu3 + , Sc3 + , In3 + , La3 + , Ta5 + , Ca2 + ) at doping concentrations ranging from 5 at.% to 30 at.%, with an interval of 5 at.%. Meanwhile, the introduction of Hf2 + was adopted to simulate the melting point variation of non-stoichiometric systems, that is, HfO2- x , with x equal to the amount of Hf2 + cation."
- P112, PDF page 2, Results: "During the construction of supercells with different doping levels, charge neutrality was strictly maintained in accordance with defect chemistry principles."
