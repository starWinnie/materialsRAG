# Stage: Enumerating candidate compounds in unexplored composition space (e.g., perovskite oxides)

- Stage ID: `S_P005_06`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`

## Goal

Systematically generate vast candidate sets for stability screening

## Input

- element lists (up to Bi)
- perovskite stoichiometric templates (ABO3, A2BB′O6, etc.)
- charge neutrality constraints

## Output

- 4,524,608 unique perovskite oxide candidates

## Datasets used

- Perovskite oxide candidate space — candidate_pool: Enumerating candidate compounds in unexplored composition space (e.g., perovskite oxides)
