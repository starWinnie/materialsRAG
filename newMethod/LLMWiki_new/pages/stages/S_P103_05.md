# Stage: Quasi-static tensile testing to evaluate mechanical properties

- Stage ID: `S_P103_05`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P103_01` — Design of high-strength, high-uniform-elongation α–β titanium alloys resistant to oxygen embrittlement

## Goal

Experimentally measure yield strength (σ0.2), uniform elongation (εUEL), and total elongation (εf) to validate achievement of target properties.

## Input

- annealed Ti-0.45O-4Fe and Ti-0.5O-5Fe specimens
- Instron 5848 machine with 2 kN load cell and non-contact laser extensometer

## Output

- engineering stress–strain curves
- quantified σ0.2, εUEL, εf values

## Datasets used

- Ti-0.45O-4Fe alloy — experimental_validation: Quasi-static tensile testing to evaluate mechanical properties
- Ti-0.5O-5Fe alloy — experimental_validation: Quasi-static tensile testing to evaluate mechanical properties

## Evidence

- P103, PDF page 10, Methods: "Mechanical testing Tensile testing. Quasi-static uniaxial tensile tests were conducted using an Instron 5848 machine equipped with a 2 kN load cell and a non-contact laser extensometer at RT."
- P103, PDF page 1: "The resulting high-strength α–β alloys achieve record uniform elongations: Ti-0.45O-4Fe delivers ≥14% (total: ≥27%) at yield strengths ≥980 MPa, and Ti-0.5O-5Fe achieves ≥13% (total: ≥23%) at yield strengths ≥1075 MPa."
