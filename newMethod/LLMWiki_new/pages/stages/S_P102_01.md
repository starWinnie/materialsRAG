# Stage: pretraining dataset construction (R2CID)

- Stage ID: `S_P102_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P102_01` — crystal structure generation

## Goal

Assemble a comprehensive, curated corpus of materials science knowledge for continued pretraining.

## Input

- over 4 million peer-reviewed articles
- a curated subset of RedPajama
- 470,000 crystallographic information files (CIF)

## Output

- R2CID dataset comprising >30 billion tokens

## Datasets used

- R2CID — source: Assemble a comprehensive, curated corpus of materials science knowledge for continued pretraining.

## Evidence

- P102, PDF page 11, Methods: "Our pretraining dataset, designated R2CID, integrates three distinct components: scientific literature from materials research publications, a curated subset of RedPajama (the original pretraining corpus for LLaMA models), and crystallographic information files (CIF)."
