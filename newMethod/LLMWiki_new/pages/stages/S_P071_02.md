# Stage: Thermal analyses using DSC

- Stage ID: `S_P071_02`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P071_01` — Compositional evolution of Q-phase precipitates in an aluminum alloy

## Goal

Perform thermal analyses to determine the precipitation sequence

## Input

- as-solutionized and aged W319 samples

## Output

- DSC plots revealing precipitation sequence GP zones → Q → Q + h'

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P071, PDF page 3, Methodology: "Thermal analyses of the W319 samples were performed for the as-solutionized condition and after aging at different temperatures. A Mettler-Toledo DSC was used in an argon atmosphere at a heating rate of 10 K min"1."
- P071, PDF page 13, Conclusions: "Our DSC analyses suggest the following precipitation sequence in W319 for the aging conditions examined: GP zones ! Q ! Q + h0."
