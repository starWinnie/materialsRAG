# Stage: Thermal stability and electrical switching validation

- Stage ID: `S_P109_07`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 7
- Task: `T_P109_01` — Enhanced Magnetoelectric Coupling and Thermal Stability in Nd-Doped BiFeO3 Ceramics Near the Morphotropic Phase Boundary

## Goal

To experimentally confirm thermal robustness of ME coupling (300–480 K) and feasibility of nonvolatile, electrically controlled multistate memory operation.

## Input

- Bi0.84Nd0.16FeO3 ceramics
- cryostat
- poling setup
- magnetic field source

## Output

- αME plateau from 300–480 K
- four distinct, switchable ME states under ±E-poling and ±H-bias

## Datasets used

- Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramic composition series — source: Thermal stability and electrical switching validation of ME response

## Evidence

- P109, PDF page 8, 3 Results and Discussion: "The thermal stability of the ME coupling was further examined by tracking the evolution of αME between 80 and 480 K... the αME forms a stable plateau as illustrated in Figure 7b. This wide-temperature stability is essential for practical electronic components..."
- P109, PDF page 8, 3 Results and Discussion: "The potential of these materials for information storage is demonstrated through the electrical control of the ME response. Figure 7c presents the αME - H curves for the ceramics under both positive and negative poling directions. By manipulating the orientation of the initial poling electric field or the bias magnetic field, the ME response can be switched between distinct states. The αME values measured under a specific bias field can be resolved into four clearly separable states."
