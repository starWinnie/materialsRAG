# Stage: Application of destabilization treatment and air cooling

- Stage ID: `S_P089_03`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P089_01` — Optimizing impact abrasive wear resistance of Cr26 high-chromium cast iron via combined heat treatment

## Goal

Induce partial dissolution of M7C3 carbides and redistribution of Cr and C to promote martensite formation during cooling.

## Input

- as-cast specimens

## Output

- specimens with increased martensite fraction and reduced retained austenite

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P089, PDF page 2, Materials and methods: "All specimens requiring heat treatment were first subjected to destabilization treatment at 1050 ◦C for 4 h to promote the dissolution of alloying elements and improve the homogeneity of the as-cast microstructure. Subsequently, the specimens were austenitized at 1040 ◦C for 4 h, followed by air cooling to room temperature."
- P089, PDF page 1, Introduction: "Destabilization treatment induces the partial dissolution of eutectic M7C3 carbides, enabling alloying elements (e.g., Cr and C) to diffuse into the austenitic matrix, which improves the hardenability of austenite and promotes martensite formation during subsequent cooling."
