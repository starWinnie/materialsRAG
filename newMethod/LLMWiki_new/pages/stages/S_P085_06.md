# Stage: 3D tomographic reconstruction of dislocation configuration and slip plane

- Stage ID: `S_P085_06`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P085_01` — Resolving the fundamental behavior of pyramidal ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations in magnesium

## Goal

To experimentally validate the slip planes (pyramidal I and II) and cross-slip events of ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations using 3D structural information.

## Input

- series of TEM images acquired at different tilting angles
- IMOD software
- UCSF Chimera software

## Output

- 3D reconstructed dislocation models
- determination of slip plane for individual dislocations
- evidence of cross-slip

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P085, PDF page 2, Dipole: "We used a series of TEM images to construct 3D tomography to reveal the configuration and slip plane of hc + ai dislocation (fig. S13 and movies S8 and S9). Figure 4 shows 3D tomography of three hc + ai dislocations generated in c-axis compression. ... Dislocation 2 lies on two adjacent pyramidal II planes, indicating that cross-slip occurred."
- P085, PDF page 7, Materials and Methods: "A series of electron tomography images were acquired from large angle tilting between -33° and +33°, with 1° difference between two adjacent tilting angles. These images were aligned by the IMOD software ... then used to construct 3D tomography ... The 3D reconstructed dislocation was visualized by using UCSF Chimera."
