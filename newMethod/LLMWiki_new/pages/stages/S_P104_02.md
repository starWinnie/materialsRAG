# Stage: Fitting Boltzmann function to dilatometric data to extract master sintering curve

- Stage ID: `S_P104_02`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P104_01` — Fast firing of 3 mol% yttria-stabilized zirconia: On the effect of heating rate on sintering

## Goal

Derive the master sintering curve (MSC) by minimizing deviation of experimental Θ(T,t)–ρ data from a Boltzmann fitting function and determining optimal activation energy Q.

## Input

- dilatometric Θ(T,t)–ρ data
- Boltzmann fitting function

## Output

- master sintering curve (MSC)
- activation energy Q = 650 kJ mol−1

## Datasets used

- Dilatometric sintering data for ≈40 nm 3YSZ (TZ-3YB-E) — training: To fit a Boltzmann function and determine optimal activation energy Q for the master sintering curve.

## Evidence

- P104, PDF page 3, 2.2 Master sintering curve calculation: "The MSC was determined by minimizing the deviation of the experimental data in the Θ(T,t) −ρplane from a fitting Boltzmann function."
- P104, PDF page 4, 3.1 Identification of the master sintering curve: "The best result, minimizing the error (inset in Figure 2B), was obtained assuming an activation energy for densification of 650 kJ mol−1."
