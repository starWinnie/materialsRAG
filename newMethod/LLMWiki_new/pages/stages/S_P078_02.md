# Stage: Grafting silane coupling agents onto SiO2 surface

- Stage ID: `S_P078_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P078_01` — Constructing high-polarization silica for polyimide-based nanocomposites with enhanced dielectric permittivity and mechanical performances

## Goal

To chemically modify SiO2 surface with functionalized silane coupling agents

## Input

- SiO2 nanoparticles
- silane coupling agents: (CH3O)Si(CH2)3NH2, (CH3O)SiPh, (CH3O)SiPhCOOH, (CH3O)SiPhNH2, (CH3O)SiPhCOCH3

## Output

- SiO2 nanoparticles grafted with NH2, Ph, PhCOOH, PhNH2, or PhCOCH3 functional groups

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P078, PDF page 3, RESULTS AND DISCUSSION: "According to the chemical reaction mechanism, the methoxyl groups ( OCH3) in the silane coupling agent could be removed after forming methanol with the hydroxyl groups on the surface of the SiO2 nanoparticles. Then, NH2, Ph, PhCOOH, PhNH2, and PhCOCH3 were successfully grafted on the surface of SiO2 nanoparticles, which were shown in Figure 1a."
