# Stage: calculation of applied stress for 100,000-h lifetime (σ100,000h)

- Stage ID: `S_P086_06`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P086_01` — creep-limited lifetime prediction

## Goal

To use the validated parametric models to compute the maximum allowable applied stress at various temperatures that would result in a creep-limited lifetime of exactly 100,000 hours.

## Input

- validated LMP and Wilshire models

## Output

- σ100,000h values at temperatures from 650°C to 875°C

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P086, PDF page 3, Introduction: "The outputs of the analyses are used to calculate the applied stress at which Inconel 740/740H should reach a lifetime of 100,000 h."
- P086, PDF page 20, points: "Table VII list values of σ100,000h for various temperatures between 650 and 875°C, inclusive."
