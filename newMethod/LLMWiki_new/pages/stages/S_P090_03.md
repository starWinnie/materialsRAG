# Stage: sample preparation for characterization

- Stage ID: `S_P090_03`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P090_01` — optimizing densification and mechanical performance of ZrB2–SiC ceramics via spark plasma sintering

## Goal

Prepare sintered samples for subsequent physical and mechanical testing by polishing, etching, and dimensional conditioning.

## Input

- sintered pellets

## Output

- polished cross-sections
- thermally etched surfaces (∼1600°C, 10 min)
- flexural bars (∼3 mm × 4 mm × 25 mm)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P090, PDF page 3, Density Measurement: "Before testing, the samples were polished to remove any surface defects and dried thoroughly."
- P090, PDF page 4, Microstructural Characterization: "Polished cross-sections of the sintered specimens were analyzed [...] To ensure reproducibility and clarity in grain boundary detection, all specimens were etched thermally at ∼ 1600◦C for 10 min under inert conditions prior to SEM observation."
- P090, PDF page 4, Mechanical Properties Testing: "Sintered samples were cut and ground into bars with dimensions suitable for flexural testing (typically ∼ 3 mm × 4 mm × 25 mm).
