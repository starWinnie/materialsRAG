# Stage: Alloy composition design targeting oxygen-mediated <c + a> slip activation and β-phase-mediated slip transfer

- Stage ID: `S_P103_01`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P103_01` — Design of high-strength, high-uniform-elongation α–β titanium alloys resistant to oxygen embrittlement

## Goal

Design Ti–O–Fe compositions satisfying oxygen threshold (≥0.40% O) for <c + a> slip activation and Fe content (~4% Fe) to achieve ~20 vol.% β-phase for slip transfer mediation.

## Input

- oxygen threshold for <c + a> activation (≥0.40% O)
- JMatPro thermodynamic predictions for β-phase fraction
- empirical data on α/β lath thickness ratio in additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V

## Output

- Ti-0.45O-4Fe target composition
- Ti-0.5O-5Fe variant composition

## Datasets used

- Ti-0.45O-4Fe alloy — source: Alloy composition design targeting oxygen-mediated <c + a> slip activation and β-phase-mediated slip transfer
- Ti-0.5O-5Fe alloy — source: Alloy composition design targeting oxygen-mediated <c + a> slip activation and β-phase-mediated slip transfer

## Evidence

- P103, PDF page 8, Methods: "According to our alloy design principle, the oxygen content in the α-Ti phase must be > 0.4% to fully activate the <c + a> slip system48. However, excessive oxygen may still lead to premature failure of the alloy50. Therefore, we choose a total addition of 0.45% O."
- P103, PDF page 8, Methods: "Similarly, according to our alloy design principle, it is crucial to introduce an appropriate amount of β phase as a mediator to promote extensive slip transfer of dislocations across multiple α−β interfaces. Based on JMatPro 7.0 predictions, under equilibrium conditions, the addition of 4% Fe is expected to produce about 20 vol.% β phase at RT. Therefore, our alloy composition is determined to be Ti-0.45O-4Fe."
- P103, PDF page 6: "To further validate our design framework, we increased the O content from 0.45% to 0.50% and the Fe content from 4% to 5% according to our design criteria, targeting the Ti–0.5O–5Fe composition."
