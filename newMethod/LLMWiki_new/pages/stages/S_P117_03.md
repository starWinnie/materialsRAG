# Stage: computing depth-resolved atomic number density and subsurface partitioning

- Stage ID: `S_P117_03`
- Stage type: `candidate_generation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P117_01` — deriving exposure-conditioned hydrogen-availability boundary conditions

## Goal

To convert surface inventory into a mechanics-relevant descriptor by separating exterior adsorbed hydrogen from near-surface lattice hydrogen.

## Input

- atomic coordinates from trajectory dump files
- fixed slab-referenced surface plane (zref = 12.21 Å)

## Output

- adsorbed and subsurface H inventories (NH,ads, NH,sub)
- subsurface fraction (fH,sub)

## Datasets used

- Primary Ni(211) Production Dataset — source: To convert surface inventory into a mechanics-relevant descriptor by separating exterior adsorbed hydrogen from near-surface lattice hydrogen.

## Evidence

- P117, PDF page 4, Depth-Resolved Atomic Density and Subsurface Partitioning: "To convert surface inventory into a mechanics-relevant descriptor, hydrogen in the exterior adsorbed window must be separated from hydrogen that has entered the near-surface lattice. This spatial distribution is quantified by computing the one-dimensional atomic number density, ρi(z), for each species i ∈{Ni, C, H, O} along the surface-normal direction."
