# Stage: Preparation of alumina slurry

- Stage ID: `S_P094_01`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P094_01` — fabricate sub-micron alumina grained ceramics by forming ultra-high-density Al2O3 green bodies through low PF of high solid loading slurries

## Goal

Prepare high solid loading (55 vol%), low-viscosity (0.40 Pa·s) alumina slurry using super-fine α-Al2O3 and CE-64 dispersant

## Input

- α-Al2O3 powder (TM-DAR, d50 = 180 nm)
- Dolapix CE-64 dispersant (1.2 wt%)
- deionized water

## Output

- bubble-free alumina slurry with 55 vol% solid loading and viscosity of 0.40 Pa·s

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P094, PDF page 2, EXPERIMENTAL PROCEDURE: "Alumina slurry preparation followed these steps: First, alumina slurries with solid loadings of 50, 55, 57, and 58 vol% were prepared. For each, Dolapix CE-64 was added at concentrations of 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, and 1.5 wt% to determine the optimal dispersant dosage. Second, the dispersant was mixed with deionized water under mechanical stirring for 1 min. Subsequently, α-Al2O3 powder was then introduced to prepare slurries with different solid loadings for evaluating rheological behavior. Thereafter, the mixtures were milled using a planetary ball mill at 300 rpm for 2 h to ensure uniform particle dispersion. Finally, entrapped air bubbles in the slurry were removed using a vacuum deaerator (ARV-310, Thinky)."
