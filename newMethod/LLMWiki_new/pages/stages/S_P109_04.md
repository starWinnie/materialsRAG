## Goal

To evaluate ferroelectric polarization (P–E loops) and magnetic hysteresis (M–H loops) across compositions and identify optimal candidates for enhanced ME coupling.

## Input

- Bi1-xNdxFeO3 pellets
- ferroelectric analyzer
- vibrating sample magnetometer

## Output

- P–E hysteresis loops showing peak Pr at x = 0.10 and suppression beyond x = 0.16
- M–H loops showing enhanced coercivity and electric-field controllability at x = 0.16
