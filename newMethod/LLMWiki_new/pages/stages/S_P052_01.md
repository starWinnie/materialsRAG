# Stage: aggregating polymer property datasets from experimental, DFT, and MD sources

- Stage ID: `S_P052_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P052_01` — polymer property prediction

## Goal

Collect polymer-property datapoints across multiple measurement modalities and architectures

## Input

- None stated

## Output

- PolyBench26 dataset (~250,000 datapoints)

## Datasets used

- Polymer Benchmark 2026 — source: Aggregating polymer-property datapoints across experimental, DFT, and MD sources to form the core benchmark dataset
- VIPEA — source: Providing DFT-calculated electron affinity and ionization potential data for copolymers
- polyVERSE — source: Providing DFT-calculated electron affinity and ionization potential data for homopolymers
- PolyMetriX — source: Providing experimental glass transition temperature data for homopolymers
- OPoly26 — source: Providing MD-derived property data (density, specific heat, refractive index, radius of gyration) for homopolymers and copolymers

## Evidence

- P052, PDF page 17, Methods: "The Polymer Benchmark 2026 (PolyBench26) aggregates polymer property datasets spanning experimental measurements, density functional theory (DFT) calculations, and molecular dynamics (MD) simulations."
- P052, PDF page 17, Methods: "DFT-calculated electron affinity (EA) and ionization potential (IP) data for homopolymers were obtained from polyVERSE. Copolymer EA and IP values (42,966 for each property) were taken from the VIPEA dataset... Experimental glass transition temperature (Tg) data comes from PolyMetriX. We extracted an additional 155,930 property datapoints from existing OPoly26 MD trajectories."
