# Stage: Post-creep microstructure characterization (TEM/APT)

- Stage ID: `S_P070_07`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 7
- Task: `T_P070_01` — Design of a new Al-0.3Zr-0.2Ce-0.2Cu (wt.%) alloy for additive manufacturing that overcomes the classical tradeoff between conductivity and creep resistance

## Goal

Validate the proposed dislocation-climb suppression mechanism by examining dislocation structures and matrix composition after creep

## Input

- crept specimens (as-fabricated and aged)
- TEM and APT analysis

## Output

- evidence of absence of dislocation climb and subgrain development in as-fabricated alloy
- confirmation of Zr remaining fully in solid solution after creep

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P070, PDF page 21, 4.4.2 Zr-solute-induced dislocation creep suppression: "Post-creep dislocation structures of as-fabricated alloy (Fig. 9e-k) are consistent with the absence of dislocation climb and related subgrain development during creep."
- P070, PDF page 15, 3.4.3 Post-creep microstructures: "The as-fabricated specimen crept to 12% compressive strain was characterized using APT to determine the resultant compositional changes in the matrix interiors. Fig. S7 shows one of the several analyzed APT tips, revealing fine Sn precipitates and no Zr-rich precipitates. All Zr remained fully in solid solution, as the temperature is too low for its precipitation during creep (Table 6)."
