# Stage: analysis of atom trajectories and bond-breaking dynamics

- Stage ID: `S_P097_04`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P097_01` — interpreting field evaporation through dynamic bond-breaking processes

## Goal

Decipher the dynamic origin of preferential Ti evaporation by quantitatively comparing Ti vs. Al trajectories, force components, and bond-breaking sequences.

## Input

- TAPSim-MD output trajectories and forces
- statistical ensembles (3098 Ti, 2871 Al atoms)

## Output

- two-step (Ti) vs. one-step (Al) bond-breaking identification
- critical electrostatic force thresholds (1.8 eV/Å for Ti, 2.2 eV/Å for Al)
- velocity onset analysis

## Datasets used

- TAPSim-MD virtual [001]-oriented TiAl crystalline tip — computational_validation: Enable quantitative analysis of atom trajectories and bond-breaking dynamics by providing the full trajectory output from TAPSim-MD simulation.

## Evidence

- P097, PDF page 4, Results and discussion: "In order to examine the dynamics of the evaporation process, we look at the launch trajectories for Ti and Al, which are distinctly different as shown in Fig. 2(a) and (b): the evaporating Ti atoms initially ascend vertically along the z-direction, while the evaporating Al atoms depart at an angle of approximately 45◦between the z-axis and the xy-plane."
- P097, PDF page 5, Results and discussion: "As for Ti atoms (red lines), the continuous growth of the lateral net force starts around an electrostatic force of 3.2 eV/Å, while the growth of the z-component starts around an electrostatic force of 1.8 eV/Å... In contrast, for Ti, the in-plane and the out-of-plane bond breaking start at distinctly different times, first in the z-direction (breaking 4 Ti-Al bonds at an electrostatic force of ∼1.8 eV/Å), and second in the lateral direction (breaking 2 Ti-Ti bonds at an electrostatic force of ∼3.2 eV/Å)."
