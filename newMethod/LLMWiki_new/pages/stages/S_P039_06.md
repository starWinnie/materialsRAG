# Stage: finetuning the foundation model for ab initio accuracy on specific challenging systems

- Stage ID: `S_P039_06`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P039_01` — developing a foundation machine learning interatomic potential with polarizable long-range interactions

## Goal

to adapt the foundation model to achieve quantum-accurate predictions for targeted systems through efficient finetuning

## Input

- 20% subsets of configurations from Na8/9Cl8+ clusters and Au2 dimers on MgO surfaces
- mean squared force error loss function

## Output

- finetuned model with good agreement with DFT references
- accurately distinguished potential energy surfaces for positive charged clusters and surface conditions

## Datasets used

- Ko et al. charge-state dataset — candidate_pool: to perform targeted finetuning for ab initio accuracy on specific challenging systems
