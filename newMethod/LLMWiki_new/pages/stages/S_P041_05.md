# Stage: evaluating PES accuracy on argyrodite and non-argyrodite test sets

- Stage ID: `S_P041_05`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P041_01` — fine-tuning pretrained universal machine-learning interatomic potentials

## Goal

quantify improvements in energy/force prediction accuracy and softening scales across diverse Li SSEs

## Input

- reEWC-fine-tuned MLIP model
- test sets of argyrodite-type and non-argyrodite-type Li SSEs

## Output

- energy and force MAEs
- softening scales
- element-specific accuracy analysis

## Datasets used

- argyrodite-type test set — benchmark: quantify improvements in energy/force prediction accuracy and softening scales across argyrodite-type Li SSEs
- non-argyrodite-type test set — benchmark: quantify improvements in energy/force prediction accuracy and softening scales across non-argyrodite-type Li SSEs

## Evidence

- P041, PDF page 5, PES evaluation of Li SSEs: "We evaluate the predictive accuracy of the fine-tuned MLIPs across a range of Li SSEs with diverse compositions and structures. This allows us to assess whether the PES predictions can be improved beyond the fine-tuning domain despite the training dataset being limited to LPSC, and thus, evaluate the extent of knowledge transfer enabled by each fine-tuning method."
