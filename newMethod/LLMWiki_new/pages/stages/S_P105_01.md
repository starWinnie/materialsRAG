# Stage: Provenance of Industrially Produced Glasses

- Stage ID: `S_P105_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P105_01` — Immobilization of Plutonium-Contaminated Materials in Calcium Aluminosilicate Glass

## Goal

To obtain samples from industrial trials carried out in a collaboration between Costain Ltd and Tetronics Technologies Ltd at the Tetronics’ pilot plasma operating facility in Swindon, UK.

## Input

- industrial trials
- simulant (half-sized) 100-L PCM waste drums
- nonradioactive surrogates for five different end-member compositions of the PCM inventory

## Output

- industrial glass samples (Inorganic PCM-1 and Organic PCM-2)

## Datasets used

- Industrial PCM Glass Samples — source: To obtain physical samples for characterization of glass homogeneity, structure, and chemical durability.

## Evidence

- P105, PDF page 3, Methodology: "Samples were obtained from industrial trials carried out in a collaboration between Costain Ltd and Tetronics Technologies Ltd at the Tetronics’ pilot plasma operating facility in Swindon, UK [ 9, 10 ]. Trials vitrified simulant (half-sized) 100-L PCM waste drums, supplied by Sellafield, containing nonradioactive surrogates for five different end-member compositions of the PCM inventory: (1) high inorganic, (2) high metal, (3) mixed waste, (4) high organic (PVC), and (5) high organic (wood) (Table S1 ) [ 9, 10 ]. Samples were provided from two of these melts (melts 1 and 5), renamed in this study (Inorganic PCM-1) and (Organic PCM-2)."
