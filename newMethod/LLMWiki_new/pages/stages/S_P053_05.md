# Stage: evaluation of MLIP models on Matbench-Discovery and systematic softening benchmarks

- Stage ID: `S_P053_05`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P053_01` — training Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials (MLIPs) for inorganic materials

## Goal

to assess model performance on thermodynamic stability prediction and systematic softening reduction

## Input

- pretrained and fine-tuned models
- WBM high energy states dataset
- PBE MDR phonon dataset

## Output

- F1 scores, energy-above-hull MAE, softening scales (energy, forces, phonons)

## Datasets used

- WBM high energy states dataset — computational_validation: to evaluate zeroth-order and first-order systematic softening (energy and force underprediction)
- PBE MDR phonon dataset — computational_validation: to evaluate second-order systematic softening (phonon vibrational frequency underprediction)

## Evidence

- P053, PDF page 6, 2.2 OMat24 model evaluations: "We evaluate the performance of MLIPs trained on the OMat24 dataset and compare against models trained on alternative datasets using the popular Matbench-Discovery benchmark29."
- P053, PDF page 7, 2.2.2 Model evaluation of systematic softening: "Building on the work of Deng et al.23, we quantify systematic softening at three levels based on the order of potential energy derivatives used for prediction."
