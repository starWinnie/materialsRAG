# Stage: Measurement of sound velocities and Young’s modulus

- Stage ID: `S_P076_02`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P076_01` — Porosity dependence of sound velocities in partially sintered ceramics

## Goal

To directly measure longitudinal and transverse sound velocities using ultrasonic wave propagation pulse-echo technique (UPT), and Young’s modulus using impulse excitation technique (IET).

## Input

- ceramic samples with known porosity
- UPT equipment (Olympus 38DL Plus)
- IET equipment (RFDA 23)

## Output

- measured Vt and Vl (UPT)
- measured Young’s modulus E (IET)
- derived shear modulus G and Poisson’s ratio ν (for discs) or estimated ν (for bars)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P076, PDF page 4, EXPERIMENTAL: "The sound velocities were measured by the ultrasonic wave propagation pulse-echo technique (UPT) using an advanced thickness gauge Olympus 38DL Plus (Olympus, Japan) with longitudinal wave probe V110-RM (5 MHz, diameter 5 mm) and transverse wave (shear wave) probe V156-RM (5 MHz, diameter 5 mm)."
- P076, PDF page 4, EXPERIMENTAL: "For comparison, the elastic constants were also measured by the impulse excitation technique (IET), using the resonant frequency and damping analyser RFDA 23 (IMCE, Belgium)."
