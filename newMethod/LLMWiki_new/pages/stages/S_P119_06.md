# Stage: Photocatalytic H2-evolution testing

- Stage ID: `S_P119_06`
- Stage type: `experimental_validation`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P119_01` — Band gap narrowing in oxychlorosulfide photocatalysts through aliovalent Cl-to-S anion substitution

## Goal

Validate functional performance as visible-light-responsive photocatalysts for water reduction.

## Input

- Rh-loaded La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x samples
- Na2S/Na2SO3 sacrificial reagents
- visible light irradiation (λ > 400 nm)

## Output

- H2 evolution rates
- wavelength-dependent activity (e.g., comparable rates under λ > 520 nm)
- confirmation of stability and non-corrosive behavior

## Datasets used

- La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x (x = 0, 0.5, 0.75, 1) — experimental_validation: Functional validation as visible-light-responsive photocatalysts for H2 evolution under λ > 400 nm irradiation.

## Evidence

- P119, PDF page 4: "The photocatalytic properties of each La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x (0 ≤x ≤0.75) sample were evaluated using photocatalytic water reduction as a model reaction in the presence of sacrificial electron donors (Na2S/Na2SO3) under visible light irradiation (λ > 400 nm). Rh (1 wt %) was loaded onto the samples via in situ photodeposition... All samples functioned as stable H2-evolving photocatalysts after an induction period of 1–2 h... Among the La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x samples, the x = 0 sample exhibited the highest H2 evolution rate... Under λ > 520 nm irradiation, the H2 evolution rates of these two samples became comparable... These results indicate that band gap narrowing through Cl-to-S substitution improves the intrinsic long-wavelength photoresponse."
