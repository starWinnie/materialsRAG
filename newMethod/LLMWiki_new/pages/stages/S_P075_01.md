# Stage: Sample preparation

- Stage ID: `S_P075_01`

- Task: `T_P075_01` — Measuring the onset of sintering using laser ultrasonics

## Goal

Produce alumina pellets with controlled green density and subject them to partial sintering at defined temperatures to generate a range of sintering states

## Input

- alumina powder (A15SG, Almatis, D50 = 1.7 μm)
- carbide die and hardened steel punches
- hydraulic press
- furnaces (tube and box)

## Output

- alumina pellets partially sintered at temperatures from 130°C to 1600°C

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P075, PDF page 3, 2.1 Sample preparation: "Samples were prepared by uniaxially pressing alumina powder with D50 of 1.7 μm as per the manufacturer (A15SG, Almatis) into a pellet at a pressure of 35.0 ± 0.1 MPa with a carbide die and hardened steel punches using a hydraulic press (Model# 3912, Carver)."
- P075, PDF page 3, 2.1 Sample preparation: "Pellets were then placed in a furnace and heated to different dwell temperatures ranging from 130◦C to 1600◦C, using a 10◦C/min ramp rate up and down, and a 1-h dwell time at temperature."
