# Stage: High-throughput screening with progressive criteria

- Stage ID: `S_P038_05`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P038_01` — Rational design of nanoscale stabilized oxide catalysts for OER

## Goal

Screen candidates using a set of progressive criteria including Pourbaix stability, Wulff shape analysis, overpotential assessment, energy above hull, and material cost.

## Input

- interpolated database
- Pourbaix decomposition energy (EPBX)
- Wulff shape
- overpotential (η)
- energy above hull (Ehull)
- material cost

## Output

- 122 and 68 viable candidates for OER under bulk and nanoscale regime respectively

## Datasets used

- Interpolated OC22 oxide database — screening: Screen candidates using progressive criteria including Pourbaix stability, Wulff shape analysis, overpotential assessment, energy above hull, and material cost.

## Evidence

- P038, PDF page 6, Results and discussion: "Fig. 5 summarizes the selection criteria that we employed to screen for candidate electrocatalysts for OER. The first criteria in our high-throughput screening framework was to determine if a material exists in the original training dataset of OC22. ... The second criterion describes the Pourbaix stability, i.e., the electrochemical stability of a material in aqueous environment. ... The third selection criterion ... assesses the surface energy ... to construct the Wulff shape ... The fourth criterion assesses the overpotential ... The fifth criterion assesses the thermodynamic stability ... The final criterion assesses the material cost ..."
