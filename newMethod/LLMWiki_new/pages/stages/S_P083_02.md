# Stage: Equilibrating semicrystalline MPE systems via MD simulation

- Stage ID: `S_P083_02`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P083_01` — Enhancing the degradability of polyethylene (PE) without compromising its mechanical integrity

## Goal

Generate equilibrated semicrystalline structures of MPE systems by energy minimization, heating, controlled cooling, and final equilibration to confirm thermodynamic equilibrium before mechanical testing.

## Input

- initial MPE chain structures
- General Amber Force Field (GAFF)
- GROMACS software

## Output

- equilibrated semicrystalline MPE systems

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P083, PDF page 2, CLASSICAL MOLECULAR DYNAMICS SIMULATIONS: "Initially, a single MPE chain was energy minimized and then equilibrated in a vacuum at 300 K in the isothermal (NVT) ensemble within a cubic box of 100 Å for 100 ps. Subsequently, we randomly packed 316 MPE chains into a simulation box of 200 Å. After energy minimization to adjust atomic coordinates, the systems were equilibrated at 500 K for 100 ns and then cooled at a rate of 0.5 K/ns from 500 to 300 K. Final equilibration was performed at 300 K for 100 ns. Equilibrium was confirmed by monitoring the convergence of key system observables—namely the total potential energy, density, radius of gyration and crystallinity —over the final 100 ns of equilibration."
