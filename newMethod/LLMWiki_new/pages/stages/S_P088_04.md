- Stage ID: `S_P088_04`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 4

## Goal

prepare samples for microstructural characterization by grinding, mounting, polishing, and etching

## Input

- as-cast HCWCI alloys

## Output

- mounted, ground, polished, and etched metallographic samples

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P088, PDF page 2, Materials and experimental methods: "For microstructural examination, HCWCI alloys were prepared using standard metallographic techniques following the ASTM E3 [14]. The sections were mounted in PolyFast resin and sequentially ground with silicon carbide paper (from 300 to 1200 grit) and polished with 1 μm diamond paste. The polished surfaces were etched with a 5 wt% Nital solution, prepared by mixing nitric acid in ethanol."
