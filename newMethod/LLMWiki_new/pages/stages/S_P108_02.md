# Stage: Molten-salt-assisted synthesis of HEC powders

- Stage ID: `S_P108_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P108_01` — Carbon Stoichiometry Regulation of High-Entropy Carbide–Silicon Carbide Composites

## Goal

Synthesize carbon-deficient and carbon-rich high-entropy carbide powders with controlled stoichiometry

## Input

- Ti, Ta, Nb, V, C powders
- KCl–NaCl molten salt
- ball-milling equipment

## Output

- (Ti0.25 Ta0.25 Nb0.25 V0.25)Cx powders (x = 0.8, 1.0, 1.2)

## Datasets used

- (Ti0.25 Ta0.25 Nb0.25 V0.25)Cx high-entropy carbide powders — source: Synthesis of carbon-deficient and carbon-rich high-entropy carbide powders with controlled stoichiometry

## Evidence

- P108, PDF page 2, Materials and Methods: "(Ti0.25 Ta0.25 Nb0.25 V0.25 )Cx ( x = 0.8, 1.0, and 1.2) HEC powders were prepared using a molten-salt-assisted synthesis route. Stoichiometric amounts of raw powders were mixed with salts at a powder-to-salt mass ratio of 1:10 and ball-milled in alumina jars with alumina balls at 400 rpm for 30 min. The mixtures were then heated to 1400◦C at 3◦C/min and maintained for 1 h under an Ar atmosphere (Figure 1 ). The synthesized powders were purified by washing with deionized water and ethanol to eliminate residual salts, followed by drying (Figure 2 )."
