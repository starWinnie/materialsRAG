# Stage: Synthesis of randomly cross-linked single networks

- Stage ID: `S_P095_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P095_01` — Investigate the effect of cross-link homogeneity on the high-strain behavior of elastic polymer networks

## Goal

To prepare randomly cross-linked poly(n-butyl acrylate) networks with controlled target molecular weights between cross-links (Mx) of 5–30 kg/mol.

## Input

- n-butyl acrylate (monomer)
- hexanediol diacrylate (cross-linker)
- benzoyl peroxide (initiator)
- toluene (solvent)

## Output

- randomly cross-linked single networks (RANx)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P095, PDF page 3, EXPERIMENTAL METHODS: "Randomly cross-linked networks were synthesized by free-radical polymerization of n-butyl acrylate and hexanediol diacrylate."
- P095, PDF page 3, RESULTS: "Randomly cross-linked networks with target molecular weights between cross-links (Mx) of 5−30 kg/mol were synthesized using thermally initiated free-radical polymerization."
