# Stage: Quantification of Si deposition via EDS

- Stage ID: `S_P091_03`
- Stage type: `computational_validation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P091_01` — Postsynthetic modification of yttria-stabilized zirconia aerogels with silica coatings for enhanced thermal stability

## Goal

Validate the successful deposition and quantify the amount of Si on the aerogel surface.

## Input

- SiO2-coated YSZ aerogels

## Output

- Atomic ratio of Zr:Y:Si

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P091, PDF page 4, RESULTS: "The amount of Si deposited on the aerogel was quantified with EDS for 30YSZ. Attempts to use inductively coupled plasma - optical emission spectroscopy were unsuccessful in quantifying the silicon content accurately. The average atomic ratio of Zr:Y:Si in 30YSZ–SiO2 was 1.00:0.55:3.01 with no discernable dependence of composition on location."
