# Stage: Synthesis of W/Mn-co-doped NBT ceramics

- Stage ID: `S_P110_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P110_01` — Synergistically Enhanced Piezoelectricity in W/Mn Co-Doped NBT Ceramics

## Goal

Prepare a series of Na0.5Bi4.5Ti4−2xWxMnxO15 (NBTWM100x) ceramic samples with controlled dopant concentrations (x = 0, 0.01, 0.03, 0.05, 0.07)

## Input

- Na2CO3 (99.8%)
- TiO2 (98%)
- Bi2O3 (99.99%)
- WO3 (99.99%)
- MnCO3 (99.95%)
- anhydrous ethanol
- polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) binder

## Output

- NBTWM100x ceramic pellets (disk-shaped, 13 mm diameter, ~1 mm thickness)

## Datasets used

- NBTWM100x ceramic series — source: Synthesis of W/Mn-co-doped NBT ceramics via solid-state reaction

## Evidence

- P110, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedure: "Na0.5 Bi4.5 Ti4-2 x Wx Mnx O15 ( x = 0, 0.01, 0.03, 0.05, and 0.07, NBTWM100 x ) ceramics were prepared using a conventional solid-state reaction method. Raw materials, including Na2 CO3 (99.8%), TiO2 (98%), Bi2 O3 (99.99%), WO3 (99.99%), and MnCO3 (99.95%), were weighed according to stoichiometric ratios and then subjected to planetary ball milling in anhydrous ethanol for 12 h."
