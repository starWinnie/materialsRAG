# Stage: synchrotron X-ray computed tomography during in-situ tensile loading

- Stage ID: `S_P079_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P079_01` — A dataset of micro-scale tomograms of unidirectional glass fibre/epoxy and carbon fibre/epoxy composites acquired via synchrotron computed tomography during in-situ tensile loading

## Goal

Acquire 3D tomographic projections of composite specimens while under controlled tensile load.

## Input

- double-edge notched glass/epoxy and carbon/epoxy specimens
- in-situ tensile loading rig with displacement control (~1.5 μm/s)
- synchrotron X-ray source (20 kV, 60 mm propagation distance, 1000 projections, 9 ms exposure)

## Output

- 3D projection datasets for each loading step (~0%, 40%, 73%, 95% failure load)

## Datasets used

- Micro-scale tomograms of unidirectional glass/epoxy and carbon/epoxy composites under in-situ tensile loading — source: Source data acquired via synchrotron X-ray computed tomography during in-situ tensile loading
