# Stage: material representation encoding

- Stage ID: `S_P015_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P015_01` — extrapolative prediction of material properties

## Goal

To convert chemical structures into numerical descriptors suitable for model input.

## Input

- polymer repeating unit
- perovskite crystal structure

## Output

- 2048-dimensional Morgan fingerprint vector
- 32-dimensional latent vector from MPNN

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P015, PDF page 10, Methods: "The MPNN44 was employed as the encoder for the crystal structures in all models. The MPNN architecture was designed in a manner similar to that proposed by Na and Park42, with an embedding size of 32."
