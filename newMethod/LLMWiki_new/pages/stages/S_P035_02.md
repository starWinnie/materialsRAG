# Stage: Evaluate zero-shot fMLIP formation energies on DFT geometries

- Stage ID: `S_P035_02`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P035_01` — Correcting DFT formation energies towards experimental accuracy

## Goal

Assess improvement in formation energy accuracy using foundational MLIPs (PET-OMATPES) without structural relaxation

## Input

- DFT-relaxed geometries (PBEsol)
- PET-OMATPES r2SCAN MLIP

## Output

- zero-shot r2SCAN formation energies
- reduced MAE (e.g., >40% improvement)

## Datasets used

- Materials Cloud three-dimensional crystals database — source: Provide DFT-relaxed geometries (PBEsol) for zero-shot evaluation of PET-OMATPES r2SCAN MLIP

## Evidence

- P035, PDF page 3, RESULTS: "Fig. 2b shows that zero-shot formation energies [62] from the PET-OMATPES model [63, 64]—one of the top-performing models in community benchmarks at the time of publication [65], trained on the OMAT [66] and MATPES [37] datasets at the r2SCAN level—reduce the MAE by more than 40% when evaluated at the DFT (GGA) relaxed geometry."
