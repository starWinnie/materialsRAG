# Stage: Rare-earth doping feasibility screening

- Stage ID: `S_P113_06`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 6
- Task: `T_P113_01` — Solid Solution in BaS–La2S3 Ceramics Produced by Mechanosynthesis and Their Potential for Rare-Earth Doping

## Goal

Assess whether rare-earth ions (Dy3+) can be incorporated into the solid solution matrix without phase separation.

## Input

- 15 BaS–85 La2S3 base powder
- Dy2S3 dopant (1 and 6 mol%)

## Output

- Dy-doped XRD patterns (Figure 8)
- absence of orthorhombic Dy2S3 peaks (26°, 35°)
- unit cell parameter decrease with Dy content

## Datasets used

- 15 BaS–85 La2S3 doped with 1 and 6 mol% Dy2S3 — screening: Assess rare-earth doping feasibility by evaluating phase separation and unit cell parameter shift upon Dy incorporation

## Evidence

- P113, PDF page 4, Rare Earth Solubility: "Figure 8 shows the XRD pattern of 15 BaS–85 La2 S3 doped with 1 and 6 mol% Dy2 S3 . A single phase is present for all compositions, therefore suggesting that Dy2 S3 is effectively solubilizes in the 15 BaS–85 La2 S3 matrix. In particular, no peaks are present at 26◦or 35◦, which would indicate the presence of orthorhombic Dy2 S3 (Figure 9 )."
