# Stage: Analysis of recrystallization mechanisms

- Stage ID: `S_P099_04`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P099_01` — Investigation of isothermal continuous hot deformation behavior

## Goal

To identify and distinguish the dominant recrystallization mechanisms (DDRX, CDRX, SRX) and their dependence on deformation parameters.

## Input

- EBSD data (KAM, GOS, IPF)
- TEM observations
- stress-strain curves

## Output

- identification of DDRX as main DRX mechanism
- confirmation of SRX dominance during holding
- evidence of PSN mechanism

## Datasets used

- as-cast alumina-forming austenitic (AFA) alloy — source: To identify and distinguish the dominant recrystallization mechanisms (DDRX, CDRX, SRX) and their dependence on deformation parameters.

## Evidence

- P099, PDF page 1, Abstract: "The main mechanism of DRX is discontinuous DRX (DDRX). The continuous DRX (CDRX) is less occurring. Static recrystallization (SRX) occurs during the isothermal holding time between deformation passes."
- P099, PDF page 13, Conclusion: "Discontinuous DRX (DDRX) is the main mechanism for DRX during continuous isothermal deformation. The continuous DRX (CDRX) also exists but is developing slowly. When the segregated phase particles are distributed in bands, the static recrystallization (SRX) grains will produce microstructures with approximate orientations of incomplete recrystallization due to the particle-stimulated nucleation (PSN) mechanism."
