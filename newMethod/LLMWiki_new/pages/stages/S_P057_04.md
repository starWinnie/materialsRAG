# Stage: latent diffusion model training

- Stage ID: `S_P057_04`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 4
- Task: `T_P057_01` — crystal property prediction

## Goal

train latent diffusion model to refine latent representations by learning the distribution of VAE-encoded latents

## Input

- latent representations Z from VAE encoder

## Output

- refined latent representations
- pretrained LDM denoiser Fθ

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P057, PDF page 4, 3.2.2. LATENT DIFFUSION MODEL (LDM): "The VAE encoder Eφ projects crystal materials into a smoother, lower-dimensional latent space. To further enhance these latent representations, we apply a diffusion model over this space. ... We train a denoising network Fθ to approximate the conditional vector field ut(Zt | Z1). The network takes as input the intermediate noisy latent Zt along with the time step t and predicts the corresponding clean latent representation as:  ̄Z1 = Fθ(Zt, t)."
