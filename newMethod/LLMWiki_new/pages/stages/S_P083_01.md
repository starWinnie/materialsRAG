# Stage: Building initial MPE chain structures with functional groups

- Stage ID: `S_P083_01`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P083_01` — Enhancing the degradability of polyethylene (PE) without compromising its mechanical integrity

## Goal

Construct atomistic models of modified polyethylene chains containing keto, ester, or amide functional groups evenly distributed at a functional-group-to-ethylene-unit ratio of 0.04.

## Input

- polyethylene chain with 100 ethylene repeating units
- keto, ester, or amide functional groups
- functional-group-to-ethylene-unit ratio of 0.04

## Output

- initial MPE chain structures

## Datasets used

- None stated

- P083, PDF page 2, CLASSICAL MOLECULAR DYNAMICS SIMULATIONS: "The initial structures of MPE chains were built using Avogadro software.31 The polyethylene chain contains 100 ethylene repeating units, sufficient to establish intermolecular entangle- ments. MPE chains contain different in-chain functional groups, with the ratio between ethylene groups and functional groups set at 0.04, and the functional groups evenly distributed (see Figure S1)."
