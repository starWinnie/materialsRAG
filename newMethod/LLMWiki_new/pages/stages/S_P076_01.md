# Stage: Preparation and characterization of partially sintered ceramic samples

- Stage ID: `S_P076_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P076_01` — Porosity dependence of sound velocities in partially sintered ceramics

## Goal

To prepare alumina, zirconia, ATZ, and mullite ceramics with controlled porosities via partial sintering and measure their bulk densities.

## Input

- commercial powders (alumina, zirconia, ATZ, mullite)
- uniaxial pressing
- conventional firing or spark plasma sintering (SPS)

## Output

- bar- or disc-shaped ceramic samples
- bulk density ρ
- total porosity φ calculated via φ = 1 − ρ/ρ0

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P076, PDF page 3, EXPERIMENTAL: "In this work various types of partially sintered and fully dense ceramics were prepared by conventional firing or spark plasma sintering (SPS). Alumina, zirconia, and alumina—zirconia composite ceramics were prepared from commercial powders by uniaxial pressing followed by conventional firing."
- P076, PDF page 4, EXPERIMENTAL: "The samples were characterized by the Archimedes technique to determine the bulk density ρ, from which the total porosity φ was calculated via the theoretical densities ρ0 (see Table 2) according to the following equation: φ = 1 − ρ/ρ0."
