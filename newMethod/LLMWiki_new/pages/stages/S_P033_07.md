## Goal

- Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset — screening: Input for SHAP-based feature importance analysis to identify key chemical components influencing bandgap

## Evidence

- P033, PDF page 2, Build model: "Through the means of ML explanation (SHapley Additive exPlanations, abbreviated as SHAP), it is found that the contents of B-site metal and X-site halogen ion have the key influence on the bandgaps of ABX3-type perovskites."
- P033, PDF page 4, four: "As shown in Fig. 6(a) and Fig. 6(b), the density scatter plots show all samples. The order of the features is in descending order of the average absolute value of the SHAP, which is the most important feature for the model."
- P033, PDF page 4, four: "Although the algorithm models and their parameters are different, we can see from the common features of Fig. 6(a) and (b) that the main influence of the eight compositions on ABX3-type perovskites is Cation B and Halogen X, among which the I has the greatest influence, followed by Pb, and the least influence is the element at Cation A."
