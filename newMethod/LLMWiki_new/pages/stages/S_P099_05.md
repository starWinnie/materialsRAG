## Goal

To correlate observed microstructural changes (grain refinement, segregated phase breakdown, texture evolution) with specific experimental parameters (temperature, strain, holding time).

## Input

- microstructural data
- deformation parameters table (Table 2)

## Output

- quantitative relationships: e.g., 'increase in deformation temperature contributes significantly to DRX'
- 'SRX becomes more and more apparent with temperature and the holding time'

## Datasets used

- as-cast alumina-forming austenitic (AFA) alloy — source: To correlate observed microstructural changes (grain refinement, segregated phase breakdown, texture evolution) with specific experimental parameters (temperature, strain, holding time).

## Evidence

- P099, PDF page 1, Abstract: "The increase in deformation temperature contributes significantly to dynamic recrystallization (DRX), and the main mechanism of DRX is discontinuous DRX (DDRX). The continuous DRX (CDRX) is less occurring. Static recrystallization (SRX) occurs during the isothermal holding time between deformation passes. SRX becomes more and more apparent with temperature and the holding time."
- P099, PDF page 10, Microtextural evolution: "The effect of 1200 °C/30 min and 1200 °C/10 min process parameters on the microstructure of the alloy is mainly reflected in the difference in the SRX degree during the isothermal holding period."
