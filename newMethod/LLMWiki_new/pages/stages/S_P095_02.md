# Stage: Synthesis of tetrafunctional star polymers

- Stage ID: `S_P095_02`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P095_01` — Investigate the effect of cross-link homogeneity on the high-strain behavior of elastic polymer networks

## Goal

To prepare bromine-functionalized tetrafunctional poly(n-butyl acrylate) star polymers as precursors for regular networks.

## Input

- n-butyl acrylate (monomer)
- pentaerythritol tetrakis(2-bromoisobutyrate) (tetrafunctional initiator)
- CuBr2 (deactivator)
- activated copper wire (catalyst)
- tris[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]amine (ligand)
- anhydrous dimethyl sulfoxide (solvent)

## Output

- bromine-terminated tetrafunctional poly(n-butyl acrylate) star polymers

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P095, PDF page 3, EXPERIMENTAL METHODS: "Bromine-functionalized poly(n-butyl acrylate) star polymers were synthesized using a single-electron transfer living radical polymerization (SET-LRP)."
