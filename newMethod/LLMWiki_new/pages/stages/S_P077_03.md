## Goal

Quantify density, phase composition, microstructure, and mechanical properties of sintered samples.

## Input

- sintered samples

## Output

- density values
- α/β phase ratios
- grain size and aspect ratio
- Vickers hardness
- fracture toughness

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P077, PDF page 2, 2.3 Characterizations: "The density of the sintered samples was determined via the Archimedes principle. [...] Phase composition analysis was performed using an X-ray diffractometer (XRD; D8 Advance, Bruker, Germany) utilizing Cu-Kα radiation. The microstructural evolution of the samples was revealed by scanning electron microscopy (SEM; Merlin, Zeiss, Germany). Quantitative microstructural analysis was performed by measuring the dimensions of over 500 distinct grains from the SEM micrographs using ImageJ software to determine the grain size distribution. Mechanical properties, including Vickers hardness and fracture toughness, were evaluated using a Vickers hardness tester (TH700, Beijing TIME High Technology Ltd., China)."
