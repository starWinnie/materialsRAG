# Stage: Molecular dynamics simulation execution

- Stage ID: `S_P100_03`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P100_01` — Grain boundary-induced plasticity during thin film buckling

## Goal

Apply compressive strain incrementally and run MD simulations to observe plasticity initiation and evolution at the GB.

## Input

- Strain increments of −0.01% from ε_a = −1.3% to ε_a = −3%
- NVT thermostat at T = 300 K
- Clamping conditions at regions 0 and 3

## Output

- Time-resolved atomic configurations showing dislocation nucleation, glide, and stacking fault formation

## Datasets used

- Al thin film molecular dynamics simulation system with Σ51 (551) grain boundary — training: Execute molecular dynamics simulations under incremental compressive strain to observe dislocation nucleation and evolution

## Evidence

- P100, PDF page 2, 2. Numerical details: "The strain is applied along (Ox) with increments of −0.01% from εa= −1.3%, assumed to be lower than the critical strain for buckling (Hutchinson et al., 1992; Hutchinson and Suo, 1991a; Freund and Suresh, 2004), to εa= −3%. The edges of the film are clamped after application of the strain by imposing no further displacement for all the atoms in regions 0 and 3. At each strain step, a molecular dynamics is performed during 10 ps using a NVT thermostat at T= 300 K."
