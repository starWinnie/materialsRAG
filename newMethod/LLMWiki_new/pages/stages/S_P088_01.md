acquire a systematic compositional matrix of twelve as-cast alloys spanning 13.2–30.8 wt% Cr and 1.95–3.96 wt% C (Cr/C = 4.19–15.32; CVF = 22.5–44.2%)

## Input

- compositional targets

## Output

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P088, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "A systematic compositional matrix of twelve as-cast alloys (13.2–30.8 wt% Cr; 1.95–3.96 wt% C) was designed to elucidate the combined influence of chromium-to-carbon ratio (Cr/C = 4.19–15.32) and carbide volume fraction (22.5–44.2%) on microstructure and multi-property response."
- P088, PDF page 2, Materials and experimental methods: "The HCWCI alloys were produced in a foundry laboratory set-up at the University of Johannesburg in South Africa. The compositions were selected to ensure variations in the %CVF and Cr/C ratio."
