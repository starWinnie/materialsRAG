# Stage: Laboratory-Made Glass Samples

- Stage ID: `S_P105_02`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P105_01` — Immobilization of Plutonium-Contaminated Materials in Calcium Aluminosilicate Glass

## Goal

To prepare four series of small-scale laboratory glasses (a total of 15 glasses) based on the average composition of the CAS glass from the 2019 Tetronics industrial trials.

## Input

- base CAS glass composition
- waste additions (Ce, Hf, metal, PVC)

## Output

- laboratory-made glass samples (CAS0, Ce-series, Hf-series, M-series, PVC-series)

## Datasets used

- Laboratory-Made CAS Glass Series — source: To prepare physical samples for exploring the solubility limits of individual waste components (Ce, Hf, metal, PVC) in CAS glass.

## Evidence

- P105, PDF page 3, Methodology: "Four series of small-scale laboratory glasses (a total of 15 glasses) were made based on the average composition of the CAS glass from the 2019 Tetronics industrial trials: CaO (32 wt %/ 36.0 mol %), Al2 O3 (17.0 wt %/10.5 mol %), SiO2 (51 wt %/ 53.5 mol %), and with compositions adjusted to accommodate increasing concentrations of waste components. Waste additions were Ce, Hf, metal, and PVC."
