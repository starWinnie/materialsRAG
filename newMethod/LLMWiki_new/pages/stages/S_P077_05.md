# Stage: Phase Transformation and Microstructure Screening

- Stage ID: `S_P077_05`
- Stage type: `candidate_screening`
- Stage order: 5
- Task: `T_P077_01` — Low-Temperature Densification of Si3N4 Ceramics With MnO as a Sintering Additive

## Goal

Screen sintering conditions that enable continuous tuning of α/β phase ratio and associated microstructural evolution.

## Input

- XRD patterns (Figure 1)
- SEM images (Figures 3–6)
- phase content data from Table 1

## Output

- tunable α/β phase ratio (e.g., 10% to 100% β-Si3N4)
- microstructural transition from equiaxed α-grains to elongated β-grains

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P077, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "The α–β phase transformation was found to be highly sensitive to the sintering conditions, enabling continuous tuning of the α/ β phase ratio over a wide range. This controlled phase transformation resulted in pronounced microstructural changes, from fine equiaxed α- Si3 N4 grains to elongated columnar β-Si3 N4 grains, leading to a broad and tunable hardness-fracture toughness range."
- P077, PDF page 3, 3.2 Phase Composition: "By adjusting sintering parameters, the β-phase content can be flexibly controlled. Under a constant holding time of 20 min, the β-Si3N4 content increases significantly from 50% in the 1600-100-20 sample to 100% in the 1700-100-20 sample. At a constant temperature of 1600◦C, extending the holding time from 5 to 20 min raised the β-Si3N4 content from 10% in the 1600-100-5 sample to 50% in the 1600-100-20 sample."
