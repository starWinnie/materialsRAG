## Goal

Determine peak-aging condition and produce aged specimens

## Input

- as-fabricated AM Al-Zr-Ce-Cu alloy specimen

## Output

- peak-aged specimens
- two-step aged specimens

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P070, PDF page 4, 2. Experimental Procedures: "Isothermal and isochronal aging studies were conducted to determine the peak-aging condition by measuring alloy hardness and conductivity. Isochronal aging was performed in the temperature range of 150-550 °C, with a step size of 25 °C and a dwell time of 3 h at each temperature. Isothermal aging was conducted at 375 °C at various times up to 100 h."
