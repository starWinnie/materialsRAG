# Stage: Building molecular models of SiO2 nanoparticles and polyimide chains

- Stage ID: `S_P078_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P078_01` — Constructing high-polarization silica for polyimide-based nanocomposites with enhanced dielectric permittivity and mechanical performances

## Goal

To construct atomistic models of nano-SiO2 and polyimide for simulation

## Input

- bulk α-quartz
- 4,40-diamino diphenyl ether and pyromellitic dianhydride repeat unit

## Output

- spherical SiO2 nanoparticles (8 Å radius) capped with H atoms
- PI chains with degree of polymerization 15

## Datasets used

- bulk α-quartz — source: To build spherical SiO2 nanoparticles by trimming
- 4,40-diamino diphenyl ether and pyromellitic dianhydride repeat unit — source: To construct PI molecular chains

## Evidence

- P078, PDF page 2, MOLECULAR SIMULATION: "The nano-SiO2 nanoparticles could be built by trimming from bulk α-quartz in the form of a sphere. SiO2 nanoparticles were spherical with a radius of 8 Å. The bonds which were cleaved at the shape boundaries of the SiO2 nanoparticles were capped with hydrogen atoms."
- P078, PDF page 3, RESULTS AND DISCUSSION: "The repeat unit of the PI chain was composed of the 4,40-diamino diphenyl ether and pyromellitic dianhydride. The degree of polymerization of each PI molecular chain was 15."
