# Stage: spark plasma sintering (SPS) processing

- Stage ID: `S_P090_02`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 2
- Task: `T_P090_01` — optimizing densification and mechanical performance of ZrB2–SiC ceramics via spark plasma sintering

## Goal

Consolidate powder blends into dense ceramic pellets by systematically varying SPS parameters (temperature, pressure, dwell time) to explore their isolated and combined effects.

## Input

- ZSW and ZSZ powders
- graphite die and punches
- argon atmosphere

## Output

- sintered ZrB2–SiC pellets (30 mm diameter, 3–5 mm thickness)
- sample codes reflecting milling method and sintering parameters (e.g., ZSW.1–ZSW.7, ZSZ.1–ZSZ.7)

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P090, PDF page 3, Materials and Methods: "Samples were heated at a constant rate of 100◦C/min to target temperatures ranging from 1950 to 2100◦C, monitored using a thermocouple embedded in the graphite die wall near the sample. Pressure was applied uniaxially at two levels: 50 and 65 MPa. Holding times at maximum temperature were set at either 15 or 60 min."
