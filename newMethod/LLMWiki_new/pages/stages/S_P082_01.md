# Stage: Fiber surface characterization using SEM

- Stage ID: `S_P082_01`
- Stage type: `data_acquisition`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P082_01` — Understanding the fiber–matrix interface properties governing the fiber-direction compressive strength improvement of the hybrid HM CFRPs

## Goal

To examine and compare the surface topology of IM and HM carbon fibers.

## Input

- IM carbon fiber MR70
- HM carbon fiber HS40

## Output

- SEM images revealing longitudinal grooves on IM fiber surfaces and smoother surfaces on HM fiber surfaces

## Datasets used

- None stated

- P082, PDF page 3, 2. IM and HM Carbon Fibers: "To investigate the microstructure of IM and HM fibers as a potential factor affecting the interface shear-strength measurements, the fiber surface was characterized using SEM."
- P082, PDF page 4, unknown: "Figure 2a,b shows well-defined longitudinal grooves on the surfaces of the IM fiber. Unlike the ridged surface of the IM fibers, reflecting the structure of the polymeric precursor caused by the wet spinning process during carbon fiber manufacturing, HM fibers have a relatively smooth surface and longitudinal grooves are less noticeable, as shown in Figure 2c,d."
