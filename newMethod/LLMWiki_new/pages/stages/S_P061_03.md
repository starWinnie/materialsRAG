# Stage: training of MEGNet models for multiple properties

- Stage ID: `S_P061_03`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P061_01` — accurate property prediction in both molecules and crystals

## Goal

Train MEGNet models on molecular and crystal data to predict target properties

## Input

- graph representations
- target property labels (e.g., formation energy, band gap)

## Output

- trained MEGNet models for U0, U, H, G, Ef, Eg, KVRH, GVRH, metal/nonmetal classification

## Datasets used

- QM9 — training: Training MEGNet models for multiple molecular properties including internal energy, enthalpy, Gibbs free energy, band gap, and others
- Materials Project — training: Training MEGNet models for crystal formation energy
- MP-crystals-2018.6.1 band gap subset — training: Training MEGNet models for band gap regression
- MP-crystals-2018.6.1 elasticity subset — training: Training MEGNet models for elastic moduli prediction
