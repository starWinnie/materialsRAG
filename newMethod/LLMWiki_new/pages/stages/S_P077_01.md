# Stage: Powder Preparation

- Stage ID: `S_P077_01`
- Stage type: `data_preparation`
- Stage order: 1
- Task: `T_P077_01` — Low-Temperature Densification of Si3N4 Ceramics With MnO as a Sintering Additive

## Goal

Prepare homogeneous mixture of starting powders for sintering.

## Input

- α-Si3N4 powder
- MnO powder
- Al2O3 powder
- Si3N4 balls
- anhydrous ethanol

## Output

- mixed slurry
- dried and sieved powder mixture

## Datasets used

- None stated

## Evidence

- P077, PDF page 2, 2.1 Powder Preparation: "The initial powders were mixtures of α-Si3N4 (SN-E10, UBE Industries Ltd., Japan), MnO (purity > 99.9%, Sinopharm Chemical Reagent Co., Ltd., China), and Al2O3 (purity > 99.9%, Sinopharm Chemical Reagent Co., Ltd., China). The starting composition was fixed at a weight ratio of Si3N4 : MnO: Al2O3 = 93: 6: 1. The powders were mixed by wet ball milling for 2 h at 300 r/min with Si3N4 balls, and anhydrous ethanol was used as the mixing medium. Then, the slurry was fully dried and passed through an 80-mesh sieve."
