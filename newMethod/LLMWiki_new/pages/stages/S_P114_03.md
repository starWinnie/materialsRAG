# Stage: Expanding plug test for hoop strength measurement

- Stage ID: `S_P114_03`
- Stage type: `model_evaluation`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P114_01` — Effect of Steam Oxidation on Microstructure and Hoop Strength of Duplex SiCf /SiC Tube

## Goal

Characterize the residual hoop strength of the SiCf/SiC tube after steam oxidation.

## Input

- oxidized SiCf/SiC tube specimens

## Output

- hoop strength values (MPa)
- load–displacement curves
- fracture morphology

## Datasets used

- SiCf/SiC tube oxidized at 600°C — source: Subject for expanding plug test to measure residual hoop strength
- SiCf/SiC tube oxidized at 900°C — source: Subject for expanding plug test to measure residual hoop strength
- SiCf/SiC tube oxidized at 1200°C — source: Subject for expanding plug test to measure residual hoop strength

## Evidence

- P114, PDF page 2, Experimental Procedures: "Subsequently, the expanding plug test was employed to characterize the hoop strength of the SiCf /SiC tube and to assess its mechanical properties."
- P114, PDF page 3, Experimental Procedures: "Hoop strength was measured by an expanding-plug test, which was performed in a universal testing machine (Shimadzu, Japan). During the process of burst tests, the polyurethane plug (McMaster-Carr, USA) was compressed with a loading rate of 2 mm/min and expanded along the radial direction, exerting tensile loads on the tubular SiCf /SiC composite specimen walls through internal pressurization until fracture."
