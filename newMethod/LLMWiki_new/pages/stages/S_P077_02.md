## Evidence

- P077, PDF page 2, 2.2 Spark Plasma Sintering (SPS): "The densification of the Si3N4 ceramics was achieved via liquid sintering using SPS. Mixed powders (3.5 g) were charged into a high-strength carbon-matrix composite die (20 mm inner diameter) with a porous carbon felt. The sintering process was conducted using the SPS method (SPS-725, Fuji Electronic Industrial Co., Ltd., Japan) with a constant heating rate of 100◦C/min. To investigate the densification behavior, the processing envelope included sintering temperatures ranging from 1400◦C to 1700◦C, uniaxial pressures between 100 and 150 MPa, and holding times spanning 5 to 20 min."
