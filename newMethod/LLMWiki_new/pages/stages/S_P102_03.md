# Stage: instruction finetuning (IFT) on CIF

- Stage ID: `S_P102_03`
- Stage type: `model_training`
- Stage order: 3
- Task: `T_P102_01` — crystal structure generation

## Goal

Train the LLaMat models to understand and reason about crystallographic concepts and principles.

## Input

- LLaMat base models
- CIF instruction-output pairs (6,941,865 training instances)

## Output

- LLaMat-CIF models

## Datasets used

- CIF Instruction Dataset — training: Train the LLaMat models to understand and reason about crystallographic concepts and principles.

## Evidence

- P102, PDF page 2, Results: "Concurrently, we developed LLaMat-CIF models through IFT of LLaMat models on crystallographic information files, a hand-curated dataset comprising five syntactic and four semantic tasks."
- P102, PDF page 7, Crystal Generation: "The instruction finetuning phase employed a dual-framework approach comprising syntactic tasks focused on CIF file structure interpretation... and semantic tasks targeting crystal stability principles..."
