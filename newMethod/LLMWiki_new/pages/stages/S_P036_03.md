## Goal

to replace simple atomic ratio features with physical features to facilitate physical interpretations

## Input

- initial feature pool based on a priori knowledge (Pauling’s electronegativity, μ, τ, IR, EA, DP, AR, d/f orbital electrons)

## Output

- 11 groups of highly correlated descriptors and the retained ones
- 7 best physical features
