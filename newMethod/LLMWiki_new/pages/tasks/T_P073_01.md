# Task: generation of lignin structures

- Task ID: `T_P073_01`
- Canonical name: `generate_lignin_structures`
- Paper: `P073`

## Objective

To generate a molecular structural dataset of lignin macromolecules that matches experimentally determined structural compositions.

## Material scope

- lignin
- milled wood lignin
- coniferyl alcohol
- syringyl alcohol
- phenylpropane units

## Target properties

- structural composition
- monomer ratio (S/G)
- interunit linkage frequencies
- degree of polymerization
- branching patterns
- functional group counts

## Constraints

- must match experimental structural compositions from literature
- must cover DP range of 3 to 25
- must include only G and SG type lignin structures
- must be chemically correct and legible 2D structures

## Input requirements

- S/G ratio
- bond ratio
- degree of polymerization
- experimental characterization data on monomer and linkage frequencies

## Output goal

- 60 K unique lignin polymer structures in multiple formats (MOL, CSV, JSON)
- validated structures matching experimental data with ~90% accuracy

## Research stages

1. selection and curation of experimental input data (`data_acquisition`)
2. sequencing lignin monomers and conditional linkage generation (`candidate_generation`)
3. generation of topological matrices and molecular graph representations (`candidate_generation`)
4. generation of molecular structures and consistency validation (`candidate_generation`)
5. technical validation against experimental data and alternative models (`computational_validation`)
6. assembly and formatting of the LGS dataset (`data_preparation`)

## Dataset uses

- technical validation against experimental data and alternative models: Lignin-KMC model structures (computational_validation, DU_P073_004)
- assembly and formatting of the LGS dataset: Lignin Structural (LGS) Dataset (benchmark, DU_P073_005)

## Evidence

- P073, PDF page 1: "Herein, we present the Lignin Structural (LGS) Dataset that includes the molecular structure of milled wood lignin focusing on two major monomeric units (coniferyl and syringyl), and the six most common interunit linkages (phenylpropane β-aryl ether, resinol, phenylcoumaran, biphenyl, dibenzodioxocin, and diaryl ether)."
- P073, PDF page 1: "The dataset constitutes a unique resource that covers a part of lignin’s chemical space characterized by polymer chains with lengths in the range of 3 to 25 monomer units."
- P073, PDF page 1: "Structural data were generated using a sequence-controlled polymer generation approach that was calibrated to match experimental lignin properties."
