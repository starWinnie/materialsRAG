# Task: accelerating the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs)

- Task ID: `T_P037_01`
- Canonical name: `discovery_of_transparent_conducting_materials`
- Paper: `P037`

## Objective

accelerate the discovery of new transparent conducting materials (TCMs)

## Material scope

- transparent conducting materials (TCMs)
- oxides
- doped semiconductors
- metal oxides

## Target properties

- electrical conductivity
- band gap

## Constraints

- high electrical conductivity
- low absorption in visible light (i.e., band gap > 3 eV)

## Input requirements

- stoichiometry alone
- chemical formulas

## Output goal

- identify new, previously unseen TCMs
- highlight material candidates that may have been previously overlooked

## Research stages

1. creating and validating experimental databases of electrical conductivity and band gap (`data_acquisition`)
2. preprocessing and expert validation of experimental datasets (`data_preparation`)
3. training state-of-the-art ML models on stoichiometry alone (`model_training`)
4. assessing model performance via K-fold, LOCO-CV, and leave-one-TCM-family-out (`model_evaluation`)
5. compiling a list of 55 compositions across MPDS, Pearson, and ICSD (`candidate_generation`)
6. screening 55 candidates using ML-predicted conductivity and band gap against TCM criteria (`candidate_screening`)
