# Task: Precursor recommendation for inorganic synthesis

- Task ID: `T_P044_01`
- Canonical name: `precursor_recommendation_for_inorganic_synthesis`
- Paper: `P044`

## Objective

To recommend precursor sets for the synthesis of a novel inorganic target material by learning materials similarity from precedent synthesis recipes.

## Material scope

- inorganic materials
- solid-state synthesis

## Target properties

- None stated

## Constraints

- element conservation
- synthesis pathway viability
- precedent-based analogy

## Input requirements

- chemical composition of target material
- knowledge base of prior synthesis recipes

## Output goal

- ranked list of precursor sets for the target material

## Research stages

1. Text-mining synthesis recipes from scientific literature (`data_acquisition`)
2. Data cleaning and expansion of material formulas (`data_preparation`)
3. Temporal data split for historical validation (`data_preparation`)
4. Training PrecursorSelector encoding model via self-supervised representation learning (`model_training`)
5. Ranking known target materials by similarity to novel target (`candidate_generation`)
6. Recipe completion via masked precursor completion (MPC) (`candidate_screening`)
7. Historical validation of precursor recommendation success rate (`model_evaluation`)

## Dataset uses

- Text-mining synthesis recipes from scientific literature: Text-mined dataset of inorganic materials synthesis recipes (source, DU_P044_001)
- Data cleaning and expansion of material formulas: Text-mined dataset of inorganic materials synthesis recipes (source, DU_P044_002)

## Evidence

- P044, PDF page 2, Abstract: "We use a knowledge base of 29,900 solid-state synthesis recipes, text-mined from the scientific literature, to automatically learn which precursors to recommend for the synthesis of a novel target material."
