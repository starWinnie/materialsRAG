# Task: Rational design of nanoscale stabilized oxide catalysts for OER

- Task ID: `T_P038_01`
- Canonical name: `rational_design_of_nanoscale_stabilized_oxide_catalysts_for_oer`
- Paper: `P038`

## Objective

Identify viable candidate anode catalysts for OER under varying reaction conditions for bulk, surface, and nanoscale Pourbaix stability as well as material cost, overpotential, and metastability.

## Material scope

- oxide materials

## Target properties

- oxygen evolution reaction (OER) activity
- nanoscale stability
- bulk Pourbaix stability
- overpotential
- material cost

## Constraints

- bulk and nanoscale Pourbaix stability
- material cost less than RuO2
- overpotential < 0.75 V
- metastability

## Input requirements

- pre-trained OC22 ML models
- 4119 oxide materials
- slabs up to Miller index 1
- O*, OH*, OOH* surface intermediates

## Output goal

- 122 and 68 viable candidates for OER under bulk and nanoscale regime respectively
- 190 total candidates

## Research stages

1. Obtaining bulk materials for slab construction (`data_acquisition`)
2. Slab generation (`data_preparation`)
3. Fine-tuning pre-trained OC22 S2EF-Total model (`model_training`)
4. Interpolating total energy predictions for slabs and surface intermediates (`candidate_generation`)
5. High-throughput screening with progressive criteria (`candidate_screening`)
6. DFT validation of ML predictions (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- Obtaining bulk materials for slab construction: Materials Project (source, DU_P038_001)
- Fine-tuning pre-trained OC22 S2EF-Total model: Open Catalyst 2020 (pretraining, DU_P038_002)
- Fine-tuning pre-trained OC22 S2EF-Total model: Open Catalyst 2022 (training, DU_P038_003)
- Interpolating total energy predictions for slabs and surface intermediates: Interpolated OC22 oxide database (candidate_pool, DU_P038_004)
- High-throughput screening with progressive criteria: Interpolated OC22 oxide database (screening, DU_P038_005)
- DFT validation of ML predictions: Interpolated OC22 oxide database (computational_validation, DU_P038_006)

## Evidence

- P038, PDF page 1, Introduction: "In this work, we interpolated a database of the total energy predictions for all slabs and OER surface intermediates for 4119 oxide materials in the original OC22 dataset using pre-trained models from the OC22 framework. This database includes all terminations of all facets up to a maximum Miller index of 1. To demonstrate the full utility of this database, we constructed a flexible screening framework to identify viable candidate anode catalysts for OER under varying reaction conditions for bulk, surface, and nanoscale Pourbaix stability as well as material cost, overpotential, and metastability."
