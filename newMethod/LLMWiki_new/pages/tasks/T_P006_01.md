# Task: Generate universal atomic embeddings (UAEs) for crystal property prediction

- Task ID: `T_P006_01`
- Canonical name: `generate_universal_atomic_embeddings`
- Paper: `P006`

## Objective

To generate universal atomic embeddings (UAEs) that serve as broadly applicable atomic fingerprints capable of enhancing the prediction accuracy of crystal properties (e.g., formation energy, bandgap) across diverse deep learning models and material databases.

## Material scope

- crystals
- hybrid organic-inorganic perovskites

## Target properties

- formation energy
- bandgap
- total energy
- total magnetization

## Constraints

- must be transferable across different back-end models (CGCNN, MEGNET, ALIGNN)
- must be invariant to rotational and translational symmetry
- must capture complex atomic features without relying on predefined atomic attributes

## Input requirements

- crystal structure data (CIF files)
- atomic species information (one-hot encoded)
- atomic coordinates

## Output goal

- dense, low-dimensional tensor representations (embeddings) for each atom in a crystal, forming a universal atomic embedding (UAE) tensor

## Research stages

1. Acquire crystal structure datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. Preprocess crystal structure data (`data_preparation`)
3. Train CrystalTransformer to generate ct-UAEs (`model_training`)
4. Evaluate ct-UAE transfer performance on back-end models (`model_evaluation`)
5. Screen optimal clustering configuration for interpretability (`candidate_screening`)
6. Validate ct-UAE physical interpretability via reverse prediction (`computational_validation`)
