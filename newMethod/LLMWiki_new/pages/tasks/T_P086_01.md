# Task: creep-limited lifetime prediction

- Task ID: `T_P086_01`
- Canonical name: `creep_limited_lifetime_prediction`
- Paper: `P086`

## Objective

To predict long-term creep-limited lifetimes (e.g., ≥100,000 h) of Inconel 740/740H from shorter-term experimental data and evaluate the relative effectiveness of Larson-Miller parameter and Wilshire analytical methods.

## Material scope

- Inconel 740/740H

## Target properties

- creep-rupture lifetime
- applied stress for 100,000-h lifetime (σ100,000h)

## Constraints

- prediction must be physically realistic (no 'turnback')
- extrapolation beyond experimental data range
- applicable to precipitation-strengthened Ni-based superalloy

## Input requirements

- creep-rupture experimental data (temperature, applied stress, time to rupture)
- ultimate tensile strength values
- activation energy (Qc*)

## Output goal

- parametric models (LMP and Wilshire equations) with fitted constants
- predicted rupture times
- predicted σ100,000h values at target temperatures

## Research stages

1. generation of creep-rupture experimental data (`data_acquisition`)
2. preparation of creep-rupture data for analysis (`data_preparation`)
3. fitting of Larson-Miller parameter (LMP) and Wilshire equations to experimental data (`model_training`)
4. evaluation of model predictive accuracy against experimental rupture times (`model_evaluation`)
5. screening of LMP and Wilshire models for long-term extrapolation capability using short-time data (`candidate_screening`)
6. calculation of applied stress for 100,000-h lifetime (σ100,000h) (`computational_validation`)
