# Task: bandgap prediction of ABX3-type perovskites

- Task ID: `T_P033_01`
- Canonical name: `bandgap_prediction_of_abx3_type_perovskites`
- Paper: `P033`

## Objective

To fast and accurately predicate the bandgaps of perovskite for solar cell devices

## Material scope

- ABX3-type perovskites
- all-inorganic perovskites
- inorganic-organic hybrid perovskites
- Tin-free perovskites

## Target properties

- bandgap
- conduction band minimum (CBM)
- valence band maximum (VBM)

## Constraints

- bandgap greater than 1.63 eV for top cell in tandem solar cells

## Input requirements

- chemical composition data of perovskite
- 8 chemical components as features: A-position monovalent cations (Cs+, MA+, FA+), B-position divalent metal ions (Pb2+, Sn2+), X-position halogen anions (I−, Br−, Cl−)

## Output goal

- predicted bandgap value in eV
- predicted CBM value in eV
- predicted VBM value in eV

## Research stages

1. search and collection of experimental bandgap data (`data_acquisition`)
2. database establishment and cleaning (`data_preparation`)
3. training of bandgap prediction models (`model_training`)
4. evaluation of bandgap prediction models (`model_evaluation`)
5. validation against DFT calculations (`computational_validation`)
6. experimental validation of ML predictions (`experimental_validation`)
7. screening of chemical composition effects using SHAP (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- search and collection of experimental bandgap data: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (source, DU_P033_001)
- database establishment and cleaning: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (training, DU_P033_002)
- training of bandgap prediction models: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (training, DU_P033_003)
- evaluation of bandgap prediction models: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (test, DU_P033_004)
- training of bandgap prediction models: Experimental CBM and VBM dataset for ABX3-type perovskites (training, DU_P033_005)
- evaluation of bandgap prediction models: Experimental CBM and VBM dataset for ABX3-type perovskites (test, DU_P033_006)
- validation against DFT calculations: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (computational_validation, DU_P033_007)
- experimental validation of ML predictions: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (experimental_validation, DU_P033_008)
- screening of chemical composition effects using SHAP: Experimental ABX3-type perovskite bandgap dataset (screening, DU_P033_009)
- database establishment and cleaning: Subdivided ABX3-type perovskite datasets (training, DU_P033_010)
