# Task: charge density prediction

- Task ID: `T_P030_01`
- Canonical name: `charge_density_prediction`
- Paper: `P030`

## Objective

To accurately and efficiently predict the 3D real-space electronic charge density ρ(r) of atomic systems (molecules and materials) given their atomic composition and coordinates.

## Material scope

- small organic molecules
- crystalline materials

## Target properties

- charge density

## Constraints

- SE(3)-equivariance
- computational scalability for large systems and ab initio molecular dynamics
- flexible accuracy–efficiency trade-off

## Input requirements

- atomic numbers A = {ai | i = 1, ..., N}
- atomic coordinates R = {ri | i = 1, ..., N}

## Output goal

- predicted charge density ˆρ(r) at grid coordinates r ∈ Rc

## Research stages

1. acquisition of DFT-calculated charge density data (`data_acquisition`)
2. construction of atomic and virtual node representations (`data_preparation`)
3. end-to-end training of equivariant neural network with fine-tuning for scaling factors (`model_training`)
4. benchmarking against normalized mean absolute error (NMAE) and efficiency metrics (`model_evaluation`)
5. ablation analysis of recipe components (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- acquisition of DFT-calculated charge density data: QM9 charge density dataset (source, DU_P030_001)
- acquisition of DFT-calculated charge density data: MD charge density dataset (source, DU_P030_002)
- acquisition of DFT-calculated charge density data: Cubic charge density dataset (source, DU_P030_003)

## Evidence

- P030, PDF page 1, Abstract: "In density functional theory, the charge density is the core attribute of atomic systems from which all chemical properties can be derived. Machine learning methods are promising as a means of significantly accelerating charge density predictions, yet existing approaches either lack accuracy or scalability. We propose a recipe that can achieve both."
- P030, PDF page 5, 3.2 Prediction Model: "Using the atomic orbital basis set representation of charge density, the prediction model aims to predict the basis set coefficients ci,j,m and the scaling factors si,j for each real and virtual node such that the predicted charge density matches the ground truth density obtained from DFT calculations."
