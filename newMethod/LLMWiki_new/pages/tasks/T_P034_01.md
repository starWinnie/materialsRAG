# Task: training machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs)

- Task ID: `T_P034_01`
- Canonical name: `train_machine_learned_interatomic_potentials`
- Paper: `P034`

## Objective

to learn an MLIP, which can accurately predict the potential energy and atomistic forces for an atomic system exposed to local perturbations

## Material scope

- molecular and material systems
- organic molecules
- inorganic materials
- bulk materials
- transition-metal oxide
- Li-Mo-Ni-Ti oxide (LMNTO)
- aspirin
- buckyball catcher

## Target properties

- potential energy
- atomistic forces
- total energies
- atomic forces

## Constraints

- sparse training data sets
- limited sizes of available data sets
- high computational cost of ab initio or first-principles approaches
- force labels inaccessible

## Input requirements

- atomic configurations
- atom positions
- atom types
- reference energies
- reference atomic forces (when available)

## Output goal

- accurate prediction of potential energy and atomistic forces
- improved robustness during MD simulations
- reduced energy and force errors

## Research stages

1. generating training data sets (`data_acquisition`)
2. training MLIPs with PIWSL loss (`model_training`)
3. benchmarking models trained with PIWSL (`model_evaluation`)
4. evaluating robustness during MD simulations (`experimental_validation`)
5. fine-tuning foundation models (`model_evaluation`)
