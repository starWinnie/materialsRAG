# Task: fine-tuning pretrained universal machine-learning interatomic potentials

- Task ID: `T_P041_01`
- Canonical name: `fine_tuning_pretrained_mlips`
- Paper: `P041`

## Objective

improve accuracy of a pretrained MLIP for a specific system while preserving its generalizability and preventing catastrophic forgetting

## Material scope

- Li6PS5Cl (LPSC)
- other argyrodite-type materials
- non-argyrodite-type materials (sulfides, oxides, nitrides, halides, hydrides)

## Target properties

- potential energy surface (PES) accuracy
- Li-ion diffusivity
- structural stability (quasi-melting behavior)

## Constraints

- prevent catastrophic forgetting
- maintain computational efficiency
- enable knowledge transfer to chemically distinct systems

## Input requirements

- pretrained universal MLIP (SevenNet-0)
- fine-tuning dataset (AIMD trajectories of LPSC)
- pretrained dataset (MPtrj) for Replay and FIM computation

## Output goal

- forgetting-aware fine-tuned MLIP model (reEWC) with improved PES accuracy and preserved generalizability

## Research stages

1. constructing the fine-tuning dataset from AIMD trajectories of LPSC (`data_acquisition`)
2. constructing the Replay set by random sampling from the pretrained dataset (`data_acquisition`)
3. fine-tuning SevenNet-0 using reEWC method (`model_training`)
4. evaluating forgetting prevention and learning performance on sMPtrj and fine-tuning validation sets (`model_evaluation`)
5. evaluating PES accuracy on argyrodite and non-argyrodite test sets (`model_evaluation`)
6. performing MD simulations to assess dynamical properties (Li diffusivity and quasi-melting behavior) (`computational_validation`)
