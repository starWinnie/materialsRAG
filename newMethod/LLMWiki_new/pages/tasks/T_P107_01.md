# Task: Properties and Structures of MgO–CaO–Al2O3–SiO2 Glasses: Effects of Low-Iron Oxide and Redox

- Task ID: `T_P107_01`
- Canonical name: `elucidate_effects_of_low_iron_oxide_and_redox_on_glass_properties_and_structure`
- Paper: `P107`

## Objective

To elucidate the local environments of ferric and ferrous ions in the glass network and understand how low-iron oxide content and redox state affect a wide range of glass properties and structures.

## Material scope

- MgO–CaO–Al2O3–SiO2 glasses
- mixed alkaline aluminosilicate glasses

## Target properties

- melt viscosity
- liquidus temperature
- Rosseland mean radiative absorption/conduction
- fiber tensile strength
- water contact angle
- surface tension
- local environments of ferric and ferrous ions
- glass network structure

## Constraints

- low iron concentrations (0.04–0.64 wt% Fe2O3)

## Input requirements

- baseline mixed-alkaline-earth aluminosilicate glass composition (61.0SiO2, 13.5Al2O3, 20.5CaO, 4.6MgO, 0.36(Na2O + K2O), plus 0.04 wt%Fe2O3)
- added Fe2O3, MnO2, SnO2, and CeO2 at specified concentrations

## Output goal

- understanding of how Fe2O3 content and redox ratio affect glass properties and structure
- identification of structural roles of Fe3+ and Fe2+ ions
- quantification of property trends with iron content
