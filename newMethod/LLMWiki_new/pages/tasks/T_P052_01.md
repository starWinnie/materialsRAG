# Task: polymer property prediction

- Task ID: `T_P052_01`
- Canonical name: `polymer_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P052`

## Objective

Enable rigorous comparison of machine-learning methods for predicting physical properties of polymers across diverse architectures

## Material scope

- homopolymers
- alternating copolymers
- random copolymers
- block copolymers

## Target properties

- glass transition temperature (Tg)
- electron affinity (EA)
- ionization potential (IP)
- refractive index
- density
- specific heat (Cp)
- specific heat (Cv)
- radius of gyration

## Constraints

- models must generalize across polymer architectures
- models must handle increasing repeat-unit complexity
- evaluation must support in-distribution and out-of-distribution (architecture-held-out) scenarios

## Input requirements

- polymer chemical structure encoded as PSMILES or wPSMILES
- repeat unit composition and connectivity for copolymers

## Output goal

- predicted numeric value of a physical property

## Research stages

1. aggregating polymer property datasets from experimental, DFT, and MD sources (`data_acquisition`)
2. converting and standardizing polymer representations (PSMILES/wPSMILES) (`data_preparation`)
3. selecting and configuring ML model candidates (graph-based, descriptor-based, language models) (`candidate_generation`)
4. training models using five-fold cross-validation on homopolymer and copolymer datasets (`model_training`)
5. evaluating models across four benchmark tasks (in-distribution, dataset-size scaling, repeat-unit complexity, architecture transfer) (`model_evaluation`)
