# Task: material property prediction and human-AI interaction for materials science

- Task ID: `T_P050_01`
- Canonical name: `material_property_prediction_and_human_ai_interaction`
- Paper: `P050`

## Objective

To enable accurate, interpretable, and interactive prediction of material properties and generation of scientific reasoning (e.g., synthesis protocols, stability explanations) by unifying atomic structural data and natural language inputs.

## Material scope

- inorganic materials
- crystalline structures
- materials up to Plutonium

## Target properties

- chemical formula
- space group
- crystal system
- metallicity
- direct bandgap
- stability
- experimental observation
- magnetic status
- magnetic order
- formation energy
- energy above hull
- bandgap

## Constraints

- full-resolution atomic structure integration
- human-AI interaction via textual prompts
- multi-task capability (classification and numerical prediction)

## Input requirements

- material structure (as graph)
- textual user prompt (e.g., 'What is the formation energy of this material?')

## Output goal

- text-based response containing predicted property value or descriptive explanation

## Research stages

1. curating dataset of crystalline structures from Materials Project (`data_acquisition`)
2. preprocessing material structures into graph representations and generating task-specific instruction-answer templates (`data_preparation`)
3. pretraining the bridge model via contrastive, conditional, and association losses (`model_training`)
4. instructive finetuning of the full MatterChat system on 12 multimodal tasks (`model_training`)
5. comprehensive evaluation across 9 material property tasks on test set (`model_evaluation`)
6. multi-modal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) inference with L2-similarity retrieval (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- curating dataset of crystalline structures from Materials Project: MPtrj dataset (source, DU_P050_001)

## Evidence

- P050, PDF page 2, Introduction: "MatterChat integrates material structure data with textual user queries, combining insights from materials science and NLP. It enhances the capabilities of large language models by overcoming their traditional limitations in quantitative predictions and improving the handling of scientific material-related tasks, as demonstrated through comparisons with other LLMs."
- P050, PDF page 3, Results: "Figure 1(a) presents the architecture of MatterChat, designed to process both material structures and user requests as inputs to generate text-based outputs for tasks such as material property prediction, structural analysis, and descriptive language generation."
