# Task: Band gap narrowing in oxychlorosulfide photocatalysts through aliovalent Cl-to-S anion substitution

- Task ID: `T_P119_01`
- Canonical name: `band_gap_narrowing_via_aliovalent_anion_substitution`
- Paper: `P119`

## Objective

To narrow the band gap of La5Ti6O15S3Cl3 to enhance visible-light absorption and enable visible-light-driven photocatalytic H2 evolution.

## Material scope

- La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x (x = 0, 0.5, 0.75, 1)
- oxychlorosulfides

## Target properties

- narrowed band gap (<3 eV)
- elevated valence-band maximum (VBM)
- visible-light absorption
- H2-evolution photocatalytic activity under visible light (λ > 400 nm)

## Constraints

- charge balance requiring simultaneous Ta5+ for Ti4+ substitution
- solubility limit between x = 0.75 and 1.0
- S volatilization during calcination

## Input requirements

- La5Ti6O15S3Cl3 parent compound
- S2– and Ta5+ precursors for substitution
- solid-state synthesis conditions

## Output goal

- La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x compounds with systematically tuned S/Cl and Ti/Ta ratios and narrowed band gaps

## Research stages

1. Selection of model compound La5Ti6O15S3Cl3 (`data_acquisition`)
2. Design of aliovalent substitution series La5Ti6–xTaxO15S3+xCl3–x (`candidate_generation`)
3. Solid-state synthesis and compositional characterization (`data_preparation`)
4. Rietveld refinement and DFT calculations (`computational_validation`)
5. Optical and electronic characterization (`model_evaluation`)
6. Photocatalytic H2-evolution testing (`experimental_validation`)

## Evidence

- P119, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "Here, we demonstrate aliovalent Cl-to-S substitution in the oxychlorosulfide La5Ti6O15S3Cl3 by simultaneously replacing Cl– with S2– and Ti4+ with Ta5+. This substitution elevates the valence-band maximum by increasing the contribution of S 3p orbitals, thereby enhancing visible-light absorption. The obtained compounds function as visible-light-responsive H2-evolution photocatalysts."
