# Task: Design of a new Al-0.3Zr-0.2Ce-0.2Cu (wt.%) alloy for additive manufacturing that overcomes the classical tradeoff between conductivity and creep resistance

- Task ID: `T_P070_01`
- Canonical name: `design_alloy_for_conductivity_and_creep_resistance`
- Paper: `P070`

## Objective

Overcome the classical tradeoff between electrical conductivity and creep resistance in Al alloys

## Material scope

- Al-0.3Zr-0.2Ce-0.2Cu (wt.%) alloy

## Target properties

- high creep resistance at 200 °C
- high electrical conductivity (~48 %IACS in as-fabricated state)
- yield strength of ~65 MPa at 200 °C in as-fabricated state

## Constraints

- solute-lean composition
- avoid primary Al3Zr phase formation
- maximize solubility of Zr in α-Al
- maintain compatibility between Zr and Ce for maximized contribution of each component

## Input requirements

- rapid solidification via laser powder-bed fusion (LPBF)
- supersaturation of Zr in α-Al matrix
- formation of submicron (Ce,Cu)-rich intermetallic particles on solidification

## Output goal

- new alloy with near-zero secondary creep rates up to yield stress at 200 °C and high electrical conductivity

## Research stages

1. Alloy fabrication via laser powder-bed fusion (LPBF) (`data_acquisition`)
2. Isothermal and isochronal aging treatments (`data_preparation`)
3. Strengthening mechanisms modeling (`model_training`)
4. Comparison of theoretical and experimental yield stress values (`model_evaluation`)
5. Creep performance evaluation (`candidate_screening`)
6. First-principles density functional calculations and molecular dynamics modeling (`computational_validation`)
7. Post-creep microstructure characterization (TEM/APT) (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- First-principles density functional calculations and molecular dynamics modeling: PanAl2024c thermodynamic database (source, DU_P070_007)
