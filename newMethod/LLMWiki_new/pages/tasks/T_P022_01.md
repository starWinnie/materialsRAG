# Task: Predicting physical properties of materials from their crystal structures

- Task ID: `T_P022_01`
- Canonical name: `crystal_structure_to_physical_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P022`

## Objective

Predicting physical properties of materials from their crystal structures without actually synthesizing materials is important for accelerating the discovery of new materials with desired properties

## Material scope

- crystal structures

## Target properties

- formation energy
- bandgap
- bulk modulus
- shear modulus
- total energy
- energy above hull

## Constraints

- periodicity
- permutation invariance
- SE(3) invariance

## Input requirements

- crystal structure represented as unit cell points and lattice vectors

## Output goal

- predicted physical property values

## Research stages

1. using Materials Project and JARVIS-DFT datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. representing crystal structures with unit cell points and lattice vectors (`data_preparation`)
3. training Crystalformer model with mean absolute error loss (`model_training`)
4. evaluating model performance with mean absolute errors (`model_evaluation`)

## Dataset uses

- using Materials Project and JARVIS-DFT datasets: Materials Project (source, DU_P022_001)
- using Materials Project and JARVIS-DFT datasets: JARVIS-DFT 3D (source, DU_P022_002)
- training Crystalformer model with mean absolute error loss: Materials Project (training, DU_P022_003)
- evaluating model performance with mean absolute errors: Materials Project (test, DU_P022_004)
- evaluating model performance with mean absolute errors: JARVIS-DFT 3D (test, DU_P022_005)
