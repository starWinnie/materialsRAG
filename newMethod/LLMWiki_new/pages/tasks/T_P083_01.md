# Task: Enhancing the degradability of polyethylene (PE) without compromising its mechanical integrity

- Task ID: `T_P083_01`
- Canonical name: `enhance_polyethylene_degradability_without_compromising_mechanical_integrity`
- Paper: `P083`

## Objective

Enhancing the degradability of polyethylene (PE) without compromising its mechanical integrity remains a major challenge in polymer design.

## Material scope

- polyethylene
- keto-modified polyethylene (KMPE)
- ester-modified polyethylene (EMPE)
- amide-modified polyethylene (AMPE)

## Target properties

- tensile strength
- toughness
- crystallinity
- interchain cohesion
- degradability

## Constraints

- low concentrations of functional groups
- preservation of mechanical performance
- predetermined scission sites

## Input requirements

- functional-group-to-ethylene-unit ratio of 0.04
- amide, keto, or ester in-chain functional groups

## Output goal

- AMPE as a promising candidate to replace conventional PE in applications demanding superior toughness and strength while enabling improved degradability

## Research stages

1. Building initial MPE chain structures with functional groups (`data_preparation`)
2. Equilibrating semicrystalline MPE systems via MD simulation (`model_training`)
3. Performing uniaxial tensile deformation simulations (`model_evaluation`)
4. Performing DFT calculations to analyze intermolecular interactions and binding energies (`computational_validation`)
5. Comparing mechanical and structural performance across KMPE, EMPE, and AMPE (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- None stated
