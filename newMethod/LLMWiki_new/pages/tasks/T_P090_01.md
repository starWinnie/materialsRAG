# Task: optimizing densification and mechanical performance of ZrB2–SiC ceramics via spark plasma sintering

- Task ID: `T_P090_01`
- Canonical name: `optimize_sps_processing_of_zrb2_sic_ceramics`
- Paper: `P090`

## Objective

Optimize densification, microstructure, and mechanical performance of additive-free ZrB2–20 vol% SiC composites through precise control of spark plasma sintering parameters.

## Material scope

- ZrB2–20 vol% SiC composites

## Target properties

- relative density
- hardness
- flexural strength
- fracture toughness
- grain size
- porosity

## Constraints

- no sintering additives
- additive-free processing
- energy-efficient processing

## Input requirements

- ZrB2 powder (99.5% purity, ∼5.5 μm)
- SiC powder (beta-phase, 99.8% purity, ∼0.62 μm)
- two powder preparation routes: WC-milling or ZrO2-milling

## Output goal

- near-theoretical relative density (≥99%)
- peak flexural strength (e.g., 516 MPa)
- high hardness (e.g., 17.08 GPa)
- fracture toughness (e.g., 3.85 MPa m1/2)
- controlled grain size and low porosity

## Research stages

1. powder preparation and characterization (`data_acquisition`)
2. spark plasma sintering (SPS) processing (`model_training`)
3. sample preparation for characterization (`data_preparation`)
4. density measurement (`model_evaluation`)
5. microstructural characterization (`model_evaluation`)
6. mechanical properties testing (`model_evaluation`)

## Evidence

- P090, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "This study systematically investigates the independent and combined effects of three critical spark plasma sintering (SPS) parameters, that is, temperature, applied pressure, and dwell time, on the densification behavior, microstructure, and mechanical properties of ZrB2 –20 vol% SiC composites."
- P090, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "Building upon prior work on powder preparation effects (e.g., Tungsten Carbide (WC) vs. ZrO2 milling), this research uniquely focuses on how precise control of sintering conditions alone can tailor final material characteristics."
- P090, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "The results demonstrate that optimizing sintering parameters yields significant property enhancement, achieving a maximum relative density of 99.2% (at 2100◦C, 65 MPa, 15 min) and peak flexural strength of 516 MPa (at 2000◦C, 65 MPa, 60 min). Hardness and fracture toughness reached 17.08 GPa and 3.85 MPa m1/2 , respectively, under optimized conditions."
