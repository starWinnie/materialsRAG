# Task: Preparation of TiC-based composites via reactive sintering

- Task ID: `T_P115_01`
- Canonical name: `prepare_tic_based_composites_via_reactive_sintering`
- Paper: `P115`

## Objective

Synergistically enhance the sinterability and mechanical properties of TiC-based composites

## Material scope

- TiC-based composites
- TiC matrix
- (Ti,Zr,Hf)C solid solution
- nano-SiC particles
- micro-scale Ti3SiC2

## Target properties

- near-full densification (relative density > 99%)
- flexural strength of 972 MPa
- fracture toughness of 6.7 MPa ⋅m1/2

## Constraints

- suppress formation of silicide by-products
- eliminate free carbon in the matrix
- achieve efficient densification and microstructural strengthening-toughening at relatively low temperature

## Input requirements

- TiC
- (Zr,Hf)C
- Si
- hot-pressing sintering at 1600–1800°C under 30 MPa

## Output goal

- multiphase microstructure consisting of nano-SiC particles, micro-scale Ti3SiC2, and (Ti,Zr,Hf)C solid solution within TiC matrix

## Research stages

1. Selection and acquisition of raw materials (`data_acquisition`)
2. Powder mixing and milling (`data_preparation`)
3. Design of compositional variants (`candidate_generation`)
4. Thermodynamic analysis of reaction pathways (`computational_validation`)
5. Systematic investigation of sintering behavior and microstructural evolution (`candidate_screening`)
6. Hot-pressing sintering and mechanical property evaluation (`experimental_validation`)
