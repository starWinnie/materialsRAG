# Task: developing universal interatomic potentials for atomistic modelling of materials

- Task ID: `T_P063_01`
- Canonical name: `develop_universal_interatomic_potentials`
- Paper: `P063`

## Objective

to achieve ab initio accuracy with dramatically increased speed and scalability for atomistic modelling of materials

## Material scope

- inorganic materials
- crystals
- MOFs
- molecular crystals
- layered materials
- small organic molecules
- bulk crystalline materials

## Target properties

- total energy
- per-atom forces
- unit cell stress
- geometry optimization
- molecular dynamics stability
- adsorption properties
- heat of adsorption
- free energy landscape

## Constraints

- must be stable under simulation for out-of-distribution materials
- must be 3-6x faster than existing universal potentials
- must be non-conservative (direct force prediction)
- must support periodic and non-periodic systems
- must handle van der Waals interactions via learned dispersion corrections

## Input requirements

- atomic positions
- unit cell (implicit via edge construction)
- atomic types

## Output goal

- scalar total energy
- per-atom force vectors
- unit cell stress in reduced Voigt notation

## Research stages

1. curating dataset of minimum energy configurations of materials for pretraining (`data_acquisition`)
2. combining MPtraj and Alexandria datasets for finetuning (`data_acquisition`)
3. diffusion model pretraining (`model_training`)
4. NNP finetuning (`model_training`)
5. evaluating on Matbench-Discovery benchmark (`model_evaluation`)
6. evaluating on MD17-10k benchmark (`model_evaluation`)
7. evaluating molecular dynamics of crystalline materials (`model_evaluation`)
8. evaluating adsorption properties (`model_evaluation`)

## Dataset uses

- combining MPtraj and Alexandria datasets for finetuning: MPtraj (training, DU_P063_002)
- combining MPtraj and Alexandria datasets for finetuning: Alexandria (training, DU_P063_003)
- evaluating on Matbench-Discovery benchmark: Matbench-Discovery (benchmark, DU_P063_004)
- evaluating on MD17-10k benchmark: MD17-10k (benchmark, DU_P063_005)
