# Task: Correcting DFT formation energies towards experimental accuracy

- Task ID: `T_P035_01`
- Canonical name: `correct_dft_formation_energy`
- Paper: `P035`

## Objective

Improve the agreement of DFT-calculated formation energies with experimental formation enthalpies

## Material scope

- three-dimensional inorganic crystal structures
- experimentally known compounds

## Target properties

- formation energy
- energy above the convex hull

## Constraints

- must be applicable to existing GGA databases without re-running DFT
- must limit distortion of relative phase stability

## Input requirements

- DFT-relaxed geometries (e.g., PBEsol)
- foundational MLIP (e.g., PET-OMATPES)
- experimental formation enthalpy references

## Output goal

- formation energies with MAE < 50 meV/atom vs. experiment
- stability classifications (e.g., energy above hull) preserved or physically justified

## Research stages

1. Compare DFT formation energies against experimental formation enthalpies (`model_evaluation`)
2. Evaluate zero-shot fMLIP formation energies on DFT geometries (`model_evaluation`)
3. Train classical ML models in delta-learning framework using fMLIP latent features (`model_training`)
4. Evaluate ML-corrected formation energies against experiment (`model_evaluation`)
5. Select optimal model via regularization tuning (`candidate_screening`)
