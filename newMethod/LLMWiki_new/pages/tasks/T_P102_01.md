# Task: crystal structure generation

- Task ID: `T_P102_01`
- Canonical name: `crystal_structure_generation`
- Paper: `P102`

## Objective

Generate novel, stable, and physically realistic crystal structures.

## Material scope

- inorganic materials
- crystals

## Target properties

- structural validity
- compositional validity
- thermodynamic stability
- coverage across the periodic table

## Constraints

- structural integrity (distance between atom centers > sum of atomic radii)
- charge neutrality
- realistic physical and chemical features

## Input requirements

- crystallographic information files (CIF)
- natural language descriptions of crystal structures

## Output goal

- novel crystal structures with high structural and compositional validity, thermodynamic stability, and broad elemental coverage

## Research stages

1. pretraining dataset construction (R2CID) (`data_acquisition`)
2. continued pretraining (CPT) (`model_training`)
3. instruction finetuning (IFT) on CIF (`model_training`)
4. parameter-efficient finetuning (PEFT) for generation (`model_training`)
5. quantitative assessment of crystal generation (`model_evaluation`)

## Dataset uses

- pretraining dataset construction (R2CID): R2CID (source, DU_P102_001)
- continued pretraining (CPT): R2CID (pretraining, DU_P102_002)
- instruction finetuning (IFT) on CIF: CIF Instruction Dataset (training, DU_P102_003)
- parameter-efficient finetuning (PEFT) for generation: CIF Instruction Dataset (training, DU_P102_004)
- quantitative assessment of crystal generation: Materials Project (label_source, DU_P102_005)
- quantitative assessment of crystal generation: American Mineralogist Crystal Structure Database (label_source, DU_P102_006)
- quantitative assessment of crystal generation: GNoME (label_source, DU_P102_007)
