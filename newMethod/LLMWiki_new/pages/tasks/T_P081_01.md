# Task: Optimizing the thermoelectric performance of n-type PbSe through dynamic doping driven by entropy engineering

- Task ID: `T_P081_01`
- Canonical name: `optimize_thermoelectric_performance_of_n_type_pbse_via_entropy_driven_dynamic_doping`
- Paper: `P081`

## Objective

Improve thermoelectric performance (ZT) of n-type PbSe by synergistically harnessing configurational entropy to drive dynamic doping effects across a wide temperature range.

## Material scope

- n-type PbSe
- Pb1−ySnySe1−xTexSx-2at%Cu

## Target properties

- dimensionless figure of merit (ZT)
- Seebeck coefficient (S)
- electrical conductivity (σ)
- lattice thermal conductivity (κlat)
- power factor (PF)

## Constraints

- maintain good electrical properties while reducing thermal conductivity
- decouple carrier and phonon transport
- overcome strong coupling between carrier and phonon transport
- mitigate deterioration of charge transport caused by entropy-driven disorder

## Input requirements

- high-purity elemental precursors (Cu, Pb, Sn, Se, Te, S)
- configurational entropy tuning via controlled alloying (Te/S/Sn content)
- Cu interstitial doping (2at%)

## Output goal

- high ZTmax (~1.46 at 623 K)
- high ZTave (~1.15 at 300–700 K)
- record high ZTRT (~0.64 at 300 K) for n-type PbSe

## Research stages

1. Experimental preparation of precursor materials (`data_acquisition`)
2. Synthesis and processing of samples (`data_preparation`)
3. Design of entropy-tuned compositions (`candidate_generation`)
4. Phase identification and microstructural characterization (`candidate_screening`)
5. First-principles electronic band structure calculations (`computational_validation`)
6. Measurement of thermoelectric transport properties (`experimental_validation`)
