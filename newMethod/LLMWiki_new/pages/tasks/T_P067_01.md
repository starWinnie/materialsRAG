# Task: Superconducting gap of H3S measured by tunnelling spectroscopy

- Task ID: `T_P067_01`
- Canonical name: `measure_superconducting_gap`
- Paper: `P067`

## Objective

To quantitatively determine the superconducting gap size and symmetry in hydrogen-rich superconductors H3S and D3S to provide experimental validation of the phonon-mediated pairing mechanism and complete theoretical understanding.

## Material scope

- H3S
- D3S
- HxS (H-depleted hydrogen sulfide phase)

## Target properties

- superconducting gap size (2Δ)
- superconducting gap symmetry
- isotope effect
- multigap features

## Constraints

- high-pressure environment (up to 172 GPa)
- diamond anvil cell geometry
- sample purity and phase homogeneity
- thermal smearing at elevated temperatures

## Input requirements

- planar N/I/S tunnel junctions
- tunnelling spectroscopy capability under megabar pressure
- X-ray powder diffraction for structural characterization
- electrical resistance measurement

## Output goal

- quantified gap values (e.g., 2Δ ≈ 60 meV for H3S, 44 meV for D3S)
- confirmation of fully gapped s-wave structure
- experimental evidence of isotope effect
- identification of concomitant HxS superconducting phase

## Research stages

1. Synthesis of planar tunnel junctions (`data_acquisition`)
2. Sample characterization via X-ray powder diffraction and electrical resistance (`data_preparation`)
3. Multiphase sample synthesis using alternative hydrogen source (`candidate_generation`)
4. Fitting tunnelling spectra with Dynes model (`model_evaluation`)
5. Comparison with BCS theory and isotope effect analysis (`computational_validation`)
6. Magnetic field suppression of coherence peaks (`experimental_validation`)
