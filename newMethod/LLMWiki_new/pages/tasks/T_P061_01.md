# Task: accurate property prediction in both molecules and crystals

- Task ID: `T_P061_01`
- Canonical name: `property_prediction_molecules_and_crystals`
- Paper: `P061`

## Objective

Develop universal MatErials Graph Network (MEGNet) models for accurate property prediction in both molecules and crystals

## Material scope

- molecules
- crystals

## Target properties

- internal energy at 0 K
- internal energy at room temperature
- enthalpy
- Gibbs free energy
- formation energies
- band gaps
- elastic moduli
- bulk modulus
- shear modulus
- metal/nonmetal classification

## Constraints

- must support relational reasoning and combinatorial generalization
- must incorporate global state (e.g., temperature, pressure, entropy)
- must handle data limitations via transfer learning

## Input requirements

- atomic attributes (e.g., atomic number, chirality, ring sizes, hybridization)
- bond attributes (e.g., bond type, spatial distance, expanded distance)
- global state attributes (e.g., temperature, pressure, entropy, average atomic weight, bonds per atom)

## Output goal

- scalar or vector predictions of target properties (e.g., formation energy in eV/atom, band gap in eV, bulk modulus in log10(GPa))

## Research stages

1. data collection from QM9 and Materials Project (`data_acquisition`)
2. construction of crystal graphs and attribute assignment (`data_preparation`)
3. training of MEGNet models for multiple properties (`model_training`)
4. evaluation of model performance against benchmarks (`model_evaluation`)
5. unified free energy model development (`candidate_generation`)
6. transfer learning of elemental embeddings (`candidate_screening`)
