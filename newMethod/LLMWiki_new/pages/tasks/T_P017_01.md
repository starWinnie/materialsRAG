# Task: Out-of-Distribution Property Prediction

- Task ID: `T_P017_01`
- Canonical name: `out_of_distribution_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P017`

## Objective

Train predictor models that extrapolate zero-shot to higher ranges than in the training data, given the chemical compositions of solids or molecular graphs and their property values.

## Material scope

- solid-state materials
- molecules

## Target properties

- band gap
- bulk modulus
- Debye temperature
- shear modulus
- thermal conductivity
- thermal expansion
- refractive index
- yield strength
- formation energy
- elastic anisotropy
- aqueous solubility
- hydration free energy
- lipophilicity
- binding affinity

## Constraints

- property values outside the known distribution
- zero-shot extrapolation to unseen property value ranges beyond the training distribution

## Input requirements

- chemical compositions of solids
- molecular graphs

## Output goal

- predicted property values for out-of-distribution (OOD) samples

## Research stages

1. Curating benchmark datasets for solids and molecules (`data_acquisition`)
2. Preprocessing datasets into representation spaces (`data_preparation`)
3. Defining OOD labels via top/bottom percentile thresholds (`label_generation`)
4. Training Bilinear Transduction predictor (`model_training`)
5. Screening for top OOD candidates using extrapolative precision (`candidate_screening`)
6. Evaluating OOD prediction accuracy and distributional alignment (`model_evaluation`)
