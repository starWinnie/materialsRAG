# Task: Enhancing Dielectric Breakdown Strength of BiFeO3‐BaTiO3 Ceramics via Bi2 Ti2 O7 Doping

- Task ID: `T_P111_01`
- Canonical name: `enhance_dielectric_breakdown_strength_of_bifeo3_batio3_ceramics_via_bito_doping`
- Paper: `P111`

## Objective

To develop high-performance capacitor materials

## Material scope

- BiFeO3-BaTiO3 ceramics
- Bi2Ti2O7

## Target properties

- dielectric breakdown strength
- energy storage density
- thermal stability

## Constraints

- lead-free
- high power density
- rapid charge-discharge capabilities

## Input requirements

- Bi2Ti2O7 doping
- solid-state reaction method
- perovskite solid solution formation

## Output goal

- high breakdown strength of 250 kV/cm
- high energy storage density of 4.14 J/cm3
- excellent thermal stability

## Research stages

1. Preparation of BFBT and BTO ceramic powders (`data_acquisition`)
2. Mixing of BFBT and BTO powders (`candidate_generation`)
3. Calcination and ball-milling of mixed powders (`model_training`)
4. Pressing and sintering to obtain ceramics (`candidate_screening`)
5. XRD analysis to confirm solid solution formation (`computational_validation`)
6. SEM analysis to examine microstructure and grain size (`experimental_validation`)
7. Dielectric and energy storage property testing (`experimental_validation`)
