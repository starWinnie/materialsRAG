# Task: Enhanced Magnetoelectric Coupling and Thermal Stability in Nd-Doped BiFeO3 Ceramics Near the Morphotropic Phase Boundary

- Task ID: `T_P109_01`
- Canonical name: `enhance_magnetoelectric_coupling_and_thermal_stability_via_mpb_engineering_in_nd_doped_bifeo3`
- Paper: `P109`

## Objective

To enhance magnetoelectric coupling and thermal stability in BiFeO3-based ceramics through morphotropic phase boundary engineering via Nd doping.

## Material scope

- Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramics
- 0.7BiFeO3-0.3BaTiO3 (0.7BFBT) ceramics for comparison

## Target properties

- dynamic magnetoelectric coefficient (αME)
- thermal stability of ME response
- electric-field control of magnetism
- four-state memory capability

## Constraints

- must operate at room temperature and up to 480 K
- must exhibit stable and switchable ME states
- must reside near the R3c–Pbam MPB

## Input requirements

- Nd-doped BiFeO3 composition series (x = 0.05–0.22)
- structural phase coexistence (R3c + Pbam)
- high piezoelectricity and magnetostriction

## Output goal

- Bi0.84Nd0.16FeO3 ceramic with αME ≈ 300 μV/(cm·Oe) at 9 kOe
- stable ME response from 300 K to 480 K
- electrically switchable four distinct ME states

## Research stages

1. Synthesis of Bi1-xNdxFeO3 ceramics (`data_acquisition`)
2. Structural and microstructural characterization (`data_preparation`)
3. Identification of MPB composition window (`candidate_generation`)
4. Ferroelectric and magnetic property screening (`candidate_screening`)
5. Dynamic magnetoelectric coefficient measurement (`model_evaluation`)
6. Composite ME testing and mechanical clamping experiments (`computational_validation`)
7. Thermal stability and electrical switching validation (`experimental_validation`)

## Evidence

- P109, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "In this study, Bi1- x Ndx FeO3 ceramics were synthesized to investigate the evolution of ME properties via phase boundary engineering. Structural analysis reveals a transition from the polar R3c phase to the antipolar Pbam phase, with the morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) centered around x = 0.16. A significantly enhanced dynamic ME coefficient of approximately 300 μV/(cm ⋅Oe) was observed at this critical composition."
- P109, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "These findings underline the role of the MPB in enhancing ME coupling, providing an effective route for developing multiferroic materials with high performance and thermal stability."
- P109, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "The Bi0.84 Nd0.16 FeO3 ceramics exhibit a stable ME response from room temperature up to 480 K and demonstrate potential for four-state memory applications."
