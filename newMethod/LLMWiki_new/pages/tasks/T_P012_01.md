# Task: Crystal material property prediction

- Task ID: `T_P012_01`
- Canonical name: `crystal_material_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P012`

## Objective

Predict scalar physical properties of crystalline materials from their 3D atomic structure

## Material scope

- crystalline materials

## Target properties

- Formation Energy
- Bandgap(OPT)
- Total Energy
- Ehull
- Bandgap(MBJ)
- Bulk Moduli(Kv)
- Shear Moduli(Gv)

## Constraints

- continuity under atomic position perturbations
- geometric completeness
- computational efficiency

## Input requirements

- 3D crystal structure (unit cell with atomic positions and types)
- lattice vectors

## Output goal

- scalar predictions of material properties

## Research stages

1. Use of benchmark crystal datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. Crystal graph construction with WPDD/UPDD (`data_preparation`)
3. Training PDDFormer architecture (`model_training`)
4. Quantitative evaluation on benchmark tasks (`model_evaluation`)
5. Ablation studies on (W/U)PDD contribution (`candidate_screening`)
6. Continuity and completeness validation via EMD and proof (`computational_validation`)
