# Task: molecular dynamics simulation using a neural network force field

- Task ID: `T_P032_01`
- Canonical name: `molecular_dynamics_simulation`
- Paper: `P032`

## Objective

To develop and evaluate a neural network force field for molecular dynamics simulation that relies minimally on architectural physical inductive biases (e.g., built-in rotational equivariance or energy conservation), instead learning approximate physical consistency from data.

## Material scope

- molecules
- ionic systems
- metallic systems

## Target properties

- atomic forces
- total energy conservation
- NVE/NVT simulation stability
- observables (e.g., h(r), velocity–velocity autocorrelation spectra)

## Constraints

- no built-in rotational equivariance
- no built-in energy conservation
- no automatic differentiation of energy to obtain forces
- dense attention with O(N³) complexity

## Input requirements

- 3D atomic positions
- atomic numbers
- spin and charge states
- pairwise distances and displacement vectors

## Output goal

- predicted atomic forces
- stable molecular dynamics trajectories (NVE and NVT ensembles)

## Research stages

1. acquisition of QCML dataset (`data_acquisition`)
2. dataset splitting and augmentation (`data_preparation`)
3. supervised pretraining of MD-ET on QCML (`model_training`)
4. benchmark evaluation on standard MD datasets (`candidate_screening`)
5. quantitative analysis of equivariance and energy conservation (`computational_validation`)
6. NVE and NVT molecular dynamics simulations (`experimental_validation`)
