# Task: CO dimerization on copper surfaces

- Task ID: `T_P062_01`
- Canonical name: `co_dimerization_on_copper_surfaces`
- Paper: `P062`

## Objective

Investigate CO dimerization on copper surfaces—a key step in CO2 electroreduction—to determine its free-energy profile and dependence on interfacial conditions including surface charge, cation identity, and surface facet.

## Material scope

- copper
- Cu(100)
- Cu(310)

## Target properties

- free-energy profile
- activation barrier (ΔG‡)
- reaction energy (ΔGrxn)

## Constraints

- electrified solid-liquid interface
- explicit solvent
- large cell size (>800 atoms)
- nanosecond timescales (up to 7 ns)

## Input requirements

- Cu surface
- adsorbed CO molecules
- explicit water solvent
- cations (Cs+, K+, Li+)
- controlled surface charge density

## Output goal

- quantitative free-energy profiles as function of C–C distance
- comparative ΔG‡ and ΔGrxn across charge, cation, and facet

## Research stages

1. OC25 dataset construction (`data_acquisition`)
2. Training eSEN-OC25 MLIP model (`model_training`)
3. Benchmarking OC25 models against AIMD references (`model_evaluation`)
4. DFT validation of MLIP-predicted configurations (`computational_validation`)
5. Systematic variation of interfacial conditions (`candidate_generation`)
6. Enhanced sampling with OPES to compute free-energy profiles (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- OC25 dataset construction: Open Catalyst 2025 (source, DU_P062_001)
- Training eSEN-OC25 MLIP model: Open Catalyst 2025 (training, DU_P062_002)
- Benchmarking OC25 models against AIMD references: Domínguez-Flores et al. AIMD reference for Rh(111)/water interface (benchmark, DU_P062_003)
- Benchmarking OC25 models against AIMD references: Kristoffersen and Chan AIMD reference for CO dimerization on 3×4 Cu(100)/water (benchmark, DU_P062_004)
- DFT validation of MLIP-predicted configurations: Open Catalyst 2025 (computational_validation, DU_P062_005)
