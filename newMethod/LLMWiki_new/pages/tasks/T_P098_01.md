# Task: preparation of polymethacrylate (PMA) from waste organic glass as a pour point depressor in heavy crude oil

- Task ID: `T_P098_01`
- Canonical name: `develop_polymethacrylate_pour_point_depressor_from_waste_pmma`
- Paper: `P098`

## Objective

To develop an effective, value-added pour point depressant for heavy crude oil by chemically repurposing waste polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA, organic glass) into polymethacrylate (PMA) via transesterification.

## Material scope

- polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA)
- waste organic glass
- heavy crude oil

## Target properties

- pour point depression
- viscosity reduction
- paraffin crystallization inhibition

## Constraints

- reuse of waste PMMA
- use of aliphatic alcohol feedstock
- base-catalyzed transesterification
- performance in real heavy oil samples (e.g., OS-1)

## Input requirements

- PMMA powder
- aliphatic alcohol (hexadecanol or octadecanol)
- CaO catalyst
- toluene solvent

## Output goal

- polymethacrylate (PMA) additive with demonstrated pour point depression ≥7.1 °C and viscosity reduction ≥63.7% in heavy crude oil

## Research stages

1. synthesis of polymethacrylate (PMA) from PMMA and aliphatic alcohol (`candidate_generation`)
2. evaluation of PMA candidates as pour point depressants and viscosity reducers in heavy crude oil (`candidate_screening`)
3. DSC analysis to study paraffin crystallization behavior (`computational_validation`)
4. polarized microscopy analysis of paraffin crystal morphology (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- synthesis of polymethacrylate (PMA) from PMMA and aliphatic alcohol: Jinghe Oil Field heavy crude oil samples OS-1 to OS-4 (source, DU_P098_001)
- evaluation of PMA candidates as pour point depressants and viscosity reducers in heavy crude oil: Jinghe Oil Field heavy crude oil samples OS-1 to OS-4 (test, DU_P098_002)
- DSC analysis to study paraffin crystallization behavior: Jinghe Oil Field heavy crude oil samples OS-1 to OS-4 (computational_validation, DU_P098_003)
- polarized microscopy analysis of paraffin crystal morphology: saturated hydrocarbons fraction from OS-1 (experimental_validation, DU_P098_004)
