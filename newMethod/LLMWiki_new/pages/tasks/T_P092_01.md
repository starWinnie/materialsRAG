# Task: Nitrogen Interstitial Alloying of CoCrFeMnNi High Entropy Alloy

- Task ID: `T_P092_01`
- Canonical name: `nitrogen_interstitial_alloying_of_cocrfemnni_high_entropy_alloy`
- Paper: `P092`

## Objective

To synthesize CoCrFeMnNi high entropy alloy interstitially alloyed with nitrogen via powder metallurgy routes

## Material scope

- CoCrFeMnNi high entropy alloy

## Target properties

- increased hardness
- formation of Cr2N nitride phase
- retention of FCC solid solution phase

## Constraints

- limited solubility of nitrogen in FCC phase
- need to avoid brittle phase formation
- economical and simple manufacturing route

## Input requirements

- elemental Co, Cr, Fe, Mn, Ni powders (≥99.5% purity)
- N2 gas atmosphere
- mechanical alloying equipment
- spark plasma sintering equipment

## Output goal

- nitrogen-doped CoCrFeMnNi HEA bulk material with controlled nitrogen content and enhanced hardness

## Research stages

1. Preparation of elemental powders (`data_acquisition`)
2. Reactive mechanical milling in N2 atmosphere (`candidate_generation`)
3. Mechanical milling with Cr2N addition (alternative route) (`candidate_generation`)
4. Characterization of milled powders (`candidate_screening`)
5. Spark plasma sintering (SPS) consolidation (`candidate_generation`)
6. Phase composition prediction using THERMOCALC (`computational_validation`)
7. Microstructural and mechanical characterization of SPS-ed bulks (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- Phase composition prediction using THERMOCALC: TCNI9 Ni-based alloy thermodynamic database (computational_validation, DU_P092_006)
