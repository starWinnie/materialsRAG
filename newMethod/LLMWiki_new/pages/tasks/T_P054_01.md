# Task: Creating fast and accurate force fields

- Task ID: `T_P054_01`
- Canonical name: `force_field_development`
- Paper: `P054`

## Objective

Develop fast and accurate interatomic potentials (force fields) for atomistic systems

## Material scope

- organic molecules
- drug-like molecules
- small organic molecules
- reactive molecules

## Target properties

- potential energy
- forces

## Constraints

- computational efficiency
- scalability
- parallelizability
- high accuracy
- extrapolation capability

## Input requirements

- atomic positions
- chemical elements
- DFT-computed energies and forces

## Output goal

- interatomic potential model that predicts energies and forces

## Research stages

1. Using DFT-computed energies and forces (`data_acquisition`)
2. Training MACE models (`model_training`)
3. Evaluating accuracy and computational cost on benchmarks (`model_evaluation`)

- Using DFT-computed energies and forces: revised MD17 (training, DU_P054_001)
- Using DFT-computed energies and forces: 3BPA (training, DU_P054_002)
