# Task: Grain boundary-induced plasticity during thin film buckling

- Task ID: `T_P100_01`
- Canonical name: `grain_boundary_induced_plasticity_during_thin_film_buckling`
- Paper: `P100`

## Objective

Investigate the effect of grain boundaries on the buckling of an Al thin film delaminated from its substrate and how GBs localize plasticity and modify the buckled film profile.

## Material scope

- Al thin film

## Target properties

- folding angle
- plastic strain
- buckling profile

## Constraints

- delaminated from substrate
- under compressive strain
- with grain boundary present

## Input requirements

- molecular dynamics simulations at finite temperature
- Σ51 (551) grain boundary
- Al thin film geometry and orientation

## Output goal

- quantified folding angle θ_f
- plastic strain ε_p
- analytically determined buckling profile matching simulations

## Research stages

1. Molecular dynamics simulations setup (`data_acquisition`)
2. Grain boundary positioning (`candidate_generation`)
3. Molecular dynamics simulation execution (`model_training`)
4. Plasticity mechanism analysis (`candidate_screening`)
5. Quantification of folding angle and plastic strain (`label_generation`)
6. Analytical buckling modeling with plastic parameters (`model_evaluation`)
7. Validation of dislocation-based plastic deformation model (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- Molecular dynamics simulations setup: Al thin film molecular dynamics simulation system with Σ51 (551) grain boundary (source, DU_P100_001)
- Grain boundary positioning: Al thin film MD dataset subset with three Σ51 (551) grain boundary positions (candidate_pool, DU_P100_002)
- Molecular dynamics simulation execution: Al thin film molecular dynamics simulation system with Σ51 (551) grain boundary (training, DU_P100_003)
- Plasticity mechanism analysis: Al thin film molecular dynamics simulation system with Σ51 (551) grain boundary (screening, DU_P100_004)
- Quantification of folding angle and plastic strain: Al thin film molecular dynamics simulation system with Σ51 (551) grain boundary (label_source, DU_P100_005)
- Analytical buckling modeling with plastic parameters: Al thin film MD dataset subset with three Σ51 (551) grain boundary positions (computational_validation, DU_P100_006)
- Validation of dislocation-based plastic deformation model: Al thin film MD dataset subset with three Σ51 (551) grain boundary positions (computational_validation, DU_P100_007)

## Evidence

- P100, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "The effect of grain boundaries (GB) on the buckling of an Al thin film delaminated from its substrate is investigated by means of molecular dynamics simulations at finite temperature. It is evidenced that the GB localizes the plasticity and deeply modifies the profile of the buckled thin film through the formation of a vertical fold at the location of the initial GB."
