# Task: phonon spectra and bandstructure prediction

- Task ID: `T_P027_01`
- Canonical name: `phonon_spectra_and_bandstructure_prediction`
- Paper: `P027`

## Objective

Direct prediction of Γ-phonon spectra and full phonon bandstructures from atomic coordinates

## Material scope

- crystalline materials
- alloy systems
- high-entropy alloys
- topological Weyl semimetals

## Target properties

- Γ-phonon spectra
- full phonon bandstructures
- phonon dispersion relations
- zone-center phonon energies

## Constraints

- input limited to atomic coordinates without prior knowledge of interatomic forces
- output must handle material-dependent, variable-length dimensions
- must generalize to complex materials with large unit cells (up to 400+ atoms)

## Input requirements

- atomic coordinates
- unit cell vectors
- chemical composition

## Output goal

- 3m Γ-phonon energies for m atoms per unit cell
- full phonon band structure at arbitrary k points in the Brillouin zone

## Research stages

1. acquisition of ab initio phonon data (`data_acquisition`)
2. preprocessing of crystal structures and phonon data (`data_preparation`)
3. training of virtual node graph neural networks (`model_training`)
4. evaluation of phonon prediction accuracy (`model_evaluation`)
5. validation on alloy systems and complex materials (`computational_validation`)
6. generation of Γ-phonon database for Materials Project (`candidate_generation`)

## Dataset uses

- acquisition of ab initio phonon data: DFPT computational database for phonon dispersion (source, DU_P027_001)
- acquisition of ab initio phonon data: Atsushi Togo's phonon database (source, DU_P027_002)
- preprocessing of crystal structures and phonon data: DFPT computational database for phonon dispersion (training, DU_P027_003)
- preprocessing of crystal structures and phonon data: Atsushi Togo's phonon database (test, DU_P027_004)
- evaluation of phonon prediction accuracy: DFPT computational database for phonon dispersion (test, DU_P027_005)
- validation on alloy systems and complex materials: Atsushi Togo's phonon database (computational_validation, DU_P027_006)
- generation of Γ-phonon database for Materials Project: Materials Project Γ-phonon database (candidate_pool, DU_P027_007)
