# Task: Postsynthetic modification of yttria-stabilized zirconia aerogels with silica coatings for enhanced thermal stability

- Task ID: `T_P091_01`
- Canonical name: `silica_coating_of_ysz_aerogels_for_thermal_stability`
- Paper: `P091`

## Objective

Improve the pore structure stability of yttria-stabilized zirconia aerogels by the addition of a SiO2 coating to mitigate condensation reactions, which are a driving force for shrinkage and pore structure collapse.

## Material scope

- yttria-stabilized zirconia aerogels
- 0 mol% YO1.5
- 10 mol% YO1.5
- 30 mol% YO1.5

## Target properties

- thermal stability
- pore structure stability
- specific surface area retention
- mesoporosity retention

## Constraints

- upper use temperature limit of SiO2 coating at 1200°C due to rapid sintering and densification

## Input requirements

- YSZ aerogels with 0, 10, and 30 mol% YO1.5
- tetraethyl orthosilicate (TEOS) solution
- surface hydroxyl groups on aerogel

## Output goal

- SiO2-coated YSZ aerogels with enhanced thermal stability up to 1000°C

## Research stages

1. Preparation of YSZ aerogels with 0, 10, and 30 mol% YO1.5 (`data_acquisition`)
2. Coating YSZ aerogels with silica via TEOS solution (`candidate_generation`)
3. Quantification of Si deposition via EDS (`computational_validation`)
4. Heat treatment and characterization of coated and uncoated aerogels (`experimental_validation`)
5. Analysis of thermal stability via nitrogen physisorption and XRD (`model_evaluation`)
