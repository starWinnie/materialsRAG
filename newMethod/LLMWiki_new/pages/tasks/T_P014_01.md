# Task: predicting plastic flow strength from small-scale indicator properties

- Task ID: `T_P014_01`
- Canonical name: `predict_plastic_flow_strength_from_small_scale_indicators`
- Paper: `P014`

## Objective

Predict large-scale plastic flow strength using small-scale indicator properties computed with the same interatomic potential, and estimate prediction uncertainty

## Material scope

- FCC metals

## Target properties

- plastic flow strength

## Constraints

- statistical pool of interatomic potentials must be representative
- small-scale indicators must be computable where first-principles validation is feasible

## Input requirements

- interatomic potential (IP)
- small-scale indicator properties (e.g., elastic constants, surface energies, vacancy formation/migration energies)

## Output goal

- predicted plastic flow strength with quantified statistical error bounds

## Research stages

1. collecting interatomic potentials and associated small-scale property data from OpenKIM (`data_acquisition`)
2. screening IPs for numerical instabilities and anomalous crystal responses (`candidate_screening`)
3. imputing missing or out-of-bounds small-scale indicator values (`data_preparation`)
4. developing new vacancy-related indicator properties (`candidate_generation`)
5. training multi-linear regression models on small-scale indicators (`model_training`)
6. predicting flow strength from first-principles DFT-calculated indicators (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- collecting interatomic potentials and associated small-scale property data from OpenKIM: OpenKIM Interatomic Potentials Repository (source, DU_P014_001)
- screening IPs for numerical instabilities and anomalous crystal responses: OpenKIM-derived statistical pool of 178 interatomic potentials (screening, DU_P014_002)
- imputing missing or out-of-bounds small-scale indicator values: OpenKIM-derived statistical pool of 163 interatomic potentials after instability filtering (training, DU_P014_003)
- developing new vacancy-related indicator properties: OpenKIM-derived statistical pool of 163 interatomic potentials after instability filtering (candidate_pool, DU_P014_004)
- training multi-linear regression models on small-scale indicators: OpenKIM-derived statistical pool of 153 interatomic potentials after SF jamming exclusion (training, DU_P014_005)
- predicting flow strength from first-principles DFT-calculated indicators: DFT-computed small-scale predictors for seven FCC metals (computational_validation, DU_P014_006)

## Evidence

- P014, PDF page 1: "Here we explore covariance between predictions of metal plasticity, from 178 large-scale (~108 atoms) molecular dynamics (MD) simulations, and a variety of indicator properties computed at small-scales (≤102 atoms). All simulations use the same 178 IPs. In a manner similar to statistical studies in public health, we analyze correlations of strength with indicators, identify the best predictor properties, and build a cross-scale “strength-on-predictors” regression model. This model is then used to estimate regression error over the statistical pool of IPs."
