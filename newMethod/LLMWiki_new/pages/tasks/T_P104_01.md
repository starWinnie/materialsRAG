# Task: Fast firing of 3 mol% yttria-stabilized zirconia: On the effect of heating rate on sintering

- Task ID: `T_P104_01`
- Canonical name: `heating_rate_effect_on_sintering_of_3ysz`
- Paper: `P104`

## Objective

To investigate the effect of heating rate on the sintering behavior of ≈40 nm 3YSZ powder and determine whether fast firing enhances densification compared to conventional sintering.

## Material scope

- 3 mol% yttria-stabilized zirconia (3YSZ)
- ≈40 nm particle size
- TZ-3YB-E powder (Tosoh Corporation)

## Target properties

- relative density
- open porosity
- closed porosity
- pore size distribution
- grain size
- microstructure

## Constraints

- sintering in static air atmosphere
- particle size intermediate between previously studied 20 nm and 90 nm powders
- no electric field or microwave radiation applied

## Input requirements

- ≈40 nm 3YSZ powder (SSA = 16 m2 g−1)
- uniaxial pressing to green pellets (49% relative density)
- debinding at 600°C for 30 min

## Output goal

- quantified effect of heating rate on final density (≤3% improvement)
- validation of master sintering curve applicability to fast firing
- characterization of pore and microstructural evolution under different heating schedules

## Research stages

1. Dilatometric measurement of sintering behavior at multiple heating rates (`data_acquisition`)
2. Fitting Boltzmann function to dilatometric data to extract master sintering curve (`model_training`)
3. Preparation of green pellets and application of three sintering protocols (`candidate_generation`)
4. Finite element modeling of sample thermal history during fast firing (`computational_validation`)
5. Comparison of experimental sintering densities against master sintering curve (`model_evaluation`)
6. Characterization of density, porosity, and microstructure across sintering conditions (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- Dilatometric measurement of sintering behavior at multiple heating rates: Dilatometric sintering data for ≈40 nm 3YSZ (TZ-3YB-E) (source, DU_P104_001)
- Fitting Boltzmann function to dilatometric data to extract master sintering curve: Dilatometric sintering data for ≈40 nm 3YSZ (TZ-3YB-E) (training, DU_P104_002)
- Preparation of green pellets and application of three sintering protocols: Experimental characterization dataset of sintered 3YSZ pellets under three thermal schedules (candidate_pool, DU_P104_003)
- Finite element modeling of sample thermal history during fast firing: FEM-simulated temperature evolution inside 3YSZ pellet during fast firing (computational_validation, DU_P104_004)
- Comparison of experimental sintering densities against master sintering curve: Dilatometric sintering data for ≈40 nm 3YSZ (TZ-3YB-E) (benchmark, DU_P104_005)
- Characterization of density, porosity, and microstructure across sintering conditions: Experimental characterization dataset of sintered 3YSZ pellets under three thermal schedules (experimental_validation, DU_P104_006)
