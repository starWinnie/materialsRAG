# Task: fast screening and mapping energy landscape for multicomponent systems

- Task ID: `T_P064_01`
- Canonical name: `screen_and_map_energy_landscape`
- Paper: `P064`

## Objective

To rapidly screen candidate materials and map their energy landscape (e.g., convex hull) for multicomponent systems using ML-predicted formation energies

## Material scope

- 3D bulk materials
- 2D layered materials
- multicomponent systems up to senary compounds
- materials with up to 6 components, 201 space groups, 7 crystal systems

## Target properties

- formation energy
- exfoliation energy
- bandgap
- static refractive index
- magnetic properties
- modulus of elasticity

## Constraints

- exfoliation energy < 200 meV/atom for exfoliability
- ground-state structure identification required
- models must reproduce DFT convex hull

## Input requirements

- chemo-structural descriptors (CFID): radial distribution, nearest neighbor, bond-angle, dihedral-angle, core-charge, and chemical property distributions

## Output goal

- list of exfoliable 2D semiconducting materials satisfying bandgap (1.2–3 eV), negative formation energy, and exfoliation energy < 200 meV/atom
- convex hull plots of energy landscape for Mo-S, W-S, and Mo-W-S systems

## Research stages

1. collect training data from JARVIS-DFT database (`data_acquisition`)
2. compute classical force-field inspired descriptors (CFID) (`data_preparation`)
3. train gradient boosting decision tree (GBDT) models (`model_training`)
4. evaluate model performance using MAE, ROC, and learning curves (`model_evaluation`)
5. generate candidate 2D layered materials using lattice constant and data-mining approaches (`candidate_generation`)
6. screen candidates using ML-predicted properties (`candidate_screening`)
7. validate screened candidates with DFT exfoliation energy calculations (`computational_validation`)
8. verify ML formation energy model via GA-driven convex hull generation (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- collect training data from JARVIS-DFT database: JARVIS-DFT (source, DU_P064_001)
