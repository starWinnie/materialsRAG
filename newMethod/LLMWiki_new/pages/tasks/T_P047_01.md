# Task: generative design of crystal materials with symmetry constraints

- Task ID: `T_P047_01`
- Canonical name: `generative_design_of_crystal_materials_with_symmetry_constraints`
- Paper: `P047`

## Objective

efficient crystal material design with high structural diversity and symmetry

## Material scope

- ternary inorganic crystal materials
- 20 space groups (5 crystal systems)

## Target properties

- structural validity
- symmetry preservation
- thermodynamic stability
- lattice parameter accuracy
- formation energy
- energy above hull

## Constraints

- symmetry constraints
- atomic pairwise distance constraints
- space group constraints
- fractional coordinate constraints

## Input requirements

- random noise Z
- element set E
- space group sgp
- affine matrix O

## Output goal

- valid crystal structures represented as unit cells (E, B, P, O) with correct symmetry, realistic atom coordinates, and stable lattice parameters

## Research stages

1. collecting material data from MP, ICSD and OQMD (`data_acquisition`)
2. dataset curation and train/validation/test split (`data_preparation`)
3. training PGCGM with physics-guided losses (`model_training`)
4. generating candidate crystal structures (`candidate_generation`)
5. filtering candidates using pymatgen readability and symmetry retention (`candidate_screening`)
6. Bayesian Optimization with Symmetry Relaxation (BOWSR) (`computational_validation`)
7. Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- collecting material data from MP, ICSD and OQMD: Materials Project (source, DU_P047_001)
- collecting material data from MP, ICSD and OQMD: ICSD (source, DU_P047_002)
- collecting material data from MP, ICSD and OQMD: Open Quantum Materials Database v1.4 (source, DU_P047_003)
