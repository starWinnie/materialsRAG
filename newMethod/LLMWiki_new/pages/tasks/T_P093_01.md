# Task: Study the susceptibility to SCC of 7050 aluminum alloy

- Task ID: `T_P093_01`
- Canonical name: `study_scc_susceptibility_of_7050_aluminum_alloy`
- Paper: `P093`

## Objective

Determine how aging state and cathodic polarization affect the susceptibility to stress corrosion cracking (SCC) of 7050 aluminum alloy

## Material scope

- 7050 aluminum alloy

## Target properties

- susceptibility to stress corrosion cracking (SCC)
- critical stress intensity factor (KISCC)
- hydrogen concentration near crack tips
- crack propagation rate

## Constraints

- tested in 3.5% NaCl aqueous solution at 25 ± 10 °C
- cathodic polarization potential of −1100 mV applied
- aging states: under-aged (8 h), peak-aged (16 h), over-aged (24 h)

## Input requirements

- DCB specimens of 7050 aluminum alloy with defined aging states
- corrosive environment (3.5% NaCl)
- cathodic polarization setup

## Output goal

- quantified SCC susceptibility (Iscc) and KISCC values
- correlation between aging time, cathodic polarization, hydrogen distribution, and SCC behavior

## Research stages

1. Prepare and age 7050 aluminum alloy specimens (`data_acquisition`)
2. Fabricate DCB specimens according to ISO 7539 (`candidate_generation`)
3. Perform SCC testing with cathodic polarization (`experimental_validation`)
4. Calculate stress intensity factor (KI) and SCC susceptibility (Iscc) (`computational_validation`)
5. Analyze fracture morphology and microstructure using SEM and TEM (`experimental_validation`)
6. Measure hydrogen distribution near crack tips using ToF-SIMS (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- None stated
