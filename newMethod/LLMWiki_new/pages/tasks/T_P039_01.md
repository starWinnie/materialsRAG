# Task: developing a foundation machine learning interatomic potential with polarizable long-range interactions

- Task ID: `T_P039_01`
- Canonical name: `develop_foundation_ml_interatomic_potential_with_polarizable_long_range_interactions`
- Paper: `P039`

## Objective

to construct a mapping from atomic coordinates ri and atomic types Zi to the total potential energy Epot that integrates explicit polarizable long-range physics with an equivariant graph neural network potential

## Material scope

- materials across the periodic table up to Pu
- charged clusters (e.g., Ag3+/−, Na8/9Cl8+)
- molecular systems (e.g., water, C10H2/C10H3+)
- periodic systems (e.g., Au2-MgO, BTA-Cu, BaTiO3, c-LLZO, β-Li3PS4/Li)

## Target properties

- total potential energy
- forces
- stress
- partial charges
- electrostatic interaction energies
- polarization effects
- mechanical properties (e.g., bulk modulus)
- ionic diffusivity
- phase transition behavior
- reactive interface dynamics

## Constraints

- must maintain computational efficiency
- must capture long-range electrostatic and dispersion interactions explicitly
- must reproduce polarization effects under external electric fields
- must be transferable across diverse chemical systems and charge states

## Input requirements

- atomic coordinates (ri)
- atomic types (Zi)
- boundary conditions

## Output goal

- total potential energy Epot = E0 + EPQEq + ED3
- forces
- stress
- partial charges
- accurate prediction of materials properties and behaviors

## Research stages

1. acquiring training datasets (`data_acquisition`)
2. training the foundation equivariant neural network potential with polarizable long-range interactions (`model_training`)
3. benchmarking the foundation model on diverse charge-state datasets (`model_evaluation`)
4. benchmarking the foundation model on the MPtrj dataset and long-range dimer interactions (`model_evaluation`)
5. applying the foundation model to materials modeling applications (`computational_validation`)
6. finetuning the foundation model for ab initio accuracy on specific challenging systems (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- acquiring training datasets: MPtrj dataset (training, DU_P039_001)
- benchmarking the foundation model on diverse charge-state datasets: Ko et al. charge-state dataset (benchmark, DU_P039_002)
- benchmarking the foundation model on diverse charge-state datasets: Maruf et al. charge-state dataset (benchmark, DU_P039_003)
- benchmarking the foundation model on the MPtrj dataset and long-range dimer interactions: MPtrj dataset (test, DU_P039_004)
- finetuning the foundation model for ab initio accuracy on specific challenging systems: Ko et al. charge-state dataset (candidate_pool, DU_P039_005)
