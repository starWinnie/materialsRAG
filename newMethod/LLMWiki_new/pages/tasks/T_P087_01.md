# Task: modelling of the pre-strain effect on the creep behaviour

- Task ID: `T_P087_01`
- Canonical name: `model_pre_strain_effect_on_creep_behaviour`
- Paper: `P087`

## Objective

To establish a steady-state creep rate model that captures the influence of room-temperature pre-strain on the creep behaviour of P/M Ni-based superalloy FGH96.

## Material scope

- P/M Ni-based superalloy FGH96

## Target properties

- steady-state creep rate
- creep strain

## Constraints

- pre-strain applied at room temperature
- creep testing at 700 °C and 690 MPa
- 70 h duration
- micro-twinning as dominant creep mechanism

## Input requirements

- pre-strain amount (plastic strain)
- microstructural parameters (e.g., γ′ volume fraction, diameter, dislocation density)
- material constants (e.g., Burgers vector, formation energies, diffusion coefficient)

## Output goal

- predicted steady-state creep rate as a function of pre-strain

## Research stages

1. room-temperature pre-tension experiments and subsequent creep tests (`data_acquisition`)
2. microstructural characterization of pre-strained and crept specimens (`data_preparation`)
3. establishment of the steady-state creep rate model with pre-strain effect (`model_training`)
4. validation of the creep rate model against experimental data (`model_evaluation`)

- None stated
