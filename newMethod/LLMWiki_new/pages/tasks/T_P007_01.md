# Task: Accurate piezoelectric tensor prediction

- Task ID: `T_P007_01`
- Canonical name: `piezoelectric_tensor_prediction`
- Paper: `P007`

## Objective

To accurately predict the complete third-rank piezoelectric tensor for crystalline materials while respecting crystal symmetry and rotational equivariance.

## Material scope

- bulk crystals
- two-dimensional materials

## Target properties

- piezoelectric tensor (eijk)

## Constraints

- absence of inversion symmetry
- non-conductor

## Input requirements

- crystal structure

## Output goal

- full third-rank piezoelectric tensor in Voigt notation (3×6 matrix for bulk, 3×3 for 2D)

## Research stages

1. Data acquisition from computational databases (`data_acquisition`)
2. Data resampling and symmetry correction (`data_preparation`)
3. Training EATGNN with mean-squared-error loss (`model_training`)
4. Evaluation using MAE and RMSE on test sets (`model_evaluation`)
5. High-throughput screening of candidate materials (`candidate_generation`)
6. DFPT validation of candidate materials (`computational_validation`)
