# Task: reverse regulation from particles to clusters

- Task ID: `T_P040_01`
- Canonical name: `reverse_regulation_from_particles_to_clusters`
- Paper: `P040`

## Objective

Achieve thermodynamically stable subnanometer Ni clusters via in situ dispersion of pre-formed Ni particles under high-temperature reducing conditions

## Material scope

- Ni
- La-doped CeO2
- CeO2

## Target properties

- thermal stability
- high H2 production rate
- atomic utilization efficiency

## Constraints

- high-temperature operation (>500 °C)
- resistance to sintering
- structural integrity of support

## Input requirements

- Ni particles on La-doped CeO2 support
- high-temperature reducing atmosphere (NH3 or H2)

## Output goal

- stable Ni clusters (1–2 nm)
- thermodynamic steady state
- enhanced catalytic performance in ammonia decomposition

## Research stages

1. synthesis of catalysts (`data_acquisition`)
2. pretreatment to induce structural evolution (`candidate_generation`)
3. DFT and ML-MD simulations of adsorption and dispersion energetics (`computational_validation`)
4. in situ ETEM and HAADF-STEM observation of dynamic dispersion (`experimental_validation`)
5. correlative analysis of structural, electronic, and catalytic properties (`model_evaluation`)
