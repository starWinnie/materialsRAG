- Task ID: `T_P003_01`

## Objective

map crystal structures directly to their full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors

## Material scope

- bulk semiconductors

## Target properties

- frequency-dependent dielectric tensor
- full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors

## Constraints

- symmetry-aware predictions consistent with crystalline symmetry constraints

## Input requirements

- crystal structures

## Output goal

- full photon-frequency-dependent optical tensors
