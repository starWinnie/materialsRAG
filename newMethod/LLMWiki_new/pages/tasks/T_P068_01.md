# Task: improved the thermoelectric efficiency in SnS crystals by promoting the convergence of energy and momentum of four valance bands, termed quadruple-band synglisis

- Task ID: `T_P068_01`
- Canonical name: `improve_thermoelectric_efficiency_via_quadruple_band_synglisis`
- Paper: `P068`

## Objective

Improve thermoelectric efficiency in earth-abundant tin sulfide (SnS) crystals

## Material scope

- tin sulfide (SnS)
- p-type SnS crystals
- SnS0.9Se0.1+xSnS2

## Target properties

- dimensionless figure of merit (ZT)
- power factor (PF)
- electrical conductivity (σ)
- Seebeck coefficient (S)
- hole carrier concentration (nH)
- cooling temperature difference (ΔTmax)

## Constraints

- earth-abundant
- wide-bandgap (Eg ≈46 kBT)
- p-type
- crystal form

## Input requirements

- Sn vacancies
- SnS2 induction
- Se-alloying
- Na doping

## Output goal

- high ZT of ~1.0 at 300 K
- average ZT of ~1.3 at 300 to 773 K
- power factor (PF) of ~58 mW cm−1 K−2 at 300 K
- experimental efficiency of ~6.5%
- cooling temperature difference of ~48.4 K at 353 K

## Research stages

1. refined the composition of p-type Se-alloyed SnS crystal, 2% Na–doped SnS1−ySey (`data_preparation`)
2. introduced SnS2 into the SnS0.9Se0.1 matrix (`candidate_generation`)
3. measured the electrical transport properties of SnS0.9Se0.1+xSnS2 (`candidate_screening`)
4. characterized the temperature-dependent band structures by using high-temperature synchrotron radiation x-ray diffraction (SR-XRD), angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES), and density functional theory (DFT) calculations (`computational_validation`)
5. fabricated a single-leg thermoelectric device and characterized power generation performance; assembled a seven-pair thermoelectric cooling device (`experimental_validation`)
