# Task: Low-Temperature Densification of Si3N4 Ceramics With MnO as a Sintering Additive

- Task ID: `T_P077_01`
- Canonical name: `low_temperature_densification_of_silicon_nitride_ceramics_with_mno_additive`
- Paper: `P077`

## Objective

Achieve near-theoretical densification of Si3N4 ceramics at low sintering temperature using MnO as a sintering additive, while enabling controllable α–β phase transformation and tailored mechanical properties.

## Material scope

- Si3N4 ceramics
- MnO–Al2O3 additive system

## Target properties

- density
- α/β phase ratio
- grain morphology
- hardness
- fracture toughness

## Constraints

- low sintering temperature (e.g., 1450°C)
- non-rare-earth additive
- transition metal oxide usage

## Input requirements

- α-Si3N4 powder
- MnO powder
- Al2O3 powder
- spark plasma sintering (SPS) equipment

## Output goal

- dense Si3N4 ceramics with tunable phase composition and mechanical properties

## Research stages

1. Powder Preparation (`data_preparation`)
2. Spark Plasma Sintering (SPS) (`candidate_generation`)
3. Characterizations (`model_evaluation`)
4. Densification Analysis (`candidate_screening`)
5. Phase Transformation and Microstructure Screening (`candidate_screening`)
6. Mechanical Properties Correlation (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- None stated

## Evidence

- P077, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "Dense Si3N4 ceramics were fabricated by spark plasma sintering (SPS) using a MnO–Al2O3 additive system, and the effects of sintering temperature, pressure and holding time on densification behavior, phase transformation, microstructure development and mechanical properties were systematically investigated. Near-theoretical densification was achieved at a relatively low temperature of 1450◦C, demonstrating the strong fluxing effect of MnO in promoting liquid-phase-assisted sintering."
