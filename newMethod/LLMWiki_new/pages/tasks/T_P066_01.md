# Task: AI-driven design of multiprincipal element alloys for optimal water splitting

- Task ID: `T_P066_01`
- Canonical name: `design_multiprincipal_element_alloys_for_water_splitting`
- Paper: `P066`

## Objective

Identify the optimal multiprincipal element alloy composition for bifunctional (HER and OER) water splitting electrocatalysis with ultralow overpotentials and robust stability.

## Material scope

- multiprincipal element alloys
- high-entropy alloys
- Fe12Co28Ni33Mo17Pd5Pt5
- Co50Ni40Pd5Pt5
- Mn-Fe-Co-Ni-Cu-Mo-Pd-Pt system

## Target properties

- hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) overpotential
- oxygen evolution reaction (OER) overpotential
- overall water splitting overpotential (ηOWS)
- stability
- electrocatalytic activity

## Constraints

- compositional boundaries (e.g., xi ≥ 0, Σxi = 1)
- high-entropy region preference (1/9 ≤ xi ≤ 4/9)
- solid solution phase formation
- experimental synthesizability

## Input requirements

- elemental composition (7 elements: Mn, Fe, Co, Ni, Cu, Mo, Pd, Pt)
- synthesis conditions (precursor concentration, solvent, additive, time, temperature)
- configuration entropy (Sconfig)

## Output goal

- optimal alloy composition (Fe12Co28Ni33Mo17Pd5Pt5)
- ultralow overpotentials (24 mV HER, 204 mV OER at 10 mA·cm−2)
- robust stability

## Research stages

1. Synthesis condition tuning via confounding method (`data_acquisition`)
2. Compositional space partitioning via Design of Experiments (DoE) (`data_preparation`)
3. Bayesian optimization over 16.2 million candidates (`candidate_generation`)
4. Automated synthesis and overpotential measurement of candidates (`candidate_screening`)
5. Density functional theory (DFT) calculations and structural analysis (`computational_validation`)
6. Electrochemical and physical characterization of the optimal alloy (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- None stated
