# Task: Ag precursor-assisted low-temperature growth of Cu(In,Ga)Se2 solar cells

- Task ID: `T_P118_01`
- Canonical name: `ag_precursor_assisted_low_temperature_cigs_growth`
- Paper: `P118`

## Objective

Enable low-temperature (350 °C) growth of high-quality Cu(In,Ga)Se2 (CIGS) absorber layers using the conventional three-stage deposition process by introducing a thin Ag precursor layer.

## Material scope

- Cu(In,Ga)Se2
- (Ag,Cu)(In,Ga)Se2
- ACIGS

## Target properties

- minority-carrier lifetime
- diode parameters
- Urbach energy
- power conversion efficiency
- net acceptor density
- grain size
- crystallinity
- bandgap
- shunt resistance
- fill factor

## Constraints

- substrate temperature ≤ 350 °C
- maintain conventional three-stage deposition sequence
- avoid severe performance degradation
- suppress GaOx formation on TCOs
- enable bifacial and tandem integration

## Input requirements

- Ag precursor layer of controlled thickness
- Mo-coated soda-lime glass substrate
- elemental fluxes for Cu, In, Ga, Se
- three-stage MBE deposition process

## Output goal

- (Ag,Cu)(In,Ga)Se2 solar cells with ~15% power conversion efficiency at 350 °C

## Research stages

1. Varying Ag precursor thickness to identify optimum level (`candidate_generation`)
2. Evaluating Ag precursor thickness effects on absorber quality and device performance (`candidate_screening`)
3. Device simulation to clarify origin of Ag-induced changes (`computational_validation`)
4. Low-temperature deposition and characterization of ACIGS absorbers (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- Varying Ag precursor thickness to identify optimum level: ACIGS absorber series with varied Ag precursor thickness at 600 °C (candidate_pool, DU_P118_001)
- Evaluating Ag precursor thickness effects on absorber quality and device performance: ACIGS absorber series with varied Ag precursor thickness at 600 °C (screening, DU_P118_002)
- Device simulation to clarify origin of Ag-induced changes: SCAPS-1D material and defect parameters used in simulations (computational_validation, DU_P118_003)
- Low-temperature deposition and characterization of ACIGS absorbers: ACIGS absorber series with fixed 10-nm Ag precursor at 600, 475, and 350 °C (experimental_validation, DU_P118_004)
