# Task: fabricate sub-micron alumina grained ceramics by forming ultra-high-density Al2O3 green bodies through low PF of high solid loading slurries

- Task ID: `T_P094_01`
- Canonical name: `fabricate_fine_grained_alumina_ceramics_via_high_density_green_body_formation`
- Paper: `P094`

## Objective

fabricate sub-micron grained alumina ceramics by forming ultra-high-density Al2O3 green bodies through low pressure filtration of high solid loading slurries

## Material scope

- α-Al2O3

## Target properties

- relative density ≥ 67.4% (green)
- relative density ≥ 99.9% (sintered)
- average grain size ≤ 0.70 μm
- flexural strength ≥ 631 MPa
- hardness ≥ 18.5 GPa

## Constraints

- low sintering temperature (1300°C, pressureless)
- no sintering additives
- simplified sintering conditions

## Input requirements

- super-fine α-Al2O3 powder (d50 = 180 nm, 100–300 nm distribution)
- low molecular weight dispersant (CE-64, 320 g/mol)
- solid loading ≥ 55 vol%
- low viscosity (≤ 0.40 Pa·s)

## Output goal

- ultra-high-density Al2O3 green bodies enabling fine-grained, nearly fully dense ceramics at reduced sintering temperatures

## Research stages

1. Preparation of alumina slurry (`data_preparation`)
2. Forming green bodies via pressure filtration and slip casting (`candidate_generation`)
3. Optimization of slurry composition and PF pressure (`candidate_screening`)
4. Characterization of slurry rheology and zeta potential (`computational_validation`)
5. Sintering and mechanical property evaluation (`experimental_validation`)
