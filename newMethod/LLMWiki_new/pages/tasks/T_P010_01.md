# Task: crystal property prediction

- Task ID: `T_P010_01`
- Canonical name: `crystal_property_prediction`
- Paper: `P010`

## Objective

predict crystal properties such as formation energy, bandgap, bulk modulus, shear modulus, and energy above hull

## Material scope

- crystals

## Target properties

- formation energy
- bandgap
- bulk modulus
- shear modulus
- energy above hull

## Constraints

- SE(3)-invariance
- periodic SE(3) invariance

## Input requirements

- crystal structure represented by unit cell (A, P, L)

## Output goal

- mean absolute error for regression targets

## Research stages

1. using datasets derived from JARVIS, Materials Project (MP), and Open Quantum Materials Database (OQMD) (`data_acquisition`)
2. using consistent data splits and preprocessing (`data_preparation`)
3. training CrystalFramer with dynamic frames (`model_training`)
4. comparing mean absolute errors on crystal property prediction tasks (`model_evaluation`)
