# Task: A dataset of micro-scale tomograms of unidirectional glass fibre/epoxy and carbon fibre/epoxy composites acquired via synchrotron computed tomography during in-situ tensile loading

- Task ID: `T_P079_01`
- Canonical name: `dataset_generation_for_microstructural_analysis_of_fiber_reinforced_composites_under_tensile_loading`
- Paper: `P079`

## Objective

To generate a publicly available, high-resolution 3D tomographic dataset capturing the evolving microstructure of two common unidirectional fiber-reinforced composites during in-situ tensile loading.

## Material scope

- glass fiber/epoxy composite
- carbon fiber/epoxy composite

## Target properties

- fiber distribution
- fiber orientation
- fiber misalignment
- fiber breaks
- microstructural evolution under tensile loading

## Constraints

- voxel size = 1.1 μm
- loading steps at ~0%, 40%, 73%, and 95% of failure load
- volume of interest cropped from middle 0° ply
- double-edge notched specimen geometry

## Input requirements

- synchrotron X-ray source (TOMCAT beamline)
- in-situ tensile loading rig
- cross-ply [90/0]s laminates with specified fibers and epoxy
- waterjet-cut miniature specimens

## Output goal

- sixteen 3D image stacks: eight raw and eight filtered, organized by material (glass-epoxy/carbon-epoxy), loading step (Step0–Step3), and processing (Raw/Filtered)

## Research stages

1. synchrotron X-ray computed tomography during in-situ tensile loading (`data_acquisition`)
2. 3D reconstruction and volume registration (`data_preparation`)
3. volume of interest (VOI) cropping and coordinate alignment (`data_preparation`)
4. image filtering (ring artifact removal and non-local means) (`data_preparation`)
