# Task: atroposelective copper-catalyzed aerobic oxidation of biaryl diols

- Task ID: `T_P084_01`
- Canonical name: `atroposelective_copper_catalyzed_aerobic_oxidation_of_biaryl_diols`
- Paper: `P084`

## Objective

To develop a bifunctional chiral peptide catalyst enabling highly enantioselective aerobic oxidation of prochiral biaryl diols to axially chiral monoaldehydes using ambient air as terminal oxidant.

## Material scope

- biaryl diols
- axially chiral aldehydes

## Target properties

- enantioselectivity (er)
- chemoselectivity (mono- vs. bis-oxidation)

## Constraints

- use of ambient air as terminal oxidant
- copper(I) catalysis
- atroposelectivity
- bifunctional catalyst design (aminoxyl + bipyridyl in one peptide)

## Input requirements

- prochiral biaryl diol substrate
- copper(I) source
- bifunctional aminoxyl-bipyridine peptide catalyst

## Output goal

- axially chiral monoaldehyde product with high enantiomeric ratio (e.g., >99.5:0.5 er)

## Research stages

1. substrate acquisition and characterization (`data_acquisition`)
2. peptide catalyst synthesis and purification (`data_preparation`)
3. design and synthesis of candidate peptide catalysts (`candidate_generation`)
4. catalyst screening under standardized reaction conditions (`candidate_screening`)
5. DFT modeling of catalyst structure and mechanism (`computational_validation`)
6. kinetic studies and time-course monitoring (`experimental_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- DFT modeling of catalyst structure and mechanism: .xyz coordinate files from DFT modeling (computational_validation, DU_P084_007)

## Evidence

- P084, PDF page 1, ABSTRACT: "Herein, we report a bifunctional chiral peptide ligand featuring both an aminoxyl and a bipyridyl motif and demonstrate its utility in the copper(I)-catalyzed aerobic oxidation of biaryl diols to furnish axially chiral aldehydes."
- P084, PDF page 7, CONCLUSIONS: "we report the development of a novel class of bifunctional aminoxyl−bipyridine peptide catalysts and highlight their application in the desymmetrization of prochiral biaryl diols that allows isolation of mono-oxidized aldehydes with up to >99.5:0.5 er."
