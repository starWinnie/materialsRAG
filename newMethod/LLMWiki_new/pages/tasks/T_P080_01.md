# Task: Photocatalytic Hydrogen Generation

- Task ID: `T_P080_01`
- Canonical name: `photocatalytic_hydrogen_generation`
- Paper: `P080`

## Objective

To optimize Pt single-atom co-catalyst density on anatase TiO2 thin films for maximal photocatalytic hydrogen evolution efficiency while minimizing noble metal usage.

## Material scope

- Pt single atoms
- anatase TiO2 thin films
- fluorine-doped tin oxide (FTO) substrates

## Target properties

- photocatalytic hydrogen production rate
- charge carrier utilization efficiency
- light absorption
- charge transport

## Constraints

- critical co-catalyst density threshold
- surface confinement of Pt SAs
- absorber-limited (not co-catalyst-limited) behavior
- minimal Pt usage

## Input requirements

- ultraviolet excitation (365 nm or 275 nm)
- aqueous methanol solution (hole scavenger)
- Ar-purged reactor
- geometrically well-defined flat semiconductor thin-layer model

## Output goal

- maximal H2 production rate under UV illumination
- full utilization of photogenerated electron flux
- establishment of critical Pt SA density ≈4 × 105 μm−2

## Research stages

1. TiO2 thin film deposition and annealing (`data_acquisition`)
2. Reactive deposition of Pt single atoms (`candidate_generation`)
3. Parameter screening for Pt loading, annealing temperature, and TiO2 thickness (`candidate_screening`)
4. Photocatalytic hydrogen production measurement (`model_evaluation`)
5. Characterization of Pt speciation and loading (`computational_validation`)
6. Long-term stability and electronic property validation (`experimental_validation`)
