# Task: Resolving the fundamental behavior of pyramidal ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations in magnesium

- Task ID: `T_P085_01`
- Canonical name: `resolve_pyramidal_c_plus_a_dislocation_behavior_in_mg`
- Paper: `P085`

## Objective

To resolve the prevailing uncertainties about the ability of pyramidal ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations to accommodate plastic strain and their slip pathways in magnesium.

## Material scope

- magnesium
- Mg single crystals

## Target properties

- plasticity
- strength
- ductility

## Constraints

- room temperature
- c-axis compression
- submicrometer size

## Input requirements

- submicrometer-size Mg single-crystal pillars
- in situ TEM mechanical testing capability
- 3D image reconstruction capability
- atomistic simulation capability

## Output goal

- determination of slip planes (pyramidal I and II) for ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations
- evidence of cross-slip
- confirmation of glissile (not sessile) character
- explanation for high strength and plasticity

## Research stages

1. In situ TEM mechanical testing of Mg pillars (`data_acquisition`)
2. Fabrication and annealing of Mg pillars (`data_preparation`)
3. Generation of ⟨c+a⟩ dislocations via mechanical loading (`candidate_generation`)
4. Burgers vector determination using g·b analysis (`candidate_screening`)
5. Atomistic simulation of ⟨c+a⟩ dislocation formation and glide (`computational_validation`)
6. 3D tomographic reconstruction of dislocation configuration and slip plane (`experimental_validation`)

- None stated
