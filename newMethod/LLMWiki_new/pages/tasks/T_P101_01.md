# Task: Evaluate LLMs' question-answering capabilities in materials science and metallurgical engineering

- Task ID: `T_P101_01`
- Canonical name: `evaluate_llm_question_answering_in_materials_science`
- Paper: `P101`

## Objective

Establish a comprehensive baseline performance of existing LLMs on materials science Q&A tasks to guide future fine-tuning and RAG system development.

## Material scope

- materials science
- metallurgical engineering

## Target properties

- conceptual understanding
- numerical reasoning
- pattern recognition
- structured matching

## Constraints

- must use MaScQA benchmark
- must assess across MATCH, MCQ, MCQN, and NUM question types
- must compare closed- vs. open-source models

## Input requirements

- 644 MaScQA questions covering thermodynamics, atomic structure, mechanical behavior, and materials characterization
- 15 LLMs (including Claude, GPT, Llama, Mistral, Phi3 families)

## Output goal

- overall accuracy per model
- category-wise accuracy (MATCH, MCQ, MCQN, NUM)
- identification of top candidates for fine-tuning (e.g., Claude-3.5-Sonnet, GPT-4o, Phi3-3.8b)

## Research stages

1. Use MaScQA benchmark dataset (`data_acquisition`)
2. Evaluate LLM responses on MaScQA (`model_evaluation`)
3. Identify top-performing LLMs for future fine-tuning (`candidate_screening`)
4. Compare against random baseline (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- Use MaScQA benchmark dataset: MaScQA (source, DU_P101_001)
- Evaluate LLM responses on MaScQA: MaScQA (test, DU_P101_002)
- Compare against random baseline: MaScQA (benchmark, DU_P101_003)

To guide this future ne-tuning process and establish a baseline for evaluation, we rst assess in the present study the capabil-ities of available LLMs in materials science."
