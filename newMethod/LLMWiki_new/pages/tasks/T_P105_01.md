# Task: Immobilization of Plutonium-Contaminated Materials in Calcium Aluminosilicate Glass

- Task ID: `T_P105_01`
- Canonical name: `immobilize_plutonium_contaminated_materials_in_calcium_aluminosilicate_glass`
- Paper: `P105`

## Objective

To assess calcium aluminosilicate (CAS) glass for its ability to immobilize elements present in Plutonium-Contaminated Materials (PCM) including metals (Fe and Cr), polyvinyl chloride (PVC), and Pu (investigated using the nonradioactive surrogates Ce and Hf).

## Material scope

- calcium aluminosilicate glass
- plutonium-contaminated materials
- plutonium surrogates (Ce, Hf)
- metals (Fe, Cr)
- polyvinyl chloride (PVC)

## Target properties

- chemical durability
- waste loading capacity
- glass homogeneity
- glass structure

## Constraints

- high temperature operation (1400°C–1600°C)
- retention of chlorine
- incorporation of heterogeneous waste components
- no significant change to glass structure

## Input requirements

- simulant inorganic and organic PCM
- nonradioactive surrogates (Ce, Hf)
- industrial-scale trial melts
- laboratory-scale melts

## Output goal

- highly durable glassy matrix with anorthite crystals
- exceeded solubility limits for Ce, Hf, mixed metal, and PVC compared to borosilicate glasses

## Research stages

1. Provenance of Industrially Produced Glasses (`data_acquisition`)
