# Task: Probing out-of-distribution generalization in machine learning for materials

- Task ID: `T_P016_01`
- Canonical name: `probing_ood_generalization`
- Paper: `P016`

## Objective

To systematically examine the performance of various ML methods across over 700 OOD tasks within large materials datasets and identify what constitutes a truly challenging OOD task.

## Material scope

- inorganic crystalline solids

## Target properties

- formation energy
- band gap
- bulk modulus

## Constraints

- tasks must have at least 200 test samples
- exclusion criteria based on chemistry (element, period, group) or structural symmetry (space group, point group, crystal system)

## Input requirements

- ab initio-derived materials databases (JARVIS, Materials Project, OQMD)
- materials representations (embeddings or descriptors)

## Output goal

- quantitative assessment of OOD generalization performance (MAE, R2)
- identification of representationally OOD vs. ID test data
- characterization of scaling behavior (training time, training set size) for OOD tasks

## Research stages

1. Selection of ab initio-derived materials databases (`data_acquisition`)
2. Preprocessing and splitting for OOD tasks (`data_preparation`)
3. Training representative ML models on each OOD task (`model_training`)
4. Evaluating OOD performance with MAE and R2 (`model_evaluation`)
5. Inspecting materials representations via UMAP and kernel density estimation (`computational_validation`)
6. Analyzing learning curves for training time and training set size (`computational_validation`)
