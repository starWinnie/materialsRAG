# Task: deriving exposure-conditioned hydrogen-availability boundary conditions

- Task ID: `T_P117_01`
- Canonical name: `derive_exposure_conditioned_hydrogen_availability_boundary_conditions`
- Paper: `P117`

## Objective

To derive mechanically interpretable, exposure-conditioned hydrogen-availability boundary conditions from reactive simulations of sustained high-temperature hydrocarbon exposure on nickel surfaces.

## Material scope

- nickel
- Ni(211)
- Ni(111)
- Ni surfaces

## Target properties

- hydrogen availability
- hydrogen partitioning
- hydrogen localization
- subsurface fraction
- normalized Ni-associated H inventory

## Constraints

- accelerated closed-cell protocol
- high-temperature (1000–1350 K)
- sustained CH4 and CH4 + H2O exposure
- protocol-specific inventory states

## Input requirements

- ReaxFF-based reactive molecular dynamics simulations
- Ni slab exposing Ni(211) surface
- CH4-only and CH4 + H2O exposure configurations
- three independent stochastic seeds per condition

## Output goal

- exposure-conditioned hydrogen-availability descriptors
- protocol-specific descriptors for controlled downstream simulations of hydrogen transport, trapping, and degradation

## Research stages

1. performing reactive molecular dynamics simulations (`data_acquisition`)
2. post-processing simulation outputs to extract surface-associated inventories (`data_preparation`)
3. computing depth-resolved atomic number density and subsurface partitioning (`candidate_generation`)
4. performing site-resolved near-surface localization analysis (`candidate_generation`)
5. resolving chemical environment of Ni-associated H inventory via bond-order decomposition (`label_generation`)
6. assessing robustness through facet and finite-size checks (`model_evaluation`)
7. performing targeted ReaxFF benchmark of surface-to-first-subsurface H transfer (`computational_validation`)
