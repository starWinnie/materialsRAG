# Task: Investigation of isothermal continuous hot deformation behavior

- Task ID: `T_P099_01`
- Canonical name: `investigate_isothermal_continuous_hot_deformation_behavior`
- Paper: `P099`

## Objective

To study the grain evolution, recrystallization behavior (dynamic and static), and dissolution/evolution of the segregated phase in as-cast AFA alloy under multi-pass isothermal hot deformation conditions.

## Material scope

- alumina-forming austenitic (AFA) alloy
- as-cast microstructure
- dendritic austenite
- segregated phase (MC-type carbide)

## Target properties

- grain size
- recrystallization volume fraction
- grain boundary distribution
- misorientation angle
- texture (pole figure)
- segregated phase morphology and distribution

## Constraints

- multi-pass isothermal conditions
- deformation temperature (1150 °C or 1200 °C)
- inter-pass holding time (10 min or 30 min)
- cumulative engineering strain (20 % → 40 % → 70 %)

## Input requirements

- as-cast AFA alloy billets
- Gleeble-3500 thermal simulation tester
- EBSD and TEM characterization capability

## Output goal

- understanding of microstructural evolution during thermomechanical processing
- identification of dominant recrystallization mechanisms (DDRX, CDRX, SRX)
- guidance for industrial homogenization and forging of AFA alloys

## Research stages

1. Hot deformation experiments (`data_acquisition`)
2. Specimen preparation for microstructural characterization (`data_preparation`)
3. Microstructure characterization and analysis (`model_evaluation`)
4. Analysis of recrystallization mechanisms (`candidate_screening`)
5. Correlation of microstructural evolution with deformation parameters (`computational_validation`)
