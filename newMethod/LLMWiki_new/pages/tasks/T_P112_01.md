# Task: melting point regulation of HfO2 via cation doping and oxygen vacancy-mediated non-stoichiometry

- Task ID: `T_P112_01`
- Canonical name: `melting_point_regulation_of_hfo2`
- Paper: `P112`

## Objective

To systematically elucidate the melting point regulation behavior and underlying atomic-scale mechanism of HfO2 via cation doping and oxygen vacancy-mediated non-stoichiometry

## Material scope

- HfO2
- HfO2-based solid solutions
- non-stoichiometric HfO2-x
- ZrO2-based systems

## Target properties

- melting point

## Constraints

- stable service capability over 3000°C in oxidizing environment
- non-radioactivity
- charge neutrality
- long-term service stability under oxidizing conditions

## Input requirements

- cation dopants (Y3+, Yb3+, Lu3+, Sc3+, In3+, La3+, Ta5+, Ca2+)
- oxygen vacancy concentrations (x = 0.0–0.3)
- machine learning potential with SCAN functional accuracy

## Output goal

- predicted melting point values
- unified three-factor framework for melting point regulation
- atomic-scale guideline for rational design of ultrahigh-melting point oxides

## Research stages

1. construction of first-principles training dataset (`data_acquisition`)
2. training of DP_SCAN machine learning potential (`model_training`)
3. validation of DP_SCAN model accuracy (`model_evaluation`)
4. systematic generation of doped and non-stoichiometric HfO2 configurations (`candidate_generation`)
5. melting point calculation via two-phase coexistence method (`model_evaluation`)
6. MD solidification simulation for non-stoichiometric systems (`computational_validation`)
7. identification of optimal composition for melting point elevation (`candidate_screening`)
8. structural and thermodynamic characterization of bonding effects (`computational_validation`)

## Dataset uses

- construction of first-principles training dataset: DP_SCAN first-principles training dataset (training, DU_P112_001)
- validation of DP_SCAN model accuracy: Experimental melting points and enthalpies of fusion for HfO2 and ZrO2 (validation, DU_P112_002)
