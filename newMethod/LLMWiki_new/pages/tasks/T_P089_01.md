# Task: Optimizing impact abrasive wear resistance of Cr26 high-chromium cast iron via combined heat treatment

- Task ID: `T_P089_01`
- Canonical name: `optimize_impact_abrasive_wear_resistance_of_cr26_high_chromium_cast_iron`
- Paper: `P089`

## Objective

To reveal the mechanism underlying the regulation of strength-toughness synergy in HCCI via cryogenic treatment combined with tempering, and to provide theoretical guidance for developing heat treatment strategies for high-performance impact-wear-resistant HCCI components.

## Material scope

- Cr26 high-chromium cast iron

## Target properties

- impact abrasive wear resistance
- hardness
- impact toughness
- strength-toughness balance

## Constraints

- minimize wear rate
- suppress crack propagation
- enhance load-bearing capacity of matrix
- reduce embrittlement risk under impact loading

## Input requirements

- destabilization treatment
- air cooling
- cryogenic treatment at −60 ◦C
- tempering at 200 ◦C or 280 ◦C

## Output goal

- optimized microstructure featuring tempered martensitic matrix with fine secondary carbides and reduced retained austenite fraction

## Research stages

1. Material preparation and specimen fabrication (`data_acquisition`)
2. Design of five distinct heat treatment routes (`candidate_generation`)
3. Application of destabilization treatment and air cooling (`model_training`)
4. Application of cryogenic treatment (`model_training`)
5. Application of tempering treatment (`model_training`)
6. Microstructural characterization using XRD, SEM, and EBSD (`model_evaluation`)
7. Mechanical property testing (hardness, nanoindentation, impact toughness) (`model_evaluation`)
8. Impact abrasive wear testing (`experimental_validation`)
9. Analysis of worn surface and subsurface damage evolution (`computational_validation`)
