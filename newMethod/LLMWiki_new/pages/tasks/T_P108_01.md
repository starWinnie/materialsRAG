# Task: Carbon Stoichiometry Regulation of High-Entropy Carbide–Silicon Carbide Composites

- Task ID: `T_P108_01`
- Canonical name: `carbon_stoichiometry_regulation_of_high_entropy_carbide_silicon_carbide_composites`
- Paper: `P108`

## Objective

Design multifunctional high-entropy ceramic composites for extreme environments via vacancy-engineering strategy

## Material scope

- (Ti0.25 Ta0.25 Nb0.25 V0.25)Cx-SiC composites
- high-entropy carbide (HEC) powders
- silicon carbide (SiC)

## Target properties

- mechanical properties
- oxidation resistance
- irradiation tolerance
- sintering behavior
- microstructural evolution

## Constraints

- extreme-environment applications
- service performances
- sintering process

## Input requirements

- carbon stoichiometry (x = 0.8, 1.0, 1.2)
- 20 vol.% SiC
- molten salt-assisted synthesis
- spark plasma sintering

## Output goal

- multifunctional high-entropy ceramic composites with enhanced mechanical properties, oxidation resistance, and irradiation tolerance

## Research stages

1. Raw material procurement (`data_acquisition`)
2. Molten-salt-assisted synthesis of HEC powders (`data_preparation`)
3. Composite powder mixing (`candidate_generation`)
4. Spark plasma sintering (SPS) (`model_training`)
5. Comprehensive property characterization (`model_evaluation`)
6. SRIM-based ion irradiation simulation (`computational_validation`)
7. High-temperature oxidation testing (`experimental_validation`)
