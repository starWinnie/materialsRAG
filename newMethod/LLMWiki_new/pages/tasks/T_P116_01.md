# Task: Rapid fabrication of stretchable, ultratough, and rigid polymer materials

- Task ID: `T_P116_01`
- Canonical name: `rapid_fabrication_of_stretchable_ultratough_rigid_polymers`
- Paper: `P116`

## Objective

To overcome the inherent trade-off among stiffness, toughness, stretchability, and scalable fabrication in polymer materials by enabling rapid, simple, and scalable synthesis of polymers that simultaneously exhibit high stiffness, high toughness, and large stretchability.

## Material scope

- poly(2-hydroxyethyl acrylate-co-acrylamide) [P(HEA-co-AAm)]
- P(HEAx-co-NIPAM)
- P(HEAx-co-DMAAm)
- P(HBAx-co-AAm)
- P(HPAx-co-AAm)

## Target properties

- high Young's modulus
- high yield strength
- high toughness
- high fracture strain
- full strain recovery upon heating
- shape-memory
- impact resistance
- adhesion

## Constraints

- rapid fabrication (≤1 min)
- scalable process
- simple one-step photopolymerization
- use of commercially available and low-cost monomers
- green and sustainable manufacturing

## Input requirements

- acrylamide-based monomers (e.g., AAm, NIPAM, DMAAm)
- hydroxyl-terminated acrylate monomers (e.g., HEA, HBA, HPA)
- low amounts of photoinitiator (Irgacure 2959) and cross-linker (PEGDA)

## Output goal

- stretchable, ultratough, and rigid polymer materials with tunable mechanical properties

## Research stages

1. Selection and procurement of monomers and reagents (`data_acquisition`)
2. Preparation of precursor solution (`data_preparation`)
3. Photocuring copolymerization to form polymer networks (`candidate_generation`)
4. Mechanical property evaluation via tensile testing (`model_evaluation`)
5. FTIR and 2D-COS analysis of hydrogen-bonding behavior (`computational_validation`)
6. Functional validation through shape-memory, impact resistance, and adhesion tests (`experimental_validation`)
