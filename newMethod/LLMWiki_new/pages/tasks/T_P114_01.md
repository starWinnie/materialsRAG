# Task: Effect of Steam Oxidation on Microstructure and Hoop Strength of Duplex SiCf /SiC Tube

- Task ID: `T_P114_01`
- Canonical name: `assess_steam_oxidation_effects_on_sicf_sic_tube`
- Paper: `P114`

## Objective

Establish the relationship between oxidation-induced damage and mechanical degradation of duplex SiCf/SiC tube under high-temperature steam exposure.

## Material scope

- SiCf/SiC tube
- duplex SiCf/SiC tube
- SiC fiber-reinforced silicon carbide matrix composite

## Target properties

- microstructure
- hoop strength

## Constraints

- exposed to steam at 600, 900, and 1200◦C for 10 days
- off-normal scenario with compromised end-face protection
- H2O:air = 8.3:1 volume ratio

## Input requirements

- SiCf/SiC composite tubes (32 mm length, 9.5 mm outer diameter, 1.1 mm wall thickness)
- steam/air mixture environment
- pre-defined identical observation locations for microstructural characterization

## Output goal

- quantified hoop strength degradation at each temperature
- mechanistic understanding of damage mechanisms (interfacial oxidation, fiber-level damage, oxide-layer formation)

## Research stages

1. Steam oxidation experiments (`data_acquisition`)
2. Post-test microstructural characterization at identical locations (`data_preparation`)
3. Expanding plug test for hoop strength measurement (`model_evaluation`)
4. Analysis of weight change data (`computational_validation`)
5. Fracture morphology analysis (`experimental_validation`)
