# Task: training Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials (MLIPs) for inorganic materials

- Task ID: `T_P053_01`
- Canonical name: `train_machine_learning_interatomic_potentials`
- Paper: `P053`

## Objective

to train MLIP models that predict both equilibrium and non-equilibrium properties

## Material scope

- inorganic bulk materials

## Target properties

- energy
- forces
- cell stress
- phonon vibrational frequencies
- thermal conductivity
- thermodynamic stability (decomposition energy)
- formation energy
- energy above hull

## Constraints

- systematic underprediction of energy, forces, and phonons (systematic softening)
- extrapolation poorly to configurations that deviate substantially from near-equilibrium training data
- fixed initialization of magnetic ordering
- PBE and PBE+U DFT functional limitations
- exclusion of point defects, surfaces, non-stoichiometry, and lower dimensional structures

## Input requirements

- diverse non-equilibrium atomic configurations and elemental compositions
- structures labeled with energy, forces and cell stress
- periodic bulk structures

## Output goal

- MLIP models that predict energy, forces and stress given an input structure
- models that generalize across materials and reduce systematic softening
- models achieving high accuracy on Matbench-Discovery leaderboard (F1 > 0.9 for stability, ~20 meV/atom for formation energy)

## Research stages

1. generation of diverse non-equilibrium structures via Boltzmann sampling, AIMD, and rattled relaxations (`data_acquisition`)
2. creation of explicit training, validation and test splits (`data_preparation`)
3. DFT calculation of energy, forces and cell stress labels (`label_generation`)
4. pretraining and fine-tuning of EquiformerV2 and eSEN models on OMat24 and related datasets (`model_training`)
5. evaluation of MLIP models on Matbench-Discovery and systematic softening benchmarks (`model_evaluation`)
