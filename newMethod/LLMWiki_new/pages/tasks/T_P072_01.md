# Task: Optimizing the heat-treatment process for X6CrNiMoVNb11-2 stainless steel to meet performance requirements

- Task ID: `T_P072_01`
- Canonical name: `optimize_heat_treatment_process_for_x6crnimo_vnb11_2_steel`
- Paper: `P072`

## Objective

To optimize the parameters of the heat-treatment process to specially adjust the mechanical properties of X6CrNiMoVNb11-2 stainless steel to meet the performance requirement in practical applications such as gas turbine discs in liquid rocket engines and compressor disks in aero engines.

## Material scope

- X6CrNiMoVNb11-2 stainless steel

## Target properties

- yield strength
- tensile strength
- elongation
- impact toughness
- hardness

## Constraints

- quenching from 1040 °C
- tempering at 300–670 °C
- cooling conditions (oil, air, furnace, PAG)

## Input requirements

- as-rolled disk specimens
- quenching and tempering equipment
- mechanical testing apparatus

## Output goal

- ideal process parameters: quench at 1040 °C in oil, temper at 650 °C by air-cooling
- final hardness ~313 HV, elongation 17.9%, reduction of area 52%, impact toughness ~65 J/cm2
- tempered hardness equation

## Research stages

1. Preparation of as-rolled disk specimens (`data_acquisition`)
2. Specimen preparation for heat treatment (`data_preparation`)
3. Design of heat treatment schedules (`candidate_generation`)
4. Fitting of tempered hardness equation (`model_training`)
5. Evaluation of mechanical properties under various heat treatments (`model_evaluation`)
6. Identification of optimal heat treatment parameters (`candidate_screening`)

## Dataset uses

- None stated

## Evidence

- P072, PDF page 1, Abstract: "By optimizing the parameters of the heat-treatment process, its mechanical properties are specially adjusted to meet the performance requirement in that particular practical application during the advanced composite casting-rolling forming process."
- P072, PDF page 1, Abstract: "The ideal process parameters are to quench at 1040 ◦C in an oil-cooling medium and to temper at 650 ◦C by air-cooling; final hardness is averaged about 313 HV, with an elongation of 17.9%, the cross-area reduction ratio is 52%, and the impact toughness is about 65 J, respectively."
