# Task: Synergistically Enhanced Piezoelectricity in W/Mn Co-Doped NBT Ceramics

- Task ID: `T_P110_01`
- Canonical name: `enhance_piezoelectricity_in_w_mn_co_doped_nbt_ceramics`
- Paper: `P110`

## Objective

Enhance the piezoelectric coefficient (d33) of Na0.5Bi4.5Ti4O15 (NBT) ceramics for high-temperature piezoelectric applications (up to 500°C)

## Material scope

- Na0.5Bi4.5Ti4O15 (NBT)
- W/Mn-co-doped NBT ceramics
- NBTWM100x (x = 0, 0.01, 0.03, 0.05, 0.07)

## Target properties

- piezoelectric coefficient (d33)
- Curie temperature (TC)
- electrical resistivity
- thermal stability

## Constraints

- must retain high TC ≈ 650°C
- must operate up to 500°C
- must achieve high electrical resistivity to suppress leakage current
- must avoid secondary phase formation beyond solubility limit

## Input requirements

- Na0.5Bi4.5Ti4O15 base material
- W and Mn dopants at B-site (Ti4+ sites)
- conventional solid-state reaction synthesis

## Output goal

- NBTWM5 composition with d33 = 34 pC/N (100% increase over undoped NBT), TC = 653°C, ρ = 1.16 × 107 Ω·cm at 500°C, >94% d33 retention after 600°C annealing

## Research stages

1. Synthesis of W/Mn-co-doped NBT ceramics (`data_acquisition`)
2. Sample processing and electrode fabrication (`data_preparation`)
3. Systematic variation of W/Mn doping concentration (`candidate_generation`)
4. Comprehensive physical property characterization (`model_evaluation`)
5. Identification of optimal composition (NBTWM5) (`candidate_screening`)
6. Rietveld refinement and structural analysis (`computational_validation`)
7. Thermal stability and high-temperature performance validation (`experimental_validation`)
