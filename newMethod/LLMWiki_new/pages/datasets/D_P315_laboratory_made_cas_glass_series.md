## Dataset evidence

- P315, PDF page 3, Methodology: "Four series of small-scale laboratory glasses (a total of 15 glasses) were made based on the average composition of the CAS glass from the 2019 Tetronics industrial trials: CaO (32 wt %/ 36.0 mol %), Al2 O3 (17.0 wt %/10.5 mol %), SiO2 (51 wt %/ 53.5 mol %), and with compositions adjusted to accommodate increasing concentrations of waste components. Waste additions were Ce, Hf, metal, and PVC."
