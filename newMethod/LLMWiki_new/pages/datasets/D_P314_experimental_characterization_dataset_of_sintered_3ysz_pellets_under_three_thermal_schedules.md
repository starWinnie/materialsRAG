## Dataset evidence

- P314, PDF page 3, 2.3 Sintered pellets characterization: "The density of the sintered samples was determined by Archimedes’ method using an analytical balance Gibertini (Italy) and deionized water as the buoyancy medium. The samples were prepared using the ASTM C830-00(2016) as a reference. The open pores size and distribution were evaluated by Hg porosimetry (Porosimeter 2000, Carlo Erba, Italy). The microstructure was studied by polishing and thermally etching the sintered samples... The microstructure was then observed at the sample center with an FE-SEM (Zeiss Supra40)."
