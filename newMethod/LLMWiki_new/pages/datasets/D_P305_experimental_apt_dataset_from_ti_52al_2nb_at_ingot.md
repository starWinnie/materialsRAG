## Dataset evidence

- P305, PDF page 4, Experimental Methods: "Specimens for experimental APT were prepared using a standard lift-out and sharpening process performed with a Thermo Fisher Helios 650 Nanolab scanning electron microscope/focused ion beam (SEM/FIB) from a Ti-52Al-2Nb (at.%) ingot in as-cast condition. A [001]-oriented grain was identified by electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD)... APT experiments were conducted using a Cameca LEAP 5000XR instrument. Data collection was conducted with a base sample temperature of 50 K and a detection rate of 1% in voltage and laser modes... The APT data was reconstructed using the Cameca AP Suite software 6.3."
