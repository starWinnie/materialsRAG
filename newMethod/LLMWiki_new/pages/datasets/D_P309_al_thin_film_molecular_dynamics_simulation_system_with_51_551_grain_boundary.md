Numerical details: "An initially planar Al thin film of length Lx= 1620.00 Å and height h= 111.24 Å in the (0xz) plane, delaminated from a model substrate on a length b1 + b2, is considered in Fig. 1. Along (0y) axis, periodic boundary conditions are applied such that length of the system can be considered as infinite in this direction. This system which is composed of approximately 220,000 atoms has been generated with the help of the LAMMPS code (Plimpton, 1995), where the atomic interactions are mimicked by an EAM potential for Al (Zope and Mishin, 2003), with a= 4.05 Å, the lattice parameter of Al."
