## Dataset evidence

- P317, PDF page 3, 2.1 Design of Ideal Glass Compositions: "The premix compositions were then converted into oxide compositions based on historical data for BFS, FA(F), and OPC (shown in Figure 1 for SiO2 , Al2 O3 , and CaO)."
- P317, PDF page 2, Introduction: "In terms of chemistry, BFS is primarily comprised of CaO, SiO2 , Al2 O3 , and MgO—while minor concentrations ( < 2 wt%) of other oxides such as Fe2 O3 , K2 O, Mnx Oy , Na2 O, P2 O5 , and TiO2 are present. In addition, sulfate, often added in the form of gypsum, can be present in BFS that is used in the cement and concrete industries. The composition of BFS in terms of its major components is shown in Figure 1 along with the composition of OPC and Class F fly ash (FA(F)) [ 8–19 ]."
