## Dataset evidence

- P315, PDF page 3, Methodology: "Samples were obtained from industrial trials carried out in a collaboration between Costain Ltd and Tetronics Technologies Ltd at the Tetronics’ pilot plasma operating facility in Swindon, UK [ 9, 10 ]. Trials vitrified simulant (half-sized) 100-L PCM waste drums, supplied by Sellafield, containing nonradioactive surrogates for five different end-member compositions of the PCM inventory: (1) high inorganic, (2) high metal, (3) mixed waste, (4) high organic (PVC), and (5) high organic (wood) (Table S1 ) [ 9, 10 ]. Samples were provided from two of these melts (melts 1 and 5), renamed in this study (Inorganic PCM-1) and (Organic PCM-2)."
