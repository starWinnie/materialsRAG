# 11_DPF - Task 1

## Task Description

Predicting key physical and electronic properties of crystalline materials—including formation energy, band gap (both OPT and MBJ variants), total energy, energy above hull (Ehull), bulk modulus, and shear modulus—using deep learning models trained on limited labeled data by first pre-training on large-scale unlabeled crystal structures via denoising reconstruction tasks.

## Supporting Datasets

### [380,743 Unlabeled Crystal Structures (Merchant et al. 2023-derived)](../datasets/380_743_Unlabeled_Crystal_Structures_Merchant_et_al._2023-derived_dataset_39bdea8c48d7.md)

- Usage page: [usage note](../dataset_uses/11_DPF_380_743_Unlabeled_Crystal_Structures_Merchant_et_al._2023-deri_set_use_f0b8bbd9b30f.md)
- Original title in paper: 380,743 Unlabeled Crystal Structures (Merchant et al. 2023-derived)
- Link: None

A curated set of 380,743 crystal structures generated by a deep generative tool (Merchant et al. 2023), filtered to remove duplicates with downstream benchmarks and physically or chemically irrational structures. Contains full crystal structure representations: atom types (from the 95-element periodic table), fractional atomic positions in the unit cell, and six lattice constants (a, b, c, α, β, γ). Used exclusively for unsupervised denoising pre-training—specifically to train models to reconstruct masked atom types, perturbed atom positions, and perturbed lattice constants—thereby learning generalizable structural representations before fine-tuning on property prediction tasks.

### [JARVIS dataset](../datasets/JARVIS_dataset_dataset_22e101c9fff4.md)

- Usage page: [usage note](../dataset_uses/11_DPF_JARVIS_Dataset_dataset_use_3b0d2afbcbdb_set_use_3b0d2afbcbdb.md)
- Original title in paper: JARVIS Dataset
- Link: https://jarvis.nist.gov/

A publicly available benchmark dataset containing 55,722 experimentally and computationally derived crystal structures, each annotated with multiple material properties including formation energy, band gap (OPT and MBJ), total energy, energy above hull (Ehull), bulk modulus, and shear modulus. Used as a primary downstream evaluation benchmark to fine-tune and validate the pre-trained models’ performance on crystal property prediction tasks, with MAE as the evaluation metric.

### [Materials Project Dataset](../datasets/Materials_Project_Dataset_dataset_5c0a4679df96.md)

- Usage page: [usage note](../dataset_uses/11_DPF_Materials_Project_Dataset_dataset_use_24776716bf5a_set_use_24776716bf5a.md)
- Original title in paper: Materials Project Dataset
- Link: https://materialsproject.org/

A large-scale computational materials database aggregating DFT-calculated properties for over 100,000 inorganic crystals. The subset used in this paper includes 69,239 structures labeled with formation energy and band gap, and 5,451 structures labeled with bulk modulus and shear modulus. Serves as the second major downstream benchmark for fine-tuning and evaluating the denoising-pre-trained models across diverse crystal property prediction tasks under consistent experimental protocols.
